I'm a student building a quantitative, source-graded map of what determines human height. Your

The 2016 report gives near-final height at 97.6% of adult height attained, mean age 17.4 years,
bone age 15.3 — height SDS −1.0 for AI/GH against −1.4 for either agent alone (P=0.06), and
absolute change +22.5 / +20.6 / +18.2 cm (P=0.01). Those boys reached adult height around a
decade ago.

**Was true final height ever captured, and was bone density measured at that point?**

Both matter for different reasons. The height because the SDS difference sat at P=0.06 at 97.6%
attained, and the remaining 2.4% is exactly where a duration agent's advantage would either
consolidate or evaporate — this drug class has a poor record of converting predicted height, and
your trial is the clearest instance of one that largely did. The bone density because your 2016
safety comparison covered 24–36 months, and the genetic proof of concept for oestrogen blockade
— men with ESR1 or CYP19A1 loss of function — carries a lumbar spine BMD Z of −3.85 after a
lifetime of it. A three-year safety window and a lifetime exposure are not the same question,
and nothing in the literature bridges them.
