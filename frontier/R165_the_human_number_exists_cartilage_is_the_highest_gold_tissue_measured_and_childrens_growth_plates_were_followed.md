## => ⛔⛔ PART 1 — **THE NEGATIVE, FIRST, BECAUSE IT IS IN EXACTLY OUR TISSUE**

> **`Tonna, Brecher, Cronkite & Schwartz, Arthritis Rheum 1963;6(1):1-10` — "The Autoradiographic
> Localization and Distribution of Neutron Activated Gold (¹⁹⁸Au) in **Skeletal Tissues** and Synovia of Mice."**

This is the only study that ever looked at gold in the **growth plate itself**, and it is negative three times over:

> ⛔ ***"Radioactivity was not seen over cartilage."***
> ⛔ ***"Cartilage cells of both the articulating surfaces and **epiphyseal plate** are NON-LABELED."***
> ⛔ ***"Gold was NOT observed in cartilage."***

⭐ **Gold went everywhere else in the growing skeleton:** periosteal osteogenic layer (heaviest at the
perichondrial zone), bands within cortical bone, hot spots in metaphyseal marrow and along trabeculae,
secondary ossification centres, synovial surface, fat-pad histiocytes. ⛔ **Cartilage alone was blank.**

### ⭐⭐ WHY THIS DOES **NOT** OVERTURN THE POSITIVE FINDINGS — THE DESIGNS DIFFER ON EVERY AXIS THAT MATTERS

| | ⛔ **Tonna 1963 (negative)** | ⭐ **Oryschak 1976 (positive)** |
|---|---|---|
| dosing | ⛔ **a SINGLE i.p. injection**, 10 mg | ⭐ **20 injections, one per week, 0.8 mg/kg — chronic loading** |
| washout before sacrifice | ⛔ **1–2 MONTHS** | ⭐ **2 weeks** |
| detection | light-microscope autoradiography, 5 µm sections | ⭐ **electron microscopy + electron-probe X-ray** |
| what it can resolve | bulk regional gold above film threshold | ⭐ **individual lysosomal deposits in single cells** |

> ### ⭐⭐ **AND ORYSCHAK'S OWN FULL TEXT SUPPLIES THE RECONCILIATION IN ONE CLAUSE: *"only a FEW chondrocytes in Zones II and III contained aurosomes."* A sparse, focal, intracellular deposit in a MINORITY of chondrocytes is precisely what light-microscope autoradiography would fail to detect — the more so after a single dose followed by a one-to-two-month washout.**

⭐ **Tonna's own data show the clearance is real and fast in this compartment:** *"In mice killed 1 month after
injection, some activity was seen in the marrow or trabeculae in the immediate proximity of the epiphyseal
plate, while in mice killed **2 months** after injection, the immediate neighbourhood of the plate was
**free of activity**."*

⛔ **Honest residue: nobody has ever seen gold IN growth-plate chondrocytes. The one study that looked said
no. Its design was the least likely to find it, but that is a mitigation, not a positive result.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — **THE HUMAN NUMBER, AND IT INVERTS MY ORIGINAL OBJECTION**

> **`Lawrence JS, Ann Rheum Dis 1961;20:341` — "Studies with Radioactive Gold", Salford Royal Hospital.**
> Ten patients, **intramuscular sodium aurothiomalate labelled with ¹⁹⁸Au.** Biopsies taken at operation
> from a patient having an inflamed popliteal bursa excised, **dissolved in aqua regia** and assayed.

| tissue | ⭐ **gold, mg/100 g** |
|---|---|
| Skin | 0.05 |
| Subcutaneous fat | 0.12 |
| ⛔ **Synovial membrane** | **0.02** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **ARTICULAR CARTILAGE** | ⭐⭐⭐ **0.64** |
| Fibrin clot (bursa) | 0.67 |
| **plasma** | **0.01 mg/100 mL** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **CARTILAGE IS THE HIGHEST TISSUE IN THE TABLE. 64× PLASMA. 32× SYNOVIUM. 13× SKIN. IN A HUMAN, AFTER SYSTEMIC DOSING, WITH THE SAME Au(I)-THIOLATE CHEMOTYPE AURANOFIN BELONGS TO.**
> Lawrence's own words: ***"There was thus a very high concentration in both the fibrin clot and the
> articular cartilage."*** and ***"Previous workers do not seem to have made any estimate of gold
> concentrations in articular cartilage, whether normal or diseased."***

### ⭐⭐ AND HIS PROPOSED MECHANISM INVERTS THE OBJECTION I RAISED IN R156
R156's hole 9 said: *"cartilage matrix is **size-selective**… fed by diffusion through **dense
proteoglycan matrix**."* **I treated the proteoglycan as the barrier.** Lawrence proposes the opposite:

> *"Dunstone (1959) found that… **copper and beryllium were bound to a greater extent**. This he attributed
> to the greater binding by **CHONDROITIN SULPHATE** of multinuclear ions. **It is possible that a similar
> affinity exists for gold.**"*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE PROTEOGLYCAN MATRIX IS NOT THE BARRIER — IT IS PLAUSIBLY THE SINK. A polyanionic sulphated matrix that binds metal cations would CONCENTRATE gold rather than exclude it, which is exactly what the 64× partition shows.** ⚠ **Lawrence offers this as a possibility; it is a hypothesis, not a measurement, and I label it as one.**

### ⛔⛔ THE CAVEATS, WHICH THE AUTHOR STATES HIMSELF AND SO DO I
1. ⛔⛔ **n = 1 patient, and the sample was 0.03 g.** Lawrence: *"The latter must be accepted with caution as
   only a very small sample was available."*
2. ⛔ **Diseased joint** (inflamed popliteal bursa) — not normal cartilage.
3. ⚠ **The synovium value (0.02) is anomalously LOW** — below skin, and contradicting Cottrill's rat data
   where joint > bone. **That inconsistency in the same single sample is itself a reason for caution about
   the whole table.**
4. ⚠ **Total gold, not free or active gold** — this file's own repeatedly-named error class (R141/R155/R157).

## => ⭐⭐ PART 3 — **THE DOSE ARITHMETIC, BOTH WAYS, BECAUSE THEY DISAGREE**

### ⛔ AGAINST US — the rabbit regimen was stronger than ours
| | |
|---|---|
| Oryschak: **0.8 mg/kg/week** sodium aurothiomalate IM; the salt is **50% gold** | ⭐ **≈ 0.40 mg Au/kg/week, essentially fully absorbed** |
| Ours: auranofin **0.15–0.20 mg/kg/day**; auranofin is **29.0% gold** (Au 196.97 / MW 678.5) | 0.305–0.406 mg Au/kg/week **administered** |
| ⛔ oral auranofin gold absorption **≈ 25%** | ⛔ **≈ 0.076–0.102 mg Au/kg/week absorbed** |

> ⛔ **On absorbed gold dose-rate we sit ~4–5× BELOW the regimen that produced chondrocyte aurosomes in immature rabbits.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ FOR US — the human partition makes it moot
Lawrence's patient had **plasma gold 0.01 mg/100 mL** when his cartilage held **0.64 mg/100 g**.
This file's own conservative auranofin figure (R154) is **Cmax 0.312 µg/mL = 0.0312 mg/100 mL** —

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **AURANOFIN GIVES ~3× HIGHER PLASMA GOLD THAN THE PATIENT IN WHOM CARTILAGE REACHED 0.64 mg/100 g.** At the same 64× partition that implies cartilage ≈ **2.0 mg/100 g = 20 µg/g ≈ 100 µM total gold**.
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **AGAINST A TET1 IC50 OF 76 nM, THAT IS ~1,300× IN TOTAL GOLD. Even at a punishing 1% free/active fraction it is still ~13× the IC50 — and R157's DIRECTLY MEASURED nuclear gold (4.6–12.5 µM) sits inside exactly that band, from a different study by a different method.**

⚠ **Assumptions made explicit:** single-dose Cmax used rather than steady state (**conservative** — with a
35-day half-life the steady-state level is higher); partition assumed to transfer between individuals and
from aurothiomalate to auranofin; **total gold throughout**. ⭐ **The two independent estimates converging on
the same micromolar band is the part worth weighting, not the point estimate.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 4 — **CHILDREN WITH OPEN GROWTH PLATES, GOLD, AND YEARS OF RADIOGRAPHS**

> **`Ahlberg Å & Pettersson H, Acta Orthop Scand 1979;50:513-517` — "Synoviorthesis with Radioactive Gold in
> Hemophiliacs: Clinical and Radiological Follow-up."**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **population** | ⭐ **17 children (16 joints), aged 2–15 at treatment** — *"Children were, **by definition**, those in whom **EPIPHYSEAL FUSION HAD NOT OCCURRED**"* |
| comparison group | 13 adults (14 joints), 17–46 y |
| agent | **intra-articular ¹⁹⁸Au** (radiocolloid synoviorthesis) |
| ⭐ **follow-up** | ⭐ **1–15 y (mean 5.2) before and 3–9 y (mean 5.6) AFTER treatment** — one case shown at ages 3 → 8 → 14 |
| ⭐⭐ **endpoint deliberately assessed** | ⭐ *"The radiographs were also examined for **the time and mode of epiphyseal fusion, and skeletal growth**."* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE RESULT, VERBATIM: *"Acceleration or retardation of epiphyseal fusion or dysplastic skeletal growth was NOT NOTED IN ANY CASE."***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS AN A FORTIORI SAFETY ARGUMENT AND IT IS IN HUMANS. Radiosynoviorthesis puts a deliberately ABLATIVE β-emitting gold colloid directly into the joint of a child with open epiphyses — orders of magnitude more aggressive, locally, than systemic oral auranofin — and the growth plates were followed radiographically THROUGH FUSION for up to nine years with no effect on fusion timing and no dysplasia.**

⚠ **Limits, stated plainly:** ¹⁹⁸Au **colloid** is Au(0), not Au(I)-thiolate (the R156 species objection
applies to the *agent*, though not to the *anatomical* conclusion); the route is **intra-articular**, so
systemic exposure was low and this tests one joint's plates rather than all; **n = 17 children**; and the
endpoint is radiographic fusion timing and dysplasia — **coarse, and would not detect a few-percent height
change**. ⭐ **But the specific worry it answers is not subtle — it is "does gold in a child's joint damage
the growth plate," and the answer over 3–9 years is no.**

## => ⭐⭐ PART 5 — **THE ORYSCHAK FULL TEXT REVERSES MY OWN R164 HARM SIGNAL**

R164 raised a harm flag from `Ghadially & Lalonde 1978`: **intra-articular gold caused loss of Zone I
chondrocytes**, and I said *"zone-specific cell loss is exactly the shape of harm that matters."*
**The 1976 full text answers it directly, same authors, same drug, different route:**

> ⭐⭐ ***"intra-articularly injected gold salt produced aurosomes in Zones I and II chondrocytes, while
> INTRAMUSCULARLY administered gold salt produces aurosomes in Zones II and III chondrocytes but
> CHONDROCYTES OF ZONE I ARE SPARED."***

| route | zones loaded | Zone I outcome |
|---|---|---|
| ⛔ intra-articular | I and II | ⛔ **Zone I depleted (Ghadially 1978)** |
| ⭐⭐ **intramuscular = SYSTEMIC, our route** | ⭐ **II and III (deep)** | ⭐⭐ **Zone I SPARED** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE R164 HARM SIGNAL IS ROUTE-SPECIFIC, AND OUR ROUTE IS THE ONE THAT SPARES THE DAMAGED ZONE. It is withdrawn as a systemic-dosing concern.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **AND THE ZONAL GRADIENT RUNS THE RIGHT WAY FOR US. Systemic gold labels the DEEP zones and spares the surface — consistent with delivery FROM THE VASCULAR/SUBCHONDRAL SIDE rather than from the joint space. That is precisely R156's own stated mitigation: "the RESTING ZONE — the compartment this arm targets — abuts the epiphyseal vasculature, the shortest diffusion path in the tissue."**

⭐ **And the design detail that matters:** *"Two groups… **six mature rabbits aged 12 to 18 mth**… and **six
immature rabbits aged 3 to 4 mth**… Each animal received **20 intramuscular injections; one per week, at a
dose of 0.8 mg/kg**… This **simulates the regime commonly employed for human patients** with rheumatoid
arthritis in the initial loading phase of chrysotherapy."* ⭐ **Chronic dosing, human-scaled, killed 2 weeks
after the last injection.**

## => ⛔ PART 6 — **THE FIVE-YEAR EXTENSION: I HAVE IT, AND IT SETTLES THE QUESTION NEGATIVELY**

> **`Giannini, Barron, Spencer, Person, Baum, Bernstein, Kredich, Jacobs, Zemel, Gibbas, Passo & Brewer,
> J Rheumatol 1991;18:1240-2` — the 88-child five-year open-label extension.** *(Read by page rendering —
> the file is a scan and yields no extractable text.)*

⛔ **There is no growth or height measurement anywhere in the paper.** ⭐ **But the exposure ledger is now
exact, and the adverse-effect enumeration is complete:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **population** | **88 children (68 girls, 20 boys)**, 0.15 → 0.2 mg/kg/day, 1 mg tablets, max 9 mg/day |
| ⭐⭐ **duration (Table 1)** | patient/parent decision **784 ± 350 d** · insufficient effect **751 ± 427** · adverse effects **803 ± 480** · administrative **991 ± 395** · **range out to 1,780 days**; ⭐ **11 completed the full 5 years** |
| ⭐ **dose at final visit** | **≥ 0.2 mg/kg/day in 26 of 88** |
| **monitoring** | monthly CBC + urinalysis; quarterly rheumatologic evaluation under **standardised PRCSG protocols** |
| ⭐⭐ **complete adverse-effect list (18 discontinuations)** | hematuria (3); anemia, proteinuria, abdominal pain, diarrhea (2 each); orthostatic hypotension with syncope, oral lesions, gastric ulcer, tachycardia, vesicular rash, foot vasculitis, one coagulopathy (1 each) |
| ⭐ | *"**No patient experienced clinically significant residua** from the adverse events, and all adverse effects ultimately **resolved** after discontinuation."* |

> ### ⭐⭐ **GROWTH FAILURE, SHORT STATURE AND GROWTH RETARDATION APPEAR NOWHERE IN THAT ENUMERATION — in 88 growing children, median ~2 years of exposure and individuals beyond 4.8 years, assessed quarterly by paediatric rheumatologists, in a disease where growth failure is a primary and closely-watched complication.**
> ⚠ **That is evidence of no GROSS harm. It is not a measurement, and I am not going to call it one.**

⚠ **A confound I have to flag: concomitant prednisone was permitted** (up to 10 mg/day or 5 mg/m²/day).
**Steroids stunt growth**, so even if height data were recovered from the source records it would be
confounded in the direction of harm.

⭐ **One new denominator from its reference list:** `Blodgett & Pietrusko, Scand J Rheumatol 1986;suppl 15:66-78`
— **134 of 413 (32%) adult RA patients took auranofin for more than 5 years.** Adults, but a large chronic
safety base.
