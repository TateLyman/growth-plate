## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 1 — **GOLD REACHES SKELETAL TISSUE AFTER ORAL DOSING, AND IS RETAINED THERE**

`Cottrill, Sharma, Dyson, Parish & McAuliffe, J Chem Soc Perkin Trans II 1989, 53–58` — **¹⁹⁹Au- and
³⁵S-labelled auranofin, 2.5 mg/kg ORAL**, Wistar rats ~200 g (**young — growth plates open**), 7-day
time course, **Table 4, % of initial dose per gram**:

| organ | 2 h | 5 h | 24 h | 48 h | **168 h** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood | 0.93 | 1.3 | **1.8** | 0.83 | ⛔ **0.076** |
| Kidney | 1.1 | 3.1 | 6.8 | 7.1 | ⛔ **6.6** |
| ⭐ **Joint** | 0.12 | 0.24 | **0.45** | 0.37 | ⭐ **0.30** |
| ⭐ **Bone** | 0.11 | 0.15 | **0.31** | 0.21 | ⭐ **0.14** |
| Muscle | 0.068 | 0.12 | 0.20 | 0.17 | 0.059 |

### ⭐⭐ THE RETENTION PATTERN IS THE FINDING, NOT THE PEAK

| tissue : blood ratio | 2 h | 24 h | **168 h** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Joint** | 0.13 | 0.25 | ⭐ **3.95** |
| ⭐ **Bone** | 0.12 | 0.17 | ⭐ **1.84** |
| Muscle | 0.07 | 0.11 | 0.78 |
| ⛔ Kidney | 1.18 | 3.78 | ⛔ **86.8** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **BLOOD FALLS 24-FOLD BETWEEN 24 h AND 168 h. JOINT FALLS 1.5-FOLD (67% RETAINED) AND BONE 2.2-FOLD (45% RETAINED). BY ONE WEEK, JOINT GOLD IS ~4× BLOOD GOLD. GOLD PARTITIONS INTO SKELETAL TISSUE AND STAYS THERE.**

The authors say it directly: *"Both drugs give small but significant gold levels in the **joint**, which
are **2–3 times higher than those in the surrounding bone**… it is significant that the therapeutic metal
**reaches this area and that, once there, it is retained**."*

### CONVERTED TO ABSOLUTE CONCENTRATION
Auranofin is 29.0% gold by mass, so 2.5 mg/kg auranofin = **0.726 mg Au/kg**:

| tissue | time | %dose/g | **µg Au/g** | ⭐ **µM** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Joint** | 24 h | 0.45 | 3.27 | ⭐ **16.6** |
| **Joint** | 168 h | 0.30 | 2.18 | ⭐ **11.1** |
| **Bone** | 24 h | 0.31 | 2.25 | **11.4** |
| **Bone** | 168 h | 0.14 | 1.02 | **5.2** |

**Against the 0.10 µM that lowered cellular 5hmC (Chen 2023, in serum-containing medium): joint tissue at
one week is 111×.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — **AND SHARMA MEASURED IT INSIDE THE NUCLEUS**

`Sharma, Smillie & Laverty, J Pharm Pharmacol 1984;36:441–5` — *"Triethylphosphine gold: cellular uptake
and disposition after single and repeated **ORAL** doses in rats."* Differential centrifugation to purified
**nuclear, mitochondrial, lysosomal, microsomal** fractions.

**Single dose:** *"between **60–80% of hepatic and 50–70% of renal gold was present in the cytosol**"* —
so gold is **intracellular**, not stuck extracellularly.

**Repeated dosing (5 / 10 / 15 mg/kg/day × 3), µg Au per g fraction:**

| fraction | LIVER 5 / 10 / 15 | as µM | KIDNEY 5 / 10 / 15 | as µM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| whole tissue | 1.78 / 2.89 / 4.49 | 9–23 | 9.04 / 13.54 / 16.55 | 46–84 |
| ⭐ **NUCLEAR** | ⭐ **1.04 / 0.90 / 0.95** | ⭐ **4.6–5.3** | ⭐ **2.09 / 2.33 / 2.46** | ⭐ **10.6–12.5** |
| mitochondrial | 1.21 / 1.46 / 1.52 | 6–8 | 1.28 / 1.43 / 1.93 | 6.5–9.8 |
| cytosol | 0.76 / 1.14 / 1.96 | 4–10 | 2.93 / 4.85 / 6.06 | 15–31 |

⭐ And in kidney, *"the **nuclei consistently contained significantly (P<0.05) higher gold concentrations
than those in the mitochondria**."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **TET1 IS A NUCLEAR ENZYME. ORAL AURANOFIN PUTS 4.6–12.5 µM GOLD IN THE NUCLEAR FRACTION — 46 TO 125× THE CONCENTRATION THAT LOWERS CELLULAR 5hmC. THE LAST STEP OF THE DELIVERY CHAIN IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED.**

### ⭐⭐ AND THE NUCLEUS IS **SATURABLE** — A SECOND INDEPENDENT BRAKE

| | 3× dose raises TOTAL tissue | but raises **NUCLEAR** |
|---|---|---|
| liver | 1.78 → 4.49 (**2.5×**) | ⭐ **1.04 → 0.95 (0.91×)** |
| kidney | 9.04 → 16.55 (**1.8×**) | ⭐ **2.09 → 2.46 (1.18×)** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **TRIPLING THE DOSE CHANGES NUCLEAR GOLD BY 0–18%. THE NUCLEAR COMPARTMENT IS BUFFERED. For an agent whose entire thesis is "reach ~50% and do not exceed it," that is a SECOND self-limiting mechanism on top of the 2-OG competition from R154 — and this one is measured in vivo.**

## => ⭐ PART 3 — TWO MORE THINGS THESE PAPERS SETTLE

**1. The active moiety is the GOLD, not the thiol ligand.** Cottrill labelled both: *"the **rapid and
effectively complete elimination of the sulphur label** suggests that the thiolate parts of the drugs are
**not responsible for the therapeutic action**"* — the Au–S bond is cleaved early. ⭐ **This independently
confirms the thiol-exchange model that R156's albumin patch rests on: gold is handed off, the ligand is not
the drug.**

**2. Oral absorption is complete, and formulation decides it.** *"the effectively **complete absorption**
of the administered Auranofin; this is attributed to its administration **as a solution (5% ethanol-water)
rather than as the solid**."* ⭐ A formulation variable that changes absorption from partial to complete.

**3. `Madeira 2013` corroborates the cellular active range independently:** auranofin acts on human
astrocytes at **0.1–5 µM, IC50 0.5–1 µM** — the same window as the Chen 5hmC data. Two unrelated
laboratories, two unrelated readouts, same concentration range.

### ⚠ AND THE HOLE INSIDE THE PATCH, WHICH I AM NAMING BEFORE ANYONE ELSE DOES
⛔ **Total tissue gold is not free gold.** Sharma is explicit that cytosolic gold is *"largely bound to
proteins including **metallothionein**."* **The free, exchangeable fraction available to inhibit TET1 is
unmeasured** — this is the identical error class that cost me R141 and R155, and I am not going to walk
into it a third time.
⭐ **What makes it survivable this time is the size of the margin: at 46–125× in the nucleus, even a
1–2% free fraction clears the 0.1 µM bar.** That is an argument from margin, not from measurement, and I
am labelling it as such.

⚠ **Two further limits, stated plainly:** "joint" and "bone" are **whole-tissue homogenates**, not
growth-plate cartilage — though the rats were ~200 g Wistar with **open growth plates**, so the plate is
inside the "bone" sample rather than excluded from it. And ⛔ **`Intoccia 1982, J Rheumatol Suppl` could
not be retrieved** — the PMIDs returned are unrelated papers; it is a 1982 supplement and appears not to
be indexed with an abstract.

### ⛔ ONE NEW LIABILITY THIS DATA CREATES
**Kidney is 86.8× blood at 168 h and 46–84 µM in tissue** — by far the highest of any compartment. ⭐ That
is the mechanistic explanation of gold nephropathy, and it makes **renal monitoring the non-negotiable
term** — matching Giannini's own exclusion thresholds (proteinuria >200 mg/24 h, creatinine >1.5).
