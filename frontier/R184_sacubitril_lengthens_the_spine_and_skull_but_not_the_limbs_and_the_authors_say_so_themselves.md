## => ⛔⛔⛔ PART 1 — **THE COMPARTMENT. THIS ALONE ENDS IT.**

R183 read only the abstract, which says *"skeletal overgrowth."* **The abstract's endpoint is naso-anal
length — an AXIAL measure. The paper's own X-ray analysis separates the compartments, and the separation
is total.**

> ⭐ *"lengths of the **anteroposterior diameter of the cranium** (P < .01) and **lumbar vertebrae**
> (P < .001)… were significantly lengthened… in a dose-dependent manner, ⛔⛔⛔ **but there were no
> differences in bone lengths of the front limbs and hind limbs**."*
>
> And again in the Discussion, unhedged: ⛔⛔⛔ *"NEP administration led to an augmentation in the
> anteroposterior diameter of the cranium and the lengths of lumbar vertebrae, **with no alterations in
> the limbs**."*

| compartment | measured | result |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ cranium (AP diameter) | soft X-ray | ⭐ **lengthened, P<.01, dose-dependent** |
| ⭐ lumbar vertebrae (L1–L5) | soft X-ray | ⭐ **lengthened, P<.001, dose-dependent** |
| ⛔⛔⛔ **humerus, radius, ulna** | soft X-ray | ⛔⛔⛔ **NO DIFFERENCE** |
| ⛔⛔⛔ **FEMUR, TIBIA** | soft X-ray | ⛔⛔⛔ **NO DIFFERENCE** |

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **OUR TARGET IS `180.3 → 195.6 cm`, AND THAT IS OVERWHELMINGLY LEG LENGTH. A DRUG THAT ADDS SPINE AND SKULL AND LEAVES THE FEMUR AND TIBIA UNTOUCHED IS NOT AN `h_term` AGENT FOR THIS SUBJECT. IT IS THE WRONG SKELETON.**

⭐⭐ **And it makes a flagged-but-never-obtained measurement suddenly diagnostic.** R175 listed *sitting
height vs subischial leg length* as an outstanding operator measurement six times over. **This is exactly
the failure mode it detects: an axial-only gain raises sitting height and changes nothing below the pelvis.**

## => ⛔⛔ PART 2 — **THE WINDOW, AND THEY TESTED IT DIRECTLY**

The effect was confined to **3–4 weeks of age**, and the authors did not leave that to inference — they ran
a dedicated experiment splitting **sacubitril 3–4** vs **sacubitril 4–5** vs vehicle.

> ⭐ *"The growth velocity of the mice treated with 0.05% sacubitril was significantly higher than…
> vehicle **only at 3 to 4 weeks of age** (P < .01), **with no difference at 4 to 7 weeks**."*

⛔⛔ **3–4 weeks in a mouse is weaning to early juvenile — PRE-pubertal.** Mouse puberty begins ~4–6 weeks,
and the window had already closed by then. **Our subject is at bone age 14–16, i.e. late puberty.**

⭐ **The one honest counter, and it is a real one:** the authors attribute the window to *"a period when
endogenous CNP and NEP expression was higher."* ⭐⭐ **In humans, NT-proCNP peaks at 14.1 years in boys —
coincident with peak height velocity (R175, Olney 2012, n=258).** ⭐⭐⭐ **So if the operative variable is
"high endogenous CNP and NEP," the human window is mid-adolescence, not infancy — which is our window.**
⚠ **That is a cross-species inference about which developmental stage corresponds, not a demonstrated
fact, and it does not rescue the compartment problem.**

## => ⛔⛔ PART 3 — **THE DOSE, WHICH R183 COULD NOT SEE**

The dose is a **drinking-water concentration**, not mg/kg — which is why the abstract gave no number.
Converting it:

| | value |
|---|---|
| dosing form | **0.05% sacubitril calcium in free drinking water = 0.50 mg/mL** |
| mouse intake / weight (3–7 wk) | 4–6 mL/day at 12–20 g |
| ⛔ **mouse dose** | ⛔ **~100–250 mg/kg/day; midpoint `~167 mg/kg/day`** |
| human comparator | Entresto max = 97 mg sacubitril BID = **194 mg/day = 2.77 mg/kg/day** at 70 kg |
| ⛔⛔ **ratio, mg/kg** | ⛔⛔ **`~60×`** |
| ⛔⛔ **allometric HED (÷12.3)** | ⛔⛔ **13.6 mg/kg/day = `949 mg/day` — `4.9×` THE MAXIMUM APPROVED DOSE** |
| ⛔ and the effect it buys | ⛔ **naso-anal length 103% of vehicle (0.05%) and 102% (0.025%) — `+3%` and `+2%`, AXIAL ONLY** |

> ### ⛔⛔ **FIVE TIMES THE MAXIMUM APPROVED HUMAN DOSE, CARRYING AN ARB, TO BUY A `+2–3%` AXIAL EFFECT IN THE WRONG COMPARTMENT DURING A PRE-PUBERTAL WINDOW. THAT IS NOT A CLOSE CALL.**

⭐ **And R183's third caution is now measured, not hypothetical:** *"Sacubitril inhibited ERK
phosphorylation in ATDC5 cells as well as CNP."* **The redundancy with `erdafitinib` — which also
suppresses the FGFR3→MAPK/ERK arm — is confirmed directly.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐⭐ PART 4 — **THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST, AND IT IS NOT ABOUT SACUBITRIL**

The histology was done on **tibias and femurs**. Those growth plates **thickened** — proliferative and
hypertrophic zones both. Those same tibias and femurs **did not get longer.** The authors state the
tension themselves:

> ⭐⭐⭐ *"while histological examination of the growth plate indicated an **enlargement of the hypertrophic
> and proliferative zones**, mirroring the effects observed with CNP administration, soft X-ray analysis
> demonstrated… **no alterations in the limbs**."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **GROWTH-PLATE ZONE THICKENING IS *NOT* BONE LENGTHENING. A THICKER HYPERTROPHIC ZONE IN THE TIBIA PRODUCED EXACTLY ZERO ADDITIONAL TIBIAL LENGTH.**

**This is the third time this project has found the obvious readout to be invalid, and they now form a set:**

| round | readout that looked right | why it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **R175** | ⛔ **height velocity / NT-proCNP** | Mauras: **+6.7 cm with FLAT velocity** — the whole effect was in `ΔBA/ΔCA` |
| **R181** | ⛔ **resting-zone SIZE** | achondroplasia **EXPANDS** the resting zone and causes **dwarfism** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **R184** | ⛔⛔ **growth-plate zone THICKNESS** | ⛔⛔ **tibial plates thickened, tibiae did not lengthen** |

> ⭐⭐⭐ **DIRECT CONSEQUENCE FOR OUR OWN SCHEME: if the auranofin arm ever produced growth-plate thickening on imaging or histology, that would NOT be evidence of height gain. Every zone-morphology surrogate is now disqualified. R175's Tier 4 — the hand film read for `ΔBA/ΔCA` and predicted adult height — survives as the ONLY readout whose sign matches the objective, and it has now outlasted three competitors.**

R183 SUBSTITUTED THE ENZYMATIC ROUTE AND IT FAILED ON LIMBS. THE ORIGINAL REASONING WAS CORRECT — AND `OSTEOCRIN` IS THE ENDOGENOUS `NPR3` DECOY THAT DOES IT.**

⭐⭐ **What `osteocrin`/`musclin` (`OSTN`) is, and why it is not a dead end:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **an endogenous peptide that binds `NPR3` and blocks natriuretic-peptide clearance** — the decoy

⛔⛔ **What it is not:** the limb-elongation result is **transgenic overexpression**, not a drug. **There is
no marketed recombinant osteocrin.** ⚠ And whether achievable exercise-induced musclin approaches
transgenic levels is entirely unknown — almost certainly not.

> ⭐⭐ **STATUS: `osteocrin` is the mechanistically correct `h_term` route with a demonstrated LIMB effect,
