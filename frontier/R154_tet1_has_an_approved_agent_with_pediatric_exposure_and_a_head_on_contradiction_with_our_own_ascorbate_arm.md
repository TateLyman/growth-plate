## => ⭐⭐ PART 2 — THE DOSE ANCHOR, AND IT IS CLEANER THAN SPIN4's

> ### **Heterozygous pLoF = ~50% functional gene dosage → +7.74 cm in 90 people. THE HUMAN-VALIDATED PERTURBATION IS ~50% LOSS OF TET1 ACTIVITY. A drug producing ~50% catalytic inhibition phenocopies it directly.**

This is a **better** calibration constant than R140's SPIN4 38–45%, because that was a downstream
transcriptional readout (TOPFLASH/Axin2) while **this is gene dosage — the same quantity a drug titrates.**

### ⚠ AND ONE THING I ALMOST GOT WRONG: **"0 homozygotes" IS NOT A SAFETY SIGNAL**
At AAF 3.22e-5 in 1.45M people, expected homozygotes = N·p² = **0.0015**. **Observing zero is exactly what
chance predicts.** It is **not** evidence that complete TET1 loss is lethal or intolerable — it is simply
**untested in humans**. (Contrast NRK, X-linked, where 34 hemizygous nulls existed and *were* informative.)

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 4 — THE AGENT: **AURANOFIN**

`Chen et al., "Direct inhibition of dioxygenases TET1 by the rheumatoid arthritis drug auranofin
selectively induces cancer cell death in T-ALL," J Hematol Oncol 2023` (PMID 37993905, **open access**).
Found by **structure-guided virtual screening + cell-based HTS of 2,059 FDA/EMA-approved drugs.**

| property | value |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **TET1 IC50** | ⭐ **76 nM** (in vitro fluorometric) |
| TET1 KD (SPR) | 1.804 µM |
| cellular effect | global 5hmC down at **0.1 µM**, 24 h |
| ⭐ **mechanism** | ⭐ **direct, competitive at the 2-OG/Fe(II) substrate pocket** — NOG and Fe(II) compete it off; raising 2-OG attenuates inhibition; docked adjacent to 2-OG and Fe(II) |
| ⭐ **selectivity** | ⭐ **≥13× over TET2 and KDM6B** (very low inhibition of both at 1 µM) |
| **DNA binding** | unaffected (EMSA) — it is a cosubstrate-site inhibitor, not a DNA-competitive one |
| ⭐ **TrxR/ROS confound** | ⭐ **experimentally excluded** — neutralising ROS or genetically manipulating TrxR did **not** affect the T-ALL phenotype |
| ⭐ **regulatory** | ⭐ **FDA-approved (ChEMBL max_phase 4), oral, in human use since 1985** |

> ⭐ **≥13× selectivity over TET2 matters more than it looks: TET2 is the CHIP/myeloid-malignancy gene ("TET2 mutations are important in driving CHIP"; TET2 deficiency promotes myeloid malignancies). Selectivity AWAY from TET2 is precisely the selectivity we need.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE THING NO OTHER CANDIDATE IN THIS FILE HAS EVER HAD: **PAEDIATRIC EXPOSURE, RANDOMISED, FOR YEARS**

> **`Giannini et al., Arthritis Rheum 1990;33:466–76` — USA–USSR double-blind, parallel, randomised,
> placebo-controlled multicentre trial. n = 231 CHILDREN with juvenile rheumatoid arthritis.
> Auranofin 0.15–0.20 mg/kg/day, 6 months.**
>
> **`Giannini et al., J Rheumatol 1991;18:1240–2` — 88 of those children entered an OPEN-LABEL

> "Though relatively well tolerated…"**

**Compare what the NRK arm had: a tool compound with no human exposure, then a molecule that failed on
measured selectivity. This arm opens with an approved oral drug that hundreds of children have already
taken at a defined mg/kg dose for years.**

## => PART 5 — THE DOSE ARITHMETIC

Healthy-volunteer Phase I: **plasma Cmax 0.312 µg/mL gold on day 7, t½ ≈ 35 days.**
0.312 µg/mL ÷ 197 g/mol = **1.58 µM total gold = 21× the 76 nM TET1 IC50.**

### ⚠ THE PROTEIN-BINDING PROBLEM, STATED EXPLICITLY BECAUSE THIS IS WHERE I OVERSTATED A NUMBER IN R141
Gold is carried on **albumin Cys34**; total plasma gold is **not** free auranofin, and the IC50 was
measured on intact auranofin in a cell-free assay. So the honest calculation is a band:

| free fraction | free conc | **TET1 engagement** | vs the 50% target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100% | 1.584 µM | 95.4% | ⛔ overshoot — the Tet1-null regime |
| 20% | 0.317 µM | 80.6% | ⛔ overshoot |
| 10% | 0.158 µM | 67.6% | ⛔ overshoot |
| ⭐ **5%** | **0.079 µM** | ⭐ **51.0%** | ⭐ **ON TARGET** |
| 2% | 0.032 µM | 29.4% | below |
| 1% | 0.016 µM | 17.2% | below |

> ### ⭐ **THE 50% TARGET SITS INSIDE THE PLAUSIBLE BAND — the same structure as R148's rentosertib result: the achievable range BRACKETS the validated magnitude rather than missing it by an order of magnitude (which is what killed moxidectin at 3.5–7% vs 38–45%).**

**And the dose is adjustable downward**: depending on free fraction, 50% engagement needs between **5% and
96% of the standard RA exposure.** Unlike moxidectin, the problem here is potentially *too much*, not too
little — and too much is fixable by dosing less.

### ⭐⭐ THE BUILT-IN BRAKE — A GENUINELY FAVOURABLE PIECE OF MECHANISM

Auranofin is **2-OG-competitive** (shown directly: raising 2-OG attenuates inhibition). And the Dnmt1
paper shows that **reducing demethylation demand makes 2-OG ACCUMULATE**.

> ### ⭐ **TET1 inhibition raises 2-OG → rising 2-OG competes auranofin off TET1. THAT IS NEGATIVE FEEDBACK. The system self-limits toward PARTIAL inhibition — which is the exact regime the human genetics validates, and the opposite of the runaway R137's ladder warns about.**

## => ⛔⛔ PART 6 — THE HOLES. ALL OF THEM.

### ⛔⛔⛔ HOLE 1 — **THIS CONTRADICTS AN AGENT ALREADY IN OUR OWN STACK, IN THE SAME CELL TYPE**

**F-R087 put ASCORBATE in the stack — explicitly to RAISE 5hmC, i.e. to ACTIVATE TETs:**

> *"Thaler, PLoS One 2019 — primary mouse **GROWTH PLATE chondrocytes**, 284 µM ascorbate, 3 d:
> **5hmC +30–90% (dot blot) / +160–790% (ELISA)**, aggrecan UP, Col2 UP, Col10 DOWN… 'delayed maturation
> toward hypertrophy, maintaining cells in a proliferative state.'"*
> And R086: *"TETs are Fe(II)/α-ketoglutarate dioxygenases **with ascorbate as cofactor** — both
> obtainable… **Reaches the same output as DNMT3A inhibition.**"*

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **R087 ADDED AN AGENT TO ACTIVATE TETs IN GROWTH-PLATE CHONDROCYTES. R154 PROPOSES A DRUG TO INHIBIT TET1 IN GROWTH-PLATE CHONDROCYTES. SAME ENZYME FAMILY, SAME CELL TYPE, OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. BOTH CANNOT BE RIGHT, AND THEY CANNOT BOTH BE IN THE STACK.**

**Adjudicating it honestly, and it goes against the older arm:**

| | ascorbate / TET activation (R087) | ⭐ TET1 inhibition (R154) |
|---|---|---|
| endpoint | ⛔ **5hmC + marker genes in vitro, 3 days. NO BONE LENGTH.** | ⭐ **ADULT HEIGHT in 90 humans** |
| statistics | Col10 only **P<0.1** | ⭐ **P = 8.84e-27, 88% replication** |
| dose gap | ⛔ **284 µM in vitro vs ~70–80 µM plasma at oral saturation — a 4× gap my own file already flagged as unbridged** | dose band brackets target |
| attribution | ⛔ **ascorbate is wildly pleiotropic** — antioxidant, and a cofactor for collagen prolyl hydroxylase — the chondrocyte effect cannot be attributed to TET activation specifically | direct, competitive, ≥13× selective |
| ⭐ **passes R131's rule?** | ⛔ **NO — no length endpoint. It should never have been "IN."** | ⭐ human final-height endpoint |

⭐ **And a second, independent contradiction of the ascorbate arm:** ascorbate raises 5hmC = more
demethylation, and the **Dnmt1 paper shows hypomethylation ACCELERATES differentiation and SHORTENS bone
by up to 55%.** Thaler says raising 5hmC *delays* hypertrophy. **Those two results are in direct tension,
and only one of them has a bone-length endpoint.**

> ### ⛔ **VERDICT: F-R087's "ASCORBATE: KEEP" IS WITHDRAWN.** It fails R131's own rule (no length endpoint), its dose gap was never bridged, its mechanism is unattributable, and it is now contradicted by both a 1.45M-exome human result and a mouse limb-conditional KO with a length readout. ⚠ **This does not mean ascorbate is harmful — it means it was never earned, and it cannot coexist with the TET1 arm.**

### ⛔⛔ HOLE 2 — **Tet1-NULL MICE ARE SMALLER. THE OVERSHOOT PHENOTYPE IS MEASURED.**
> *"Mice lacking TET1 are **viable but smaller** and have significantly reduced female germ cell numbers
> and fertility."* *"TET1 KO… exhibited a **slightly smaller body size at birth**."*

⭐ **This is R137's magnitude ladder again, and it is the same shape:** the **partial** perturbation
(human het, ~50%) is beneficial; the **complete** perturbation (mouse null, 100%) is harmful.
**Magnitude, not direction.** ⛔ **It means overshoot is not hypothetical — it has a published phenotype —
and it makes the ~50% ceiling a hard constraint rather than a preference.** ⚠ Mitigating: "smaller at
birth" is prenatal body size, not pubertal longitudinal bone growth, and germline null ≠ adult
pharmacological partial inhibition.

### ⛔ HOLE 3 — **THE 2-OG ARM OF THE Dnmt1 MECHANISM CUTS AGAINST US**
The Dnmt1 paper's own mechanism: *"the demand for DNA demethylation is reduced, leading to **accumulation
of 2KG**. This accumulation of 2KG **decreases collagen synthesis** via upregulation of collagen prolyl
hydroxylase."* ⛔ **TET1 inhibition also reduces 2-OG consumption, so it shares that arm.** The
methylation arm runs our way and the 2-OG arm runs against us; **the human net effect is +7.74 cm, so the
net is favourable at 50% — but the mechanism is not clean and I am not going to present it as clean.**

### ⛔ HOLE 4 — **Tet1/Tet2 DOUBLE KO IN Prx1:Cre REDUCES TRABECULAR BONE**
*"significant reductions to trabecular structure and volume as well as reduced osteoblast numbers,"*
worse with age. ⚠ Bone **mass**, not length; a **double** KO; but **the same Prx1 driver that refuted
verteporfin**.

### ⛔ HOLE 5 — **THE 35-DAY HALF-LIFE MAKES TITRATION NEARLY IMPOSSIBLE**
Steady state takes ~5 half-lives ≈ **6 months**, and if you overshoot it takes **months to come down**.
For an agent whose entire thesis is *hit 50%, do not exceed it*, that is a serious operational defect.

### ⛔ HOLE 6 — **THE OFF-TARGET PROFILE AT TET1-RELEVANT CONCENTRATIONS**
ChEMBL, auranofin: **D3 dopamine 224 nM · α2A-adrenergic 331 nM · α2B 363 nM · D4 394 nM ·
⚠ glucocorticoid receptor 599 nM · adenosine A3 954 nM · Fyn 1197 nM.** These sit within ~3–8× of the
76 nM TET1 IC50. ⚠ **GR at 599 nM is the one that matters here — glucocorticoids suppress growth — but I
am NOT claiming a direction; agonist vs antagonist is unstated. Flagged, not claimed.**

### ⛔ HOLE 7 — **TrxR IS THE CANONICAL TARGET AND IT IS MORE POTENT**
The ratio is backwards in the R146/VinSpinIn sense: TET1 is not auranofin's primary target. ⭐ **Mitigating,
and it is a real mitigation: 40 years of human RA use and a 231-child randomised trial mean the
consequences of that primary engagement at 0.15–0.20 mg/kg/day are already characterised** — which is
categorically different from a tool compound.

### ⛔ HOLE 8 — **FREE FRACTION UNKNOWN.** The whole dose calculation is a band, not a number, and the R141
error was exactly this class. **Nobody has measured free auranofin against TET1 engagement in vivo.**

### ⛔ HOLE 9 — **TET1 IS ONLY 7/10 IN HUMAN GROWTH PLATE.** Low target expression in the tissue of interest.

### ⚠ HOLE 10 — **GOLD TOXICITY:** diarrhoea (the commonest, and dose-limiting), rash, **proteinuria /
gold nephropathy**, thrombocytopenia, hepatotoxicity. With a 35-day half-life these accumulate.
