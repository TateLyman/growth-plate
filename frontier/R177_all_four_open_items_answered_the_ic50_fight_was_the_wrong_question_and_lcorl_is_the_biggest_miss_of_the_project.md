## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 1 — **OPEN ITEM #1 (THE `IC₅₀`): RESOLVED — AND THE ARGUMENT I WAS HAVING WAS THE WRONG ONE**

R176 left the arm's largest question open: is auranofin's TET1 `IC₅₀` `0.076 µM` or is the SPR `K_D` of `1.804 µM` the honest number — and if the latter, is the arm inert at any tolerable dose?

**I had never read the source paper. It is open access.**

> **`Chen L, Ren A, Zhao Y, … "Direct inhibition of dioxygenases TET1 by the rheumatoid arthritis drug
> auranofin selectively induces cancer cell death," J Hematol Oncol 2023`
> (PMID 37993905, PMC10666452).**

### ⛔⛔ FIRST, IT KILLS MY OWN COVALENT DEFENCE — AND IT DOES SO WITH EXPERIMENTS, NOT DOCKING
R163 concluded a **covalent Au–thiolate** mechanism, and R176 leaned on it to reconcile the 24× gap. **The paper demonstrates the opposite, four ways:**

| experiment | result |
|---|---|
| SPR + NOG titration | ⭐ *"the binding of auranofin to TET1 was **competed by increasing concentration of** TET1 substrate analogue **NOG**"* |
| 5hmC dot blot + 2-OG titration | ⭐ *"auranofin induced TET1 catalytic activity inhibition was **attenuated by increasing concentration of 2-OG**"* |
| Fe(II) titration | ⭐ *"increasing concentration of 2-OG **or Fe(II)** attenuated auranofin inhibition"* |
| EMSA | ⭐ *"auranofin **did not affect DNA substrate binding** to TET1"* |
| conclusion | ⭐⭐⭐ *"auranofin inhibits TET1 by **competing with substrates** for binding to TET1"* — at the **2-OG / Fe(II) cofactor pocket**, not the DNA site |

> ⛔⛔ **THE COVALENT MODEL IS WITHDRAWN. R163's structural inference and R176's defence built on it are both wrong.** ⭐ **And competitive-at-the-2-OG/Fe(II)-pocket is a BETTER answer for us: it is the same pocket `R-2HG` and `IOX1` occupy, which makes the whole cross-comparison in R176 legitimate.**

⛔⛔ **And it makes the `24×` gap thermodynamically impossible rather than merely odd:** for a reversible competitive inhibitor `IC₅₀ ≥ K_i` always. An `IC₅₀` **24× BELOW** the `K_D` cannot happen. **One of the two numbers in that paper is simply wrong.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THEN THE PAPER MAKES THE WHOLE ARGUMENT MOOT, BECAUSE IT MEASURED THE THING DIRECTLY
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"Quantification of genome-wide 5hmC distribution in T-ALL Jurkat cells treated with or without
> **`0.1 μM` auranofin for 24 h. Auranofin treatment DECREASED global 5hmC levels. n = 5 replicates.**"*
> ⭐⭐ *"…induced **dose-dependent decrease in cellular 5hmC** and increase in 5mC, as indicated by **dot blot
> and genome-wide 5hmC/5mC sequencing**… **LC–MS/MS CONFIRMED** inhibition of TET1 catalyzed 5mC to 5hmC
> conversion by auranofin."*
> ⭐⭐ On-target: *"overexpression of the catalytic domain of **TET1**, but **not** the catalytic dead mutants
> or **TET2/TET3**-CD… attenuated the cytotoxicity of auranofin."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **A CELL'S GLOBAL 5hmC FALLS AT `0.1 µM` AURANOFIN, CONFIRMED BY DOT BLOT, GENOME-WIDE SEQUENCING (n=5) AND LC–MS/MS. THAT IS AN OUTCOME MEASUREMENT. IT DOES NOT CARE WHICH `IC₅₀` IS RIGHT.**

### ⛔ AND I CAUGHT MYSELF MAKING THE ERROR AGAIN — SO HERE IS THE COMPARISON IN MATCHED UNITS
My first instinct was to compare `0.1 µM` against R174's **nuclear** estimate of `0.062–0.086 µM`. ⛔⛔ **Those
are different quantities. `0.1 µM` is a MEDIUM concentration; R174's figure is a NUCLEAR concentration.
That is the `TOTAL ≠ FREE` error class (R141/R155/R157/R172) for the sixth time, and I nearly published it.**

**The honest comparison is medium-gold against blood-gold:**

| | value |
|---|---|
| `0.1 µM` auranofin (MW 678.48, **29.03% Au**) | **0.0678 µg/mL auranofin = `19.7 ng/mL` GOLD** |

| dose | steady-state blood gold (FDA, R174) | **× the 5hmC-lowering gold concentration** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 mg/day | 0.103 µg/mL | ⭐ **5.2×** |
| ⭐⭐ **2 mg/day** | **0.207 µg/mL** | ⭐⭐ **10.5×** |
| 3 mg/day | 0.310 µg/mL | 15.7× |
| 6 mg/day (marketed) | 0.620 µg/mL | 31.5× |
| 9 mg/day | 0.930 µg/mL | 47.2× |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **HUMAN THERAPEUTIC BLOOD GOLD IS `5–31×` ABOVE THE GOLD CONCENTRATION THAT DEMONSTRABLY LOWERS CELLULAR 5hmC. "IS AURANOFIN TOO WEAK?" RESOLVES *NO*, IN MATCHED UNITS, WITHOUT NEEDING EITHER CONTESTED `IC₅₀`. R176's ARM-KILLING SCENARIO DOES NOT SURVIVE.**

⚠ **Three things this does NOT do, stated plainly:**
1. ⚠ **Protein binding is not matched.** Culture medium (~10% serum) is far less protein-bound than blood
   (gold is ~95%+ albumin/RBC-bound). **The 5–31× margin erodes after free-fraction correction by an amount

2. ⚠ **Jurkat cells, 24 h, TET1-overexpressing leukaemia — not chondrocytes, not chronic.**

⭐ **Net: R174's dose table stops being load-bearing.** The dose question is now anchored to a measured

## => ⭐⭐ PART 2 — **OPEN ITEM #2 (GOLD IN THE GROWTH PLATE): THE ONE NEGATIVE STUDY DOES NOT SURVIVE READING**

Hole #1 has always been stated as *"gold has never been measured in a growth plate, and the one study that
looked was negative."* **The second clause is `Tonna 1963`. I had its full text on disk and had never
interrogated its design.**

> **`Tonna EA, Brecher G, Cronkite EP, Schwartz IL. "The Autoradiographic Localization and Distribution of
> Neutron Activated Gold (Au¹⁹⁸) in Skeletal Tissues and Synovia of Mice," Arthritis Rheum 1963;6:1`**

| design element | value | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| n | **20 mice total** (10 per age group, 2 uninjected controls per group) | ⛔ tiny |
| age | **1 month and 2 months old at injection** | ⭐ **GROWING — so the `Ghadially` age-defence does NOT rescue this. Stated honestly.** |
| dosing | ⛔⛔ **a SINGLE intraperitoneal injection** of 10 mg gold thioglucose or thiomalate | ⛔⛔ **not chronic dosing** |
| chase | ⛔⛔⛔ **killed 1 or 2 MONTHS after that single injection** | ⛔⛔⛔ **fatal — see below** |
| compound | ⛔ gold thioglucose / thiomalate | ⛔ **not auranofin; aurothioglucose is the compound `Kirkpatrick` found INERT to chondrocytes (no phosphine, R173)** |
| method | neutron-activation autoradiography, scored as **"hot spots"** | ⚠ a **localisation** method, not a quantification method; diffuse low-level signal invisible |

### ⛔⛔⛔ THE FATAL FLAW, AND THE AUTHORS DESCRIBE IT THEMSELVES
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"In the metaphysis, deposits of gold **may be expected to MOVE TOWARD THE SHAFT AS THE BONE GROWS
> LONGITUDINALLY**, thus accounting for maximal deposition **SOME DISTANCE FROM THE EPIPHYSEAL PLATE**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"In mice killed **1 month** after injection, some activity was seen in the marrow or trabeculae **in
> the IMMEDIATE PROXIMITY of the epiphyseal plate**, while in mice killed **2 months** after injection, the
> **immediate neighborhood of the plate was FREE of activity**."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE GROWTH PLATE IS A CONVEYOR BELT. IT EXTRUDES ITS OWN CELLS INTO THE METAPHYSIS. ANY CHONDROCYTE THAT TOOK UP GOLD AT THE MOMENT OF INJECTION HAD BEEN CONVERTED TO BONE AND LEFT BEHIND WEEKS BEFORE THESE ANIMALS WERE KILLED — AND THE AUTHORS' OWN 1-MONTH-vs-2-MONTH COMPARISON DOCUMENTS EXACTLY THAT SWEEP. A SINGLE-INJECTION, 1–2 MONTH-CHASE DESIGN IS STRUCTURALLY INCAPABLE OF DETECTING GROWTH-PLATE GOLD EVEN IF IT WERE THERE.**

⭐⭐ **And gold WAS found in every tissue immediately contiguous with the plate:** *"the **osteogenic layer of
the PERICHONDRIAL periosteum**"*, *"osteogenic cells along the surface of the **metaphyseal trabeculae**"*,
and *"**secondary centers of ossification**"* — ⭐ **the perichondrial ring is the structure that surrounds
and feeds the growth plate, and it loaded with gold.**

> ### ⭐⭐ **HOLE #1 IS RECLASSIFIED. It was "someone looked and found nothing." It is now "nobody has adequately looked." Combined with R176's `Ghadially 1976` (systemic gold enters chondrocytes in IMMATURE but not MATURE rabbits), the position is: gold enters growing cartilage cells, and the single negative growth-plate study could not have detected it.**
⛔ **Still not CLOSED — no one has measured gold in an epiphyseal plate. But it is no longer a negative
finding; it is an absence of evidence, and I had been treating it as the former.**

## => ⭐⭐ PART 3 — **OPEN ITEM #3 (HOLE 16, RENAL TUMOURS): THE HUMAN ANSWER EXISTS AND IT IS REASSURING**

R174 quantified the rat finding (renal carcinomas at 8× and 21× human dose; NOAEL margin `9.0×`) and R170
logged one unfavourable human datum. **Both now have context.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ THE BEST HUMAN COHORT HAS AN INTERNAL COMPARATOR AND GOLD COMES OUT AHEAD
> **`Am J Med 1985` (PMID 3970042): 805 consecutive RA patients, prospective, mean 12 years, 94% follow-up,
> 233 deaths.**
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"**Gold-treated patients trended toward LOWER malignancy rates (11 versus 17 percent)** than did
> those not treated with gold."*
> ⭐⭐ *"…at least two treatments, **gold** and prednisone, are **NOT associated with higher risk of
> malignancy**."*
> ⭐ Malignancy types: colon 7, lung 4, breast 3, prostate 3, leukaemia 3, ovary 3, stomach 2,
> ⭐⭐ **lymphoma ZERO** — ⭐ **and NO RENAL CANCER AT ALL.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND R170's UNFAVOURABLE DATUM IS EXPLICITLY IDENTIFIED AS A COMPARATOR ARTEFACT
> **`Semin Arthritis Rheum 1999` (PMID 10622679), systematic review of 1966–1998:**
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"Studies that showed an increased risk of cancer associated with **GOLD** or cyclosporine therapy in
> RA patients are **INCONCLUSIVE as they have used cancer incidence in the GENERAL POPULATION as the
> reference**."*
> ⭐ *"the relative risk of lymphomas associated with **RA** is about **twofold higher** than in the general
> population"* — **i.e. the disease, not the drug.**

⭐ **And the Scandinavian study (PMID 7747148) says so itself:** the excess *"was **NOT correlated to dosage
or duration** of either therapy"* and is *"**consistent with earlier reports of an increased risk of
hematopoietic malignancies in RA patients**."* ⚠ It also reports ascertainment problems in both directions.
> ⭐⭐ **A putative drug effect that is neither dose- nor duration-related is, by standard
> pharmacoepidemiology, not a drug effect. R170's flag is downgraded to confounding by indication.**

### ⭐⭐ AND THE RAT FINDING NORMALISES AWAY ON DURATION
| | |
|---|---|
| rat 24-month bioassay ÷ ~30-month lifespan | **80% of lifespan** |
| our exposure: ~2.5 years in an adolescent ÷ 80-year lifespan | **3.1% of lifespan** |
| ⭐ **duration ratio** | ⭐ **26× longer as a fraction of life** |
| ⭐⭐ **combined with the 9.0× dose margin** | ⭐⭐ **≈230× on a dose × lifespan-fraction basis** |

⭐ **Two supporting facts: the genotoxicity battery is NEGATIVE (Ames, Saccharomyces, BALB/T3, Dominant
Lethal — R174) ⇒ a non-genotoxic accumulation mechanism, which is threshold- AND duration-dependent; and
per the FDA, oral auranofin is `~100%` eliminated by six months with `0.4%` retained ⇒ no lifetime
accumulation.**

⛔ **The honest limit on power:** the two cohorts give **~11,950 person-years**. At an RCC incidence of
~15/100,000/year that is **1.8 expected cases** — **so these data could only have excluded a large (>3–5×)
excess, not a modest one.**

> ### ⭐⭐ **HOLE 16 DOWNGRADED FROM ⛔⛔ TO ⚠. No human renal signal exists; the one human cancer signal is a different organ system, dose-independent, and attributed by a systematic review to the comparator; the rat exposure is ~26× longer as a fraction of life; and the mechanism is non-genotoxic. It is not eliminated — the human data are underpowered for RCC specifically — but it is no longer a first-rank concern.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 4 — **OPEN ITEM #4 (HOLE #2-bis, COLLAGEN SYNTHESIS): 88 CHILDREN ANSWER IT**

> open label extension trial," J Rheumatol 1991` (PMID 1941832):**
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"**Eighty-eight children** with JRA… received auranofin at a dosage of **`0.15–0.2 mg/kg/day`
> (9 mg/day maximum)**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"**Eleven (12.5%) patients completed 5 YEARS of AF therapy**… Mean duration of therapy for those who
> discontinued was **646 days**."*
> ⭐⭐ *"**Parental/patient decision and insufficient therapeutic effect** were the 2 most frequent reasons
> for early termination, **followed by** adverse effects."*
> ⭐⭐ *"**Though relatively well tolerated**…"*

| | |
|---|---|
| paediatric dose | **0.15–0.2 mg/kg/day** = at 45 kg, **6.75–9 mg/day** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **vs our 2 mg/day target (0.044 mg/kg/day)** | ⭐⭐⭐ **3.4–4.5× HIGHER** |

| ⭐⭐⭐ growth failure as a reason for discontinuation | ⭐⭐⭐ **NOT REPORTED. The stated reasons are efficacy and parental choice; adverse effects rank third.** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **CHILDREN — GROWING CHILDREN — TOOK ORAL AURANOFIN AT `3.4–4.5×` OUR TARGET DOSE FOR UP TO FIVE YEARS AND THE TRIAL'S OWN VERDICT IS "RELATIVELY WELL TOLERATED." IN A MULTI-YEAR PAEDIATRIC TRIAL, GROSS GROWTH SUPPRESSION WOULD HAVE BEEN THE HEADLINE ADVERSE EVENT. IT IS NOT MENTIONED.**
> ⭐⭐ **This also re-frames the whole safety picture: the marketed adult dose, and the dose the FDA called "excessive," were BOTH given to children for years. Our 2 mg/day sits 3.4–4.5× BELOW what 88 children tolerated.**

⛔⛔ **The honest limit, and R155 found it first: the trial's outcome variables are entirely articular.
Growth was not an endpoint. Absence of a reported growth signal excludes a GROSS effect; it cannot exclude
a few-centimetre one.** ⚠ **And a few centimetres is exactly the size of effect this project is about — so
this is reassurance about harm, not evidence about the endpoint.**

> ### ⭐⭐ **HOLE #2-bis DOWNGRADED FROM ⛔⛔ TO ⚠. An organ-culture collagen-synthesis result at 1 µM is outweighed by ~155 patient-years of growing children dosed above it. The toxophore argument is no longer the only defence — which was R176's specific worry.**

## => ⛔⛔ PART 5 — **`LCORL` IS THE BIGGEST MISS OF THIS PROJECT, AND `STC2` IS A WHOLE NEW AXIS**

R176 flagged that `LCORL` (+9.99 cm) and `ZFAT` (+7.86 cm) had sat unworked since R144. **I worked them.
`LCORL` should have been the lead gene.**

> **`Bai F, Cai Y, … Jiang Y, Cui P. "LCORL and STC2 Variants Increase Body Size and Growth Rate in Cattle
> and Other Animals," Genomics Proteomics Bioinformatics 2025` (PMID 40094447, PMC12448305):**
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"The **ACT-to-A FRAMESHIFT mutation in LCORL**… **stimulates growth**. Remarkably, **convergent
> TRUNCATING mutations were also found in commercial breeds of SHEEP, GOATS, PIGS, HORSES, DOGS, RABBITS,
> and CHICKENS**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"We validated the two natural mutations in **gene-edited mouse models**, where both variants in
> homozygous carriers **significantly increase the average weight by 11%**."*
> ⭐⭐ *"a seemingly **recurring gene target of body size enhancing TRUNCATING mutations across domesticated
> species**"*

### ⭐⭐⭐ THE DIRECTION-OF-EFFECT EVIDENCE FOR `LCORL` IS STRONGER THAN ANYTHING `TET1` HAS
| evidence | `TET1` | ⭐⭐⭐ **`LCORL`** |
|---|---|---|
| human pLoF height | ⭐ **+8.32 cm** (P=2.2e-14, replicated, 90 carriers) | ⭐⭐ **+9.99 cm** (P=6.2e-33 discovery, 71 carriers; **the LARGEST non-syndromic positive in 1.45M exomes**) |

⭐ **And it is mechanistically coherent with the project's chromatin thesis:** the paper's own mechanism is

⛔⛔ **The reason `LCORL` lost to `TET1` in R144 was druggability — it is a transcription factor, not an

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE SAME PAPER HANDS OVER A SECOND, FULLY-MAPPED, MORE DRUGGABLE AXIS
The `STC2 → PAPP-A → IGFBP-4 → free IGF-I` axis is **complete, bidirectional, and ends in the growth plate**:

| link | evidence |
|---|---|
| **STC2 covalently inhibits PAPP-A/PAPP-A2** | `Cell Discov 2022` (PMID 36550107) — **cryo-EM structure of the inhibited complex** |
| **PAPP-A cleaves IGFBP-4/-5 → releases free IGF-I** | established |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **PAPP-A is REQUIRED in the growth plate** | `PMC11364822` (2024): PAPP-A KO mice — *"**femur length was reduced** and the mice showed a **disorganized columnar organization of growth plate chondrocytes**"*; zebrafish `pappaa` KO phenocopies, **rescued by exogenous IGF-I** |
| ⛔ **less PAPP-A activity → SHORTER** | human `PAPP-A2` LoF → short stature |
| ⭐⭐ **more PAPP-A activity → TALLER** | human **`STC2` LoF p.R44L / p.M86I → +1.9 cm / +0.9 cm**, by *"disrupt[ing] the inhibition of PAPP-A-mediated proteolysis of IGFBP-4"* |
| ⭐⭐ animal | cattle **`STC2` A60P** + LCORL frameshift → **+11% weight in gene-edited mice** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **treatable, with ADULT-HEIGHT data** | `PMID 34358737`: two `PAPP-A2`-deficient siblings on **rhIGF-1 for SIX YEARS** — *"Growth velocity continued to increase and **both patients ACHIEVED THEIR TARGET HEIGHT**… **no… adverse effects**"* |
| ⭐⭐ **measurable in serum** | `PMID 38245583`, n=394 — **PAPP-A, PAPP-A2, STC2, free IGF-1 and IGFBPs all by ELISA** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS AN AXIS THE STACK DOES NOT TOUCH: LOCAL IGF BIOAVAILABILITY AT THE GROWTH PLATE, SET BY A SECRETED PROTEASE AND ITS ENDOGENOUS INHIBITOR. THE DIRECTION IS NAILED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS IN HUMANS, AND IT IS THE ONLY NEW ARM I HAVE FOUND WITH SIX-YEAR HUMAN ADULT-HEIGHT DATA.**

⛔⛔ **But state the obtainability problem honestly:** there is **no small molecule that inhibits STC2 or
activates PAPP-A**. The only obtainable agent on this axis is **rhIGF-1 (mecasermin/Increlex)** — FDA
approved — and it is **downstream**, it bypasses the axis rather than modulating it, it carries
hypoglycaemia and tonsillar hypertrophy, and ⛔⛔ **it ADVANCES BONE AGE, which is precisely what the
anastrozole arm exists to prevent.** ⭐ **So: top-tier target, no usable agent — the same status as SPIN4
and NRK.**
⭐⭐ **What IS immediately usable is the assay panel.** `STC2 / PAPP-A / PAPP-A2 / free IGF-1 / IGFBP-4`
are all commercial ELISAs, and they read **growth-plate IGF bioavailability directly** — a better-motivated
Tier-3 readout than NT-proCNP, which R175 demoted for sign-ambiguity.

### ⛔ `ZFAT`: A SIGNAL WITHOUT A MECHANISM
⭐ Open Targets ranks *"Abnormality of the skeletal system"* as `ZFAT`'s **top** association (**0.595** —
higher than `TET1`'s 0.406 or `LCORL`'s 0.303), and the human pLoF effect is **+7.86 cm**. ⛔ **But the
entire `ZFAT` literature is immune/erythropoiesis/apoptosis/angiogenesis — a title search for `ZFAT` with
skeletal, growth, height or bone returns essentially nothing relevant.** It is OMIM (non-height,
autoimmune thyroid). ⭐ **Verdict: real human signal, no mechanistic handle, no agent. Parked with an
honest label rather than left blank.**

## => ⭐⭐ PART 6 — **VITAMIN C: OPEN SINCE R158, AND THE ANSWER IS AN INTERACTION, NOT A SUPPLEMENT**

> **`Exploring the Ascorbate Requirement of the 2-Oxoglutarate-Dependent Dioxygenases`, 2025 (PMID 39883951):**
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"Enzyme activity is **specifically stimulated by L-ascorbic acid (ascorbate, vitamin C)**, an effect
> **not well mimicked by other reducing agents**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"…evidence supporting **stereospecific binding of ascorbate IN THE ENZYME ACTIVE SITE**."*
> **`PMID 41287077` (2025):** *"Loss of TET2 activity **limits the ability of vitamin C to ACTIVATE DNA
> demethylation**"* — confirming ascorbate is a **TET agonist**.

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **VITAMIN C IS A DIRECT COMPETITIVE ANTAGONIST OF THIS ARM, AT THE SAME SITE. Auranofin competes at the `2-OG/Fe(II)` pocket (Part 1); ascorbate binds stereospecifically in that active site and regenerates Fe(II). HIGH-DOSE VITAMIN C WOULD PARTIALLY REVERSE THE INTERVENTION.**

⚠⚠ **And the nuance that makes the naive conclusion wrong:** the same enzyme class includes **collagen
prolyl-4-hydroxylase** — the paper names *"the formation of collagen"* explicitly. **Ascorbate deficiency
is scurvy, and scurvy arrests growth.** ⭐ **So the correct statement is: maintain NORMAL DIETARY vitamin C;
avoid SUPRAPHYSIOLOGICAL gram-dose supplementation while on this arm. Not "avoid vitamin C."**
⭐ **R158's item is closed with a directional answer and an actionable rule.**
