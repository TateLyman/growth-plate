## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ THE CORRECT MODEL: POPULATION ASYMMETRY

> **Chu NTL, Zhou B, … Ohlsson C, Chagin AS. *PNAS* 2025;122(48):e2512316122 — "Growth hormone regulates
> the stem cell population in the growth plate."**
> - **"GH reduces the pool of slow-cycling, label-retaining stem cells by promoting their differentiation
>   into transient progenitors."**
> - **"Clonal and lineage-tracing analyses reveal that these stem cells renew via POPULATION ASYMMETRY and
>   that GH promotes their COMMITTED cell division, leading to STEM CELL DEPLETION."**
> - GH-receptor deletion in stem cells **impairs their ability to generate chondrocytes** — a direct effect
> - **"provide a potential explanation for certain related clinical observations, such as the DECLINING
>   LONG-TERM EFFICACY OF GH THERAPY."**

**Population asymmetry** means the pool is maintained *statistically* — individual cells divide either
renewing or committed, and the population holds if renewal ≈ commitment. **It is an equilibrium, not a tank.**

### Why this changes everything
| stock model (what I used) | ratio model (what is true) |
|---|---|
| N is finite and drains one way | N is a steady state that can be held, raised, or lost |
| spending it is irreversible | spending is only net loss if commitment exceeds renewal |
| "charge" then "discharge" | **shift and HOLD the renewal:commitment ratio** |
| exhaustion is inevitable | **exhaustion is a ratio failure, and ratios are correctable** |
| a small effect adds a little | **a small persistent ratio shift COMPOUNDS over years** |

**A tank empties. An equilibrium can be moved and held.** That is the difference between a fixed ceiling
and an open-ended one, and it is why the oestrogen-null men kept producing for a decade.

## 4. ⭐⭐ THE HUMAN NICHE HAS NOW BEEN DEFINED — AND IT NAMES A NEW AGENT CLASS

> **Chu NTL, … Adameyko I, Chagin AS. *Sci Transl Med* 2026;18(845):eadw3590 — "A transcriptional atlas of
> the pubertal HUMAN growth plate reveals two populations of stem cells and direct effect of growth
> hormone."** (published April 2026)

- **TWO distinct stem-like populations in the human resting zone**, differing in proliferative activity,
  molecular identity and regulatory cues
- ⭐ **The "root" stem cells express multiple skeletal stem cell markers but NOT PTHrP, and reside in a
  specialised microenvironment LOW IN WNT AND TGF-β.** Marked by **Prrx1**; generate extensive chondrocyte
  clones; differentiate into stromal and osteoblastic lineages
- **GH directly activates JAK/STAT, TGF-β and ERK, inhibits AKT**, and stimulates proliferation of cartilage
  stem cells and proliferative-zone chondrocytes in human explants

### Three consequences
1. **The root population is PTHrP-NEGATIVE.** Every n₀ number in this file was computed on PTHrP⁺ (0.72%) or
   FOXA2⁺ (2.80%). **The root stem cells were not counted.**
2. **The niche is WNT-low AND TGF-β-low.** The Wnt half this file has (R117, R120). **The TGF-β half is
   entirely new — TGF-β inhibition has never appeared in this programme as a niche-holding agent**, and the
   class is deeply druggable (galunisertib, vactosertib, and others).
3. ⭐ **GH activates TGF-β — which is precisely the signal the root niche is defined by being LOW in.** That
   is a plausible mechanism for the PNAS depletion result, and it means **a TGF-β inhibitor is the
   mechanistically matched partner for GH**: keep GH's proliferative drive, blunt its niche-dissolving arm.

### And it independently confirms R129
Zhou 2015 claimed TGF-β/Smad3 rises across the RZ→PZ transition and is the gate. **My GSE9160 replication
found SMAD3 higher in the resting zone and the whole TGF-β axis at chance.** The 2026 human atlas

replication failure in R129 was correct.**
