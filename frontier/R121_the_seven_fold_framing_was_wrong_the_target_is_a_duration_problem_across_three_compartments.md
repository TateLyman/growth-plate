## 1. WHAT I GOT WRONG

`k = 6.99×` came from: BP male SA 16.0 = 98.8% of adult height → 2.19 cm remaining → +15.3 cm needs
6.99× that. Three assumptions inside it, none of them stated, all of them wrong:

**(a) That the gain is a MULTIPLIER on a remainder.** It is not. Gain = **velocity × years the plate keeps
producing**. Framing it as a multiplier makes it sound like a seven-fold drug effect is required. It isn't.

**(b) That stature is ONE account.** It is at least three, and they close at different times.

**(c) That the disc doesn't exist.** It is ~25% of the spinal column, it is set by osmotic swelling
against load rather than by chondrocyte production, and **it is not limited by fusion at all.**
