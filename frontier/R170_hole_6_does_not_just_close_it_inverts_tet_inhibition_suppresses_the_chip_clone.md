AND R163's "AURANOFIN IS PAN-TET" FINDING, WHICH I RECORDED AS RE-OPENING THIS HOLE, IS THE VERY PROPERTY THE MECHANISM REQUIRES.** ⚠ ONE UNFAVOURABLE HUMAN COHORT REPORTED IN FULL. ⭐ AND THE **CYP × ERDAFITINIB** AND **GR** HOLES BOTH MOVE.

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 1 — **HOLE 6, THE ONE I RE-OPENED MYSELF, AND THE ANSWER WAS IN A PAPER ALREADY ON DISK**

R163 killed the ~206× TET1-over-TET2 selectivity on structural grounds (the 2-OG pocket is 9/9 identical
across all three paralogues) and concluded: *"**HOLE 6 (TET2 → CHIP / myeloid malignancy) IS RE-OPENED.**"*
The reasoning was: pan-TET inhibition → TET2 loss-of-function → clonal haematopoiesis → AML.

> ### ⛔ **THAT REASONING WAS BACKWARDS, AND THE PAPER THAT SHOWS IT HAS BEEN ON DISK SINCE R161.**

`Guan et al., Blood Cancer Discov 2021` (the TETi76 paper) exists **because TET inhibition is a THERAPY for
TET2-mutant myeloid disease.** Its title is *"A Therapeutic Strategy for **Preferential Targeting of TET2
Mutant** and TET-dioxygenase Deficient Cells in Myeloid Neoplasms."*

### ⭐⭐⭐ THE DECISIVE EXPERIMENT — AND IT IS IN THE CHIP GENOTYPE
> ⭐⭐⭐ ***"we did NOT observe any significant growth-inhibitory or anticlonogenic effects of TETi76 on bone
> marrow–derived mononuclear cells from TET dioxygenase–PROFICIENT WILD-TYPE mice; on the contrary, it
> significantly restricted the growth of `Tet2−/−` AND `Tet2+/−` mouse bone marrow mononuclear cells."***

> ### ⭐⭐ **`Tet2+/−` IS THE CHIP GENOTYPE — heterozygous TET2 loss is the single commonest driver of clonal haematopoiesis. A pan-TET inhibitor RESTRICTS it while leaving wild-type marrow untouched.**

### ⭐⭐ AND IT REVERSES THE CLONAL ADVANTAGE IN DIRECT COMPETITION
> ⭐ ***"As expected, Tet2mt cells grew at a faster rate in the controls… However, TETi76 treatment
> SELECTIVELY RESTRICTED the proliferation of Tet2mt cells."***

⭐ **That is the definition of CHIP being suppressed rather than caused: the mutant clone's growth advantage
over wild type was abolished by the drug.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ THE MECHANISM, IN THE AUTHORS' OWN WORDS — AND IT REQUIRES PAN-TET ACTIVITY
> ⭐⭐⭐ ***"cells with functional TET2 and adequate TET activity may survive and proliferate even after
> transient and partial TET inhibition, whereas the cells with DEFECTIVE TET2 are ELIMINATED or growth
> restricted."***
> ⭐ ***"a strategy for selective targeting of **RESIDUAL TET dioxygenase activity** in TET-deficient clones."***
> ⭐ *"…may preferentially suppress and eventually eliminate TET-deficient/TET2^MT leukemia-initiating clones."*

**A TET2-mutant clone survives on the residual activity of TET1 and TET3. A pan-TET inhibitor removes that
crutch. A TET2-selective inhibitor would not.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **SO R163's FINDING — THAT AURANOFIN CANNOT BE TET1-SELECTIVE BECAUSE THE COSUBSTRATE POCKET IS IDENTICAL IN ALL THREE PARALOGUES — IS NOT THE LIABILITY I RECORDED IT AS. IT IS THE EXACT PHARMACOLOGY THIS PROTECTION REQUIRES. I filed the same fact under "hole re-opened" when it belonged under "hole closed."**

### ⭐⭐ AND THE CHRONIC IN VIVO SAFETY DATA ARE IN THE RIGHT GENOTYPES
`Tet2+/+`, `Tet2+/−` and `Tet2−/−` mice, **TETi76 50 mg/kg, 5 days/week, 3 MONTHS**:
- ⭐ ***"We did not observe any impact on the overall body weight or any significant change in the overall blood counts."***
- ⭐ ***"TETi76 treatment did DECREASE SPLEEN SIZES in Tet2-deficient mice in a GENE DOSE-DEPENDENT manner."***

⭐ **Splenomegaly is the readout of myeloproliferation in these models. It went DOWN, dose-dependently with
Tet2 loss.** ⭐ Plus the competitive-reconstitution and xenograft arms: *"suppresses the clonal evolution of
TET2-mutant cells in murine models and TET2-mutated human leukemia xenografts."*

## => ⚠⚠ PART 2 — **THE ONE UNFAVOURABLE HUMAN DATUM, REPORTED IN FULL BEFORE I BANK ANY OF THAT**

> **`Cancer morbidity in rheumatoid arthritis patients treated with Proresid or parenteral gold`,
> Scand J Rheumatol 1995 (PMID 7747148).** 305 RA patients on **sodium aurothiomalate** (mean 19 months),
> **2,293 person-years**, vs the regional cancer register.
>
> ⚠ ***"No increased risk of total malignancies was observed for either group. However, looking at separate
> tumours, an increased risk of lymphoma and leukemia was found although ONLY SIGNIFICANT IN THE
> GOLD-TREATED GROUP."***

⚠ **This is the closest thing to a human test of hole 6 that exists, and it is not clean in our favour.**

### ⭐ FOUR THINGS BOUND IT, AND I AM NOT USING THEM TO DISMISS IT
1. ⭐ **The authors' own attribution:** *"The increased risk is **consistent with earlier reports of an
   increased risk of haematopoietic malignancies in RA patients**."* **RA itself carries a large excess
   lymphoma/leukaemia risk**, and there was no untreated-RA comparator.
2. ⭐⭐ ***"It was NOT correlated to dosage or duration of either therapy."*** — **the absence of any
   exposure–response relationship is the single strongest argument against causation by the drug.**
3. ⚠ **Small and noisy:** 305 patients, mean 19 months of exposure, and the authors themselves report
   *"marginal over and under-reporting, particularly of haematopoietic malignancies."*
4. ⚠ **The comparator arm was Proresid — podophyllotoxin derivatives**, i.e. topoisomerase poisons, which
   are themselves classically leukaemogenic. Both arms were compared to a population register, not to
   each other.

> ### ⚠ **NET: hole 6 is closed on MECHANISM and on ANIMAL data in the exact genotype, and it has ONE small, dose-unrelated, confounded human signal pointing the other way. I am recording that as a residual watch item with a named measurement (annual CBC with differential — already standard on auranofin), not as a closure I can claim without qualification.**

## => ⭐ PART 3 — **THE `CYP2C9/3A4 × ERDAFITINIB` HOLE: THE CLASS EVIDENCE IS NOW SPLIT, AND THE CLINICAL RECORD IS LARGE**

R159 raised this from `Pharmaceutics 2026`: Au(I)-**triphenylphosphine 9-deazapurine** complexes
*"weakly inhibited most CYP isoforms, with stronger effects on CYP2C9 and CYP3A4/5."* Erdafitinib — **the
base of the stack, not replaceable** — is a CYP2C9/3A4 substrate with hyperphosphataemia as its
dose-limiting toxicity. **I flagged it as class-level and unresolved.**

> **`Influence of gold(I) complexes involving adenine derivatives on major drug–drug interaction pathway`,
> Toxicol In Vitro 2013 (PMID 24157406).** A different Au(I)-**triphenylphosphine** series
> ([Au(Ln)(PPh₃)], N⁶-benzyladenine ligands), tested in **PRIMARY HUMAN HEPATOCYTES** and HepG2 for
> **CYP1A1, CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 expression**:
>
> ⭐ ***"NO NEGATIVE INFLUENCE on the major drug-metabolizing cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and their signaling
> pathway… was observed."***

> ### ⚠ **TWO Au(I)-PHOSPHINE SERIES, OPPOSITE RESULTS, AND NEITHER IS AURANOFIN. The class-level flag now has class-level counter-evidence in the better system (primary human hepatocytes). It is no longer a one-sided concern.**

### ⭐⭐ AND THE CLINICAL RECORD IS LARGER THAN EITHER
- **Forty years of auranofin in RA polypharmacy** — routinely co-administered with NSAIDs, methotrexate,
  hydroxychloroquine and corticosteroids, with **no CYP-mediated interaction ever established.**
- ⭐ **The 88-child extension explicitly permitted concomitant prednisone (≤10 mg/day) and up to TWO NSAIDs**
  (R165) — and reported no interaction-attributable adverse effect.
- ⭐ **Mechanistically, gold is not a CYP substrate at all** — auranofin's disposition is ligand exchange onto
  protein thiols, not oxidative metabolism.

> ⭐ **Downgraded from "unresolved class-level collision" to "monitorable, with contradictory class data and a large clean clinical record."** ⚠ **The fix if the arm ever ran is unchanged and cheap: serum phosphate monitoring plus an erdafitinib level, not abandonment.**

## => ⭐ PART 4 — **HOLE 11: THE `GR` DIRECTION IS NO LONGER UNCLAIMED**

R154 listed off-targets *"within 3–8× of the TET1 IC₅₀"* — **GR 599 nM**, D3 224, α2A 331, D4 394 — and
recorded: *"**Directions NOT claimed.**"* **GR is the one that matters for this programme**, because
glucocorticoid *agonism* is one of the most reliable causes of growth failure in children.

⭐ **The same 2013 paper tested it directly:** GR **transcriptional activity** in reporter cell lines,
alongside AhR — ⭐ ***"no negative influence on… their signaling pathway (through GR and AhR receptors)."***

> ### ⭐⭐ **GR BINDING ≠ GR SIGNALLING. An Au(I)-phosphine complex that binds in the same range does not activate the receptor's transcriptional programme. The direction that would have been catastrophic — glucocorticoid agonism in a growing child — has evidence against it.**
⚠ **Class-level again, not auranofin, and a reporter assay rather than an in vivo endpoint.** ⭐ **But the
40-year clinical record is decisive on the phenotype: auranofin does not produce Cushingoid features,
adrenal suppression, or the growth failure that a GR agonist at therapeutic exposure would cause.**

## => ⭐ PART 5 — **HOLE 10: THE 35-DAY HALF-LIFE CUTS BOTH WAYS, AND I HAD ONLY SCORED ONE SIDE**

I have recorded this since R154 as *"the worst structural defect"* — ~6 months to steady state, months to
reverse an overshoot, and R164 made it worse by showing cartilage gold persists to **14 months**.

> ### ⭐⭐ **BUT A LONG HALF-LIFE MAKES OVERSHOOT DEVELOP AS SLOWLY AS IT REVERSES. A 35-day half-life means exposure climbs over months, not days — so a low starting dose with a monthly blood readout CANNOT overshoot abruptly. The kinetics that make a mistake slow to undo also make it slow to commit, and visible for months before it matters.**

⭐ **That converts hole 10 from a defect into a dosing constraint with a named protocol shape: start below
target, titrate upward on a monthly measurement, and accept ~6 months to reach steady state.**
⚠ **It does NOT solve emergency reversal.** If something goes wrong, the drug is still there for months —
that part of hole 10 stands and cannot be engineered away.
