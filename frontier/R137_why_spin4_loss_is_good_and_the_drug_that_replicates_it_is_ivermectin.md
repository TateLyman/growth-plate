## => ⭐⭐ PART FOUR: METFORMIN — THE SPIN4 STORY IS DEAD, AND THE REAL RESULT IS BETTER THAN THE ONE I WROTE

### ⛔ FIRST, WITHDRAW R136's FRAMING

R136 presented metformin as the cleanest SPIN4-lowering hit and speculated it might act on that node.
**The Wnt panel refutes it outright: within metformin's own SPIN4-down signatures, canonical Wnt

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE ACTUAL RESULT, NOW READ IN FULL — `de Zegher 2017`, randomised, ISRCTN84749320, n=34, BoneXpert

Metformin **425 mg/day for 2 years then 850 mg/day for 2 years**, once daily at dinner, from age 8.
Inclusion required **BMI < 22 — these girls were NOT obese** (mean BMI 18.4 at entry).

| | untreated (n=17) | metformin (n=17) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ratio ΔBA / ΔCA (GP)** | **1.16 ± 0.06** | ⭐ **0.96 ± 0.06** | **≤0.05** |
| Ratio ΔBA / ΔCA (TW2) | 1.15 ± 0.07 | **0.96 ± 0.06** | ≤0.05 |
| Δ(BA − CA) over 4 yr | **+0.7 yr** | ⭐ **−0.2 yr** | ≤0.05 |
| height, **cm per CHRONOLOGICAL year** | 5.9 ± 0.2 | 6.4 ± 0.2 | **NS** |
| ⭐⭐ **height, cm per BONE-AGE year (GP)** | **5.3 ± 0.4** | ⭐ **7.0 ± 0.5** | ⭐ **≤0.01 → +32%** |
| height, cm per bone-age year (TW2) | 5.5 ± 0.4 | **7.0 ± 0.5** | ≤0.01 |
| age at menarche | 11.5 ± 0.1 | **12.6 ± 0.2** | ≤0.005 |
| hepatic fat | 18 ± 2 % | **9 ± 1 %** | ≤0.005 |
| **BMD / BMC / lean mass** | — | — | ⭐ **NO DIFFERENCE** |

Tempo of bone maturation correlated with **hepatic fat, R = 0.55, p < 0.001** — and the between-group
difference was **independent of chronological age, follow-up time and BMI.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS THE EXACT INVERSE OF KAMP 2002.**
> **Kamp (R131):** high-dose GH — height velocity **up**, *"height SDS for bone age was NOT
> significantly different between groups."* **All rate, no yield.**

>
> **METFORMIN IS A PURE YIELD AGENT WITH ZERO RATE COST — THE MIRROR IMAGE OF GH ABOVE THRESHOLD.
> In `hunziker1994`'s law A ∝ throughput^−0.150, GH raises throughput and pays the exponent.
> Metformin does not touch throughput at all; it LOWERS THE MATURATION DENOMINATOR.**

⭐⭐ **AND IT IS THE FIRST AGENT IN THIS FILE THAT RELAXES ANOTHER AGENT'S CEILING RATHER THAN ADDING
AN INCREMENT.** R131's central operational line is that GH must stay at 0.24–0.37 mg/kg/wk because

### THE TWO OBJECTIONS THAT SURVIVE, HONESTLY WEIGHTED

1. ⚠ **POPULATION — weakened but not removed.** These girls had **already-accelerated** maturation

   are different and additive; if it runs through adipose aromatase, anastrozole already owns it.**
