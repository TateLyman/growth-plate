## => ⛔⛔ PART 1 — THE PAPER THAT CLOSES THE COMPARTMENT GAP, AND IT IS NEGATIVE

R158 ended saying the whole remaining disagreement was whether MSC/bone-mass biology transfers to the
growth plate, **and that nobody had looked there.** Someone had.

> **`Smeriglio, Grandi, Taylor, Zalc & Bhutani, JBMR Plus 2020` (PMC7587462, PMID 33134768) —
> Stanford Department of Orthopaedic Surgery —
> "TET1 Directs Chondrogenic Differentiation by Regulating **SOX9**-Dependent Activation of **Col2a1** and **Acan**."**

| finding | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ *"**Knockdown of Tet1 via shRNAs BLOCKED ATDC5 chondrogenic differentiation**"* | Tet1 KD → 40–60% loss of total 5hmC |
| ⛔ *"**54% of downregulated genes were SOX9 targets**"* | the master chondrogenic programme |
| ⛔ *"**SOX9 is unable to bind to Col2a1 and Acan after Tet1 KD**, despite no changes in SOX9 levels"* | mechanism is loss of SOX9 access |
| ⛔⛔ *"we compared this… with the genetic loss of TET1 **in the growth plate** using **Tet1−/− embryos, which are approximately 10% smaller**"* | ⛔ **a BODY LENGTH endpoint** |
| ⛔ **E13.5: WT 10.3 ± 0.2 mm vs Tet1−/− 9.7 ± 0.3 mm** | ⛔ **−6%** |
| ⛔ **E17.5: WT 20.8 ± 1.0 mm**, Tet1−/− ~10% smaller | ⛔ **−10%** |

**I searched for a heterozygote in this paper: `Tet1+/−` appears ZERO times. It is nulls only.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — AND THAT COMPLETES THE LADDER WITH **BODY LENGTH AT BOTH ENDS, IN THE SAME SPECIES**

| gene dosage | system | endpoint | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **0% (Tet1−/− null)** | mouse embryo, growth plate | **body length** | ⛔ **−6% (E13.5), −10% (E17.5)** |
| ⭐ **~50% (Tet1+/− het)** | mouse adult, IMPC DXA | **body length** | ⭐ **+0.28 SD, male p = 0.024** |
| ⭐ **~50% (human het pLoF)** | 1.45M exomes | **adult height** | ⭐ **+7.74 cm, P = 8.84e-27** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS A NON-MONOTONIC DOSE–RESPONSE MEASURED ON THE SAME ENDPOINT — BODY LENGTH — IN THE SAME SPECIES AT TWO GENE DOSAGES, PLUS THE HUMAN AT THE SAME DOSAGE AS THE BENEFICIAL MOUSE. It is the strongest form of R137's magnitude ladder this file has ever produced.**
>
> ### ⛔⛔ **AND IT IS ALSO THE SHARPEST WARNING. THE DIRECTION DOES NOT MERELY WEAKEN PAST 50% — IT FLIPS. Overshoot does not fail to help; it actively SHORTENS, by up to 10%. Nobody knows where between 50% and 100% the sign changes.**

⭐ **Which promotes the two self-limiting brakes from "nice to have" to load-bearing.** R154's 2-OG
competition and R157's measured **nuclear saturation** (3× dose → 0–18% change in nuclear gold) are now
the only things standing between the therapeutic window and a regime that shortens bone.

### ⭐ ONE GENUINELY FAVOURABLE DETAIL INSIDE THE BAD NEWS
> *"In E17.5 Tet1−/− embryos, **loss of SOX9 target gene expression is MORE MODEST than upon Tet1 KD in
> vitro**."*

⭐ **The intact animal buffers TET1 loss better than a dish does** — consistent with Tet2/Tet3 redundancy,
and consistent with the nuclear-saturation and 2-OG brakes. **The in vivo system resists the perturbation.**

### ⚠ AND THE COMPARTMENT/TIMING DISTINCTION, WHICH IS REAL BUT NOT A GET-OUT
Smeriglio measures **E13.5 and E17.5 — embryonic chondrogenesis**: mesenchymal condensation, SOX9
switching on, the cartilage anlage being **built**. Our target is a **BA-16 adolescent** whose skeleton is
long since built, where the question is the **resting-zone renewal:commitment ratio** in an established
plate. ⭐ **"TET1 is required to BUILD cartilage" is a different claim from "partial TET1 inhibition shifts
renewal:commitment in an existing growth plate."** ⚠ **But this is still the closest endpoint to ours that
exists, and at complete loss it is negative. I am not going to file that under "different compartment"
and move on.**

## => ⚠⚠ PART 3 — NEW HOLE #1: **THE `ACAN` CONFLICT, AND IT IS SHARP**

Smeriglio's mechanism is that Tet1 KD stops SOX9 binding **Col2a1 and Acan**.

> ### ⛔⛔ **`ACAN` IS THE MOST HEIGHT-NEGATIVE GENE IN THE ENTIRE kosmicki TABLE: pLoF = −14.1 cm/allele (P = 3.79e-93, 99 carriers). The single largest negative effect of the 17.**

**So the precise mechanism by which TET1 loss could hurt height is the downregulation of the one gene
whose loss costs more height than any other.** That is not a vague worry — it is a named, quantified
failure mode.

⭐ **The counter is empirical and it holds: human TET1 heterozygotes are +7.74 cm.** Whatever happens to
ACAN expression at 50% TET1 dosage, **the net in 90 people is strongly positive.** ⭐ **But this now tells
us exactly WHAT breaks on overshoot, and why the ladder flips: past some threshold, the SOX9→Acan/Col2a1
axis fails, and ACAN is worth −14.1 cm.**

## => ⚠ PART 4 — NEW HOLE #2: **A CYP COLLISION WITH THE BASE OF OUR OWN STACK**

I had never examined auranofin against the drugs already in the stack. `Pharmaceutics 2026`
(PMC13210512), ADME profiling of **Au(I)–phosphine complexes**:

> *"Both complexes **weakly inhibited most CYP isoforms, with stronger effects on CYP2C9 and CYP3A4/5**.
> A **non-competitive** inhibition mechanism was observed."*

| stack drug | primary metabolism | ⚠ collision? |
|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **Erdafitinib** — **base, not replaceable** | **CYP2C9 and CYP3A4** | ⚠⚠ **BOTH of the isoforms gold(I) phosphines hit hardest** |
| **Anastrozole** — base | CYP3A4 (also 1A2, 2C8/9) | ⚠ affected |
| Metformin | renal, OCT — not CYP | ✅ no collision |

> ⚠ **Erdafitinib has a narrow window — hyperphosphataemia is its dose-limiting toxicity — so anything that raises its exposure matters. This is a real, previously unexamined interaction.**

⚠ **Honest limit: those complexes are Au(I)-triphenylphosphine 9-deazapurines, NOT auranofin
(Au(I)-triethylphosphine-thioglucose). Same class, different molecule. This is a CLASS-LEVEL FLAG, not a
measurement on auranofin** — and the fix, if the arm ever ran, is phosphate monitoring and erdafitinib
dose review, not abandonment.
