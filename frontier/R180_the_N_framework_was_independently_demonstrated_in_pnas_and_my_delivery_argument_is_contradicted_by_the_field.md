### ⭐⭐ THE METAPHYSEAL-BAND NEGATIVE IS WORTH INTERPRETING, IN BOTH DIRECTIONS
Metals that deposit at the zone of provisional calcification — **lead, bismuth** — produce dense metaphyseal
bands that paediatric radiologists have described for a century. **Gold never has**, despite decades of
gold-treated children who all had serial hand and wrist films for JRA monitoring.
- ⛔ **Against us:** gold does not deposit at the physis in radiographically detectable amounts.
- ⭐ **For us, and it is the stronger reading:** a band requires **bone-seeking substitution into
  hydroxyapatite at gram-scale body burden**. Lead does that. **Gold is a thiophilic soft metal that binds
  protein cysteines, with a total body burden in milligrams**; at Gottlieb's `5–11 µg/g` it would be
  radiographically invisible. **The absence is EXPECTED and is not evidence against tissue penetration.**
- ⭐⭐ **What it IS good evidence for: no gold-treated child has been reported with a metaphyseal, physeal or
  epiphyseal radiographic abnormality — from a population that was radiographed serially for years. That is
  real negative safety evidence for hole #2-bis and for growth.**
