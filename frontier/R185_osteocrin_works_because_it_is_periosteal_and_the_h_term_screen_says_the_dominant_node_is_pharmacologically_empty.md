## => ⭐⭐⭐⭐ PART 1 — **THE MECHANISM QUESTION IS ANSWERED, AND THE ANSWER IS LOCALISATION**

R184 left a dissociation unexplained: **osteocrin overexpression elongated limbs; sacubitril did not.** Both
raise CNP availability. Three papers resolve it.

> **`Osteocrin is a specific ligand of the natriuretic peptide clearance receptor that modulates bone
> growth`, J Biol Chem 2007 (PMID 17951249):**
> ⭐⭐ **OSTN binds `NPR-C` with `Kd ≈ 5 nM`, with NO binding to GC-A or GC-B** — ⭐⭐⭐ **so it blocks
> clearance without any natriuretic/blood-pressure activity of its own.**
> ⭐ OSTN-transgenic mice: **elongated bones**, elevated bone cGMP. ⚠ **and "a marked kyphosis."**

> **`Circulating osteocrin stimulates bone growth by limiting CNP clearance`, JCI 2017 (PMID 28990933):**
> ⭐⭐⭐ **dose-dependent skeletal overgrowth**, ⭐⭐⭐ **abolished on CNP- OR NPR-C-depleted backgrounds** —
> a double genetic proof; ⭐⭐ **CNP + OSTN double-transgenic gave ADDITIVE bone-length gains over CNP alone.**

> **`Mechanical load regulates bone growth via periosteal Osteocrin`, Cell Reports 2021 (PMID 34260913):**
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **OSTN is produced by PERIOSTEAL OSTEOBLASTS and "binds to `NPR3` in periosteal osteoprogenitors,
> thereby preventing NPR3-mediated clearance of CNP"** — it protects CNP **in the periosteum, immediately
> adjacent to the growth plate.**

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE DISSOCIATION EXPLAINED
| | acts on | acts **where** | limbs |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **sacubitril** | `NEP` (enzymatic clearance) | ⛔ **SYSTEMIC — circulating CNP** | ⛔⛔ **nothing** (R184) |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **osteocrin** | `NPR3` (receptor clearance) | ⭐⭐⭐ **LOCAL — periosteum of long bones** | ⭐⭐⭐ **elongation** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CNP THAT LENGTHENS A LIMB IS *LOCAL PERIOSTEAL* CNP, NOT CIRCULATING CNP. A SYSTEMIC ENZYME INHIBITOR RAISES THE WRONG POOL AND LANDS ON THE AXIAL SKELETON; A LOCALLY-PRODUCED RECEPTOR DECOY RAISES THE RIGHT ONE. THAT IS WHY R184's RESULT SPLIT BY COMPARTMENT.**
⭐ **And it is the same anatomy this project keeps returning to:** the **perichondrial/periosteal ring** —
the structure `Tonna 1963` and `Roberts 1937` both found loads with gold, and the structure that surrounds
and feeds the physis.

## => ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — **AND LOADING IS THE PHYSIOLOGICAL SWITCH FOR IT**

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**physiological loading induces `Ostn` expression in periosteal osteoblasts by suppressing
> Forkhead box protein O1 (`FoxO1`)**… OSTN [is] a mechanotransducer converting mechanical loading to
> CNP-dependent bone formation."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **MECHANICAL LOADING IS AN OBTAINABLE, ZERO-DRUG INTERVENTION ACTING ON THE EXACT NODE R182 IDENTIFIED AS CORRECT (`NPR3` RECEPTOR CLEARANCE), IN THE EXACT PLACE THAT MATTERS (PERIOSTEUM OF LONG BONES), THROUGH THE PHYSIOLOGICAL MECHANOTRANSDUCER FOR LOAD-INDUCED LONG BONE GROWTH.**

⭐⭐ **And it is measurable:** `OSTN`/musclin is an **exercise-induced myokine** (PMID 38954528 —
*"Dynamic Response of Musclin… to Aerobic Exercise and Its Interplay With Natriuretic Peptides and
Receptor C"*), with ⭐ **paediatric serum reference values published** (PMID 42181195, 2026).

### ⛔⛔ THE HONEST LIMIT, AND IT IS SERIOUS
⛔⛔ **I searched for human evidence that loading increases bone LENGTH and did not find it.** The
unilateral-loading literature (racquet athletes, occupational asymmetry) is about **cortical area, density
and geometry** — I found **no study showing limb-length asymmetry** in the loaded limb. ⚠ **The mechanism
is demonstrated in mice; the human phenotype of loading is a thicker bone, not a longer one.**
> ⚠⚠ **So loading is mechanistically correct and phenotypically unproven for length in humans. I am
> recording it as a free, safe, plausibly-additive adjunct — NOT as an `h_term` agent.**

## => ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ PART 3 — **`COOPER 2013` REDEFINES `h_term`, AND IT LOOPS BACK TO AN AXIS WE ALREADY HAVE**

> **`Multiple phases of chondrocyte enlargement underlie differences in skeletal proportions`,
> Nature 2013 (PMID 23485973, PMC3606657) — quantitative phase microscopy.**
>
> ⭐⭐⭐ *"The **largest contribution to the lengthening of a skeletal element**, and to the differential
> elongation of elements, comes from a dramatic **increase in the volume of hypertrophic chondrocytes**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ **Three distinct phases**, including *"a phase of massive cell swelling in which the cellular dry
> mass is significantly diluted."*
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"it is the **duration of the FINAL phase** of volume enlargement by proportional dry mass increase
> at low density **that varies most between rapidly and slowly elongating growth plates**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"this **third phase is locally regulated through a mechanism dependent on INSULIN-LIKE GROWTH
> FACTOR**."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **`h_term`'s DOMINANT VARIABLE IS NOT CNP. IT IS THE DURATION OF AN IGF-DEPENDENT, *LOCALLY REGULATED* SWELLING PHASE. AND THE SETTER OF LOCAL FREE IGF-I IS THE `PAPP-A / PAPP-A2 / STC2 / IGFBP-4` AXIS — WHICH R177 CALLED "A WHOLE NEW AXIS THE STACK DOES NOT TOUCH," AND WHICH R181 THEN SHOWED **`TET1` REGULATES VIA `Pappa2`**.**
> ⭐⭐⭐ **THE STACK MAY ALREADY BE TOUCHING `h_term`, THROUGH THE ARM IT ALREADY HAS.**

### ⛔⛔⛔ AND THAT SHARPENS R181's COUNTER-PATHWAY AGAINST US
R181 found `Tet` loss → ↓`Pappa2` → ↓free IGF-I, and classified it as a **bone-mass** concern.
> ⛔⛔⛔ **IF PHASE 3 — THE DOMINANT DETERMINANT OF ELONGATION — IS IGF-DEPENDENT, THEN ↓FREE IGF-I DOES NOT
