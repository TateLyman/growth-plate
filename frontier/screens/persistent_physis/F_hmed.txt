A 32-year-old man presented with knee pain. He had undergone electric shock treatment 
17 years ago, which had incidentally caused a left femoral shaft fracture. Lateral knee 
radiography showed a normal right knee joint and a persistent distal femoral physis line 
in the left knee. Sagittal T1-weighted magnetic resonance imaging clearly revealed the 
persistent left distal femoral physis (arrows) ( Figure 1a), whereas magnetic resonance 
imaging of the right knee was normal (Figure 1b). The distal femoral physis line that is seen 
in children has usually resolved spontaneously by adulthood (Zbojniewicz and Laor, 2014).
