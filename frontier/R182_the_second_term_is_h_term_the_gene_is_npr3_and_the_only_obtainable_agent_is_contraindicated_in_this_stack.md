## => ⛔⛔⛔ PART 3 — **AND THE ONLY OBTAINABLE AGENT IS CONTRAINDICATED HERE. THREE INDEPENDENT REASONS.**

`vosoritide` (Voxzogo) is an approved CNP analogue. It is the obvious move. **It is the wrong move in
*this* stack, and each reason stands alone.**

### ⛔⛔⛔ REASON 1 — IT DOUBLE-BLOCKS THE AXIS `erdafitinib` ALREADY BLOCKS
The basket trial states it plainly: ***"Vosoritide, a C-type natriuretic peptide analog that INHIBITS THIS
PATHWAY"*** — the **Ras-MAPK** pathway, i.e. the downstream output of **FGFR3**.

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **`erdafitinib` BLOCKS THE FGFR3 RECEPTOR. `vosoritide` SUPPRESSES ITS DOWNSTREAM MAPK OUTPUT. THEY ARE THE SAME AXIS IN SERIES — REDUNDANT, NOT A NEW TERM. ADDING VOSORITIDE DOES NOT BUY `h_term`; IT BUYS A SECOND BLOCKADE OF A PATHWAY ALREADY BLOCKED, IN A SUBJECT WHOSE FGFR3 IS NORMAL TO BEGIN WITH.**

### ⛔⛔⛔ REASON 2 — IN NON-ACHONDROPLASIA CHILDREN IT CAUSED SLIPPED CAPITAL FEMORAL EPIPHYSES
> **`A phase II basket trial of vosoritide in children with RASopathies, ACAN, and NPR2 deficiency`,
> JCEM 2026 (PMID 41967490) — n=30, 15 µg/kg/day, 6-month observation then 12-month treatment:**
> ⭐ AGV **4.53 → 8.09 cm/year** (P<.0001); height **+0.65 SD**
> ⛔⛔⛔ *"with longer use, **5 subjects discontinued medication due to adverse events, including 3 SLIPPED
> CAPITAL FEMORAL EPIPHYSES and 4 cases of GENU VALGUM**."*

⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE COMPARISON WITH ACHONDROPLASIA IS THE DECISIVE FINDING:**

| | achondroplasia (meta-analysis, PMID 42306228) | ⛔ **basket trial, non-achondroplasia** |
|---|---|---|
| n | **542 across 11 studies; 4 RCTs (n=326)** | 30 |
| ⭐ AGV gain | **+1.36 cm/year** | ⛔⛔ **+3.56 cm/year — `2.6×` LARGER** |
| height gain | +1.24 cm | +0.65 SD |
| ⭐ adverse events | *"Overall and serious AE rates were **COMPARABLE** between CNP analogs and placebo"* | ⛔⛔⛔ **3/30 SCFE (10%), 4/30 genu valgum (13%)** |
| authors' verdict | *"**slight** but statistically meaningful improvements"* | — |

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **IN CHILDREN WITH NORMAL FGFR3 THE EFFECT IS `2.6×` BIGGER — AND SO IS THE HARM. THE ACHONDROPLASIA SAFETY RECORD DOES NOT TRANSFER. OUR SUBJECT HAS NORMAL FGFR3 AND IS ALREADY ON AN FGFR INHIBITOR, SO THE BASKET TRIAL IS THE RELEVANT COMPARATOR — WHICH MEANS BOTH THE LARGE EFFECT *AND* THE `10%` SCFE RATE ARE WHAT WE SHOULD EXPECT.**
> ⛔⛔ **SCFE IS A PERMANENT, SURGICAL, GROWTH-PLATE FAILURE IN AN ADOLESCENT. It is the single worst outcome available in this project — it does not reduce the height gain, it ends the growth plate.**
