## => ⭐ PART 3 — HOW SERIOUS IS IT? THE HONEST WEIGHING

### ⛔ WHAT MAKES IT SERIOUS
- It is **TET1-specific**, not the Tet1/Tet2 double KO I retired in R155.
- It is **partial loss (shRNA knockdown)**, not a null — closer to our perturbation.
- It is in **human** cells.
- It has an **in vivo bone endpoint**.
- It converges with **R087/Thaler**, which found raising 5hmC in *growth-plate chondrocytes* delayed
  hypertrophy and maintained the proliferative state. ⚠ **Two independent lines now say raising 5hmC helps
  the mesenchymal compartment.**

### ⭐ WHAT LIMITS IT — AND ONE OF THESE IS DECISIVE
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **IMPC MEASURED BMD IN THE EXACT Tet1 HETEROZYGOTE AND IT WENT *UP* (male p = 0.0148), NOT DOWN.**
   **If TET1 haploinsufficiency caused MSC senescence and osteoporosis, bone density in the het would
   FALL. It rises — and body length rises with it.** That is the same genotype, measured systematically,
   and it directly contradicts the extrapolation from knockdown to haploinsufficiency.
2. ⭐ **MAGNITUDE.** shRNA knockdown typically removes 70–90% of a transcript. Heterozygous pLoF removes
   ~50%. **R137's ladder, again: these are different rungs, and this file has now watched that distinction
   decide the answer three times** (Wnt, Tet1-null vs het, and now this).
3. ⭐ **COMPARTMENT.** I searched the paper: **"chondrocyte" appears ZERO times. "bone length" ZERO. "femur
   length" ZERO. "growth plate" twice, in passing.** It is entirely MSC → osteoblast → **bone mass**
   biology. **Bone mass and bone length are different terms in this file's own identity**, and the whole
   programme rests on that distinction.
4. ⭐ **ENDPOINT HIERARCHY.** Against a cell-culture proliferation assay stands **+7.74 cm of final adult
   height in 90 humans at P = 8.84e-27, replicated at 88%.** For *our* endpoint, that outranks it.

> ### ⭐⭐ **RECONCILIATION, AND IT IS THE SAME SHAPE AS EVERY OTHER RESOLUTION IN THIS FILE: deep TET1 loss is bad for MSC proliferation and bone mass; ~50% loss is good for adult height. Ho's mice are AGED with LOW baseline 5hmC — restoring toward normal helps them. A healthy adolescent starting at normal and moving to −50% is a DIFFERENT MOVE ON THE SAME CURVE.**
> ⚠ **That reconciliation is coherent, it is not proven, and I am labelling it as reasoning rather than data.**
