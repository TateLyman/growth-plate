## => ⭐⭐⭐⭐ PART 1 — **NEPRILYSIN INHIBITION IS THE `h_term` ROUTE**

> **`Neprilysin Inhibition Promotes Skeletal Growth via the CNP/NPR-B Pathway`, Endocrinology 2024
> (PMID 38752331, DOI 10.1210/endocr/bqae058) — sacubitril in C57BL/6 mice.**

**What the abstract establishes:**

| finding | why it matters |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **dose-dependent skeletal overgrowth** | ⭐ a graded effect, not a threshold artefact |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **growth plate: thickening of the HYPERTROPHIC *and* PROLIFERATIVE zones** | ⭐⭐⭐ **that is `h_term` measured directly — hypertrophic zone thickness is the dominant determinant of elongation rate** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **effect ABOLISHED by cartilage-specific `NPR-B` knockout** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **a genetic epistasis proof that it acts through CNP/NPR-B in cartilage — not off-target, not systemic** |
| ⭐⭐ **worked in fetal tibial explant organ culture** | ⭐ direct on the tissue, independent of circulation |
| ⚠⚠ **promoted growth "only at 3 to 4 weeks of age"**, when endogenous CNP and NEP expression were higher | ⚠⚠ **a NARROW DEVELOPMENTAL WINDOW — see the caution below** |

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE MECHANISM IS EXACTLY THE "RIGHT WAY" R182 IDENTIFIED AND THEN COULDN'T FILL
R182 drew the distinction that matters: **`NPR3` loss (tall stature, no SCFE reported) works by blocking
CLEARANCE — endogenous ligand, tonic, feedback intact. Vosoritide (SCFE 10%) is a supraphysiological
pulsatile bolus.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NEPRILYSIN INHIBITION IS CLEARANCE BLOCKADE BY THE OTHER ROUTE — `NPR3` IS THE RECEPTOR-MEDIATED CLEARANCE ARM, `NEP` IS THE ENZYMATIC ONE. SACUBITRIL RAISES *ENDOGENOUS* CNP BY BLOCKING ITS DEGRADATION. IT IS THE PHARMACOLOGICAL EQUIVALENT OF THE GENETICS THAT GIVES TALL STATURE WITHOUT PHYSEAL FAILURE — WHICH IS PRECISELY WHAT R182 SAID DID NOT EXIST.**

### ⭐⭐ AND THE HUMAN GENETIC SAFETY OF THIS AXIS IS REASSURING
> **Two-sample Mendelian randomisation, multi-ancestry GWAS >5M individuals, 296,824 women
> (PMID 40406480, 2025):** ⭐⭐ genetically proxied **reduced `NPR3` function → LOWER preeclampsia risk
> (OR `0.46`, 95% CI 0.30–0.69)**.

⭐⭐ **At population scale, partial lifelong reduction of NPR3-mediated clearance is cardiovascularly
benign-to-beneficial.** ⚠ That partly answers the **aortic dilatation** seen in the bi-allelic NPR3-LoF
families — a complete knockout phenotype does not transfer to partial pharmacological reduction.

## => ⛔⛔⛔ PART 2 — **AND THE SAME DIG KILLED THE ENTIRE DOWNSTREAM CLASS. THIS IS THE CLEANEST NEGATIVE I HAVE FOUND ALL PROJECT.**

The obvious cheap route was **PDE5 inhibition** — sildenafil and tadalafil are approved, oral, cheap, and
already dosed in children for pulmonary hypertension. **It fails, and it fails for a reason that
generalises.**

> **`Analysis of short-term treatment with the phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitor tadalafil on long bone
> development in young rats`, Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab 2018 (PMID 29920215, PMC6230700).**

| step | result |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **is the target even there?** | ⭐⭐ **YES — "robust expression of PDE5 as the major phosphodiesterase hydrolyzing cGMP" in primary rat epiphyseal chondrocytes** |
| ⭐ **does the drug engage it?** | ⭐⭐ **YES — CNP-stimulated intracellular cGMP peak `+37%`, decline significantly attenuated** |
| ⭐ **does it engage in vivo?** | ⭐⭐ **YES — 10 mg/kg/day × 3 wk in 1-month-old rats → tissue cGMP `+52%` (P<0.01)** |
| ⛔⛔⛔ **does the bone grow?** | ⛔⛔⛔ **NO — "did not alter long bone length, cortical or trabecular bone properties, and histological features"** |
| ⛔ side effect | ⛔ **9% reduction in bodyweight gain (P<0.01)** |

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **TARGET PRESENT, TARGET ENGAGED, DOSE PAEDIATRIC-EQUIVALENT, ANIMAL GROWING, cGMP UP `52%` — AND ZERO GROWTH EFFECT. THAT IS NOT A DOSE FAILURE. IT IS A MECHANISM FAILURE, AND IT IS THE IDEAL FORM OF NEGATIVE EVIDENCE.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE CONTRAST WITH SACUBITRIL TELLS US SOMETHING GENERAL AND USEFUL
| intervention | where it acts | cGMP | ⭐ **bone growth** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **tadalafil** | ⛔ **downstream — cGMP degradation** | **+52% tissue** | ⛔⛔ **NONE** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **sacubitril** | ⭐⭐⭐ **upstream — LIGAND degradation** | — | ⭐⭐⭐ **dose-dependent overgrowth** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CNP AXIS IS *LIGAND-LIMITED*, NOT DEGRADATION-LIMITED DOWNSTREAM. AMPLIFYING cGMP DOES NOTHING; RAISING LIGAND AT THE RECEPTOR WORKS. THAT RULES OUT THE WHOLE DOWNSTREAM CLASS IN ONE STROKE — PDE5, PDE3, PDE1 INHIBITORS, sGC STIMULATORS — AND IT MEANS ANY FUTURE `h_term` CANDIDATE MUST ACT AT OR ABOVE THE RECEPTOR.**

## => ⚠ PART 3 — **THREE CAUTIONS I FOUND WHILE LOOKING FOR THEM**

### ⚠⚠ 1. THERE IS A COMPENSATORY FEEDBACK, AND IT IS SEX-DEPENDENT
> **`Is C-type natriuretic peptide regulated by a feedback loop?`, PLoS One 2020 (PMID 33002060,
> PMC7529242)** — 4-week-old rats, exogenous CNP 0.5 mg/kg/day × 3 d: **CNP mRNA fell in cartilage-containing
> tissue**, and the ⚠ **decrease was greater in female rats**. ⭐ No *direct* autoregulation in cultured
> chondrocytes or fetal tibias — so the feedback is **indirect/systemic, not cell-autonomous**.

⚠ **Raising CNP is partly self-limiting. That predicts sacubitril's effect will blunt over time, and it is a
reason the mouse result may not sustain across a multi-year course.**

### ⚠⚠ 2. IT IS NOT AVAILABLE WITHOUT AN ARB
**Sacubitril is marketed only as sacubitril/valsartan.** ⛔ **No monotherapy formulation exists** — searched
and confirmed. ⚠ In a **normotensive adolescent** that means carrying an angiotensin receptor blocker for a
skeletal indication: hypotension, RAAS blockade during growth, and the class's angioedema risk from
bradykinin accumulation. **That is a real and unavoidable cost of this route.**

### ⚠⚠ 3. IT PARTLY INHERITS VOSORITIDE'S FIRST OBJECTION
Raising CNP raises NPR2/cGMP, which suppresses the **MAPK** arm downstream of FGFR3 — the axis
`erdafitinib` already blocks (R182). ⚠ **The redundancy objection is REDUCED but not eliminated:** the
amplitude is endogenous rather than supraphysiological, the exposure is tonic rather than pulsatile, and
the tadalafil result argues the cGMP arm is not where growth is set. ⚠ **But it is the same pathway, and I
am not going to pretend otherwise.**
