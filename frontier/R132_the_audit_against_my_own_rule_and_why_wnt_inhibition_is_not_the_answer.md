## 1. ⛔ FIRST, THE RULE KILLED MY OWN LEADING CANDIDATE

R116–R129 carried **sacubitril** as the most obtainable addition in the file — approved, paediatric use,
dose-dependent skeletal overgrowth in wild-type mice, clean NPR-B epistasis, and blocking the half of CNP
clearance nothing else touched (MME 1062 vs NPR3 979 in human plate).

**It is redundant with the agent it was meant to support.**

> **Vosoritide is engineered to RESIST neprilysin.** It is the 37 C-terminal residues of human CNP53 plus a
> Pro-Gly N-terminal extension, and *"this structural modification conveys resistance to neutral
> endopeptidase (NEP) degradation, resulting in prolonged half-life compared to endogenous CNP."*

**Blocking neprilysin protects a peptide that was designed not to be a neprilysin substrate.** And
sacubitril's other use — raising *endogenous* CNP — is substrate-limited exactly where this subject is:
hakata2024's effect appeared only in the window when endogenous CNP was highest, and NT-proCNP peaks at
14.1 y in boys.

**Sacubitril is withdrawn from the stack.** ⚠ The **NPR3 decoy is NOT affected by this** — vosoritide
resists the *enzyme* but is still cleared by the *receptor*, which is why kanai2017's CNP × OSTN
double-transgenic still gains additional bone length. The two clearance routes are not interchangeable and
I had been treating them as one class.

## 3. ⭐ WNT — THE RIGHT TARGET, THE WRONG DRUGS, AND THE SAME TRAP I JUST FELL INTO TWICE

**Wnt has the best evidence of anything on the renewal side.** It is not close:

| evidence | endpoint |
|---|---|
| Wnt **activation** (Apc cHet) in PTHrP⁺ cells | **−34 to −39% of the resting pool; −72% of long columns** |
| **SPIN4 loss** (lowers canonical Wnt output in resting cells) | **RZ height ↑, resting chondrocyte NUMBER ↑, tibia length ↑ at 2 wk, 10 wk AND 18 months, h_term untouched** |
| **Human SPIN4 frameshift** | **+4.5 to +5 SDS vs midparental**, replicated in a second family |

**That passes the rule twice over — cell counts and length, in mouse and human.**

### And then the drugs point the other way
| intervention | result |
|---|---|
| **Col2a1-ICAT** — chondrocyte-restricted canonical Wnt reduction | ⛔ **SHORTENS bone** |
| **PORCN inhibitors (LGK974)** — organism-wide Wnt ligand blockade | elongates tibiae in organ culture but **REDUCES the proliferative zone** and **impairs trabecular and cortical bone mass and strength in vivo** |
| **Axin2⁺ stem cells** | ⛔ **REQUIRE Wnt/β-catenin** — lowering it helps one stem population and harms another |

> **SPIN4 is a partial, cell-intrinsic reduction of Wnt TRANSCRIPTIONAL OUTPUT in resting cells. The
> available drugs are global ligand blockade or chondrocyte-wide β-catenin blockade. They are not the same
> intervention, and the one that most closely resembles a targeted chondrocyte Wnt reduction — ICAT —
> ALREADY SHORTENS BONE.**

**This is structurally the identical trap to R130's TGF-β error and R126's AR error**: a compartment
signature read as a therapeutic vector, where the systemic drug does something else entirely. **The
difference is that this time I am catching it before adding the agent rather than one round after.**

**Verdict: do not add Wnt inhibition.** The target is right and the human genetics is the strongest in the
file. **No existing drug implements it, and the nearest one shortens bone.** The atlas's own standing target
— *"find an agent that lowers canonical Wnt transcriptional output in resting cells, not ligand secretion"*
— remains unfilled, and β-catenin/CBP inhibitors (PRI-724) are flagged as possibly sign-wrong for exactly
this reason (R120).

## 4. SO WHAT SHOULD ACTUALLY BE ADDED

**One thing passes, and it is not a drug: axial decompression / spinal unloading.** +1.6 cm acute with
~1.2 cm persisting, stiffness reduced across the whole column and staying below baseline after reload,
fusion-independent, additive to everything, works at any age, and costs nothing.

**And two measurements, which are worth more than any remaining compound:**
1. **Sitting height vs subischial leg length, plus ring-apophysis staging.** Hand bone age reports the
   compartment that is *finished*. Flagged four times now, still not done.
2. **NT-proCNP** — decides whether the CNP arm is doing anything at all in this subject.

> **The honest position: the stack is essentially complete on available evidence. Four agents are in on
> real endpoints, and every remaining candidate either fails the rule, runs the wrong way, or has no
> obtainable form. The missing arm is renewal, and it has no agent that passes.**

**That is not a stalemate — it is a specification.** The programme now knows exactly what it is looking for:
a partial, chondrocyte-intrinsic reducer of canonical Wnt transcriptional output, with a bone-length
endpoint. Nothing in the pharmacopoeia does that, and that is a findable object, not a mystery.
