## 2. ⛔ BUT "SPEND FAST" DOES NOT FOLLOW — THE RATE-YIELD LAW IS THE COUNTERWEIGHT

R360, fitted from hunziker1994's four groups: **A ∝ throughput^−0.150.**

| throughput | amplification | total height from a fixed pool |
|---|---|---|
| 2× | ×0.901 | **−9.9%** |
| 3× | ×0.848 | −15.2% |
| 5× | ×0.786 | **−21.4%** |

**In a budget regime, speed costs total height.** Driving throughput 5× surrenders 21% of what the pool

deadline still exists. Under an aromatase inhibitor the deadline is precisely what has been removed.

### ⭐ NEW AGENT: OXANDROLONE
Non-aromatisable, so it cannot be converted to oestradiol and *"exerts a relatively limited effect on
epiphyseal plate closure,"* while raising IGF-1. In Turner syndrome, **rhGH + oxandrolone gave greater
final height with no significant increase in bone maturation compared with rhGH alone.**

**And the concept has a name and a 1961 paper:** *"Dissociation of growth-stimulating and skeleton-maturing
actions of the synthetic androgen, fluoxymesterone."* **The androgen class splits into a growth arm and a
maturation arm, and they are separable.** This file has never held that.

⚠ **Honest counterweight:** in CDGP, oxandrolone showed **no** significant effect on final adult height,
and one source reports little difference from short-term low-dose testosterone. **It is established as
bone-age-sparing; it is NOT established as final-height-adding outside Turner syndrome.**
