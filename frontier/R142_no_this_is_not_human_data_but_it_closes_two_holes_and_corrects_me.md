## => ⛔ THE DIRECT ANSWER: **`Gupta 2005` IS NOT HUMAN PHARMACOKINETICS. THE TITLE IS MISLEADING.**

**What the study actually did:**
- six adult dogs, one topical Revolution application (240 mg selamectin, label dose ~6 mg/kg)
- **cotton gloves** worn while petting the dogs for 5 minutes → selamectin measured **on the gloves**
- **blood drawn from the DOGS** → selamectin measured in **dog** plasma
- ⛔ **no human was dosed. No human was sampled. No human blood, urine or tissue was analysed.**

**The authors say it themselves, in the paper:**
> *"Assessing the risk that selamectin residue may present to humans is difficult due to the levels
> absorbed by the skin as opposed to the cotton glove. **No data concerning the effects in humans are
> currently available since selamectin is recommended for use in dogs and cats.**"*

> ### **THIS PAPER CONFIRMS R141's BLOCKER RATHER THAN CLOSING IT. It is a contact-transfer surrogate study — how much comes off a dog onto a glove — not a human exposure measurement.**

## => ⛔⛔ AND `Gupta` CAUGHT AN ERROR I MADE IN R141, IN THE FLATTERING DIRECTION

**R141 stated:** *"the marketed topical dose in dogs already gives Cmax 86.5 ng/mL = 0.112 µM = 52%
engagement — above the 40% target… the dose that this analysis says is therapeutic is approximately
the dose that has been sold since 1999."*

⛔ **THAT WAS WRONG. `Novotny/Boy 2002`'s topical arm was 24 mg/kg — FOUR TIMES the 6 mg/kg label
dose.** I read a 4×-label figure as a label figure.

**`Gupta` measured the actual label dose, and it is the correction:**

| measurement | ng/mL | µM | **% engaged** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Novotny` topical **24 mg/kg (4× label)** | 86.5 | 0.1123 | 52.2% |
| ↳ linear-scaled to the **6 mg/kg label** | 21.6 | 0.0281 | **21.4%** |
| ⭐ **`Gupta` topical 6 mg/kg LABEL, 72 h, n=6** | ⭐ **10.26 ± 1.06** | **0.0133** | ⭐ **11.5%** |
| **target** | **52.9** | **0.0687** | **40%** |

> ### **THE MARKETED TOPICAL PRODUCT AT THE MARKETED DOSE DELIVERS ~12–21% ENGAGEMENT, NOT 52%. My R141 claim was 3–4× too generous, and it was too generous in the direction I wanted. Withdrawn.**

**Two independent measurements now bracket the dog topical route** (`Gupta` 10.26 at 6 mg/kg;
`Novotny` 86.5 at 24 mg/kg — a 4× dose giving 8.4× the concentration, supra-linear but within the
noise of two small topical studies).

⭐ **What survives, and it is still useful:** the topical dose needed to reach 40% in a dog is
**~15–31 mg/kg = 2.4–5.2× the label dose.**

> ⭐⭐ **AND 5× TOPICAL IS A FORMALLY TESTED DOSE — TESTED IN THE TWO POPULATIONS THAT MATTER MOST:
> six-week-old puppies over seven treatments and 196 days, and avermectin-sensitive (P-gp-null)

⛔ **The oral calculation is UNAFFECTED** — R141's 0.166 mg/kg came from the *oral* 24 mg/kg →
7,630 ng/mL arm, scaled linearly within the oral dataset. That arithmetic stands.

**Those two gaps are different in kind and it matters which is which:**
- **The human PK gap is procedural.** A microdose study closes it. Nothing in the biology stands in
  the way; the molecule simply has never had a reason to be given to a person.
- ⛔ **The efficacy gap is scientific and it is the real one.** *No macrocyclic lactone has ever been
  given to a growing bone with a length endpoint, in any system, at any dose.* The entire case for
  this arm is: SPIN4 loss lowers Wnt output ~40% and lengthens bone; selamectin lowers Wnt output;
  therefore selamectin may lengthen bone. **The middle step has never been tested. Everything in
  R136–R142 is preparation for an experiment nobody has run.**

**I would rather be blunt about that than let seven clean safety studies read as evidence of efficacy.
They are not. They are evidence that the experiment is affordable.**

## => WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY BE "IT"

**One experiment, and it is small:**

**Fetal tibial / E16.5 femur explant. Selamectin at 0.02 / 0.07 / 0.2 / 0.6 µM. Bryostatin matched, as
the internal negative control. Endpoint: BONE LENGTH, plus Axin2 mRNA (calibrate to Spin4-KO's 45%),
Sfrp5+ resting-zone cell count, and terminal hypertrophic cell height.**

- If selamectin at ~0.07 µM produces ~40% Axin2 reduction, raises Sfrp5+ resting-zone number, leaves
  h_term unchanged, and lengthens the bone — **the arm is real, and the human PK study is worth doing.**
- If it does not lengthen, or if bryostatin does too, **the arm is dead and six rounds of work resolve
  cleanly to a negative.**

**That single experiment is worth more than every remaining document.**
