## => ⛔⛔ PART 1 — THE CORRECTION, FIRST

R160 claimed hole 8 was patched at the class level on the strength of one abstract sentence. **With the
full text in hand, that claim was too strong on two counts.**

### ⛔ CORRECTION 1 — **THE 5hmC MEASUREMENTS ARE ALL IN CELLS**
I traced every `5hmC` mention in the paper. The dot blots are in **K562, MEG-01, SIG-M5, OCI-AML5 and
MOLM13** — cell lines. ⛔ **There is no tissue 5hmC measurement from a treated animal anywhere in the paper.**

The abstract reads *"decreases cytosine hydroxymethylation and restricted clonal outgrowth of TET2 mutant
but not normal hematopoietic precursor cells **in vitro and in vivo**."* ⛔ **The "in vitro and in vivo"
attaches to the CLONAL OUTGROWTH claim, not to the 5hmC claim. I read it the way I wanted to read it.**

> ### ⛔ **HOLE 8 IS NARROWED, NOT CLOSED. Nobody has measured 5hmC or TET activity in the tissue of a treated animal — not for gold, and not for any TET inhibitor.**

### ⛔ CORRECTION 2 — **"REVERSIBLE" IS AN ABSTRACT CLAIM I CANNOT SOURCE**
R160 leaned hard on *"its pharmacologic effects on normal stem cells are, unlike mutations, reversible"*
as the direct answer to Ho 2025 and Smeriglio 2020. **I searched the full text for `reversib`, `washout`,
`withdraw`, `discontinu`, `recover`. The word "reversible" appears ONLY in the abstract.** The nearest
supporting statement is a *discussion proposal*: *"therapeutic intervention with TETi would be applied
transiently and discontinued upon elimination of vulnerable TET2-deficient cells."* ⛔ **That is a plan,
not an experiment. The reversibility argument is downgraded from evidence to authors' assertion.**

**R160 raised a new stack conflict:** Tet1+/− mice develop hepatic steatosis (Yuan 2021), and hepatic fat
is the variable the metformin arm is keyed to — so a TET1-inhibition arm might worsen it. **I called liver
fat the new #1 measurement on that basis.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DRUG GOES THE OTHER WAY. In vivo auranofin *improves insulin sensitivity*, *lowers leptin* and *exerts anti-diabetic effects* in obese mice (GSE202935) — and *prevents liver fibrosis* (GSE176346).**

> ### ⭐⭐ **THE GENETIC Tet1+/− STEATOSIS PHENOTYPE DOES NOT TRANSFER TO THE DRUG. This is the SECOND instance in two rounds of the same lesson — genetic loss ≠ pharmacological inhibition — and this time it is the actual molecule, in vivo, from two independent datasets.**

⚠ **Honest limits:** these are metabolic and fibrosis endpoints, not direct hepatic-fat quantification in a

that this does not touch; and the anti-diabetic effect was shown in **obese** mice, where there is more to
improve. ⭐ **Liver fat remains a monitoring item — but it is no longer a predicted CONFLICT, and R160's
promotion of it to #1 is withdrawn.**
