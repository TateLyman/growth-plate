### ⭐⭐⭐ THE DIRECTION PARADOX RESOLVES, AND IT RESOLVES IN OUR FAVOUR

This was an open item for several rounds: DNMT3A loss causes overgrowth **and** TET1 loss causes
overgrowth — opposite enzymes, same direction. It is settled by a paper with an actual bone-length endpoint:

> ⭐ **`Yanagihara et al., "Dnmt1 determines bone length by regulating energy metabolism of growth plate chondrocytes," Nat Commun 2025` (PMC12586564):**
> *"Long bones in Dnmt1-deficient (**Dnmt1^ΔPrx1**) mouse limbs are **significantly shortened** due to
> **decreased chondrocyte proliferation and accelerated differentiation**. Reduced DNA methylation…
> leads to increased expression of genes related to energy metabolism and to **ossification**."*

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **LESS METHYLATION → SHORTER BONE, via ACCELERATED DIFFERENTIATION. TET1 is the enzyme that REMOVES methylation. So TET1 loss → MORE methylation → LONGER bone — which is exactly what the human genetics says (+8.32 cm/allele, 42 carriers, 1.45M exomes). TWO INDEPENDENT LINES, A MOUSE LIMB-CONDITIONAL KO AND A HUMAN EXOME COHORT, POINTING THE SAME WAY.**

⭐ **And the mechanism is the N-axis, not a new one.** *"Accelerated differentiation"* **is** commitment —
R130's renewal:commitment ratio and R138's *"reduced recruitment into the proliferative columns."*
**TET1 inhibition is a candidate implementation of R132's standing specification, arrived at from
methylation instead of Wnt — and therefore potentially additive to the rentosertib Wnt arm rather than
competing for R137's narrow shelf.**

### ⛔ WHAT IS WRONG WITH IT, STATED FIRST-CLASS

1. ⛔ **No clinical-phase TET1 inhibitor exists.** All 76 potent molecules are unnamed research compounds;
   **zero have a development phase.** Better than NRK's zero-ligands, but still **no drug**.
2. ⛔⛔ **The most famous TET inhibitor is an artifact.** `PMC9109264`: *"Bobcat339 activity is mediated by
   contaminating copper."* Any TET literature resting on Bobcat339 must be discounted.
3. ⛔ **Counter-evidence with a bone endpoint:** `PMC12277962` — **Tet1/Tet2 double KO in Prx1:Cre** gives
   *"significant reductions to trabecular structure and volume as well as reduced osteoblast numbers,"*
   worse with age. ⚠ **Mitigating: that is bone MASS, not LENGTH, and it is a DOUBLE knockout** — it does
   not isolate TET1 and does not measure the quantity we care about. **But it is the same limb driver
   (Prx1) that refuted verteporfin, and I am not going to soft-pedal it.**
4. ⚠ **Selectivity across TET1/2/3 is unproven** — though `PMC10983004` reports the three paralogs have
   **different sensitivities to 2-HG**, which is a real selectivity handle rather than a hope.
5. ⚠ **The class is 2-OG/Fe(II) dioxygenase**, shared with the JmjC demethylases, the PHDs and FTO —
   so broad-class inhibitors would be promiscuous by construction. ⚠ **Note the approved HIF-PHIs
   (roxadustat, daprodustat, vadadustat) are 2-OG-competitive and oral — an obvious thing to check, and
   NOT yet checked. Flagged, not claimed.**
