## 1. ⛔ CORRECTION TO MY OWN R117 AND R118 — THE REVERSE WNT EXPERIMENT HAS BEEN RUN

available drug is the wrong one. The atlas's own restatement of the target stands:
**find an agent that lowers canonical Wnt TRANSCRIPTIONAL OUTPUT in resting cells — not ligand secretion.**

## 3. THE LAW THAT EXPLAINS WHY N LOOKED AGENTLESS

Every pool expander found, without exception, produces **pool without flux** — it expands by blocking
the exit, and blocking the exit is what prevents length:

- **mTORC1/Tsc1**: symmetric division ↑, recruitment into proliferative layer **unchanged**; and
  independently, chondrocyte Tsc1 deletion gives *"blockage of differentiation… reduced bone length."*
- **PDGF-BB**: proliferation ↑ and matrix ↑, but *"maturation along the endochondral pathway inhibited"*,
  preventing the normal alkaline-phosphatase rise.
- **Hedgehog/SAG sustained**: pool ↑ but fate converts to **trabecular osteoblasts** (R117), which is why
  +61% pool bought +3.63% length.
- **Axitinib**: charge without discharge (R364).

**This is the charge/discharge law, now confirmed at the stem-cell level by four independent agents.**
It is why N has no single-agent solution: *the mechanism that fills the pool is the mechanism that stops
it emptying.* **Any N strategy must be two-phase — charge, then release — and no one has ever measured
length after the release.**

### The stage rule, and it favours this subject
Hedgehog's sign depends on **stage, not duration**. Before SOC formation both LDE225 and SAG *reduce*
PTHrP⁺ cells. After SOC formation, SAG promotes resting-zone proliferation and vismodegib causes
premature fusion. orikasa2024 pulsed at P6 (pre-SOC) → clonal derangement, osteoblast conversion, length
decrement. trompet2024 dosed at P28/30/32 (post-SOC) → clean pool doubling and +3.63% femur.
**A subject at bone age 16 is as far post-SOC as it is possible to be** — the favourable side.

## 4. NEW THIS ROUND: PDGF-BB, AND IT IS OBTAINABLE

Not previously held in this atlas as a pool agent (the existing PDGF nodes are physeal bar formation and
the extra-plate progenitor reservoir). Resting-zone chondrocytes under PDGF-BB show **increased
proliferation and proteoglycan production while maturation along the endochondral pathway is inhibited**;
it promotes a resting-zone matrix phenotype and **prevents the normal alkaline-phosphatase rise**.

**rhPDGF-BB is commercially obtainable and approved** — becaplermin (Regranex) topically, and
**Augment Bone Graft (rhPDGF-BB + β-TCP)** as an implanted local matrix, which is precisely the local
delivery format this programme already uses for the SAG fibrin depot.

**It is a charge agent.** On the law above, it will produce pool without flux and requires a release
step. It should be evaluated as the charge half of a two-phase protocol, never alone.

## 5. ALSO RECOVERED: LOCAL GH IS A POOL AGENT AND R110 DID NOT TEST THAT

R110 killed GH as null on the length axis. That was **systemic GH on attained height**, and it stands.
**ohlsson1992 is a different experiment:** hypophysectomised rats, [³H]thymidine minipump, **local GH by
cannula into one proximal tibia against the contralateral leg** — label-retaining cells in the germinal
layer rose to a ratio of **1.95 ± 0.13**. In the same animals **IGF-I did nothing** (0.96 ± 0.04).

So local GH nearly **doubles** the germinal pool, GH and IGF-1 are not interchangeable at the resting
zone, and the atlas's own `dual_effector_hypothesis` holds the resolution: GH both **recruits** germinal
cells into the proliferative pool (gevers1996a) **and multiplies them** (ohlsson1992), with net sign
depending on dose and background. Logged in the atlas as CORR-187.

**GH is an approved drug that raises the resting pool when delivered locally in a deficient background.**
Whether it does so in a GH-replete subject is exactly the gap chu2025 names and ohlsson1992 does not fill.

## 6. THE FIVE POPULATIONS, AND WHICH ONE IS MEASURABLE IN A HUMAN

| population | location | note |
|---|---|---|
| PTHrP⁺ | central/lower RZ, emerge P3–6 | exhausted by passage 5 |
| CD73⁺ | RZ adjacent to SOC, ~P28 | self-renewal acquired post-SOC |
| Axin2⁺ | outermost plate, Ranvier's groove | appositional growth; **requires** Wnt/β-catenin |
| FoxA2⁺ | uppermost RZ at cartilage–bone interface | persists beyond passage 9 |
| **ApoE⁺** | entire RZ, **~97% of resting chondrocytes** | **the ONLY marker validated in BOTH mouse and human** |

avijgan2026 (PRISMA systematic review) concludes **the hierarchy is not established** — neither
single-cell sequencing at any age nor label-retention has shown whether the resting zone holds distinct
quiescent sub-populations. **Every experiment in this programme labels one of the five and generalises.
The denominator of every yield in this file is a marker, not a cell type.**

Note **Axin2⁺ cells REQUIRE Wnt/β-catenin** while PTHrP⁺/resting cells are maintained by Wnt inhibition.
A Wnt-lowering agent helps one population and harms another. That is a second reason the pathway-wide
hammer fails and the SPIN4-like intrinsic route is the target.
