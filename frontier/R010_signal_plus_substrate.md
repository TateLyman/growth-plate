## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ THE LAW — and it reconciles every result in this branch

> ### **Longitudinal growth = f(SIGNAL) × g(SUBSTRATE). Raising either alone fails. mTORC1 is the multiplier, not the driver.**

| system | signal | substrate | length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **pig, HMB alone** (`tatara2007`) | ✔ raised (GH +38%, IGF-1 +20%) | ✘ proline *lowest of all groups* | ⛔ **−2.0%** |
| **pig, AKG alone** (`tatara2012`) | ✘ | ✔ proline 2.1× | **0%** |
| **pig, AKG + HMB** (`tatara2012`) | ✔ | ✔ **proline 5.3×** | ⭐ **+1.8%** |
| **mouse metatarsal + Baf/CQ** (`newton2015`) | ✔✔ mTORC1 10.1× | ✔✔ **culture medium — unlimited amino acids** | ⭐⭐ **+91% to +170%, h_term +71%** |
| **mouse Tsc1 cKO metatarsal** (`newton2018`) | ✔✔ genetic | ✔✔ culture medium | ⭐⭐ **+52%** |
| **mouse Tsc1 cKO in vivo** (`yan2016`) | ✔✔✔ saturating, embryonic | ✘ dying animal, restricted thorax | ⛔ chondrodysplasia |
| **deer antler** (`ba2025`) | ✔ | ✔✔✔ **vascularised cartilage — "overcomes the metabolic limits that constrain hypertrophic expansion in avascular cartilage"** | ⭐⭐⭐ **365×** |

**The single best predictor of the length outcome in that table is not the signal. It is the
substrate column.** An organ culture is functionally a *perfused* growth plate — the bone is bathed
in complete medium, and the diffusion distance in a metatarsal rudiment is small. **That is why the
same manipulation gives +91% ex vivo and −2% in a pig: ex vivo, supply is not the binding
constraint.**

**This is F-R005's thesis, confirmed by intervention rather than by inference.** F-R005 argued the
ceiling is avascularity because every limit the atlas found — transport, extensibility, matrix
acceptor/donor supply, the secretory ceiling, the redox tax, the cysteine auxotrophy — is a supply
limit. **`tatara2012` is the controlled test of that claim in a mammal, and it comes out the right
way: add the substrate and the same signal changes sign.**

### What it says about every failed lever in this atlas

**GH, CNP, IGF-1, FGFR3, hedgehog — every one is a SIGNAL.** They have been given, for thirty years,
to children whose growth plates are avascular, cysteine-auxotrophic and secreting at the plasma-cell
ceiling. **The 2–4% convergence is what you get when you multiply a raised signal by an unraised
substrate.** It is not a property of the system. **It is a property of only ever manipulating one
factor of a product.**

## 5. ⛔ The honest limits of the pig data

- **Prenatal maternal, not postnatal direct.** Sows dosed for the last 2–3 weeks of gestation; the
  offspring were never treated. This is developmental programming and does not establish that the
  same combination given to a growing juvenile does anything.
- **+1.8% is small.** It is a real, significant, n=259 length effect — and it is 1.8%, not 52%.
- **The proline link is correlative.** Their amino-acid table and their femur table are in the same
  paper on the same groups, and the association is striking — but nobody measured proline flux into
  cartilage collagen, and I am not asserting causation.
- **HMB alone SHORTENS the bone** and that is a real negative that must be carried forward. Anyone
  reading the leucine axis as straightforwardly pro-height is contradicted by 141 pigs.
- **The Lublin series' other papers** (articular-cartilage FTIR, small-intestine neurons, ovarian
  stroma) carry no additional length endpoint — I read them and they do not bear on this.
