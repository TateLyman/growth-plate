## 1. WHAT THE TWO PAPERS ACTUALLY SAY

### Raz, Nasatzky, Boyan, Ornoy & Schwartz 2005 — *J Cell Biochem* 95:108–19 (PMID 15723286)
Rat costochondral chondrocytes, resting-zone (RC) and growth-zone (GC = prehypertrophic + upper
hypertrophic) cells separated, male and female:

- **Only MALE cells respond to testosterone**, although receptors are present in both sexes
- **GC cells:** testosterone and DHT gave a **comparable dose-dependent increase in [³H]-thymidine
  incorporation**, and a comparable increase in **alkaline phosphatase**
- ⭐ **RC cells responded differently: testosterone DECREASED DNA synthesis in male RC cells, and DHT had
  NO effect.** ALP in RC cells was unaffected by either hormone
- ⭐⭐ **Finasteride (1, 5, 10 µg/mL) reduced the GC response to testosterone dose-dependently — metabolism
  to DHT is REQUIRED**
- Both sexes express **SRD5A1** mRNA, **neither expresses SRD5A2**; **only male cells have 5α-reductase
  activity**. Female cells fail to respond because they **aromatise** the hormone instead

### Peralta, Arnold, Currie & Thonney 1994 — *J Anim Sci* 72:2629
20 rams, 20 wethers, 20 wethers + testosterone; 10 slaughter ages, 49→217 days; metacarpal physis:
- **Testosterone increased weight and LENGTH of the metacarpal (P < 0.03)**
- **The length gain was associated with a higher labeling index in the PROLIFERATIVE zone (P < 0.05)**
- Resting-zone labeling indices in rams were higher **near the time of puberty**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **Authors' own stated mechanism:** *"Testosterone may mediate this accelerated growth by first
  increasing bone growth and **then depleting the source of stem cells** in the cartilage growth plate."*

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ THE COHERENT MODEL — AND IT IS R125'S EXHAUSTION THESIS IN THE 1994 AUTHORS' OWN WORDS

Both papers say the same thing from opposite directions:

> **Androgen pulls cells OUT of the resting zone and INTO the growth zone, drives proliferation there, and
> depletes the stem source. It is a pool-SPENDING agent.**

- Raz: T ↓ resting-zone DNA synthesis; T and DHT ↑ growth-zone thymidine incorporation
- Peralta: ↑ proliferative-zone labeling index, ↑ bone length, **then depletion of the stem source**

The operator proposed exactly this in R125 — *"they are that tall because they exhausted the pool"* — and
Peralta wrote it in 1994 about testosterone specifically.

## 3. ⛔ WHICH REFUTES MY OWN R126 HYPOTHESIS, ONE ROUND AFTER I RAISED IT

R126 found AR is the highest-expressed sex-steroid receptor in the human resting zone (1077 vs ESR1 278,
r = +0.671 with the stem signature) and proposed **AR as an N agent — a resting-zone stem-cell lever.**

**That is refuted by function.** The receptor is there, but:
- **testosterone DECREASES resting-zone DNA synthesis** (Raz)
- **DHT does nothing at all in the resting zone** (Raz)
- **testosterone DEPLETES the stem source** (Peralta)

**Androgen is a growth-zone agent, not an N agent. High AR expression in the resting zone does not mean
androgen expands the resting zone — it means androgen empties it.** Expression located the receptor;
function inverted the prediction. R126's entry is withdrawn.

## 4. THE NAAS VERDICT — REDUNDANT, AND NOW FOR THE RIGHT REASON

R126 argued redundancy from receptor occupancy. **That argument was weak. Here is the strong one:**

1. **DHT is the ACTIVE species in the growth plate** — finasteride abolishes testosterone's effect. The
   plate's androgen response is *already* a non-aromatisable-androgen response.
2. **The plate makes its own DHT from testosterone via SRD5A1** — expressed in all zones of the human plate
   (R126: 208–320), and it is the type-1 enzyme, exactly the isoform Raz identifies as the active one.
3. **An aromatase inhibitor raises testosterone ~2×** (265→513 ng/dL; letrozole 2.5×) **while blocking its
   diversion to oestradiol** — so more substrate reaches SRD5A1.
4. **SRD5A1's Km for testosterone is in the low-µM range while tissue testosterone sits far below it**, so
   the enzyme runs unsaturated and **local DHT scales roughly linearly with testosterone.**

> **On an aromatase inhibitor the plate is already generating roughly double its normal local DHT, through
> the exact enzyme Raz shows is required, acting on the exact zone Raz and Peralta show responds. A NAAS is
> a dose increment on a pathway already running at double strength — not a new mechanism.**

**And the rate-yield cost is now the argument against pushing it further.** Androgen's mechanism is pool
depletion (Peralta). Under A ∝ throughput^−0.150 (R125), driving depletion harder spends a small remaining
pool at reduced amplification. **At bone age 16 that is the high-dose-GH failure mode wearing a different
hat.**

**Confirmed by the only direct human test in the right sex with intact gonads: oxandrolone in CDGP showed
no significant effect on final adult height.**

### What would change this verdict
Only evidence that **5α-reductase capacity is rate-limiting** at 2× testosterone. If it saturates, a direct
DHT-class agent bypasses the bottleneck and the redundancy argument fails. **No measurement of growth-plate
5α-reductase kinetics at supraphysiological substrate exists that I can find.**

## 5. ⭐⭐ THE ACTIONABLE FINDING NOBODY HAD FLAGGED

> **5α-REDUCTASE INHIBITORS ARE CONTRAINDICATED IN THIS STACK.**

**Finasteride reduced the growth plate's response to testosterone dose-dependently.** Dutasteride blocks
both isoforms. These drugs are extremely common in young men (androgenetic alopecia, and finasteride is
frequently taken alongside an aromatase inhibitor precisely because AI-raised testosterone worsens hair
loss).

**A 5α-reductase inhibitor taken alongside this stack would block the growth plate's entire androgen
response at the required enzymatic step**, and would do so silently — serum testosterone would look fine or
better. **This is the first drug–drug interaction this programme has identified, it is highly likely to
occur in exactly this population, and it directly antagonises a mechanism the stack depends on.**

Also recorded: **the sex-specificity is favourable here.** Female chondrocytes lack 5α-reductase activity
and aromatise instead; **male cells have the enzyme activity.** A male subject on an AI is the configuration
in which this pathway works.
