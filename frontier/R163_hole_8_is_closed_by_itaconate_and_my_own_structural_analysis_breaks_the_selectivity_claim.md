## => ⛔⛔⛔ PART 3 — **I RAN THE STRUCTURE MYSELF, AND IT BREAKS OUR OWN SELECTIVITY CLAIM**

R154 put two claims about auranofin side by side and this file has carried both ever since:

| claim | source |
|---|---|
| **A — mechanism** | *"direct, **competitive at the 2-OG/Fe(II) substrate pocket** — NOG and Fe(II) compete it off; raising 2-OG attenuates inhibition"* |
| **B — selectivity** | *"**≥13× over TET2**"* (functional, later ~206×), the basis on which **hole 6 (TET2 → CHIP/AML) was marked PATCHED** |

**I had never tested whether those two can both be true.** Using `4NM6` (TET2 catalytic domain + DNA +
Fe(II) + 2-OG) I extracted every residue within 4.2 Å of the bound 2-OG and asked whether TET1 and TET3
conserve them.

### ⛔⛔⛔ THE RESULT

| TET2 2-OG pocket | dist to 2-OG | TET1 | TET3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1261** | 2.58 Å | R1551 ✅ | R956 ✅ |
| ⭐ **C1374** | **3.27 Å** | **C1664 ✅** | **C1069 ✅** |
| H1382 | 3.29 Å | H1672 ✅ | H1077 ✅ |
| D1384 | 3.60 Å | D1674 ✅ | D1079 ✅ |
| H1416 | 2.86 Å | H1706 ✅ | H1111 ✅ |
| H1881 | 3.27 Å | H2028 ✅ | H1673 ✅ |
| T1883 | 3.32 Å | T2030 ✅ | T1675 ✅ |
| R1896 | 2.78 Å | R2043 ✅ | R1688 ✅ |
| S1898 | 2.75 Å | S2045 ✅ | S1690 ✅ |

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **9/9 IN TET1. 9/9 IN TET3. THE 2-OG POCKET IS 100% IDENTICAL ACROSS ALL THREE PARALOGS.**

**And the three structural Zn sites are the same story: 9 zinc-coordinating cysteines, 9/9 conserved in
TET1 and 9/9 in TET3.** Cysteine content of the catalytic domains is indistinguishable (TET1 2.64%,
TET2 2.72%, TET3 2.49%).

> ### ⛔⛔⛔ **THIS IS R151's KILL ARGUMENT, AIMED AT OUR OWN LEAD. I killed the NRK arm because rentosertib's contact surfaces were 23/23 IDENTICAL to its paralogs — "they carry over because there is nothing to discriminate." An agent acting at the 2-OG pocket CANNOT be 13× selective, let alone 206×, because the pocket is the same in all three enzymes. CLAIM A AND CLAIM B CANNOT BOTH BE TRUE.**

### ⭐⭐ AND THE CHEMISTRY SAYS THE SAME THING — WITH THE NUMBER THE OPERATOR JUST SUPPLIED
The pocket is lined by **R1261, R1896, H1416** at salt-bridge distance: it is a **cationic, anion-recognition
site**, built to grip a dicarboxylate.

- **Itaconate — a dicarboxylate, shaped for exactly this pocket — manages IC50 171 µM.**
- **Auranofin's active species is `[Au(PEt₃)]⁺`, a CATION, and is claimed at 76 nM.**

> ### ⛔⛔ **THAT IS A 2,250-FOLD POTENCY ADVANTAGE FOR A CATION OVER A PERFECTLY-SHAPED DIANION, IN AN ARGININE-LINED ANION POCKET. Chemically that is very hard to believe. The itaconate paper the operator supplied for a different reason turns out to be the calibration standard that makes our own mechanism claim look wrong.**

### ⭐⭐ THE RECONCILIATION THAT ACTUALLY FITS EVERY OBSERVATION
Look again at the pocket table: ⭐ **`Cys1374` sits 3.27 Å from the bound 2-OG.** A cysteine, inside the
cosubstrate pocket — **Au(I)'s natural partner.** If Au(I) enters the 2-OG site and coordinates Cys1374:

| observation | explained? |
|---|---|
| potent (76 nM), far beyond a dicarboxylate | ⭐ **yes — Au–thiolate bonds are strong; this is not H-bonding** |
| NOG / excess 2-OG competes it off | ⭐ **yes — same pocket, steric occlusion** |
| Fe(II) sensitivity | ⭐ **yes — Fe(II) is 3.3 Å away** |
| DNA binding unaffected (EMSA) | ⭐ **yes — cosubstrate site, not the DNA interface** |
| ⛔ **≥13–206× selectivity over TET2** | ⛔ **NO — Cys1374 is conserved as TET1 C1664 and TET3 C1069** |

> ### ⭐⭐ **EVERY MECHANISTIC OBSERVATION IN THE SOURCE PAPER IS EXPLAINED BY A CONSERVED ACTIVE-SITE CYSTEINE — AND THAT MECHANISM PREDICTS A NON-SELECTIVE, PAN-TET DRUG.**
>
> ### ⭐ **AND THE SPR DATA ALREADY SAID SO. TET1 KD 1.804 µM, TET2 7.820, TET3 6.280 — 4.3× and 3.5×, i.e. essentially none. Hole 15's "24× binding-vs-activity inconsistency" was the symptom. The structure says the SPR numbers are the honest ones.**

### ⛔ CONSEQUENCES, STATED WITHOUT SOFTENING
1. ⛔⛔ **HOLE 6 (TET2 → CHIP / myeloid malignancy) IS RE-OPENED.** It was marked *"PATCHED functionally
   (~206×)"*. That patch has no structural basis and is contradicted by the same paper's own SPR.
2. ⛔⛔ **ONE OF THE TWO LOAD-BEARING BRAKES IS IN DOUBT.** R159 promoted R154's **2-OG competition** and
   R157's **nuclear saturation** to load-bearing — *"the only things standing between the therapeutic
   window and a regime that shortens bone."* If the mechanism is a conserved-cysteine adduct rather than
   reversible cosubstrate competition, **the negative-feedback brake weakens or disappears.**
   ⭐ **R157's measured nuclear saturation is unaffected and is now carrying the load alone.**
3. ⚠ **A covalent-type Au–thiolate adduct also fits the 35-day half-life (hole 10) better than reversible
   competition does** — which makes hole 10 *worse*, not better.

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 4 — **AND THEN THE HUMAN GENETICS RESCUES IT FROM AN ANGLE I DID NOT EXPECT**

If auranofin is effectively **pan-TET**, the right question is no longer *"is it TET1-selective?"* but
**"what does partial loss of TET1 + TET2 + TET3 together predict for height?"** That is answerable — the
kosmicki 1.45M-exome tables are on disk. **I searched all 29 supplementary tables for all three paralogs.**

| gene | rows across 29 kosmicki tables | height effect |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **TET1** | ⭐ **38 rows, in Tables S4, S5, S6, S7** | ⭐ **singleton pLoF +1.04 SD = +8.32 cm, SE 1.09, P = 2.23e-14** |
| **TET2** | ⛔ **0 rows — appears NOWHERE** | no significant height association |
| **TET3** | ⛔ **0 rows — appears NOWHERE** | no significant height association |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **OF THE THREE TET PARALOGS, ONLY TET1 CARRIES A HEIGHT SIGNAL IN 1.45 MILLION EXOMES. TET2 and TET3 loss-of-function is height-NEUTRAL at that power. So a PAN-TET inhibitor's height effect is DOMINATED BY THE TET1 ARM — the selectivity loss costs us almost nothing on OUR endpoint.**

⚠ **State the limit precisely: absence from a significance-filtered table is "not significant", not
"measured zero."** But TET2 pLoF carriers are **not rare** — TET2 is the canonical clonal-haematopoiesis
gene — so at 1.45M exomes an effect anywhere near TET1's +8.32 cm would have surfaced. ⭐ **The inference is
sound for magnitude even though it is not a published point estimate.**

> ⭐⭐ **NET: the selectivity collapse is a SAFETY problem (TET2/CHIP), not an EFFICACY problem. Hole 6 re-opens; hole 1 does not.**

## => ⭐ PART 5 — **THE ONLY REAL SELECTIVITY SURFACE THERE IS, AND A SPECIES PROBLEM INSIDE IT**

If any TET1 selectivity exists, it cannot be in the pocket — so I looked for **cysteines TET1 has that its
paralogs lack**, across the catalytic domain (**TET1-CD vs TET2-CD = 52.3% identity over 719 columns** —
unlike the NRK case, there IS room to discriminate).

| | |
|---|---|
| Cys conserved TET1↔TET2 | 16 |
| ⭐ **Cys UNIQUE to TET1-CD** | ⭐ **3 — C1746 (TET2 M1456), C1870 (A1605), C1881 (N1616)** |
| ⭐ unique vs **TET3** as well | ⭐ **the same 3** |

**Geometry of the one that sits in ordered structure (`C1746`, via TET2 M1456 in 4NM6):**

| measurement | value | reading |
|---|---|---|
| SASA | **47.0 Å²** (mean Met in chain 36.3) | ⭐ **solvent-EXPOSED — reachable by a soft electrophile** |
| distance to Fe(II) | **23.7 Å** | ⛔ **not the active site** |
| distance to 2-OG | 22.8 Å | ⛔ **allosteric, NOT cosubstrate-competitive** |
| distance to DNA | 18.7 Å | — |

⛔ **C1870 and C1881 fall in TET2's residues 1464–1841, which are absent from the crystal** — the
low-complexity insert. Their accessibility cannot be assessed.

> ⭐ **So a TET1-exclusive, solvent-exposed, Au(I)-reactive cysteine does exist.** ⛔ **But it is 23.7 Å from the catalytic centre, so if THAT is where selectivity comes from, the drug is allosteric — and then it is NOT 2-OG-competitive, and claim A fails instead of claim B. There is no reading in which both survive.**

### ⛔⛔ AND A SPECIES HOLE I DID NOT KNOW WE HAD
I aligned human TET1 to **mouse Tet1** (Q3URK3) at those three positions:

| human TET1 | mouse Tet1 | |
|---|---|---|
| **C1746** | ⛔ **R1725** | **NOT conserved** |
| **C1870** | ⛔ **G1792** | **NOT conserved** |
| C1881 | C1803 | ✅ conserved |

> ### ⛔⛔ **TWO OF THE THREE CANDIDATE SELECTIVITY CYSTEINES DO NOT EXIST IN THE MOUSE. Every in vivo experiment this arm could ever run — and every mouse result already in the ledger — is testing a different molecular surface from the human one. That is a cross-species validity hole affecting the whole in vivo programme, and it was invisible until the structure was actually run.**
>
> ⭐ **It has a favourable corollary worth keeping: if human TET1 carries two extra Au(I)-reactive cysteines that mouse Tet1 lacks, human TET1 should be MORE sensitive than mouse. Any dose scaled from mouse would be an OVER-estimate for a human — the safe direction of error, given that overshoot is what shortens bone.**

## => ⚠ PART 6 — **THREE SMALLER THINGS, TWO OF THEM NEW HOLES**

### ⛔ NEW OFF-TARGET, AND IT IS INSIDE OUR OWN PATHWAY
> **`The zinc finger domains of PARP-1 are selectively and potently inhibited by the Au(I)-based drugs
> sodium aurothiomalate and aurothioglucose` (PMC12720219):** Au(I) drugs act as **"zinc ejectors"** —
> *"Au(I) and (III) interact with cysteine residues of various zinc fingers leading to Zn²⁺ displacement
> and formation of so-called gold fingers."*
> **PARP-1 IC50: sodium aurothiomalate 24.8 nM · aurothioglucose 206 nM · ⚠ AURANOFIN 2,554 nM.**
> PARP-2, which has no zinc fingers, is inhibited only in the µM range.

⚠ **PARP1 is not an incidental off-target — it is the middle of R158's `TRIM37–PARP1–TET1` axis.** Auranofin
at 2.55 µM is above our working exposure, so this is a **ceiling flag rather than an active conflict** —
but the drug hits two consecutive nodes of the same published axis, and the paper's own warning
(*"ca. 3,000 zinc-binding proteins in the human genome"*) belongs in hole 11.

⭐ **And a genuinely useful side-observation: TET1 has a CXXC zinc finger (584–625, 8 Cys / 42 residues =
19.05%) and TET3 has one (50–90); TET2 has NONE.** ⭐ That is the one place in the whole protein where
TET1/TET3 are structurally distinguishable from TET2 by gold-finger chemistry — **but it is outside the
catalytic domain, and Chen's rescue experiments used TET1-CD, so it cannot be the source of the reported
selectivity.** Recorded as a hypothesis, not a patch.

### ⚠ NEW CONFLICT ON THE ALBUMIN QUESTION
> **`Synthesis and in vitro characterization of [¹⁹⁸Au]Auranofin`, EJNMMI Radiopharm Chem 2025
> (PMC12589756):** *"stability in human serum albumin, maintaining **96.9 ± 2.5% integrity over 72 h**."*

⛔ **That is hard to square with R155's basis for withdrawing the R154 dose table** — Nguyen/Østergaard's
finding that auranofin *reacts* with albumin Cys34, **50% of unbound drug gone in 2–3 minutes.**
⚠ **I am NOT reinstating the dose table on this** — one radio-HPLC stability assay does not overturn a
mechanistic study, and radiolabel integrity is not the same measurement as free-drug persistence.
⭐ **But it is a direct measurement conflict on a load-bearing point, and it is now on the record.**

### ⛔ HOLE 9 IS STILL OPEN AND I FOUND NOTHING
**Growth-plate delivery — no measurement of gold in cartilage exists.** I searched Europe PMC five ways
(gold × cartilage × concentration; chrysotherapy tissue distribution; aurothiomalate × synovium;
autoradiography × chondrocyte; auranofin × bone/cartilage across 1,070 hits). ⛔ **Nothing. Fifty years of
chrysotherapy and nobody measured gold in cartilage.** ⭐ **The tool now exists — [¹⁹⁸Au]auranofin was
synthesised in 2025** — but that paper is **explicitly in vitro only** (*"further in vivo and translational
studies are warranted"*). ⛔ **Hole 9 remains the largest untested assumption in the delivery chain.**
