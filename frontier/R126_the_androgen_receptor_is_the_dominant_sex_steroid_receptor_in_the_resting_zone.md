## 4. ⭐⭐ THE DECISIVE ARGUMENT: AN AI ALREADY *IS* THE NAAS INTERVENTION

An aromatase inhibitor does two things at once:
- **oestradiol falls** — the closure signal is removed
- **testosterone RISES roughly two-fold** — 265 → 513 ng/dL on anastrozole; letrozole **2.5×**; 323–343 →
  525–572 at three months

> **On an aromatase inhibitor, the subject's own testosterone becomes a functionally non-aromatisable
> androgen, at twice the concentration. That IS the NAAS intervention, delivered endogenously, at a dose
> no oral agent would match.**

**Adding oxandrolone on top is a marginal increment onto an already-doubled endogenous ligand at a receptor
being driven harder than it ever is physiologically.** Combined with the null CDGP result, the velocity case
for adding a NAAS to this stack is weak.

**The honest counterweight:** AR is *not* saturated at physiological free T (Kd ~1 nM vs free T ~0.2–0.5 nM),
so doubling T raises occupancy meaningfully but does not max it — there is some headroom, and oxandrolone
binds SHBG poorly so more of it is free. **The argument is redundancy, not zero.**
