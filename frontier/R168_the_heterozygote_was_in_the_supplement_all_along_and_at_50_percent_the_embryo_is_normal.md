## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — **THE `ACAN` CONFLICT, QUANTIFIED FOR THE FIRST TIME — AND IT IS AN IN-VITRO ARTEFACT**

R159 named this the sharpest new hole: TET1 loss stops SOX9 binding at `Acan`, and **ACAN pLoF is the most
height-negative gene in the whole kosmicki table at −14.1 cm/allele.** It was never quantified.
**Smeriglio's supplementary s005 has both arms.**

### ⛔ THE KNOCKDOWN ARM — IN A DISH, DEEP shRNA — IS CATASTROPHIC
| gene | NT control | Tet1 shRNA (mean of sh1/sh2) | **fold change** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **Acan** | 1426.8 | 191.1 | ⛔⛔ **−7.5×** |
| ⛔ Col10a1 | 913.4 | 219.7 | ⛔ **−4.2×** |
| ⛔ Col2a1 | 4914.4 | 1844.9 | ⛔ **−2.7×** |

### ⭐⭐⭐ THE KNOCKOUT ARM — THE ACTUAL ANIMAL — IS NOT
> ⭐⭐⭐ **The entire Tet1 KO differentially-expressed list is 28 GENES. Fourteen up, fourteen down.
> `Acan` IS NOT ONE OF THEM. Neither is `Col2a1`. Neither is `Sox9`.**
>
> ⭐ Only two cartilage genes appear at all: **Col9a1 (−2.7×, p = 0.023)** and **Col10a1 (−2.6×, p = 0.017)**.

> ### ⛔⛔ **AND EVERY q-VALUE IN THAT LIST IS BETWEEN 0.68 AND 0.76 — NOTHING SURVIVES MULTIPLE-TESTING CORRECTION. The KO "differentially expressed genes" are nominal-p hits only.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **SO THE ACAN CONFLICT LIVES ENTIRELY IN THE DISH. A 7.5-FOLD COLLAPSE OF THE MOST HEIGHT-NEGATIVE GENE IN THE GENOME UNDER deep shRNA KNOCKDOWN OF A CELL LINE BECOMES *NO DETECTABLE CHANGE AT ALL* IN THE INTACT KNOCKOUT ANIMAL.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **THIS IS R137's MAGNITUDE LADDER FOR THE FIFTH INDEPENDENT TIME, AND IT PUTS NUMBERS UNDER SMERIGLIO'S OWN SENTENCE THAT R159 QUOTED WITHOUT DATA: *"loss of SOX9 target gene expression is MORE MODEST than upon Tet1 KD in vitro."***

⚠ **The honest counterweight, and it is real: the KO transcriptome is UNDERPOWERED.** n = 3 per group, no
FDR-significant gene anywhere. ⛔ **Absence of `Acan` from an underpowered DE list is weak evidence of no
change — the same error class I flagged for TET2/TET3 in the kosmicki tables.** ⭐ **But the asymmetry is
still informative: the KD arm found a −7.5× effect on Acan easily, and the KO arm, at COMPLETE loss, did
not find it at all.**
