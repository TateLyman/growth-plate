## 1. Gafni 2001, in full — and the gap is the last sentence of the results

`Catch-Up Growth Is Associated with Delayed Senescence of the Growth Plate in Rabbits.`
Five-week-old rabbits, **dexamethasone 0.5 mg/kg/day s.c. × 5 weeks**, then 0–16 weeks recovery,
untreated concurrent controls, n=7–8 per time point.

| readout | result |
|---|---|
| femoral length deficit at end of treatment | **−17.4 ± 1.4 mm**, P<0.001 |
| **femoral length deficit at 16 weeks recovery** | **−1.6 ± 1.6 mm, not significant** |
| growth rate | no difference week 1; **marked increase weeks 1–2** of recovery, P<0.001 |
| senescence curves (plate, PZ, HZ heights) | **right-shifted ≈ 3 weeks**, P<0.001 |
| chondrocyte columns at a given time point | **longer than controls** |
| **epiphyseal fusion at 16 weeks** | **88% of controls fused vs 14% of treated, P<0.01** |

**Read those last two rows together, because that is the whole finding.**

**The treated animals recovered essentially all of the 17.4 mm they had lost — and finished with 86% of
their growth plates still open, while 88% of controls had fused.**

**They ended level on length and ahead on runway.** The banked capacity was not consumed by catch-up.
Catch-up returned them to the normal trajectory and stopped there, because nothing was driving them past
it — and **the experiment ended at 16 weeks, at exactly the moment when every subsequent millimetre would
have been growth the controls could no longer make.**

**Nobody has ever run a banking experiment past the control group's fusion point.** That is the cheapest
unexploited experiment in this entire file, and dexamethasone is the most obtainable agent in it.

## 2. Forcinito — the counter is division-based, now on three independent conditions

`Growth-inhibiting conditions slow growth plate senescence.` Forcinito, Andrade, Finkielstain, Baron,
Nilsson, Lui — NICHD.

They reasoned that if delayed senescence were a *specific* consequence of hypothyroidism it would not
generalise, so they used **tryptophan deficiency** instead:

> *"structural, functional, and molecular markers of growth plate senescence were **delayed** by prior
> tryptophan deficiency, indicating that the developmental program of senescence had occurred **more
> slowly** during the period of growth inhibition… growth plate senescence is **not simply a function of
> time per se but rather depends on growth**."*

| condition | slows growth | slows senescence |
|---|---|---|
| hypothyroidism (prior study) | ✓ | ✓ |
| **tryptophan deficiency** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **dexamethasone** (Gafni) | ✓ | ✓ |

**Three unrelated insults, one result. The counter counts divisions, not days.** F-R100's row 2 is not a
hypothesis — it is established, with a mechanism, in the primary literature.

## 3. And now the number I have never had: what our two obtainable arms are actually worth

I went looking for the human trial that combines a flux agent with a deadline agent, because that is two
of our four arms and it has been done.

**`10.1210/clinem/dgaf224`** — JCEM 2025, midpubertal boys, retrospective, tertiary centre:

| group | n | adult height achieved | **vs baseline predicted adult height** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI alone** (2.8 y) vs matched controls | 27 | 166.6 ± 3.1 vs 163.4 ± 1.3 cm, **P=0.003** | **+3.8 ± 3.5 cm** vs −0.3 ± 5.0, **P=0.001** |
| **GH + AI** (2 y AI) vs GH alone | 16 | 167.3 ± 6.1 vs 164.9 ± 3.5 cm, P=0.194 **(ns)** | **+7.5 ± 5.2 cm** vs **+4.3 ± 3.6 cm**, **P=0.050** |

> *"AI treatment **extends the growth period**, resulting in an AHt **surpassing initial predictions**."*

**And they add.** GH alone beat prediction by **+4.3 cm**; AI alone by **+3.8 cm**; together by **+7.5 cm**
against a simple sum of 8.1. **Approximately additive — which is exactly what Klinefelter predicted for
setpoint plus deadline (F-R029), now confirmed pharmacologically in humans.**

The honest caveats: the head-to-head height comparison at n=16 was **not significant** (P=0.194); the
significant result is against *predicted* height, which is the right within-person measure but is exposed
to regression to the mean; and these are short-statured boys, so it is rescue, not gain above normal.

Network meta-analysis for context: AI alone **+4.91 cm**, GnRH agonist alone **+5.55 cm**, no significant
difference between them; GH alone in ISS ≈ **+4 cm**.

## 4. The arithmetic of the whole programme, for the first time

| arm | mechanism | **magnitude** | evidence | obtainable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **flux** | GH | **+4.3 cm** over prediction | human trial | **yes, approved** |
| **deadline** | anastrozole | **+3.8 cm** over prediction | human trial | **yes, approved** |
| **flux + deadline together** | GH + AI | **+7.5 cm**, ≈additive | human trial | **yes — this is today's ceiling** |
| **pool — number** | *PTCH1* haploinsufficiency | **+2.9 SD ≈ +19 cm** (Yamada mother, 173.3 cm at 17); range +0.8 to +3.8 SD | human genetics | **no drug at the right dose** |
| **pool — capacity / banking** | dexamethasone, tryptophan restriction, hypothyroidism | **3-week senescence right-shift; fusion 88% → 14%** | rabbit, rat | **yes, approved — and never exploited** |
| **reset** | partial reprogramming | **not demonstrated in any mammal** | — | no |

**Two things jump out of that table.**

**First: the pool arm is by far the largest term, and it is the one with no drug.** Genetic halving of
*PTCH1* is worth roughly two to three times what GH and anastrozole together buy. Everything F-R096
through F-R099 established says the human phenotype is real, postnatal, dose-dependent and carried to
adult height — and there is still no molecule that reproduces chronic 50% brake reduction.

**Second: banking is the only arm that is both large and unexploited.** Gafni moved fusion from 88% to
14%. Nobody has combined it with anything.

## 5. The composition that has never been tested, and every agent in it is approved

Gafni banked and did not spend. The JCEM boys spent without banking. **The sequence nobody has run:**

1. **bank** — growth inhibition to slow the division counter (Gafni: 3-week right-shift, fusion 88% → 14%)
2. **release**
3. **spend hard** — GH during the extended runway, when controls have already fused
4. **hold the door** — anastrozole, which adds ≈ additively to GH

**Every agent is approved.** The prediction is that the +7.5 cm ceiling is set by the runway, and Gafni
shows the runway is extendable by a mechanism entirely independent of oestrogen.

**And the honest reason for caution, which is the same one that has held all round:** catch-up growth is
defined as returning *toward* a trajectory. Gafni's animals stopped at −1.6 mm and did not overshoot,
with plates open. **Something has to drive them past the trajectory, and no experiment has ever supplied
that something.** This is a prediction, not a result.

## 6. Row 3 — and it has now been done in cartilage

`Local delivery of OSK factors enables partial cellular reprogramming to mitigate osteoarthritis and
cartilage fibrosis`, Exp Mol Med 2026, PMID 41786976.

AAV-OSK in cartilage: improved cartilage integrity, reduced subchondral thickening, hyalinisation of
fibrocartilage, and — importantly for the F-R081 safety question — **chondrocytes retained
chondrocyte-specific markers with no increase in stemness-associated genes.**

**But it is articular cartilage in an osteoarthritis model, not growth plate, and nobody measured
proliferative capacity or any division-counter readout.** Row 3 remains: *the reset has never been
demonstrated for the growth-plate clock in any mammal.*

**Infinite still lives here and nowhere else.** Rows 1, 2 and 4 compose to a taller finite person —
plausibly a much taller one — and none of them adds a division to any cell.

## 7. Asks

1. **Any growth-inhibition-then-release experiment followed past the control group's fusion point.**
   Gafni stopped at 16 weeks with 86% of treated plates still open. **If anyone continued, the number is
   the answer to row 2.** This is the single most valuable missing result in the file.
2. **Any experiment combining growth inhibition with subsequent growth promotion** — dexamethasone or
   PTU or dietary restriction, then GH — with a final length endpoint. The composition in §5.
3. **Nilsson & Baron's molecular senescence markers** — Forcinito refers to "molecular markers of growth
   plate senescence" from prior work. **I want the gene set**, because it is the readout that would tell
   us whether any intervention resets rather than pauses the counter.
4. **Anything measuring proliferative capacity, telomere state, or H3K4me3 at growth-plate genes after
   OSK or chemical reprogramming in chondrocytes.** Row 3's missing measurement.
5. Still open from earlier rounds: cartilage PAPS after sulfate loading; wild-type Ptch1(+/+)
   growth-plate arm; Xiu 2022 supplementary; a human with *PTEN* or *DEPDC5* loss with adult height and
   bone age.
