## 1. ⛔⛔ TGF-β: THE ANSWER IS **HURT**, AND R130's PROPOSAL IS WITHDRAWN

R130 proposed TGF-β inhibition as the missing renewal-holding arm, on the strength of Chu 2026's finding
that the human root stem-cell niche is **"low in WNT and TGF-β."** **That inference was wrong.**

### The direct experiment says shorter
> **TGF-β/Smad3 signals REPRESS chondrocyte hypertrophic differentiation** (Yang et al., *J Cell Biol*
> 2001;153:35). **Smad3-null mice show enhanced terminal differentiation of epiphyseal growth-plate
> chondrocytes shortly after weaning, and are SMALLER than wild-type.**

**Removing TGF-β/Smad3 removes a brake on hypertrophy. Cells burn through to terminal differentiation
prematurely, and the bone ends up shorter.** In this file's own vocabulary, TGF-β inhibition is
**uncontrolled discharge** — the failure mode R125 and R127 already named.

### And the human genetics agrees, once the paradox is read correctly
The two classic tall-stature TGF-β connective-tissue syndromes look superficially opposite, and are not:

| syndrome | mutation | **tissue TGF-β signalling** | stature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marfan | FBN1 loss (fibrillin normally sequesters TGF-β) | **INCREASED** | **tall**, dolichostenomelia |
| Loeys–Dietz | TGFBR1/TGFBR2 **loss-of-function** | ⭐ **paradoxically INCREASED** — *"heterozygous loss of function mutations in TGFBR1 and TGFBR2… lead to a paradoxical increase in TGFβ signalling"* | **tall**, arachnodactyly |

> **Both human tall-stature TGF-β syndromes carry ELEVATED tissue TGF-β signalling. The mouse with
> REDUCED signalling is short. Every direction points the same way: more TGF-β → longer; less → shorter.**

**⚠ Caveat kept:** Marfan/LDS tall stature is confounded by connective-tissue laxity and altered
biomechanics, so the load-bearing evidence is Smad3-null being short with accelerated terminal
differentiation. That alone is sufficient to reject the proposal.

### ⭐ AND THE REASON I GOT IT WRONG IS A PATTERN I HAVE NOW REPEATED TWICE
- **R126:** AR is the most abundant sex-steroid receptor in the resting zone → I proposed AR as an N agent.
  **R127 refuted it:** androgen *empties* the resting zone.
- **R130:** the root niche is TGF-β-low → I proposed TGF-β inhibition as a niche-holding agent.
  **This round refutes it:** lowering TGF-β accelerates hypertrophy and shortens bone.

> **Localisation is not intervention direction. A niche being low in a signal describes where quiescent
> cells sit; it does not mean lowering that signal tissue-wide will expand the niche.** Both times I read a
> zonal expression gradient as a therapeutic vector. **Rule added to the ledger: no agent enters the stack
> on zonal-expression evidence alone — a perturbation with a length or cell-count endpoint is required.**

### What this does NOT overturn
Chu 2026's niche description stands, and the **WNT-low** half is still supported by an actual perturbation
(R117: Wnt activation costs a third of the PTHrP⁺ pool and 72% of long columns). **The Wnt arm survives;
the TGF-β arm does not.** They were never equivalent — one had a functional test behind it and one didn't.

## 2. ⭐⭐ KAMP 2002 CLOSES THE GH DOSE QUESTION

**Kamp GA, Waelkens JJJ, de Muinck Keizer-Schrama SMPF, Delemarre-van de Waal HA, Verhoeven-Wind L,
Zwinderman AH, Wit JM.** *Arch Dis Child* 2002;87:215–20 — **randomised controlled**, 35 prepubertal ISS
children (17 GH / 18 control), **6.0 IU/m²/day**, five-year follow-up.

- **Bone maturation 3.6 years per 2 years treated, vs 2.0 years per 2 years in controls → BA:CA = 1.8**
- Height SDS for chronological age −2.6 → −1.3 at 2 years… **→ −1.4 at 5 years**
- ⭐⭐ **"Height SDS for BONE AGE was not significantly different between groups."**
- **GH-treated children entered puberty significantly earlier**
- Authors: *"High dose GH treatment before puberty accelerates bone age and induces an earlier onset of
  puberty. This may limit the potential therapeutic benefit of this regimen in ISS."*

> **The entire velocity gain was real, and was paid for ENTIRELY in maturation. That is the cleanest
> statement of the rate–yield trade anywhere in the human literature, and it is randomised.**

### Dose converted for comparability
6.0 IU/m²/day = 2.0 mg/m²/day = 14.0 mg/m²/week. At BSA 0.80–1.00 m² and 20–28 kg (age 7–8, height SDS
−2.9) that is **0.50–0.56 mg/kg/week.**

### The triangulated dose-response — three independent studies
| dose | bone age | height outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **0.24 mg/kg/wk** | **neutral** | +5.4 cm over predicted |
| **0.37 mg/kg/wk** | **neutral** (not different from 0.24) | +7.2 cm |
| 0.50 mg/kg/wk | **advances puberty AND bone maturation** | — |
| **~0.50–0.56 mg/kg/wk (Kamp, RCT)** | **BA:CA 1.8** | **height-for-bone-age UNCHANGED** |

> **The threshold sits at ~0.4–0.5 mg/kg/week and is now supported by three independent studies. Above it,
> GH converts entirely into bone age. Below it, the height banks.**

**And it maps exactly onto PNAS 2025's mechanism:** GH promotes committed division at the expense of the
stem pool. Higher dose → more committed division → faster maturation, earlier puberty, and the pool spent
without net gain. **Kamp is what the PNAS mechanism looks like at the level of a child.**

> **GH must be dosed at 0.24–0.37 mg/kg/week. Not 0.5. The difference between the two is the difference
