# R122 — the answer to "hold it open with time" is no, but the answer to "attack the spine"
# is yes: five levers, all unused by this programme, one of them clinically routine and
# never once run in the direction that would add height.

**Operator: "it was time — can we hold it open? or attack the spine in ways nobody has?"**
Two questions, two different answers.

---

## 1. ⛔ "HOLD IT OPEN WITH TIME" — NO, AND THE REASON IS SPECIFIC

**The counter counts divisions, not time.** lui2010 (tryptophan deprivation delayed the programme at
MATCHED body size), gafni2001 (dexamethasone delayed senescence and fusion, then catch-up), forcinito2011
(growth-inhibiting conditions slow senescence generally), and arm3's own conclusion:

> *"Every intervention that slows the program does so BY SLOWING GROWTH, which is why catch-up growth
> restores a trajectory rather than exceeding it — the divisions are spent more slowly, not spared."*

**Holding the plate open longer spreads a fixed budget over more time. It does not enlarge the budget.**
Suppress-then-release even costs a little: three independent routes put the residual at 1.2–1.9 mm.

### But anastrozole is NOT merely a clock-stopper, and that distinction is the useful part
weise2001: in vehicle rabbits **seven** parameters decline spontaneously — growth rate, proliferation rate,
plate height, proliferative cell number, hypertrophic cell number, **terminal hypertrophic cell size**, and
column density. **Oestrogen ACCELERATES all seven and INITIATES none.**

So removing oestrogen does not just buy time — **it preserves A and h_term, the yield per division, for
longer.** That is why herrmann2002's patient managed **+27 cm at 2.70 cm/yr from age 14**, rather than the
same small budget stretched thin. **Duration × preserved yield, not duration alone.** The base stack is
already doing the right thing; it just cannot make new budget.

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ THE SPINE: THE ACCOUNT THAT IS STILL OPEN

| compartment | closes | budget at hand-BA 16 |
|---|---|---|
| distal femur / proximal tibia | ~BA 16–17 | **spent** |
| **vertebral ring apophysis** | **median ~22 yr (males); 98% fused by 21; some 24–25** | **~5–6 years open** |
| intervertebral disc | never | fusion-independent |

**You do not need to hold the knee open. You need to spend an account that has not been touched.**
Nothing in this programme has ever been aimed at the spine.

### LEVER 1 — the pharmacology transfers, and it is measured
**Vosoritide produces a significant increase in annualised SITTING-HEIGHT velocity of +0.89 ± 1.05 cm/yr**,
and there is a dedicated phase-2 spine-morphology study. **The CNP axis is spine-competent, so stack arms
3–5 already work on this compartment.**
⚠ **Two caveats I am not going to bury: the SD is larger than the mean, and it is measured in
achondroplasia — a rescue.** Applying a rescue magnitude to a normal spine is exactly the violation of the
rescue law (F-R094) this file polices everywhere else. Treat +0.89 as an upper-bound-flavoured estimate,
not a transferable constant.

### LEVER 2 — ⭐ HUETER–VOLKMANN, RUN IN THE DIRECTION NOBODY RUNS IT
The law: **compression retards growth, distraction/tension stimulates it.** It is not theoretical in the
spine — it is the operating principle of anterior vertebral body tethering and growing rods, used in
children every week.

**And distraction stimulates rather than merely preserves:** in children with distraction devices,
*"extra gain in vertebral height growth compared to historical controls was objectified, however, growth
in width was diminished."* Height gained, width traded.

> **VBT applies compression to STOP growth on one side of a scoliotic spine. The reverse — symmetric
> distraction of vertebral endplates to ADD height in a straight spine — is clinically routine hardware
> pointed in a direction nobody has ever pointed it.** Magnitude in a normal spine: never measured.

### LEVER 3 — adult axial unloading (weak, confounded, reported anyway)
200 adults aged 20–50, hooks and rods two levels above and below, simple distraction, ≥1 year:
- first distal vertebra anterior height **30.11 → 30.48 mm, P = 0.037**
- combined proximal + distal anterior **28.51 → 28.83 mm, P = 0.021**
- posterior heights: no change

**⛔ It is a FRACTURE cohort with instrumentation, so this may be fracture healing rather than unloading.
The authors say plainly: "It could not be concluded that axial unloading promoted endochondral
ossification," and note mechanosensitivity "begins to decline soon after the stimulus is initiated."**
At +0.37 mm × 17 presacral units the absolute upper bound is **+0.63 cm**, and I would not bank it.

### LEVER 4 — the disc, fusion-independent
+1.6 ± 0.5 cm after 4 h flotation, only −0.4 ± 0.3 cm reversed by 15 min upright → **~1.2 cm persisting**,
with passive vertebral stiffness reduced across the whole column and **staying below baseline after
reloading**. Works at any age, including after every plate has closed.

### LEVER 5 — the base stack already acts here and has never been measured here
Oestrogen closes the ring apophysis as it closes the knee. **Anastrozole should be extending the spinal
account right now, and nobody has measured sitting height to find out.**

**Against +15.3 needed. Shortfall 1.8 cm.**

**⚠ THE HONEST HEALTH WARNING ON THAT NUMBER.** It is a ceiling built from one measured-but-rescue-derived
velocity, one assumption about closure timing, one speculative N term, and one acute mechanical effect.
**And the deepest caveat: the spine has the same N problem.** Its budget is set by its own resting zones
and its own proliferative exhaustion. Extending ring-apophysis closure by three years does not create
spinal N any more than anastrozole created knee N. **If the vertebral plates exhaust on schedule, most of
the CNP and AI terms evaporate.**

What is genuinely new is not the total. It is that **an entire compartment with a five-to-six-year open
account has been sitting unexamined while every arm of this programme was aimed at a joint with no budget
left.**

## 4. WHAT WOULD DECIDE IT — AND THE FIRST ONE IS FREE

1. ⭐ **Measure sitting height and subischial leg length separately, and stage the ring apophysis on a
   spine film.** Hand bone age reports the compartment that is finished, not the one with budget.
   **This is a tape measure and one radiograph and it re-aims the entire programme.**
2. **Serial sitting height on anastrozole.** If the AI is already extending the spinal account, it is
   visible now, for free, retrospectively.
3. **Does a vertebral growth plate have the same resting-zone structure and the same N trajectory as a
   long-bone physis?** Unknown in this file. **GSE-searchable and never searched** — the whole programme's
   zone biology is femoral/tibial.
4. **Symmetric vertebral distraction for height in a straight spine.** The hardware exists, the principle
   is established, the direction has never been tried.
