## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE POOL IS TWO POPULATIONS AND THIS FILE HAS ONLY EVER TARGETED ONE

`FoxA2+ long-term stem cell population necessary for growth plate cartilage regeneration after injury`
(PMC9076650):

- **FoxA2⁺ cells sit at the TOP of the resting zone, adjacent to the secondary ossification centre.**
  They are **geographically separate** from PTHrP⁺ cells, which occupy the BOTTOM of the resting zone.
- FoxA2⁺ have **higher clonogenicity and longer longevity** than PTHrP⁺.
- R112 measured both without knowing they were different niches: **PTHrP⁺ = 0.72%, FOXA2⁺ = 2.80%**
  of chondrocytes. **The larger, more clonogenic population is the one nothing in this file has touched.**

Every pool intervention in the atlas — Trompet's SAG bead, the round-373/388/393 fibrin depot, the whole
Hedgehog thread — aimed at the PTHrP⁺/Hedgehog axis. **The FoxA2⁺ population has never been targeted
pharmacologically by anyone.**

## 2. AND IT CREATES PHYSEAL TISSUE ON DEMAND

After a Salter-Harris type 1-like injury at P18:

- **2.7-fold expansion of FoxA2⁺ cells by day 3**
- **growth plate 96% regenerated by day 7, displaying all three zones (RZ, PZ, HZ)**
- healing occurred **with physeal cartilage — not fibrocartilage, not bone**
- cells formed short (4–8 cell) clonal stacks while migrating, i.e. correct columnar architecture

**That is endogenous creation of growth-plate tissue, with correct zonal organisation, in one week.**
Nothing else in this corpus does that.

## 3. ⛔ AND IT ADDS NO LENGTH — WHICH IS THE RESCUE LAW AGAIN

> **"Overall longitudinal growth was not affected at 7- and 21-day post-op."**

The tissue came back. The bone did not grow more. Regeneration **restored what was destroyed and did
not exceed it** — F-R094's rescue law, holding at the level of the tissue itself. This must be stated
plainly because it is the failure mode the whole strategy has to beat.

## 4. BUT THE TWO THINGS THAT WOULD DECIDE IT WERE NEVER DONE

1. **The trigger was never identified.** The paper states expansion is FoxA2⁺ cells labelled before
   surgery multiplying in response to injury — **no ligand, no pathway, no mechanism.**
2. **It was never tested in a mature animal.** Injury at P18; lineage tracing P0–P28; maximum follow-up
   9 months. **No mature or aged animals were studied at all.**
3. And the population **persists rather than exhausts** — FoxA2⁺ cells are still present in the resting
   zone as single cells, clusters and columns of progeny at 3 and 9 months post-labelling.

**→ "Can FoxA2⁺ expansion be induced pharmacologically, in an old plate, without injuring it" is
completely open, is precisely stated, and is the single highest-value unanswered question for this
target.** A population that persists to 9 months and expands 2.7-fold on demand is the only stock
lever anyone has demonstrated.

### Why the obvious drug does not work
Systemic porcupine inhibition (LGK974) **elongates tibiae in mouse organ culture** and expands the
hypertrophic zone with more cartilaginous ECM — but it **reduces the proliferative zone**, and in vivo
it **impairs trabecular and cortical bone mass and strength** and causes significant bone loss. Wnt is
required for osteoblasts. A systemic Wnt hammer trades the pool for the skeleton. **This is precisely
the constraint that the atlas's own intraosseous depot thread (R373/388/393) exists to solve.**

## 7. SELF-CORRECTION MADE INSIDE THIS ROUND

Mid-round I found Pthlh **rising** in the ageing rat proliferative zone (r = +0.972, Sfrp5 +0.916) and
proposed that the pool is not depleted but **stalled** — cells present, quiescent, in the wrong
compartment — which would make N an activation problem rather than a stock problem.

**That reframing is contradicted by direct cell counting and I am withdrawing it.** Schrier, Ferns,
Barnes, Emons, Newman, Nilsson & Baron, *J Endocrinol* 2006, `Depletion of resting zone chondrocytes
during growth plate senescence`: in rabbit, continuous BrdU labelling shows resting-zone proliferation
rate falls with age **and so does the NUMBER of resting zone chondrocytes per area of growth plate.**
The cells are counted and they are going away. My rat signal is more likely dissection contamination as
the plate thins — a real confound I named when I raised it, and the direct measurement resolves it
against me.

**Schrier also confirms the division-counting model and reproduces arm3's law:** dexamethasone
**decreased RZ proliferation rate AND slowed the numerical depletion.** The pool is spent by its own
divisions; slow the divisions and it lasts longer — at the cost of the growth those divisions would
have produced.
