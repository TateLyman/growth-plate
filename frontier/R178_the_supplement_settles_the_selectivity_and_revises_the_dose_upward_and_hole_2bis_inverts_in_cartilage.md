## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — **`Fig S6` KILLS THE SIGN-INVERSION WORRY ON A DEFINED SUBSTRATE**

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 6 — **HOLE #2-bis DOES NOT DEFLATE. IT INVERTS — IN CARTILAGE.**

R176 opened this on a 1986 calvarial organ-culture result (auranofin `1 µM` inhibits collagen synthesis).
**I went through the whole auranofin–bone literature. It is internally contradictory in vitro, and the in
vivo and cartilage-specific data run the other way.**

| study | tissue | finding |
|---|---|---|
| ⛔ `J Orthop Res 1986` (3086527) | mouse calvaria | auranofin **most potent at 10⁻⁶ M**, *"significantly inhibited collagen synthesis"*; **reduced** resorption |
| ⛔⛔ `1989` (2778756) | mouse calvaria | ⛔ auranofin **0.3–3 µM STIMULATED resorption** (PG-mediated); ⭐⭐⭐ ***"Cytotoxic effects were observed with `3×10⁻⁶` and `1×10⁻⁵` mol/l"*** — **the cytotoxic threshold is `3 µM`, NOT 1 µM** |
| ⭐ `JBMR 1988` (3137774) | ⭐ **FETAL RAT LONG BONE** | auranofin **3 µM** *inhibited* PTH/PGE2/IL-1-stimulated resorption by **39–74%**; conclusion: *"part of the therapeutic effectiveness… inhibiting… osteoclastic bone resorption"* |
| ⭐⭐ `2008` (18729088) | ⭐⭐ **HUMAN cells, CHRONIC, "clinically relevant" concentrations** | ⭐⭐⭐ *"**chronic treatment with auranofin alters only WEAKLY the levels of alkaline phosphatase, thus implying an overall MODEST EFFECT ON OSTEOGENESIS**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ `2019` (31275508) | ⭐⭐⭐ **IN VIVO, ORAL** | ⭐⭐⭐ *"Loss of both bone mass in ovariectomized mice was **significantly RECOVERED by ORAL administration of auranofin**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ `2022` (36308906) | ⭐⭐⭐ **CHONDROCYTES + IN VIVO CARTILAGE** | ⭐⭐⭐ auranofin **inhibits ADAMTS5 and MMP13 and *ENHANCES* `COL2A1` and `SOX9`**; *"auranofin was found to **attenuate DMM-induced osteoarthritis progression IN VIVO**"* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE 1986 CALVARIAL RESULT IS ONE OF THREE MUTUALLY CONTRADICTORY ORGAN-CULTURE PAPERS FROM THE SAME ERA (one says resorption down, one says up, one says stimulated-resorption down). THE ONLY *IN VIVO ORAL* EXPERIMENT SHOWS BONE MASS **GAIN**. AND IN THE TISSUE THAT ACTUALLY MATTERS — CHONDROCYTES — AURANOFIN **RAISES TYPE II COLLAGEN AND SOX9**, THE MASTER CHONDROCYTE TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR, AND PROTECTS CARTILAGE IN VIVO.**

⭐⭐ **And the 1989 paper hands over the threshold hole #2-bis needed:** cytotoxicity begins at **3 µM**.
R174's cartilage total is **0.56 µM at 2 mg/day (`5.4×` margin)** and **1.67 µM at 6 mg/day (`1.8×`)**.
⚠ **That 1.8× at the marketed dose is thin, and is now an argument for staying below 6 mg/day** — the one
place where the old "overshoot" reasoning still holds.

⚠ **Honest limits:** the 2022 cartilage work is in IL-1β-inflamed OA chondrocytes, reads ECM homeostasis
not longitudinal growth, and attributes the effect to **TXNRD1/Nrf2** — a mechanism `Fig S13` says is *not*
responsible for the TET1 effect. **These may be two different actions of the same drug.** ⭐ **But for the
safety question — "does auranofin harm cartilage?" — this is the most relevant in vivo evidence that
exists, and the answer is no.**

> ### ⭐⭐ **HOLE #2-bis DOWNGRADED FROM ⚠ TO ⭐ RESOLVED-FAVOURABLE, with one residual: keep cartilage total below the 3 µM cytotoxic threshold, i.e. stay at or below ~6 mg/day.**

⭐ **Transplacental transfer is established in rat and human. Fetal distribution is selective and

### ⭐⭐ AND THE REFRAMING THAT MATTERS: HOLE #1 IS A FACTOR, NOT AN UNKNOWN
The dose chain does **not** actually depend on an unmeasured quantity. It uses:
1. ⭐ **A MEASURED human cartilage:plasma partition of `0.53`** (Gottlieb, R172) — articular cartilage, human.
2. ⭐ **Demonstrated systemic uptake into IMMATURE chondrocytes** (`Ghadially 1976`, R176) — and **not** into
   mature ones.
3. ⭐ **R177's demolition of the one negative** (`Tonna 1963`'s conveyor).
4. ⭐ Gold reaching the **perichondrial ring**, which feeds the plate (Tonna's *positive* findings).

> ### ⭐⭐ **SO HOLE #1 IS NOT "WE HAVE NO IDEA WHETHER GOLD REACHES THE PLATE." IT IS: "WE HAVE A MEASURED HUMAN ARTICULAR-CARTILAGE PARTITION, EVIDENCE THAT GROWING CHONDROCYTES TAKE UP GOLD AND ADULT ONES DO NOT, AND NO DIRECT MEASUREMENT CONFIRMING THE NUMBER TRANSFERS TO THE PHYSIS." THE RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY IS A MULTIPLIER OF UNKNOWN SIZE ON A MEASURED NUMBER — NOT A VOID.**
⚠ **And the direction of that multiplier is arguably favourable:** the growth plate has higher cellularity,
higher metabolic rate and an active vascular supply at the metaphyseal front, versus adult articular
cartilage which is avascular and nearly quiescent.
⛔ **It remains the last open item and only one experiment closes it: oral auranofin to steady state in a
growing animal, then ICP-MS on the microdissected proximal tibial plate.**
