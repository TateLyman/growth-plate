## => ⛔⛔ PART 4 — **A NEW HOLE I AM RAISING MYSELF, AND IT MAY BE THE DECISIVE ONE**

**Nobody in this thread has asked whether gold reaches the growth plate.**

1. ⛔ **Growth-plate cartilage is avascular.** Its chondrocytes are fed by diffusion from epiphyseal
   vessels through a dense proteoglycan matrix. This file's own R005 is titled *"the ceiling is avascularity."*
2. ⛔ **The circulating gold species is now established to be albumin-Au — a ~66 kDa protein.** Cartilage
   matrix is size-selective, and a 66 kDa carrier is at the top of the range that penetrates it.
3. ⛔ **There is no measurement of gold concentration in growth-plate cartilage in any species.** I searched.

**Three things mitigate it, none of them measured here:**
- ⭐ **Thiol exchange means the gold does not have to travel as albumin-Au.** Dean 2017 shows facile
  Au transfer to free cysteine; small Au-cysteine / Au-glutathione species are readily diffusible.
- ⭐ **The growth plate is thin (millimetres) and the RESTING ZONE — the compartment this whole arm targets —
  abuts the epiphyseal vasculature**, so the diffusion path to the cells that matter is the shortest in the tissue.
- ⭐ **Precedent: vosoritide is a 39-residue PEPTIDE and it works on the growth plate.** Molecular size alone
  does not bar access.

> ⚠ **Status: OPEN and unmeasured. It is not a refutation — it is the largest untested assumption in the delivery chain, and it was mine to raise.**
