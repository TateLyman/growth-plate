## => ⛔ PART 0 — **MY OWN R163 STATEMENT WAS WRONG, AND IT WAS A SEARCH-TERM FAILURE**

R163 closed with this, stated flatly:
> *"Searched Europe PMC five ways… **Fifty years of chrysotherapy and nobody measured gold in cartilage.**"*

⛔ **That is false.** The literature exists, it is from 1974–1984, and the field's own word for it is not

> **`Pääkö, Anttila, Sutinen & Hakala, Ultrastruct Pathol 1984` (PMID 6537095):
> *"In the synovial lining cells and **ARTICULAR CARTILAGE**, the morphology of lysosomes containing gold,

> ### ⛔ **I searched five ways and every one of them used my vocabulary instead of the field's. This is the fourth time in this file that the choice of search term, not the state of the evidence, decided an answer. Recorded as a method failure.**

## => ⚠⚠ PART 4 — **THE HARM SIGNAL IN THE SAME LITERATURE, REPORTED BEFORE THE GOOD NEWS IS BANKED**

> **`Ghadially FN & Lalonde JM, Virchows Arch B 1978` (PMID 97856) — "Long term effects of myochrysine in
> articular cartilage":** intra-articular aurothiomalate produced chondrocyte aurosomes;
> ⚠ ***"There was a REDUCTION IN THE POPULATION OF SUPERFICIAL CHONDROCYTES (Zone I) while those in deeper
> zones (Zones II and III) showed an increased content of intracytoplasmic filaments. It is thought that
> these are REGRESSIVE OR DEGENERATIVE CHANGES PRODUCED BY GOLD."***

> ### ⚠⚠ **GOLD IN CARTILAGE IS NOT INERT. At sufficient local concentration it KILLS CHONDROCYTES IN THE MOST SUPERFICIAL ZONE. In a growth plate the analogous statement would be a zone-specific loss of cells — and this programme's entire mechanism is a zone-specific ratio (resting-zone renewal:commitment). A perturbation that depletes one zone preferentially is exactly the shape of harm that matters here.**

⭐ **Three things bound it, and none removes it:**
1. ⭐ **Route.** This is an **intra-articular bolus into the joint space** — a local concentration orders of
   magnitude above anything systemic oral dosing produces. The **systemic** paper (Oryschak 1976) reports
   aurosome *formation*, not chondrocyte loss.
2. ⭐ **Zone I is the articular surface**, the zone in direct contact with the injected drug depot — the
   highest-dose compartment. It is not the anatomical analogue of the resting zone.
3. ⭐ **Reversible.** Deposits were detectable to **14 months** and **absent at 18 months and 2 years** —
   ⭐ **cartilage gold clears.** ⚠ But *14 months* is also a long persistence, which makes **hole 10
   (35-day half-life, "months to reverse overshoot") WORSE, not better** — in cartilage the residence is
   measured in **months to a year**, not weeks.
