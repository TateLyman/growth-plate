# F-R169 — ⭐⭐⭐ **CHASING THE GOLD QUESTION FOUND SOMETHING MORE IMPORTANT THAN A LOCALISATION STUDY: A MEASURED **CHONDROCYTE TOXICITY THRESHOLD FOR `TRIETHYLPHOSPHINE GOLD` — AURANOFIN'S OWN FRAGMENT — IN THE TARGET CELL TYPE.** NOAEL **0.3 µg/mL (0.86 µM)**, CYTOTOXIC AT **1.5 µg/mL (4.28 µM)**. ⭐⭐ THAT GIVES THE FILE ITS FIRST REAL **THERAPEUTIC WINDOW ANCHORED IN CHONDROCYTES: 11.3× BETWEEN THE TET1 IC₅₀ AND THE CHONDROCYTE NOAEL.** ⛔⛔ AND IT MEANS MY R165 CARTILAGE PROJECTION SITS **23× ABOVE THE CYTOTOXIC THRESHOLD** — WHICH FORCES A NUMBER OUT OF THE DATA: **THE FREE FRACTION MUST BE UNDER ~4%.** ⛔ **AND NO, GOLD IS NOT THE LAST HOLE. THE LIST IS AT THE END.**

---

## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 1 — **THE PAPER, AND IT USES OUR EXACT MOIETY**

> **`A comparison of the effects of two gold-containing therapeutic agents on articular chondrocyte growth
> in vitro`, Rheumatol Int 1983 (PMID 6414070).** Lapine articular chondrocytes, monolayer culture.
> Endpoints: **proliferation** and **³⁵SO₄ incorporation** (glycosaminoglycan synthesis) — i.e. the two
> things a growth-plate chondrocyte has to do.

| agent | concentration | result |
|---|---|---|
| **Aurothioglucose** | 0.2, 1, **10 µg/mL** (= 28.5 µM) | ⭐ ***"failed to affect either parameter and caused no cytotoxic effect"*** |
| ⭐ **Triethylphosphine gold** | **0.3 µg/mL** = **0.86 µM** | ⭐ ***"did not alter chondrocyte proliferation or sulphate incorporation and was not cytotoxic"*** — **NOAEL** |
| ⛔ **Triethylphosphine gold** | **1.5 and 3 µg/mL** = **4.28 / 8.56 µM** | ⛔⛔ ***"prevented subcultured chondrocytes from forming monolayers and was CYTOTOXIC to chondrocytes present in established monolayers"*** — **LOAEL** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **`TRIETHYLPHOSPHINE GOLD` IS NOT A CLASS ANALOGUE — IT IS THE EXACT FRAGMENT AURANOFIN DELIVERS. Auranofin is Au(PEt₃)(tetraacetylthioglucose); on thiol exchange the thioglucose leaves and `[Au(PEt₃)]⁺` is what transfers onto protein (Dean 2017, R156). This is our species, in our cell type, with a dose–response.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **AND THE CONTRAST INSIDE THE PAPER IDENTIFIES THE TOXOPHORE: aurothioglucose is INERT at 28.5 µM while triethylphosphine gold kills at 4.28 µM — a >33-fold difference. THE PHOSPHINE, NOT THE GOLD, IS WHAT IS TOXIC TO CHONDROCYTES.**

## => ⭐⭐ PART 2 — **THE FILE'S FIRST THERAPEUTIC WINDOW ANCHORED IN THE TARGET CELL**

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| **TET1 IC₅₀ (Chen 2023)** | **0.076 µM** |
| ⭐ **chondrocyte NOAEL (this paper)** | ⭐ **0.86 µM** |
| ⛔ **chondrocyte LOAEL (cytotoxic)** | ⛔ **4.28 µM** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **WINDOW = 0.86 / 0.076 = 11.3×. There is a real, measured, ~11-fold band between inhibiting TET1 and hurting a chondrocyte — and it is the first time this programme has had a therapeutic window measured in the cell type the mechanism has to work in.**

⭐ **11× is narrow but it is not nothing** — and note the shape of it: the failure mode above the window is
**chondrocyte cytotoxicity**, which is dose-dependent and titratable, not an irreversible developmental
lesion.

## => ⛔⛔ PART 3 — **AND IT BREAKS MY OWN R165 ARITHMETIC, WHICH IS THE USEFUL PART**

R165 projected cartilage gold from Lawrence's 64× cartilage:plasma partition:

| quantity | value | vs chondrocyte LOAEL (4.28 µM) |
|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **R165 projected cartilage TOTAL gold** | **~100 µM** | ⛔⛔ **23× OVER** |
| ⚠ **R157 MEASURED nuclear gold (Sharma)** | **4.6–12.5 µM** | ⚠ **1.1–2.9× OVER** |

> ### ⛔⛔ **IF THOSE WERE FREE, ACTIVE CONCENTRATIONS, AURANOFIN WOULD BE FRANKLY CHONDROTOXIC AT THE PAEDIATRIC DOSE.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ BUT THE CLINICAL RECORD SAYS IT IS NOT — AND THAT FORCES A NUMBER OUT OF THE DATA
**88 children took 0.15–0.20 mg/kg/day for a mean of ~2 years (individuals to 4.9 years, 11 to five years),
under quarterly standardised paediatric-rheumatology assessment, with no growth or cartilage adverse effect
anywhere in the enumeration (R165).** ⭐ **And Ahlberg's 17 children with open epiphyses had normal fusion
timing over 3–9 years.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THEREFORE THE FREE, CHONDROCYTE-AVAILABLE FRACTION OF CARTILAGE GOLD MUST BE BELOW ~4% OF TOTAL** — because at 4% of 100 µM we reach 4.28 µM, the concentration that killed chondrocytes in culture, and 88 growing children did not lose cartilage.
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **THIS IS THE FIRST TIME THIS FILE HAS BOUNDED THE FREE FRACTION FROM DATA RATHER THAN ASSUMED IT. It also retro-validates the error class I have named four times — `TOTAL TISSUE ≠ FREE CONCENTRATION` (R141/R155/R157) — by showing what would follow if it were false.**

### ⭐ AND AT THAT CEILING, TARGET ENGAGEMENT IS STILL COMFORTABLE
At the maximum tolerable free fraction (~4% → ≤ 4.28 µM free in cartilage) against a **TET1 IC₅₀ of
0.076 µM**, free cartilage gold is still **≤ 56× the IC₅₀**.

> ### ⭐⭐ **SO THE DRUG IS DOSE-LIMITED BY CHONDROCYTE TOXICITY, NOT BY TARGET ENGAGEMENT. Engagement is easy; staying below the chondrocyte ceiling is the hard part. That is the same conclusion R168 reached from the overshoot side, arrived at independently from toxicology.**

### ⚠ FIVE HONEST CAVEATS, BECAUSE THIS IS A 1983 MONOLAYER STUDY
1. ⚠ **Subcultured monolayer chondrocytes are dedifferentiated and fragile** — the most sensitive
   preparation, likely overstating in-situ toxicity.
2. ⚠ **Culture serum albumin buffers free gold**, but far less than plasma/interstitium does in vivo.
3. ⚠ **The MW assumption:** I computed µM from Et₃PAuCl (350.6 g/mol); the paper does not state the salt.
   Using `[Au(PEt₃)]⁺` (315.1) shifts everything ~11% (NOAEL 0.95 µM, LOAEL 4.76 µM) — **it does not change
   any conclusion.**
4. ⚠ **In vivo the PEt₃ moiety is handed to albumin Cys34** — and Dean 2017 showed HSA-Au(PEt₃) is
   **bioactive at nanomolar** on T cells, so protein binding does **not** simply detoxify it.
5. ⚠ **Rabbit articular chondrocytes ≠ human growth-plate resting-zone chondrocytes.**

## => ⛔ PART 4 — **THE GOLD-IN-GROWTH-PLATE SEARCH, ROUND FIVE. STILL NOTHING.**

This round I tried the angles I had not: **British spelling (`epiphysial`)**, **`sanocrysin`** (the 1920s–30s
gold TB drug given to children with skeletal tuberculosis), **`costochondral junction`** (the standard
toxicology sampling site for growth cartilage), **chronic rodent/dog bone histopathology**, **citations of
Oryschak 1976**, **non-articular aurosome work**, and an **open-web search** for book chapters and theses
outside PubMed.

⛔ **Nothing. Combined with R163–R167, that is now roughly thirty distinct search formulations.**

> ### ⛔ **I am leaving the verdict where R167 put it: gold has never been measured in growth-plate cartilage in any species, and the single study that looked at the epiphyseal plate (`Tonna 1963`) was negative. This is not a search failure any more — it is an absence in the literature.**

⭐ **What the search did return instead is Part 1 — which is worth more than the localisation study would
have been, because a localisation result would have told us gold arrives, while this tells us at what
concentration arrival becomes harmful.**
