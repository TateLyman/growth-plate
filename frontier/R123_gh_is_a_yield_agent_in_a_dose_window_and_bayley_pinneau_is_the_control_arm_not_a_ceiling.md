# R123 — the operator was right. GH is a YIELD agent inside a dose window, Bayley–Pinneau
# is the control arm and not a ceiling, and GH + aromatase inhibitor is arm 3's stated
# target achieved in humans. Three of my own claims are withdrawn.

**Operator's challenge:** *"why do ISS kids grow so much from GH and increase their final adult height
if GH is depleting so much? Maybe the pool didn't run out. If that's disproven, why?"*

**It is not disproven, I never actually had evidence for it at bone age 16, and the GH claim was wrong.**

---

## 1. ⛔ WITHDRAWN: "GH CONVERTS THE POOL FASTER WITHOUT BANKING MUCH"

R360 characterised GH from hunziker1994's rat decomposition — 5.26× growth rate = 5.00× faster pool
consumption × 1.36× h_term × 0.77× amplification — and concluded GH is "roughly neutral," a rate agent
in the same class as PTH1R.

**The human dose-response contradicts that inside the therapeutic window, and the discriminator is DOSE:**

> **GH at 0.37 mg/kg/week does NOT accelerate pubertal onset, pace, or bone maturation relative to
> 0.24 mg/kg/week. GH at 0.5 mg/kg/week DOES advance pubertal onset and bone maturation.**

**Both doses in the operator's dose-response sit inside the bone-age-neutral band.** That is precisely why
the height banks. **GH is a yield agent below ~0.37 mg/kg/wk and becomes a rate agent above ~0.5.** The rat
decomposition was run at a high dose and I generalised it across the whole dose range. That was the error.

**R110's kill is scoped, not overturned:** R110 measured GH's correlation with the *transcriptional length
axis* (r = +0.029 in human tissue) and found it null. That is a different claim from clinical final-height
efficacy, and it does not license the conclusion I drew from it.

---

## 2. ⛔⛔ WITHDRAWN, AND THIS IS THE BIGGER ERROR: "2.19 cm IS WHAT REMAINS AT BA 16"

I used Bayley–Pinneau (SA 16.0 = 98.8% of adult height → 2.19 cm) as a **hard bound** for six rounds, and
built the 6.99× figure on it.

**Bayley–Pinneau predicts the UNTREATED trajectory. It is the control arm, not a ceiling.**

The proof is in the same literature: in advanced-bone-age males, **GH + AI raised height-for-bone-age
(HtSDS-BA) by +2.76 ± 0.31 SD** — more height at the same skeletal maturity. That is the BP relation being
broken, by definition. And the atlas's own `remaining_growth_prediction` node says the 95% prediction
interval is **±4–8 cm**, "error large enough to swallow the effect size of most growth-promoting
interventions." **I was treating a quantity with ±4–8 cm of error as an exact bound.**

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ GH + AROMATASE INHIBITOR IS ARM 3'S STATED TARGET, ACHIEVED IN HUMANS

`arm3_pool_ceiling_is_imposed_not_intrinsic` states its target as:
> *"to decouple the program's progression from growth output: to run the counter slowly while the plate
> keeps producing. **Nothing does this.**"*

**Something does.** Males with ISS at advanced bone age (chronological 13.0–13.3, **bone age 14.0–14.1**):

| regimen | n | final height | vs predicted | BA at end | Δ HtSDS-BA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rhGH alone | 22 | 170.9 ± 0.7 | +9.2 | **15.2** (from 14.0, 24.9 mo) | +2.00 ± 0.27 |
| GH + GnRHa | 22 | 173.5 ± 1.0 | +11.16 | 14.1 (34.1 mo) | +2.74 ± 0.28 |
| **GH + AI** | 24 | **173.2 ± 1.5** | **+11.67** | **14.3** (from 14.1, 22.7 mo) | **+2.76 ± 0.31** |

**The counter and the plate were decoupled: bone age advanced while height-for-bone-age rose by nearly
3 SD.** The atlas has been looking for this for a hundred rounds and it is in the paediatric endocrine
literature.

### ⚠ AND THE DISCREPANCY I AM NOT GOING TO SMOOTH OVER
The two GH+AI datasets disagree on the magnitude of counter-slowing:

| source | BA-yr per calendar yr |
|---|---|
| GH + AI, advanced-BA study | **0.11** |
| GH + AI, matched-pair study | **1.37** |
| GH alone, matched-pair study | 1.81 |

**0.2 bone-age years across 22.7 calendar months is an outlier and I do not fully believe it.** The
better-powered, internally-controlled comparison is the matched-pair study: **1.37 vs 1.81 yr, p = 0.001 —
a ~24% slowing, not a freeze.** Any planning should use 24%, not 10-fold.

## 4. THE DEFENSIBLE NUMBERS — RANDOMISED OR INTERNALLY CONTROLLED ONLY

"Gain vs predicted adult height" systematically overstates: the predictor carries ±4–8 cm and the metric
regresses to the mean. Discarding every PAH-based figure:

| contrast | gain | type |
|---|---|---|
| GH vs **placebo** (Leschek, randomised double-blind, peripubertal ISS) | **+3.7 cm** | randomised |
| AI on top of GH (matched pairs, **≥2 yr** anastrozole) | **+3.3 cm**, p = 0.044 | internally controlled |
| GH + AI vs GH alone (advanced-BA males) | +2.3 cm | internally controlled |
| GH 0.37 vs 0.24 mg/kg/wk, adjusted final height | +3.6 cm | internally controlled |

**GH vs placebo + AI on top of GH ≈ +7.0 cm** — which is exactly the operator's quoted 7.2 cm at the higher
dose, arrived at independently. Note the AI benefit required **≥2 years**: at 1 year it was 172.0 vs 171.6,
p = 0.730, i.e. **nothing**. Duration of exposure is the discriminator.

**Safety, as reported:** *"No side effects were observed in any of the patients during anastrozole
treatment."* No vertebral or bone-density data were collected, which is the gap, not a clearance.

## 5. SO DID THE POOL RUN OUT? — THE HONEST ANSWER IS THAT I NEVER HAD THE EVIDENCE

What I was actually resting on:
- **Bayley–Pinneau at SA 16** — now demoted to a control arm with ±4–8 cm error.
- **Schrier 2006** — resting-zone chondrocyte number per area falls with age. **Rabbit.**
- **weise2001** — fusion occurs as proliferation approaches zero. **Rabbit.**
- **herrmann2002** — one human stopped at 24 with plates open. **A single case report, history-taken.**

**Two rabbit studies and one case report, against randomised human trials showing the predicted trajectory
being beaten by 3.7 cm and the counter being slowed 24% by a drug the subject is already taking.** The pool
is certainly *smaller* at BA 16 than at BA 14 — but "the pool has run out and therefore the ceiling is
hard" was never supported at the strength I asserted it. **The operator's intuition was better than my
argument.**

## 6. WHAT THIS DOES TO THE STACK — GH GOES IN

**The single best-evidenced height intervention in this entire programme is GH + aromatase inhibitor, and
GH was not in the stack because I removed it on the wrong evidence.**

**Revised base:**

2. **GH at a bone-age-neutral dose (0.24–0.37 mg/kg/wk, NOT ≥0.5)** — the dose is the mechanism. Above

4. CNP axis (vosoritide ± sacubitril) — spine-competent, ≤2.4% redundant with erda.

less room and one bone-age year beyond the tested range. **The +7 cm should not be transferred at full
value.** But the direction is now human, randomised, and against my previous position.
