## => ⭐⭐⭐ PART 2 — **THE CHONDROCYTE CEILING: THE TOXIC SPECIES IS DESTROYED IN VIVO**

R169/R171 established Kirkpatrick's chondrocyte thresholds and the uncomfortable finding that **human
therapeutic serum gold (0.6–1.2 µg/mL) exceeds the cytotoxic concentration (0.5 µg/mL gold).** The
resolution I offered was albumin buffering (12–24×) plus the missing matrix. **There is a better one, and
it comes from the paper the operator's earlier request pointed at.**

> **`Intoccia AP et al., "Pharmacokinetics of auranofin in animals," J Rheumatol Suppl 1982` (PMID 6813497):**
> ⭐⭐⭐ ***"Evidence indicated that AF was RAPIDLY DEGRADED TO TRIETHYLPHOSPHINE OXIDE with the remaining
> molecular fragments postulated to be a PROTEIN-GOLD COMPLEX and acetylthioglucose."***

### ⭐⭐⭐ NOW PUT THAT AGAINST KIRKPATRICK'S OWN INTERNAL CONTROL
| compound | phosphine? | chondrocyte result |
|---|---|---|
| ⛔ **Auranofin (intact)** | ⭐ **YES** | ⛔ **cytotoxic at 0.5 µg/mL gold** |
| ⭐ **Aurothioglucose** | **NO** | ⭐ **INERT at 5 µg/mL gold — 10× above therapeutic** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **KIRKPATRICK'S OWN CONTROL IDENTIFIES THE PHOSPHINE AS THE CHONDROCYTE TOXOPHORE. INTOCCIA SHOWS THE PHOSPHINE IS OXIDISED OFF *IN VIVO*, LEAVING A PROTEIN–GOLD COMPLEX. THE SPECIES THAT KILLED CHONDROCYTES IN THE DISH IS NOT THE SPECIES CIRCULATING IN A TREATED ANIMAL — AND WHAT *IS* CIRCULATING IS CHEMICALLY THE AUROTHIOGLUCOSE CASE, WHICH KIRKPATRICK HIMSELF FOUND INERT.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **THAT RESOLVES THE PARADOX PROPERLY: forty years of clinical serum gold sitting ABOVE the in vitro LOAEL, with no cartilage pathology in anyone — including 88 children for ~2 years — because Kirkpatrick applied INTACT auranofin to a monolayer, and intact auranofin barely exists in vivo.**

⭐ **Kirkpatrick anticipated exactly this and said so:** *"It would be of interest to know what **metabolites**
of triethylphosphine gold are formed in vivo and what effects they might have on the chondrocyte."*
**Intoccia answered his question the year before, in the same drug's development programme.**

### ⚠ THE ONE TENSION, RECORDED
`Dean 2017` (R156) showed **HSA-Au(PEt₃) — albumin-bound with the phosphine RETAINED — is bioactive at
nanomolar.** ⚠ **So some phosphine-gold does survive on protein.** ⭐ **But Dean's is a synthetic construct
made by deliberate ligand transfer; Intoccia measured what actually happens after an oral dose. For the in
vivo question Intoccia governs, and I am weighting it accordingly rather than pretending the tension is
absent.**

## => ⭐⭐ PART 3 — **HOLE 10 GETS WORSE AND BETTER AT THE SAME TIME, AND THE DISTINCTION IS THE POINT**

> **`Vernon-Roberts B, Doré JL, Jessop JD, Henderson WJ, Ann Rheum Dis 1976;35:477–486`** — 7 autopsy cases
> who died **0 to 23 years** after stopping chrysotherapy, plus 23 serial synovial biopsies from 5 patients.
>
> ⛔⛔ ***"Gold persisted in synovial and other tissues for up to 23 YEARS after chrysotherapy was stopped."***

⛔ **That is far worse than the 14 months R164 recorded for cartilage.** ⭐ **But the same abstract separates
the compartments, and that is what matters:**

> ⭐⭐ ***"During active chrysotherapy gold was abundant in synovial LINING cells… after stopping
> chrysotherapy, gold DISAPPEARED FROM THE SYNOVIAL LINING CELLS."***
> ⭐ ***"Gold accumulated progressively in the SUBSYNOVIAL CONNECTIVE TISSUES… but was not uniformly
> distributed, and BORE NO RELATIONSHIP TO FLUCTUATIONS IN SERUM GOLD LEVELS."***
> ⭐ ***"…found within the MACROPHAGES of many organs, renal tubular epithelium…"***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **THE 23-YEAR DEPOT IS SEQUESTERED AUROSOMAL GOLD IN MACROPHAGE LYSOSOMES, EXPLICITLY DECOUPLED FROM SERUM. THE METABOLICALLY ACTIVE COMPARTMENT — THE SYNOVIAL LINING CELL — CLEARED WHEN DOSING STOPPED.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐ **SO HOLE 10's REVERSAL PROBLEM IS ABOUT THE ACTIVE POOL, WHICH FOLLOWS THE 19.5-DAY (DOG) / ~35-DAY (HUMAN) HALF-LIFE — NOT THE 23-YEAR MACROPHAGE DEPOT. THE SAME `TOTAL ≠ FREE` DISTINCTION THAT R172 FORCED ON THE PARTITION APPLIES TO THE KINETICS.**

⚠ **What it does confirm: the "gold is concentrated in INFLAMED synovium" picture** — *"the overall findings
indicated that gold is selectively concentrated within inflamed synovial tissues."* ⭐ Consistent with
Gottlieb and Grahame, and with R172's hypothesis that this sink is largely absent in a non-arthritic subject.
