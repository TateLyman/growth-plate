Protein expression and purification. 
FGFR1 (amino acids 458-765 containing mutations C488A, C584S), and lambda protein 
phosphatase were cloned into pETDuet, and transformed into BL21 DE3 cells.  Cultures were 
grown in Terrific Broth + Carbenicillin (100 µg / mL) at 37 ºC until the OD600 was 
approximately 0.4.  The temperature was lowered to 18 ºC, and protein expression was initiated 
with 0.5 mM IPTG when the OD600 was approximately 0.6. Cells were harvested 18 hours post-
induction by centrifugation at 4 ºC. Cells were lysed in Buffer A (20 mM Tris pH 7.8, 10mM 
imidazole, 300 mM NaCl, 5 mM 2-mercaptoethanol (BME)) with two passages through a

Crystallization and data collection. 
FGFR3 + TYRA-300 crystals were grown with 8 mg / mL FGFR3 plus 1 mM TYRA-300 in 
hanging drops at 18 ºC consisting of 0.5 µl of protein (10 mM HEPES pH 7.5, 100 mM NaCl, 2 
mM TCEP, 5 mM EDTA) and 0.5 µl of mother liquor (17 % (w/v) PEG Smear Medium 
(Molecular Dimensions, UK), 10 % (v/v) Tacsimate pH 5.25 (Hampton Research, Aliso Viejo), 
0.2 M Ammonium Sulfate), over a reservoir of 300 µl mother liquor (EasyXtal X-seal 
crystallization plates, NeXtal).  Shortly after setup, drops were touch seeded using a cat whisker, 
and large thin plates grew over the next 1 - 4 weeks. Crystals of FGFR3 were harvested using 
Mitegen Dual Thickness MicroLoops LD and cryo-protected by adding mother liquor 
supplemented with 25 % glycerol and 100 µM TYRA-300 22 over the crystallization drop, 
followed by a quick pass through a full cryoprotectant drop, before plunge freezing in liquid 
nitrogen. X-ray diffraction data were collected using a single crystal (T=100 K) at a wavelength 
of 1.0000 Å at ALS beamline 8.2.2, and data were processed using XDS1 and Aimless.2 FGFR1 
+ TYRA-300 crystals were grown with 9 mg / ml protein plus 1 mM TYRA-300 in hanging 
drops at 4 ºC consisting of 0.6 µl protein (25mM Tris pH 8.0, 100mM NaCl) and 0.5 µl mother 
liquor (25% PEG 8000, 25% Ethylene Glycol, 0.25 M Ammonium Sulfate, 0.1 M PCTP Buffer 
pH 7.5) over a reservoir of 300 µl mother liquor (EasyXtal X-seal crystallization plates, NeXtal).
