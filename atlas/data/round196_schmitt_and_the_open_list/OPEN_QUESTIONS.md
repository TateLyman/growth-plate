# What is still open on the PTH1R line after round 196

## Answered this round

| question | answer | grade |
|---|---|---|
| Does a pair-fed PTH growth experiment exist? | Yes — `schmitt2000`. But its healthy pair-fed length contrast does not survive recomputation (p = 0.19, n = 7); only the uraemic rescue does (p = 0.0055). | C |
| Does PTH1R agonism lengthen bone in a growing non-rat mammal? | `koh2022`: no — six endochondral length nulls in wild-type mouse pups at 50 µg/kg/day, with positive bone-volume controls in the same bones. | C |
| Does PTH tone move the growth plate at all in a growing animal? | `nakagawa2008`: a 61 % reduction in PTH changed tibia length, plate height, hypertrophic zone height, osseous front advance and BrdU by nothing. | C |
| Is the weight effect separable from the length effect? | They dissociate: raising *and* lowering PTH both raise weight gain and feed efficiency, and neither moves the plate. | C |

## Still open, in order of what would change the decision

**1. Does PTH1R agonism delay or advance growth-plate fusion?**
One observation exists in the world: `xie2012`, achondroplastic mice, PTH 100 µg/kg × 4 weeks,
*"partially prevent[ed] the premature fusion of the cranial synchondroses"* — **delay**, in a
disease model, at the cranial base. Nothing in a wild-type animal, nothing at a limb physis,
nothing in a human. Confirmed empty by the round-196 sweep.
*Needed:* bone-age-vs-chronological-age from the Winer paediatric cohorts (used as an entry
criterion, never reported as an outcome); or a fusing-species study dosed to fusion.

**2. PTH1R expression across human pubertal stage.**
Both ligands are known to fall with maturation (`kindblom2002`: |r| 0.816 Ihh, 0.911 PTHrP).
`chu2026` places the receptor in the pre-hypertrophic zone at *early* puberty only. Nobody has
measured the receptor across the range, and `kindblom2002` explicitly excluded the resting zone.
Confirmed empty by sweep.

**3. Vertebral body height under any PTH1R agonist, any species, any age.**
Every study measures vertebral BMD, BMC and cross-sectional area — the FDA tables give L6 X-area
up to +16 % — and none measures height. ~40 % of standing height is unmeasured. Confirmed empty
by sweep.

**4. The Hunziker five under a PTH1R agonist.**
Still zero: no terminal hypertrophic cell height, no cells per column, no resting-zone cycle time
under any PTH1R agonist in any species at any dose. `claramunt2015` shows the shape is achievable
(osseous front advance + terminal chondrocyte height + proliferative index + pair-fed controls) —
nobody has run it with the drug.

**5. Does the schmitt2000 uraemia rescue reach the bone?**
The endpoint is snout-to-tail-tip. No femur, no tibia, no plate. `nakagawa2008` measured all of
those in the same model and found them flat. Either the rescue is not skeletal, or it is and the
mirror experiment lacked the sensitivity to see it.

## Confirmed absent by systematic search

Ten Europe PMC queries → 1000 records → 82 deduplicated candidates pairing a PTH1R agonist with a
length, growth, maturation or zone endpoint. **Zero** on bone age under an agonist, **zero** on
human pubertal-stage PTH1R, **zero** on vertebral body height in a growing animal.
