============================================================================================
[1] THE HEADLINE SPLITS IN TWO AND ONLY HALF OF IT SURVIVES
============================================================================================
    SHAM PAIR-FED length gain: +0.84 cm, t=1.40, p=0.186, d=0.75
    -> NOT significant as a two-sample t-test. The paper's
       significance for this contrast comes from ANOVA followed by
       DUNCAN'S test across four groups.
    URAEMIC length gain      : +1.39 cm, t=3.38, p=0.0055, d=1.80
    -> robustly significant by any test.

[2] THE PATTERN IS RESCUE, NOT SUPRANORMAL GROWTH
    sham solvent 5.35 -> sham PTH 6.19 (ns on recomputation)
    uraemic solvent 4.78 -> uraemic PTH 6.17
    Uraemia costs 0.57 cm of length gain. PTH restores the uraemic
    animal to 6.17, which is the SAME as the treated sham (6.19) and
    above the untreated sham (5.35). Whether PTH pushes a healthy
    animal past its own ceiling is exactly the contrast that fails
    to reach significance.

[3] THE 'NO WEIGHT EFFECT' CLAIM IS AN UNDERPOWERED NULL
    sham pair-fed weight gain: 44.4 -> 52.8 g, +19%, p=0.34
    minimum detectable difference at 80% power: 25.6 g, i.e.
    58% of the control's own weight gain.
    A 19% weight-gain difference is not detectable in this design.
    'Did not influence body weight gain' therefore does not
    establish that body weight is uninvolved.
    AND IN EXPERIMENT 3, WITH n=11, IT IS SIGNIFICANT: 37.4 -> 45.2 g, p=0.0359,
    at IDENTICAL food intake (213 vs 210 g, ns) - so PTH raised feed
    efficiency. The larger experiment finds the weight effect the
    smaller one was too small to see.

[4] WHAT SURVIVES, PRECISELY
    (a) CONTINUOUS infusion of PTH(1-37) at the same daily dose did
        NOT alter length gain; INTERMITTENT did. That schedule
        contrast is internal to the paper and is the cleanest thing
        in it.
    (b) In URAEMIC growing rats, intermittent PTH(1-37) restores
        length gain. Robust.
    (c) In HEALTHY pair-fed growing rats the point estimate is +16%
        but the contrast does not survive a pairwise test at n=7.
    (d) The pair-feeding does NOT settle the body-weight question,
        because pair-feeding equalises INTAKE, not weight, and PTH
        raised feed efficiency in the experiment large enough to see
        it.

[5] WHAT IS STILL NOT MEASURED HERE
    Snout-to-tail-tip is not a bone. No femur or tibia length, no
    growth plate histology, no cells per column, no terminal
    hypertrophic cell height. pQCT was cross-sectional only
    (metaphysis and diaphysis), so it carries no length information.
