===== PAGE 22 =====
Preliminary data are encouraging as SURF301 continues
TYRA-300 plasma concentrations indicate adequate 
target coverage at ≥90 mg QD; further pharmacokinetic 
characterization is ongoing.
Preliminary anti-tumor activity of TYRA-300 in heavily 
pre-treated patients is encouraging, especially at doses 
≥90 mg QD.
Preliminary data from SURF301 suggest TYRA-300 to 
be generally well tolerated, with infrequent FGFR2-
and FGFR1-associated toxicities.
Phase 1 is ongoing and 
the MTD was not reached; 
the optimal dose is yet to 
be determined. Emerging 
profile warrants continued 
development in mUC.
