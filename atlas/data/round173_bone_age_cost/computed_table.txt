  bone age advance, 3 y : anastrozole+GH 2.5 y vs GH+placebo 4.1 y
  linear growth         : COMPARABLE between arms (stated by the paper)

AGENT 2 - VOSORITIDE IN NON-FGFR3 CHILDREN (dauber2026)
==============================================================================
  velocity              : 4.53 -> 8.09 cm/yr
  BA/CA ratio           : 0.94 -> 0.92 (P=0.22, weak null)
  implied BA advance    : 0.78 y per 1.0 y chronological (at CA0=7.0 y)
  -> ON TREATMENT       : 10.4 cm per BA-year
  -> PRE-TREATMENT      : 4.8 cm per BA-year (ratio steady at 0.94)
  -> improvement        : 2.15x

  ERROR IS THE STORY HERE AND IT IS LARGE. A Greulich-Pyle reading carries
  ~0.5 y of error; dBA is ~0.8 y. Propagating +/-0.5 y on dBA alone:
    dBA = 0.28 y -> 28.9 cm per BA-year  (6.00x)
    dBA = 0.78 y -> 10.4 cm per BA-year  (2.15x)
    dBA = 1.28 y -> 6.3 cm per BA-year  (1.31x)
  The direction survives the error band; the magnitude does not. And the
  underlying null is P=0.22 over 12 months with NO equivalence margin given.
  Sensitivity to the assumed mean age (cohort was 3-11 y):
    CA0 = 5.0 y -> dBA 0.82 y -> 9.9 cm per BA-year
    CA0 = 7.0 y -> dBA 0.78 y -> 10.4 cm per BA-year
    CA0 = 9.0 y -> dBA 0.74 y -> 10.9 cm per BA-year

AGENT 3 - GROWTH HORMONE
==============================================================================
  NOT PLACEABLE ON THIS AXIS, AND THAT IS THE FINDING.
  chu2025 shows GH depletes the resting-zone progenitor pool by shifting
  divisions toward the committed side. That cost is paid in PROGENITORS,
  not in bone age, and a BA/CA ratio cannot see it. Two independent lines
  converge on the same clinical signature - the waning of GH efficacy after
  1-2 years (chu2025) and the authors of dauber2026 noting that GH gave
  0.49/0.62/0.7 SD in these same three conditions but growth rates SLOWED in
  subsequent years, whereas the CNP axis sustained multi-year gains.
  A second currency is required before GH can be compared.
