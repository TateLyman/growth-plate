  ZNF3 is not a WNT-pathway gene and ZNRF3 IS, and every other panel member is a

THE ASYMMETRY IS REAL BUT IT IS A PROPORTION, NOT THE RAW COUNT. Nine against four
compares categories of different sizes and means little on its own. The defensible
statement is that GP1 has 46% of the available secreted antagonists switched on
and only 21% of the available ligands.

==============================================================================
AND THE CONFOUND, WHICH CUTS AGAINST THE FRAMING
==============================================================================
17 of the 28 columns are gp1-maximal, INCLUDING ALL FOUR LIGANDS. If GP1 were an
antagonist source in the sense rounds 241 and 243 implied, the ligands should not
peak there too. Round 244 established that root-region cells in this dataset carry
more transcript overall, and within-gene scaling does not correct for detection
depth. SO THE REDNESS OF THE gp1 ROW IS NOT EVIDENCE OF ANTAGONIST BIAS.
WHAT SURVIVES is the composition result - WHICH genes are on, where the ligand and
antagonist denominators differ - and that is unaffected by depth in the same way.
