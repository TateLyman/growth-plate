    THE PHOSPHATE ARM IS FGFR1 AND ONLY FGFR1. Dropping FGFR4 coverage does nothing to it, and
    dropping FGFR1 coverage collapses it about six-fold. Round 230's attribution was right and is

    WHAT IT STILL DOES NOT SETTLE, and this is the load-bearing gap from round 230: whether FGFR1
    coverage contributes to the HEIGHT. Both agents that produced large human growth cover FGFR1.
    No human with a normal FGFR3 has been given an FGFR3-only agent and measured.

[3] THERE IS A SECOND SET OF SERIAL PHYSEAL FILMS AND ROUND 228 DID NOT KNOW IT EXISTED
================================================================================================

    farouk2021, Methods, verbatim in substance: plain radiographs of TIBIAL GROWTH PLATES, along
    with ECG, echocardiogram and imaging response assessments, were performed EVERY 8 TO 12 WEEKS.

    In all five children. Prospectively. At one centre, with a named corresponding author.

    ROUND 228 CLOSED THE BONE-AGE LINE PARTLY ON THE PREMISE THAT APEC1621B HELD THE ONLY SERIAL
    PHYSEAL IMAGING IN EXISTENCE and that it was unreachable. The first half was wrong. There is a
    second set, it is smaller, and IT IS ATTACHED TO THE BEST PATIENT IN THE FIELD - subject 3, for
    whom serial tibial physeal radiographs at 8-to-12-week intervals should span the full FORTY
    MONTHS during which his height went from the 83rd centile to above the 99.9th.

    WHY THIS IS NOT A REOPENING OF THE BONE AGE. A tibial physeal film is not a hand-wrist film and
    does not yield a Greulich-Pyle reading. Round 228's closure - that no on-treatment bone age
    exists anywhere - is untouched.

    WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN THE BONE AGE WOULD. The surviving open question after rounds 229 and
    230 is PROPORTIONALITY: is the physeal widening proportionate to the length gained, or in excess
    of it? The gap record for that question specifies the discriminating experiment as "the pairing
    of physeal width against actual bone length gain over the same interval" and calls it the cheap
    version that needs no new procedure. THAT EXPERIMENT HAS ALREADY BEEN PERFORMED. Both halves
    exist for subject 3 - serial physeal width in the radiographs, serial length in the growth chart
    - over forty months, and neither half has been published.

[4] APEC1621B READ IN FULL - and the denominator hiding in what it does not say
================================================================================================

    WHAT IT SAYS. 20 evaluable patients, MEDIAN AGE 15, enrolled June 2018 to July 2022. Erdafitinib
    4.7 mg/m^2/dose, maximum 8 mg, daily, 28-day cycles, MAXIMUM 26 CYCLES. 17 of 20 had glioma or
    glioneuronal tumours. Partial response in 2 of 20; stable disease in 6 more with a median
    duration of 6.5 cycles; six-month PFS 45 per cent, six-month OS 89.7 per cent.

    WHAT IT SAYS ABOUT TOXICITY, COMPLETE: "treatment related adverse events included those
    previously reported with FGFR inhibitors including hyperphosphatemia and nail changes or
    infections. Grade 1 vision changes were also reported. There was one Grade 3 spinal cord
    compression and one Grade 4 intracranial hemorrhage."

    NOT ONE WORD ABOUT GROWTH, PHYSIS, EPIPHYSIOLYSIS, SLIPPED EPIPHYSIS, SCOLIOSIS OR BONE AGE -
    in the one cohort whose protocol mandated a baseline proximal tibial physeal radiograph in
    EVERY patient and serial repeats in every patient with an open plate, with central submission.
    Rounds 226 and 228 inferred this from the abstract's silence; it is now read in full and the
    silence is total.

    AND THE DENOMINATOR NOBODY HAS EXTRACTED. TWENTY CHILDREN, MEDIAN AGE 15, ON ERDAFITINIB, AND
    ZERO SLIPPED EPIPHYSES REPORTED. Against farouk2023's 3 of 7 and RAGNAR's 1 of 11. That looks
    like a contradiction and it is not - it is the DURATION thesis again, stated by the exposure
    data in the same abstract:

        median duration of stable disease ....... 6.5 cycles, about 6 months
        six-month PFS ........................... 45 per cent
        so most of the cohort was OFF DRUG by six months, through progression

    farouk2023 predicted exactly this: prolonged exposure appears necessary, all its subjects
    received more than four months, and it attributed the paucity of bony toxicity in the trials to
    median exposure being limited by early progression. APEC1621B IS THE TEST OF THAT PREDICTION
    AND IT PASSES - the trial with the shortest effective exposure is the trial with no slips,
    despite the oldest median age and protocol-mandated imaging looking for them.

    WHICH TIGHTENS THE SHAPE ROUND 230 DESCRIBED. The failure has a shorter latency than the
    benefit, but not a SHORT one in absolute terms: below roughly four months you get neither.
