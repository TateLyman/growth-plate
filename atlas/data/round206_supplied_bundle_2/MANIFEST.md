## What each settled

- **wang2018** → CORR-199 confirmed beyond doubt (zero axial terms outside the reference list) **and a new defect**: the tibial null travels with a **9 % reduction in body-weight gain** (P<0.01) at the same dose that raised tissue cGMP 52 %. Single-site *and* confounded.
- **kunihiro2020** → **inverts the premise of the round-205 resveratrol gap.** The glucuronide is the transport form; bone GUSB deconjugates it in situ; *"GUSB also catalyzed deconjugation of resveratrol and quercetin glucuronides by bone."* Substrate **>2× higher in immature (4–6 wk) mice**. In OVX mice GUSB fell 24 % while aglycone still tracked substrate → **substrate delivery, not enzyme capacity, is rate-limiting.**
- **wei2024** → a **built** cationic 50.40 nm PLGA resveratrol particle, ζ **+12.57 mV**, **35-day** joint persistence from one injection, LC-MS/MS in knee cartilage, chondrocyte uptake confirmed.

## The connection nobody has made

GUSB is a **lysosomal** enzyme. `avijgan2026` (round 201) reports by TEM that human **resting-zone chondrocytes contain more lysosomes than proliferative-zone chondrocytes**. **The compartment holding the reserve is the one most likely to carry the deconjugating enzyme.** Two papers, two literatures, no mutual citation. New gap `g_l12_does_growth_plate_cartilage_deconjugate_polyphenol_glucuronides`, tractability 1 — a fluorogenic MUG assay on microdissected zones answers it.
