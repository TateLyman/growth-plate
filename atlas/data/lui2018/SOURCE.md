# lui2018 S1 Data

`lui2018_S1Data.xlsx` — S1 Data from:

Lui JC, Jee YH, Garrison P, Iben JR, Yue S, Ad M, Nguyen Q, Kikani B, Wakabayashi Y,
Baron J. **Differential aging of growth plate cartilage underlies differences in bone
length and thus helps determine skeletal proportions.** *PLoS Biology* 2018;16(7):e2005263.
doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.2005263 — PMID 30036371.

Retrieved 2026-08-07 from
`https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.2005263.s020&type=supplementary`

**Licence: CC-BY 4.0.** PLOS Biology is fully open access; the article and its
supplementary files are redistributable with attribution. Redistributed here unmodified
so that `atlas/tools/yield_lui2018.py` is reproducible without a network fetch.

Contains per-animal raw values for Figs 1C, 1D, 2B–2G, 3A, 3B, 3I, 3J, 4D–4H, 4K and
S2B–S2I, S3, S4, S5, S7, S8.

Sheets used by this atlas:
- `Fig2B-G` — mouse resting zone cell count **per 500 µm growth plate width**, by age and bone
- `Fig1C` — mouse calcein-labelled bone growth rate **µm/day**, by age and bone
- `FigS2` — rat zonal histology including RZ cell count, PZ cells/column, BrdU+/column
