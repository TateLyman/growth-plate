# ROUND 187 — THE ABALOPARATIDE REGIMEN

**80 µg SC, once daily, 5 consecutive days per week, 2 days off. Evening, consistent time,
immediately before lying down.**

`tymlos_spl_text.txt` is the DailyMed SPL (setid 712143d9-e21e-4013-bb3b-3426a21060a8) fetched
2026-08-09. Every PK and safety number below is from it verbatim.

## The correction that made this possible

Round 185 said daily dosing is 20× too frequent for a human chondrocyte. Three facts weaken that:

1. **Half-life ~1 h** (§12.3) → exposure window ~5–6 h per 24 h. A property of the *drug*, not the species.
2. **"Pre-dose serum calcium was similar to baseline"** while 4-h calcium crossed threshold in 3% (§6.1).
   Direct human evidence that a daily dose **fully resolves within 24 h**.
3. **Daily PTH1R agonism is anabolic in humans.** If slow human cells made daily dosing effectively
   continuous, teriparatide daily should be catabolic. It's the approved anabolic regimen.

→ The invariant to match is **exposure windows per week**, not pulses per cell cycle. `ogawa2002`
dosed **5 days/week**. Five against five.

## Why each part

| element | reason |
|---|---|
| **80 µg** | approved dose *and* top of the phase-2 range (20/40/80) — the per-pulse ceiling with human data |
| **5 on / 2 off** | exact match to `ogawa2002`; preserves a 48-h weekly washout as a hedge |
| **evening** | `noonan2004`: ≥90% of tibial elongation during recumbency; also satisfies the label's lie-down instruction |
| **duration** | remaining growth window (label: not evaluated beyond 2 y) |

## The residual gap

400 µg/week = 6.67 µg/kg/wk vs a BSA-scaled rat equivalent of 64.5 → **9.7× short**. Daily 7/7 buys
40% more (6.9× short) at the cost of two extra windows — **not worth it** in a drug whose sign
inverts with schedule. Escalating per-pulse attacks the DLT (hypercalcaemia) directly with no data.

## Monitoring that changes the dose

- **Albumin-corrected serum Ca at 4 h post-dose** — the label's own DLT detection window.
- **Pre-dose Ca** — if it drifts up, exposure isn't clearing and the regimen has moved toward the
  continuous arm. That's a reason to lengthen the interval, not just a safety flag.
- **Bone age q6mo** — not on the label. If it advances bone age faster than height, it's self-defeating.

## What this is not

Not validated. Abaloparatide is **41% homologous to hPTH(1-34)** — the molecule `ogawa2002` used.
Whether the gain is amplification or faster pool consumption is unmeasured. The label directs
avoidance in open epiphyses on osteosarcoma grounds.
