==============================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS, AND HOW NOT TO
==============================================================================
Each cell is CYTL1-positive % / CYTL1-negative %. 'donors up' counts donors where
the gene is detected in at least 1% of CYTL1+ cells AND higher than in CYTL1- cells.

A GENE UP IN 4/4 DONORS IS A CANDIDATE FOR THE UNNAMED NINE. IT IS NOT A MEMBER OF
THEM. The authors' nine come from cluster-level differential expression against GP2
specifically; this contrast is CYTL1+ against everything else, which is a different
and coarser comparison. A gene can be root-enriched here and absent from their list,
or on their list and invisible here because droplet capture missed it.

CYTL1-POSITIVE IS NOT GP1. It is enriched for GP1 and contaminated by GP2 and by
non-chondrocytes. No claim about GP1 specifically may be made from this table.

THE DONORS WERE SELECTED FOR EXCESSIVE GROWTH. Whatever their root cells are doing,
they are doing it in plates that were growing too much, which is the population this
programme is trying to imitate but is not a normal-stature reference.
