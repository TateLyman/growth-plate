THE CONFOUND THIS EXISTS TO CATCH. In the raw contrast THBS1/2/3/4, DCN, BGN, FMOD,
LUM and LTBP1/3 are all up in CYTL1+ cells in 4/4 donors - but so is ACAN, by a
similar ratio. CYTL1+ cells in this dataset simply carry more matrix transcript. A
matricellular gene rising in step with ACAN is evidence of a matrix-rich cell, NOT
of a targeted antagonist screen. Each value below is the gene's CYTL1+/CYTL1-
detection ratio DIVIDED BY ACAN's in the same donor. Near 1.0 means no specific
signal beyond the matrix program.

  ACAN raw ratios (the yardstick): 2.47, 2.19, 1.02, 12.98

reports as GP1-specific; if they are NOT enriched here, the gate is failing to find the
population it is aimed at, and the candidate list below must be read as 'antagonists of
a CYTL1-positive matrix-rich chondrocyte', which is a weaker and different claim.
