THE CNP AXIS IN HUMANS, READ ACROSS ITS DOSE RANGE

[1] POPULATION DOSE - jeong2026, UK Biobank
    245 NPR2 missense variants functionally characterised:
        47 loss-of-function, 34 partial loss, 14 GAIN-OF-FUNCTION
    shape      : near-linear association between activity score and standing height
    fit        : R-squared 0.438, p = 5.8e-10
    additivity : within each functional activity category, polygenic scores combine ADDITIVELY with the effect of the NPR2 variant
    direction  : PTVs and LoF missense enriched in the SHORTEST individuals; GoF variants enriched in the TALLEST
    PheWAS     : NPR2 activity significantly associated ONLY with height and height-associated traits
    the authors' own word for the shape: "a dimmer switch"

    THIS IS THE ANSWER TO THE SATURATION QUESTION AND IT IS THE OPPOSITE
    OF THE SOMATOTROPIC ONE. Round 202 found h_term saturating against
    somatotropic drive - 40 per cent of the available gain in the first
    tripling of pool consumption, 9 per cent in the next 3.3-fold. The
    CNP axis in humans shows NO SUCH CEILING across the characterised
    range, and the gain-of-function end is populated and goes the right
    way. THE TWO AXES SHOULD THEREFORE BE DOSED BY OPPOSITE LOGIC.

[2] PHARMACOLOGICAL DOSE - pooled randomised evidence
    design        : meta-analysis, 11 studies N=542, of which 4 RCTs n=326, low risk of bias
    agv           : +1.36 cm/year (95% CI 1.05 to 1.68), P<.00001
    height        : +1.24 cm (0.47 to 2.01), P=.002; height Z +0.28 (0.20 to 0.37)
    segment_ratio : upper-to-lower UNCHANGED, MD -0.02 (95% CI -0.04 to +0.01), P=0.17, I-squared 0
    age_gradient  : AGV gain LARGER at age 5+ (1.63 cm/yr, 1.34-1.92) than under 5 (0.91 cm/yr, 0.41-1.41), subgroup P=.01
    adult_height  : EXPLICITLY NOT YET KNOWN

    rua2025 single-centre retrospective, 27 children, 15 completing 24 months
        segments : sitting height SDS +0.79 against arm span SDS +0.32, both P<=.01
        ratios   : upper-to-lower segment ratio -0.10 (P<=.01, leg-favouring) BUT sitting-height-to-height ratio UNCHANGED - two proportion measures disagreeing inside one cohort

    THE POOLED RANDOMISED ANSWER IS PROPORTIONATE GROWTH, I-squared 0.
    Against the open-site register that is the FAVOURABLE answer - the
    agent does not pour growth into one segment and starve the others.
    AND THE AGE GRADIENT RUNS THE HELPFUL WAY: the gain is LARGER in the
    older subgroup, which is the opposite of the usual assumption and the
    only age-direction evidence the atlas has on this axis. It still stops
    far short of bone age 16 and adult height is explicitly unknown.

        - macrodactyly of great toes

    miura2014  [NPR2 p.Ala488Pro, GoF]
        height : tall stature

        - slipped capital femoral epiphysis

        - macrodactyly of great toes
        - pseudo-epiphyses of mid and proximal phalanges

    lauffer2022  [NPR3 biallelic LoF]
        height : +3.03, +3.9 and +3.93 SDS; proband 205.1 cm at 14.7 y
        - NORMAL sitting-height-to-height ratio and arm span
        - normal blood pressure, normal aortic diameter in proband

    boudin2018  [NPR3 biallelic LoF, three families]
        height : +2.7, +3.43, +3.9 and +4.76 SDS
        - aortic root dilatation in 2 of 4, PROGRESSIVE in both

    TWO PATTERNS, AND BOTH MATTER FOR THE OPEN-SITE REGISTER.
    (i) DIGITAL OVERGROWTH IS THE MOST CONSISTENT NON-HEIGHT FEATURE.
        Macrodactyly, elongated phalanges, extra and pseudo-epiphyses in
        the hands and feet. THE PHALANGES CONTRIBUTE NOTHING TO STATURE.
        At constitutive activation the axis puts a visible share of its
        output into sites with zero height value.
    (ii) SPINE DEFORMITY, NOT SPINE LENGTH. Scoliosis in three of six
        reports, in one of them in all three siblings with one requiring
        SPINAL FUSION SURGERY - which ENDS spinal growth outright - and
        with lumbar BMD low for the height achieved. A curve converts
        axial length into deviation, so this is a YIELD term and not only
        a safety term.

[4] WHAT THE THREE POINTS SAY TOGETHER
    The shape is DOSE-DEPENDENT and the atlas had been reading one point
    at a time:
      mild, lifelong        -> height only, linear, PheWAS-clean
      pharmacological       -> proportionate segment growth, I-squared 0
      maximal, constitutive -> height PLUS digital overgrowth PLUS spine
                               deformity
    THE POPULATION PheWAS CANNOT EXCLUDE THE CONSTITUTIVE PHENOTYPES and
    should not be quoted as if it did - it samples the mild end of the
    activity range, where by construction nothing extreme happens.
    Equally, the constitutive families are germline and lifelong, so they
    do not bound what a months-long exposure at a late bone age does.
    WHAT IS NOT AVAILABLE AT ANY DOSE: a terminal cell height measurement
    under any CNP-axis agent, in any species. weber2025's +20 per cent is
    NPR3 LOSS, which is the clearance arm and not NPR2 agonism, and NPR3
    is independently reported to be bifunctional.
