# CNP-axis human natural experiments — source files

Both fetched by this atlas on 2026-08-07 through lawful open-access routes. No paywall was bypassed.

- `miura2012.pdf` — Miura K et al., *An overgrowth disorder associated with excessive production of cGMP
  due to a gain-of-function mutation of the natriuretic peptide receptor 2 gene.* PLoS ONE 2012;7(8):e42180.
  PMID 22870295. **CC-BY.** Retrieved from
  `https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0042180&type=printable`

- `lauffer2022.xml` — Lauffer P et al., *Broadening the Spectrum of Loss-of-Function Variants in
  NPR-C-Related Extreme Tall Stature.* J Endocr Soc 2022;6(4):bvac019. PMID 35233476, PMC8879884.
  Open access. Retrieved as full-text XML from the Europe PMC REST API
  (`.../webservices/rest/PMC8879884/fullTextXML`) after the publisher PDF returned 403.

Why they are held: these are the two human genotypes that raise CNP/cGMP signalling in an **intact FGFR3
background**, and both report height alongside **bone age** — the discriminating measurement for whether
the CNP axis is a velocity lever or a yield lever. See
`nodes/L1_growth_plate_architecture/terminal_cell_volume_is_the_undefended_term.yaml`.
