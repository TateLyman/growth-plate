WHAT THIS IS NOT:
  - NOT a self-renewal fraction. Clone size measures total output of a labelled
    founder; it says nothing about how many of those divisions were renewing.
    A clone of 200 is equally consistent with a founder that persisted and one
    that was consumed after seeding a transit-amplifying burst.
  - NOT a rate for the human plate. Mouse tibia, P30-P90, a plate that never closes.
  - n is TINY and UNEQUAL: 5, 2 and 4 animals or sections at the three timepoints,
    and the P60 column has only two values, one of which is five times the other.
  - The doubling figures are DERIVED HERE by this atlas from panel source values,
    not reported by the authors, and assume exponential growth between two
    timepoints, which the P60 point does not obviously support.
