ERDAFITINIB vs TYRA-300 - THE SELECTIVITY, AND WHY IT IS NOT A SAFETY POINT
============================================================================================

[1] THE NUMBERS
    dabogratinib (TYRA-300)
        FGFR3 over FGFR1       63-fold
        FGFR3 over FGFR2       19-fold
        FGFR3 over FGFR4       55-fold

    erdafitinib, futibatinib, pemigatinib and infigratinib all retain LOW IC50 values in FGFR1-, FGFR2- and FGFR4-driven Ba/F3 lines - i.e. no meaningful FGFR3 selectivity at all (dabogratinib2026)

    AND POTENCY ON TARGET IS NOT SACRIFICED - head to head in the UM-UC-14
    xenograft, dabogratinib 18 mg/kg once daily gave SLIGHTLY MORE tumour
    regression than erdafitinib 12.5 mg/kg twice daily.

[2] WHAT EACH RECEPTOR IS DOING IN A GROWING SKELETON

    FGFR3
        located : growth plate chondrocytes - mainly MATURE chondrocytes in human embryo-fetal long bone (delezoide1998)
        meaning : THE GROWTH TARGET. Gain-of-function shortens bone; inhibition lengthens it.

    FGFR1
        located : PERICHONDRIUM and PERIOSTEUM in human fetal long bone (delezoide1998); kidney, where it regulates phosphate and calcium reabsorption (dabogratinib2026)
        meaning : INHIBITING IT WORKS AGAINST THE GOAL. Chondrocyte-specific Fgfr1 deletion gives REDUCED stature by P4 and REDUCED tibial length at P18 DESPITE an INCREASED hypertrophic zone (karolak2015) - the zone-up/length-down dissociation. Renal FGFR1 inhibition drives the hyperphosphataemia seen in >70 per cent of erdafitinib patients.

    FGFR2
        located : perichondrium and periosteum (delezoide1998)
        meaning : structural, not the target

    FGFR4
        located : liver and elsewhere
        meaning : off-target; diarrhoea and other class effects

[3] THE MECHANISTIC ACCOUNT THIS ASSEMBLES, AND IT IS NEW TO THE ATLAS
    FGFR3 sits in the growth plate chondrocytes. FGFR1 and FGFR2 sit in the
    PERICHONDRIUM AND PERIOSTEUM - the fibrous ring that mechanically
    constrains the physis and carries the groove of Ranvier.
    A PAN-FGFR INHIBITOR THEREFORE DOES TWO THINGS AT ONCE: it accelerates
    the plate through FGFR3 AND it inhibits the ring that holds the plate
    together through FGFR1/2. That is precisely the recipe for a physis that
    grows fast and then fails mechanically - epiphysiolysis, slipped capital
    femoral epiphysis, kyphoscoliosis.
    AND karolak2015 SHOWS THE FGFR1 ARM ALSO WORKS AGAINST LENGTH DIRECTLY:
    chondrocyte Fgfr1 deletion gives a BIGGER hypertrophic zone and a SHORTER
    tibia. So in a pan-FGFR inhibitor the FGFR1 component is subtracting from
    the FGFR3 component while adding the structural failure.

[4] THE HUMAN EVIDENCE, WHICH IS WHERE THE SURPRISE IS

    erdafitinib  [erdachild2024]
        effect  : pre-pubescent boy, FGFR1-mutated glioma: 14.3 cm in 9 months = 19.06 cm/year annualised, with PRE-pubertal GH, IGF-I, IGFBP-3 and testosterone
        caveat  : severe kyphoscoliosis, spinal cord compression and cervical myelopathy FORCED DISCONTINUATION; cranial vault (membranous bone) SPARED

    erdafitinib  [erdaseries2025]
        effect  : retrospective series of paediatric CNS-tumour patients: unanticipated growth acceleration INDEPENDENT of sex steroids and of IGF1, with a DISTINCT WIDENING OF THE GROWTH PLATE and enhanced metaphyseal mineralisation
        caveat  : children were heavily pretreated with SEVERE growth impairment before therapy - so an unknown share of this is CATCH-UP GROWTH

    erdafitinib  [nadeaunguyen2026]
        effect  : FDA postmarketing review: FIVE paediatric cases of skeletal growth toxicity, median age 13, median time to onset 137 days
        caveat  : ALL FIVE permanently discontinued; THREE required surgical correction. US label revised to add SCFE and ACCELERATED LINEAR GROWTH under Pediatric Use

    infigratinib  [savarirayan2026infig / propel3_2026]
        effect  : phase 3 RCT, n=114, 2:1, oral 0.25 mg/kg/day: +1.74 cm/year annualised height velocity
        caveat  : DOSED AT A FRACTION OF THE ONCOLOGY DOSE - the achondroplasia dose was chosen for tolerability, not for maximum growth

    TYRA-300  [tyra300_2025]
        effect  : WILD-TYPE mice: naso-anal +7.3 per cent, femur +8.2, tibia +6.4 at 14 mg/kg, dose-dependent, WITH NO BODY-WEIGHT DIFFERENCE; lumbar vertebrae length up; skull and foramen magnum improved; hypertrophic chondrocytes LARGER and the plate MORE ORGANISED
        caveat  : NO HUMAN GROWTH DATA OF ANY KIND. SURF301 is a phase I/II in ADULTS with urothelial carcinoma

[5] THE VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ERDAFITINIB IS THE LARGEST GROWTH EFFECT IN THIS ENTIRE ATLAS. 19.06
    cm/year annualised in a pre-pubertal child is roughly TEN TIMES the
    phase-3 infigratinib effect and TWELVE TIMES vosoritide's. Nothing else
    on record comes close, and it is HUMAN.
    IT IS ALSO SELF-LIMITING, AND ON YIELD GROUNDS RATHER THAN SAFETY ONES.
    Kyphoscoliosis shortens the spine. Spinal fusion ends spinal growth.
    Five of five discontinued permanently and three needed surgery. A drug
    that must be stopped at 137 days does not deliver adult height.

    THE SELECTIVITY ARGUMENT IS THEREFORE A YIELD ARGUMENT: 63-fold sparing
    of FGFR1 removes the receptor whose inhibition BOTH shortens bone AND
    weakens the perichondrial ring, while leaving the FGFR3 arm that produced
    the 19 cm/year intact.

    WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT. If the growth acceleration itself is FGFR1- or
    perichondrium-mediated rather than FGFR3-mediated, an FGFR3-selective
    molecule would be safer AND weaker, and the trade would be a bad one.
    Two facts argue against that reading and neither is decisive: TYRA-300
    grows WILD-TYPE mice by 8.2 per cent at the femur with no body-weight
    change, and the erdafitinib child's CRANIAL VAULT - membranous bone with
    no growth plate - WAS SPARED, which localises the effect to endochondral
    tissue.

    THE DECISIVE UNKNOWN IS DOSE. Infigratinib gives +1.74 cm/year at 0.25
    mg/kg/day, a fraction of its oncology dose, chosen for tolerability.
    Erdafitinib gives 19 cm/year at full oncology dosing. NOBODY HAS DOSED AN
    FGFR3-SELECTIVE MOLECULE AT AN ONCOLOGY-SCALE EXPOSURE IN A GROWING
    SKELETON. That single experiment decides the whole arm.
