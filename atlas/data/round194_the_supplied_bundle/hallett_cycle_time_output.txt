[4] SENSITIVITY TO THE LABELLING INDEX ITSELF
    The s.d. on the 1-week LRC index (1.67 on a mean of 2.04) is
    82% of the mean. Propagating +/-1 s.d. at T_S=6 h, w=3 h:
       mean - 1sd: LI= 0.37%  T_c =   101.4 d
             mean: LI= 2.04%  T_c =    18.4 d
       mean + 1sd: LI= 3.71%  T_c =    10.1 d
    The lower bound on LI is within noise of zero, so the upper
    bound on T_stem from this dataset is UNBOUNDED. This measurement
    constrains T_stem from below only.

[5] COMPARISON WITH THE RAT ANCHOR ALREADY IN THE GRAPH
    Hunziker 1994 T_stem (rat proximal tibia, author-stated):
      hypophysectomised + NaCl : 50 d
      hypox + IGF-I            : 15 d
      hypox + GH               :  8 d
      intact normal            :  6 d
    The mouse LRC sweep overlaps the hypophysectomised end of that
    range and not the intact end. Two readings are possible and this
    dataset cannot separate them:
      (a) mouse resting-zone turnover really is slower than rat, or
      (b) the LRC gate (top 10% H2B-EGFP) selects the slowest tail
          of a heterogeneous resting zone, so the LI understates
          mean resting-zone cycling.
    (b) is the gating definition Hallett states, so (b) cannot be
    excluded. Recorded as a range, not a point, grade E.

[6] WHAT THIS DOES *NOT* ANSWER
    No PTH1R agonist was administered in Hallett 2021. This supplies
    a baseline T_stem for mouse; it does not supply T_stem under
    teriparatide, which remains unmeasured in every species.
