BONE LENGTH ACROSS THE FULL RANGE OF PTHrP-PTH1R SIGNALLING TONE
====================================================================================================

### TONE = ZERO
  Blomstrand chondrodysplasia (human)
      perturbation : PTH1R null
      LENGTH       : lethal, advanced skeletal maturation, severely short limbs
      human, blomstrand_chondrodysplasia
  PTHrP null (mouse)
      perturbation : ligand absent
      LENGTH       : premature hypertrophy, short bones
      mouse, correa2010

### TONE = ZERO in cartilage
  PTH1R chondrocyte-specific knockout (mouse)
      perturbation : PTH1R absent in cartilage
      LENGTH       : shortened growth plate established by E17.5
      mouse, correa2010

### TONE = LOW
  Zfp521 chondrocyte cKO (mouse)
      perturbation : PTHrP EFFECTOR removed downstream
      LENGTH       : endochondral bones smaller; growth plate -30%; resting zone 5+ layers down to 1-2; cell columns and CELLS PER COLUMN both reduced; fewer hypertrophic cells each of INCREASED height; ECM -50%
      mouse, correa2010
  PTHLH haploinsufficiency (human)
      perturbation : ligand gene dose halved
      LENGTH       : brachydactyly type E with short stature
      human, pth1r_gene

### TONE = LOW -> NORMAL
  uraemic rat + intermittent PTH(1-37)
      perturbation : restored from a suppressed state
      LENGTH       : snout-tail length gain 4.78 -> 6.17 cm, p=0.0055; restores to the treated-sham value, does not exceed it
      rat, schmitt2000
  achondroplastic mouse + PTH
      perturbation : added to an FGFR3-suppressed plate
      LENGTH       : humeral and tibial length brought toward wild type; premature cranial synchondrosis fusion partially prevented
      mouse, xie2012

### TONE = OPTIMUM
  WILD TYPE
      perturbation : endogenous
      LENGTH       : reference
      -, -

### TONE = ABOVE OPTIMUM
  wild-type mouse pup + PTH(1-34) 50 ug/kg/d x 9 d
      perturbation : agonist added
      LENGTH       : NO CHANGE in tibia length, three cranial base bone lengths, or two synchondrosis lengths, while BV/TV, Tb.N and BMD rose
      mouse, koh2022
  young male rat + PTH(1-34) 16-80 ug/kg/d x 18 d
      perturbation : agonist added
      LENGTH       : NO CHANGE in femur length while tibial bone parameters rose 10-50%
      rat, fda_forteo_pharmreview_2002
  intact male F344 rat + PTH(1-34) 8-40 ug/kg/d x 1 y
      perturbation : agonist added
      LENGTH       : did not alter linear bone growth in males
      rat, fda_forteo_pharmreview_2002
  F344 rat to 26 months + PTH(1-34) 5-30 ug/kg/d
      perturbation : agonist added
      LENGTH       : femur length 35 mm in ALL EIGHT ARMS at terminal
      rat, fda_forteo_pharmreview_2002
  hypoparathyroid children, PTH(1-34) vs calcitriol, 3 y randomised
      perturbation : agonist added at replacement dose
      LENGTH       : height percentile 47 vs 53, p=0.76, no change over time
      human, winer2010
  10-week-old FEMALE rat + PTH(1-34) 50 ug/kg/d x 15 d
      perturbation : agonist added
      LENGTH       : FEMORAL LENGTH INCREASED - the lone healthy-animal positive; systemic IGF-I fell; body weight not reported in the abstract
      rat, toromanoff1998

### TONE = HIGH
  Col2-PTHrP overexpression (mouse)
      perturbation : ligand forced up in cartilage
      LENGTH       : SHORT-LIMBED DWARFISM and delayed endochondral ossification; by 7 weeks foreshortened and misshapen but histologically near-normal
      mouse, weir1996

### TONE = MAXIMAL
  Jansen metaphyseal chondrodysplasia (human)
      perturbation : PTH1R constitutively active
      LENGTH       : SEVERE SHORT STATURE; hypertrophic exit delayed and then failing to discharge
      human, jansen_metaphyseal_chondrodysplasia

====================================================================================================
[1] THE SHAPE
====================================================================================================
    Both extremes shorten bone. Zero signalling shortens it
    (Blomstrand, PTHrP null, PTH1R cKO, Zfp521 cKO, PTHLH
    haploinsufficiency). Maximal signalling shortens it too
    (Jansen in human, Col2-PTHrP overexpression in mouse). The
    curve has a maximum, and every result in between is consistent
    with WILD TYPE SITTING AT OR NEAR THAT MAXIMUM.

[2] WHAT FOLLOWS, AND IT IS THE WHOLE ANSWER
    An agonist can only help from BELOW the optimum. That is exactly
    the set of states where agonism works:
      uraemia    - PTH-driven growth failure, rescued (schmitt2000)
      achondroplasia - FGFR3 suppressing the plate, rescued (xie2012)
    And it is exactly the set where agonism does nothing:
      wild-type mouse pup, young male rat, adult male rat, rat to
      terminal, and randomised human children.
    A healthy subject is not below the optimum. Adding agonist moves
    along the flat top of the curve, or over it.

[3] THE ONE STATE THAT MIGHT BE BELOW THE OPTIMUM, AND IT IS THE
    CASE IN QUESTION
    kindblom2002 measured both ligands of this loop in human growth
    plate biopsies across Tanner stages and found both fall
    significantly with maturation - |r| 0.816 for Ihh and 0.911 for
    PTHrP. If endogenous tone at Tanner 4-5 has dropped BELOW the
    optimum, a late subject is in the rescuable region and every
    null above was measured in the wrong animal at the wrong age.
    Nobody has measured the RECEPTOR across that range, and
    kindblom2002 excluded the resting zone by design. THIS IS THE
    ONLY ROUTE BY WHICH THIS LINE REMAINS ALIVE.

[4] THE COUNTERWEIGHT TO THAT HOPE
    Falling ligand tone with maturation is also what a plate that is
    SHUTTING DOWN looks like. The pre-hypertrophic population that
    carries the receptor (chu2026, hallett2021) shrinks with the
    plate. Falling tone is equally consistent with a vanishing
    target as with an unoccupied one, and the two predict opposite
    signs. One immunostaining series settles it.

[5] A SEPARATE PROBLEM WITH EVERY POSITIVE IN THIS FILE
    The healthy-animal positives are +2% to +6% on a length
    endpoint. wezeman2003 measured L4 vertebral body height in
    growing rats with a pair-fed design: pair-fed controls were
    7.69 mm against 8.22 mm ad libitum in males and 6.83 against
    7.19 mm in females - CALORIC INTAKE ALONE MOVES VERTEBRAL BODY
    HEIGHT BY 5 TO 6 PER CENT. Every positive in this line is inside
    the range that feeding alone produces, and only two studies in
    the whole line controlled intake.
