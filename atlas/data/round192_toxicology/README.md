# ROUND 192 — the regulatory toxicology package, and the ADH1 deflation

A source never opened: the **published carcinogenicity package**. These are the only multi-dose,
multi-duration studies of PTH1R agonists in animals dosed **from growth onward**.

| study | design |
|---|---|
| `vahle2002` | F344 rats, 2 y daily PTH(1-34), **0/5/30/75 µg/kg**, 60/sex/group, QCT + histomorphometry |
| `vahle2004` | dose (0/5/30) × duration (6/20/24 mo) × **AGE AT INITIATION (2 or 6 mo)** |
| `jolette2017` | **abaloparatide 0/10/25/50 vs hPTH(1-34) 30**, F344, 2 y |
| `vahle2008` | cynomolgus macaques, 18 mo teriparatide + **3 y off** |

## Three answers

**1. The molecules are a wash on osteosarcoma.** `jolette2017`: abaloparatide 25 vs hPTH(1-34) 30 —
"similar exposure multiples to the human therapeutic doses" — gave **similar** incidence, mortality,
age at first occurrence, metastatic potential. No oncological tiebreaker. Round 188's reversal rests on
immunogenicity and still does.

**2. Age at initiation was varied, and the no-effect regimen is the late one.** `vahle2004`: **no
neoplasms when 5 µg/kg was initiated at SIX months** and continued 6–20 months (to 70% of lifespan),
while still raising bone mass substantially. The design crossed 2 vs 6 months. **This subject is
skeletally immature** — the arm the only study to test this put on the wrong side. Stated once,
factually; risk is set aside for candidate selection but this belongs in the complete-stack assessment.

**3. Primates show none of it — but none were growing.** `vahle2008`: mature OVX macaques, 4.5 y total
observation, **no proliferative lesions, no osteosarcoma**. That's why human surveillance is clean. **No
primate has ever been dosed while growing.**

## A fifth instance of the missing endpoint

`vahle2002` ran **2 years of QCT and histomorphometry on rats dosed from 2 months of age at three
doses** — the best dose-response design in the entire literature for this question — and reported bone
mass, architecture, marrow space, tumours. **Not length.**

## The deflation — it costs us the best human positive

Round 190 leaned on `winer2018`: the CaR group attained or exceeded midparental height. `benson2025`
states the natural history: **short stature is an ADH2 (GNA11) feature in ~42% of cases; ADH1 (CASR)
typically manifests hypercalciuria.** Winer's CaR group is **ADH1**.

**A cohort that doesn't characteristically have short stature reaching MPH is the expected outcome
without any drug.** The positive inference is close to worthless.

**But the two inferences come apart and only one dies.** *Because* ADH1 children normally reach MPH, a
decade of PTH(1-34) at up to 1.49 µg/kg/day should have pulled them **below** target if the drug spent
the plate. It didn't. A clean expected-value comparator makes the absence of truncation **more**
informative. **The no-truncation claim upgrades D → C.**

## And nobody is running the experiment

ClinicalTrials.gov v2: teriparatide + growth endpoint + children → **1 hit**, a hypophosphatasia
natural-history registry with no PTH1R intervention. Abaloparatide in children → **0**.
