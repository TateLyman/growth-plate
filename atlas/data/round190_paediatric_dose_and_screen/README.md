# ROUND 190 — the paediatric dose exists, and the field is enumerated

Four user-supplied PDFs. `winer_b1.txt` = Winer 2018 *J Pediatr*; `winer_b2.txt` = Winer 2021 *Bone* +
Winer 2025 *JCEM*; `pnas_si.txt` = liao2026 SI; `fendo.txt` = the palopegteriparatide case.

## 1. The dose was never a scaling problem

`winer2018`: **14 children**, PTH(1-34) **0.75 ± 0.15 µg/kg/day** (range 0.2–1.49), **twice or thrice
daily**, for **6.9 ± 3.1 years (max 10)**. Absolute totals 8–108 µg/day.

**For a 60 kg subject: 45 µg/day at the mean, 89 µg/day at the top — 2.3× and 4.5× the approved adult
teriparatide dose per kg.** Rounds 184 and 187 spent two rounds scaling from rodents by body surface
area. The empirical paediatric dose was available the whole time.

**Mean height velocity was normal for age throughout.** Renal function stable across the 192-patient
companion cohort (`winer2021`).

## 2. The final-height data

- APS-1 patients B, C, D: **8–12 cm below** midparental height
- **All CaSR patients except one attained MPH; two exceeded it by 4 and 6 cm**
- Patient H: 1.4 µg/kg/day BID for years 0.5–6, then 0.54 TID to year 10 → **+4 cm over MPH**

**And the APS-1 shortfall is not PTH.** The same paper records their Addison's treated with oral steroid
at **15 mg/m² — "higher than typically required for physiologic replacement."** That removes the
`winer2025` between-group interaction as evidence about the drug.

**Bearing on the pool question:** a decade at up to 4.5× the adult per-kg dose, spanning the whole
growth period, normal velocity throughout, final heights at or above target. Pool spending produces
early cessation. Held at **D** — replacement dosing, n=9 informative, MPH SD is ~8–9 cm.

## 3. liao2026 SI — access-queue item CLOSED

But wild-type mice on abaloparatide 5 µg/kg/d × 30 d from 1 month (Table S15):

| | Ctrl | +ABL | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| BV/TV % | 10.05 ± 1.40 | 29.04 ± 3.63 | **+189%** |

**5 µg/kg/day is powerfully active, not a threshold** — strengthening round 188. And it's a very large
metaphyseal perturbation in a growing animal, where the primary spongiosa must be resorbed for the plate
to advance. Nobody has asked what that does.

## 4. Three more from the screen

- **`trompet2024`** — the length effect was **more pronounced at 2 and 6 months** than at 1, and runs
  through **Pthrp-creER epSSCs**. An effect that *grows* is not a pool being spent.
- **`hosokawa2015`** — PTH resets the chondrocyte circadian clock **in a circadian-time-dependent
  manner**. Consistent clock time is now a requirement, not a nicety.
- **`toribio2010`** — PTHrP knock-in lacking midregion/NLS/C-terminus: chondrodysplasia, death by day 5,
  "many of these effects are not mediated by the N terminus." The composition objection stands.
