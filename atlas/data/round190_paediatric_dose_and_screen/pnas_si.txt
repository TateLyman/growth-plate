SUPPLEMENTAL Methods 
Immunostaining: For immunohistochemistry, sections were incubated with primary antibodies (Tables 
S2–S3) overnight at 4 °C, followed by a biotin-conjugated secondary antibody, streptavidin–HRP, and DAB 
substrate (Thermo #34065). For immunofluorescence, sections were incubated with primary antibodies 
overnight, then with Alexa Fluor-conjugated secondary antibodies, and nuclei were counterstained with 
DAPI. Negative controls used normal IgG. EdU was injected (100 mg/kg, i.p.) 4 h before tissue collection 
and detected as described (1). 
 
ALP and TRAP staining: For ALP and TRAP staining, frozen sections of mouse bone tissue were selected, 
washed three times with PBS, and then stained using commercially available kits(Vector Blue Substrate 
Kit, Alkaline Phosphatase,SK5300;Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase Dye /TRAP,1-0001-5 ml, JoyTech 
Bio Co.,Ltd) followed by mounting for visualization. 
 
Reference 
1. 
J. Liao et al., Nf2-FAK signaling axis is critical for cranial bone ossification and regeneration. Nat Commun 
16, 2478 (2025).

Fig. S2 Nf2 deficiency in Col2a1⁺ lineages perturbs skeletal mineralization and growth. 
A–C: H&E staining of embryonic tibias from control (NC) and Nf2-cKO mice at E14.5 (A, n = 3), E16.5 (B, 
n = 6), and E18.5 (C, n = 6). 
D: Quantification of tibial and growth plate (GP) lengths at E14.5. Total tibial length was unchanged, while 
GP length was slightly increased, likely reflecting delayed hypertrophy and primary ossification center (POC) 
formation. 
E: Quantification of zone lengths at E16.5. GP length was modestly shortened in Nf2 mutants, with resting 
zone (RZ) and proliferative zone (PZ) lengths unaffected, but hypertrophic zone (HZ) length significantly 
reduced in Nf2 mutants. 
F: Proportional zone lengths at E16.5. The relative contributions of RZ and PZ to total GP length were 
unchanged, whereas the HZ fraction was significantly decreased in Nf2 mutants. 
G: Quantification of zone lengths at E18.5. GP length remained slightly shorter in Nf2 mutants; RZ and PZ 
lengths were comparable, but HZ length was markedly reduced in Nf2 mutants. 
H: Proportional zone lengths at E18.5. The relative length of RZ was unchanged, PZ proportion was 
modestly increased, and HZ proportion was significantly reduced in Nf2 mutants. 
Data were expressed as means ± SD and each dot represent an individual biological replicate. P values 
were calculated by unpaired Student’s t-test with two-tailed analysis without adjustments.   
Scale bar: A = 100 μm; B-C = 200 μm. * p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001, **** p < 0.0001.

Fig. S3 Impaired chondrocyte proliferation in the proliferative zone of Nf2 mutant mice. 
A: Representative images of EdU staining in the tibial growth plates of control and Nf2-cKO mice at E14.5,

chondrogenic cells compared with the controls (shCtrl).

growth plate and a slight, non-significant increase in tdTomato⁺ cells within trabecular bone of Nf2 mutants

Legends for Movies S1 to S2 
Movie S1. Dynamic PTH1R-copGFP internalization in control (shCtrl) HEK293T cells. 
Live-cell imaging of HEK293T cells stably expressing control shRNA (shCtrl) and transiently transfected 
with PTH1R-copGFP. After the addition of 20 nM ABL, images were acquired every 20 seconds for 10 
minutes using a HIS-SIM super-resolution microscope. The movie illustrates robust, ligand-induced 
internalization of PTH1R-copGFP from the plasma membrane.  
 
Movie S2. Impaired PTH1R-copGFP internalization in Nf2-deficient (shNf2) HEK293T cells.  
Live-cell imaging of HEK293T cells stably expressing Nf2-targeting shRNA (shNf2) and transiently 
transfected with PTH1R-copGFP. Following stimulation with 20 nM ABL, time-lapse images were captured 
every 20 seconds for 10 minutes. The movie demonstrates markedly attenuated receptor trafficking and 
reduces endocytic uptake compared to control cells.
