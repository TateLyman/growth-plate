## THE FINDING

| | anti-drug Ab | **neutralising** | cross-neutralises endogenous ligand |
|---|---|---|---|
| **abaloparatide** (18 mo) | 41% (318/773) | **26%** (204/773) | **3/297 neutralise PTHrP** |
| **teriparatide** | 3% (15/541) | none reported | 1/297 anti-PTH, non-neutralising |

Abaloparatide antibodies persist: 127 still positive at 1 y after stopping, 55 at 2 y, 6 at 3 y.

**The PTHrP homology that was the argument *for* abaloparatide is what creates the hazard.**
Neutralising endogenous PTHrP is the PTHLH loss-of-function direction — and human PTHLH nonsense
variants cause brachydactyly type E **with short stature** (`huang2026`).

Why it weighs differently than in osteoporosis: the window is **non-repeatable**, the endpoint is more
sensitive than BMD, and the tail is **inverted** rather than null.
