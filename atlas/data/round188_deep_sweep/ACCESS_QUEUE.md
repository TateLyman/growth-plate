# STILL BLOCKED — what would move the verdict further

1. **`winer2025`** — Winer KK et al., *JCEM* 2025, PMID **39883563**, "Bone Health and Linear Growth in
   Children With Familial Hypoparathyroidism Treated With Human Parathyroid Hormone 1-34."
   PMC12448599 returns 404 on Europe PMC (embargoed). **The single most valuable unread paper** — it is
   the only human dataset with linear growth as an endpoint under a PTH1R agonist. Needed: the actual
   HAZ trajectories and slopes, the PTH doses used (µg/kg/day), and whether growth velocity was
   reported separately from HAZ.

2. **`liao2026` supplementary** — Liao J et al., *PNAS* 2026, PMC13273255. The main text is in hand.
   **SI Appendix Fig. S7** covers the abaloparatide arm (5 µg/kg/day × 30 d from 1 month of age in
   wild-type and Nf2 mutant mice). Needed: whether femur/tibia LENGTH was measured in the ABL-treated
   wild-type controls. If it was, it is the wild-type growing-animal abaloparatide result this whole
   programme needs. Worth emailing the corresponding author.

3. **`silvestrini2007`** — PMID 17476578. The rat study where PTH alone did not affect cartilage growth
   plate width. Needed: dose, age, duration — to know whether it is a real non-replication of
   `ogawa2002` or a different experiment.

4. **Any PTH1R agonist dose-response with a growth endpoint** — searched and appears not to exist in any
   species. If a negative result is sitting in a thesis or a conference abstract it would matter.
