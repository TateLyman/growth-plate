[1] CARTILAGE PENETRATION - four grounds, and the fourth shows the question was mis-posed
================================================================================================

    GROUND ONE - PHYSICOCHEMISTRY, computed from PubChem.

        agent            MW      XLogP   TPSA    HBD   HBA
        erdafitinib      446.5   3.2      77.3    1     7
        dabogratinib     559.5   3.6     113      1     8
        infigratinib     560.5   4.7      95.1    2     8
        VOSORITIDE      4103.0 -20.6    1820     61    64

    Both tyrosine kinase inhibitors are ordinary Lipinski-compliant small molecules with almost
    identical lipophilicity. Neither is remarkable in either direction. THE ROW THAT MATTERS IS THE
    LAST ONE: vosoritide is a 39-amino-acid peptide, NINE TIMES THE MASS AND SIXTEEN TIMES THE POLAR
    SURFACE AREA, and it is an APPROVED DRUG THAT RAISES GROWTH VELOCITY. If a 4.1 kDa hydrophilic
    peptide reaches the physis well enough to work, the size-and-polarity bar for physeal access
    sits far above anything either TKI has to clear.

    GROUND TWO - THE FREE DRUG HYPOTHESIS AND THE VOLUME OF DISTRIBUTION. Cartilage interstitium is
    a low-protein compartment: the matrix sterically and electrostatically excludes albumin and,
    more so, the 44 kDa alpha-1-acid glycoprotein that carries 99.7 per cent of erdafitinib. For a
    passively distributed small molecule at steady state, UNBOUND concentration equilibrates across
    compartments, so cartilage free concentration should approximate plasma free concentration. The
    label's PK supports real extravascular distribution rather than plasma confinement:
    ERDAFITINIB'S APPARENT VOLUME OF DISTRIBUTION IS 29 L, roughly ten times plasma volume, in a
    drug that is 99.7 per cent bound.

    THE ONE LIABILITY ON THIS GROUND, AND IT IS ASYMMETRIC. The label states ERDAFITINIB IS A
    SUBSTRATE AND INHIBITOR OF P-GLYCOPROTEIN. Efflux would matter at the chondrocyte membrane,
    because FGFR3's kinase domain is intracellular and the drug has to get into the cytosol.
    DABOGRATINIB'S TRANSPORTER STATUS IS NOT KNOWN TO THIS ATLAS. So on the only axis where the two
    drugs might differ, the unknown is on the selective agent's side and the known liability is on
    erdafitinib's.

    GROUND THREE - FUNCTIONAL PROOF, WHICH BOTH DRUGS HAVE. Erdafitinib produced 19.6 cm/year in a
    child: that is target engagement in a human growth plate, whatever the tissue concentration was.
    TYRA-300 raised nasoanal and tail length in WILD-TYPE C57BL/6J mice and lengthened the growth
    plate in two FGFR3-driven models. NEITHER DRUG HAS A PENETRATION PROBLEM THAT PREVENTS IT
    WORKING, because both have been observed to work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    GROUND FOUR - THE QUANTITATIVE ONE, AND IT BOUNDS THE RATIO.

    Erdafitinib at 3 mg gives a free steady-state average of 1.72 times its Ba/F3 cellular FGFR3
    IC50. At that exposure a child grew 19.6 cm/year - and round 234 established that this is a
    SATURATED response, because tripling the dose to 7 mg (4.01x) gave 19.1.

    Now suppose cartilage free concentration were much lower than plasma free concentration. Write
    the ratio as R = [cartilage free] / [plasma free]. The receptor then sees 1.72 x R times its
    IC50, and simple mass-action occupancy is:

        R = 1.0    target sees  1.72x IC50  ->   63.2% receptor occupancy
        R = 0.5    target sees  0.86x IC50  ->   46.2% receptor occupancy
        R = 0.3    target sees  0.52x IC50  ->   34.0% receptor occupancy
        R = 0.1    target sees  0.17x IC50  ->   14.7% receptor occupancy
        R = 0.03   target sees  0.05x IC50  ->    4.9% receptor occupancy

    A SATURATED GROWTH RESPONSE CANNOT BE PRODUCED AT 15 PER CENT OCCUPANCY. For the response to be
    already maximal at 3 mg, R must be of order 1 - certainly not 0.1. THE CARTILAGE-TO-PLASMA FREE
    RATIO FOR ERDAFITINIB IS THEREFORE OF ORDER UNITY, WHICH IS THE FIRST QUANTITATIVE STATEMENT
    ABOUT GROWTH-PLATE DRUG EXPOSURE THIS ATLAS HAS. It is a bound derived from the pharmacodynamics,
    not a measurement, and it inherits every assumption in the free-concentration table.

    AND THE FIELD'S OWN PRECEDENT SAYS THE ORIGINAL QUESTION WAS THE WRONG ONE. The Voxzogo EPAR
    reports 124-iodine vosoritide biodistribution in rats: radioactivity was PREDOMINANTLY IN THE
    STOMACH, with liver and kidney, much less at the injection site, and VIRTUALLY NONE IN BRAIN.
    Cartilage and bone are not reported as sites of accumulation. YET THE SAME DOSSIER RECORDS
    RAISED cGMP IN THE DISTAL FEMUR after dosing, and the drug is approved on growth velocity.

    SO A DRUG CAN SHOW NO CARTILAGE ACCUMULATION ON BIODISTRIBUTION AND STILL ENGAGE THE PHYSIS.
    Tissue concentration is the wrong endpoint; the pharmacodynamic readout in the plate is the
    right one. "Measure cartilage penetration" was a mis-specified ask, and the correct version is
    "measure a PD marker in the physis" - which for the FGFR arm does not exist, because round 233
    established there is no FGFR3 pharmacodynamic marker in clinical use anywhere.

[2] FGFR1'S SIGN - I TREATED IT AS PURE COST AND NEVER ASKED WHAT IT DOES IN THE PLATE
================================================================================================

    Rounds 231 to 234 built a case that FGFR1 coverage buys hyperphosphataemia, slipped epiphyses
    and discontinuation, and buys no height. EVERY ONE OF THOSE ROUNDS TREATED FGFR1 INHIBITION AS
    A COST WITH NO BENEFIT TERM. That is an error of omission, and the atlas already held four
    pieces that make the sign two-sided.

    AGAINST FGFR1 CONTRIBUTING ANYTHING LOCALLY:

      delezoide1998, human embryo-fetal in situ hybridisation - FGFR3 is expressed mainly in mature
      growth plate chondrocytes while FGFR1 AND FGFR2 ARE RESTRICTED TO PERICHONDRIUM AND
      PERIOSTEUM. On this map the human growth plate is an FGFR3-DOMINANT tissue and FGFR1
      inhibition has no chondrocyte target to act on.

      The human genetics of round 232 - FGFR1 gain of function gives profound short stature with
      rhizomelia, and the antimorphic C-terminal frameshift gives adult height -1.7 SDS. NO
      DIRECTION OF FGFR1 PERTURBATION MAKES A HUMAN TALLER.

    FOR FGFR1 CONTRIBUTING SOMETHING, AND THIS IS THE HALF I NEVER WROTE:

      THIS ATLAS'S OWN RE-ANALYSIS OF GSE288028 - FGFR1 detected in 23.2 to 43.3 PER CENT of human
      growth plate cells, in FOUR OF FOUR DONORS. That is POSTNATAL human growth plate and it
      CONTRADICTS delezoide1998's fetal map. One of the two is wrong for the tissue that matters,
      and the postnatal one is the tissue that matters.

      jacob2006 - deletion of Fgfr1 in the osteo-chondrogenic lineage DELAYS HYPERTROPHIC
      CHONDROCYTE MATURATION. If FGFR1 restrains terminal differentiation, then blocking it acts on
      the HYPERTROPHIC compartment, which is a DIFFERENT TERM OF THE HUNZIKER IDENTITY from FGFR3's
      proliferative-zone action. Round 217's criterion is that agents acting on different terms
      MULTIPLY and agents on the same term do not. ON THAT READING PAN-FGFR COVERAGE IS TWO TERMS
      AND FGFR3-SELECTIVE COVERAGE IS ONE.

      ChEMBL, held by this atlas - ERDAFITINIB INHIBITS FGFR1 AT 1.20 nmol/L AND FGFR3 AT 3.00. IT
      IS FGFR1-PREFERRING. At every dose it engages FGFR1 MORE than FGFR3, so whatever FGFR1
      inhibition does in a growth plate, erdafitinib has been doing more of it than of the thing
      the atlas credited for the growth.

      fgf19cart2025 - FGF19 signalling through FGFR4 IMPAIRS chondrocyte maturation and DECREASES
      growth plate thickness, so FGFR4 INHIBITION SHOULD BE PRO-GROWTH. Another benefit term on the
      pan side that rounds 231 to 234 scored as zero.

    THE HONEST VERDICT, AND IT DOES NOT FLIP THE RECOMMENDATION BUT IT WEAKENS IT.

      THE SIGN OF FGFR1 IN THE HUMAN GROWTH PLATE IS UNRESOLVED, and I asserted it was zero. The
      two human expression datasets disagree; the functional evidence is a germline mouse lineage
      deletion that CORR-046 forbids converting into centimetres; and the human genetics runs the
      other way but is developmental.

      WHAT SURVIVES INTACT is the empirical observation that THE LARGEST HUMAN GROWTH RESPONSE ON
      RECORD CAME FROM DEBIO1347, WHICH SPARES FGFR4 - so FGFR4 coverage is empirically dispensable
      whatever fgf19cart2025 predicts. Nothing equivalent exonerates dropping FGFR1, because no
      FGFR1-sparing agent has ever been given to a growing human.

      SO THE FGFR3-SELECTIVITY RECOMMENDATION NOW CARRIES AN EXPLICIT UNPRICED RISK: if jacob2006's
      hypertrophic effect is real in a postnatal human plate, an FGFR3-selective agent gives up a
      second Hunziker term to buy the phosphate saving.

[3] THE DOSE SEPARATION WAS ALREADY IN THE BIBLIOGRAPHY - and it moves the recommendation
================================================================================================

    Round 233's ask list called the PROPEL 2 dose-cohort table "the single largest possible change
    to the FGFR arm", because it would show whether growth and the phosphate arm separate by DOSE
    inside one FGFR1-covering molecule. THE ATLAS ALREADY HELD THE ANSWER, in the recorded finding
    of `propel3_2026`:

        randomised placebo-controlled phase 3 of ORAL INFIGRATINIB in achondroplasia, n = 74 v 39
        annualised height velocity 4.28 -> 5.75 cm/year against placebo 4.57 -> 3.95
        LS mean difference +1.74 cm/year, 95% CI 1.31 to 2.17, P < 0.0001
        NO ACCELERATED PROGRESSION OF BONE AGE, no negative change in bone mineral density
        NO EVIDENCE OF FGFR1 OR FGFR2 INHIBITION at 0.25 mg/kg/day

    AN FGFR1-COVERING MOLECULE GREW CHILDREN, IN A RANDOMISED TRIAL, AT A DOSE WITH NO CLINICAL
    EVIDENCE OF FGFR1 ENGAGEMENT. If that holds, the lever is THE DOSE, NOT THE MOLECULE, and the
    whole erdafitinib-versus-dabogratinib argument is a second-order question.

    THREE CAVEATS AND THEY ARE NOT SMALL.

      THE FGFR1 CLAIM RESTS ON ABSENCE OF OCULAR ADVERSE EVENTS, not on serum phosphate - and
      phosphate is the FGFR1 marker rounds 231 to 233 established. The specific measurement that
      would settle it is exactly the one still being asked for.

      IT IS ACHONDROPLASIA. Mutant, hyperactive FGFR3, and +1.74 cm/year is RESTORATION toward
      normal, not ELEVATION above it - which is CORR-203, and CORR-203 is why this cannot be read
      across to a wild-type plate. The dose that normalises a hyperactive receptor may be far below
      the dose that pushes a normal one.

      AND NOBODY HAS TESTED BELOW 1.72x IN A NORMAL PLATE. Round 234 showed the human response is
      flat from 1.72x to 4.01x of the cellular IC50. THE BOTTOM OF THAT CURVE IS UNMAPPED. The
      saturation point could sit far below 1.72x, in which case a much lower erdafitinib dose gives
      the same growth with proportionally less FGFR1 engagement - and the molecule choice stops
      mattering.

    THAT IS NOW THE HIGHEST-VALUE UNANSWERED QUESTION ON THE FGFR ARM, and it replaces the potency
    comparison: WHAT IS THE LOWEST FGFR3 COVERAGE THAT STILL SATURATES GROWTH IN A NORMAL PLATE?

[4] THE STACK - and a zone-level finding that puts the CNP arm at risk
================================================================================================

    THE FINDING. Two independent dossiers report WHICH ZONE each arm expands.

      VOSORITIDE, from the Voxzogo EPAR's growth plate histomorphometry in the achondroplasia
      mouse: a clear dose-dependent increase in the ZONE OF MULTIPLICATION, 64.7 -> 85.7 micrometres
      from vehicle to high dose, a rise of 32 per cent - and in the ZONE OF RESERVE CARTILAGE AND
      THE ZONE OF HYPERTROPHY, NO OR ONLY SMALL CHANGES.

      AN FGFR INHIBITOR, from kot2026: growth plate lengthened in both sexes, SPECIFICALLY IN THE
      PROLIFERATIVE ZONE, with reduced phospho-ERK1/2 and SOX9 in lower hypertrophic chondrocytes.

    BOTH ARMS EXPAND THE PROLIFERATIVE ZONE AND NEITHER MOVES THE HYPERTROPHIC ZONE. And the EPAR
    states the mechanism in its own figure legend: vosoritide INHIBITS FGFR3 DOWNSTREAM SIGNALLING
    AT THE LEVEL OF RAF1.

    SO THE TWO ARMS CONVERGE ON THE SAME NODE AND THE SAME ZONE. Round 217's criterion - different
    terms multiply, the same term does not - says these are at risk of COLLAPSING rather than
    multiplying. AND ROUND 234 SHARPENS THE RISK: if FGFR3 output is already saturated at 1.72x the
    cellular IC50, the RAF1-directed contribution of CNP on top of a saturating FGFR3 inhibitor is
    being added to a node that is already maximally suppressed from that direction.

    THREE THINGS RESCUE THE CNP ARM PARTIALLY, AND THEY SHOULD BE STATED BEFORE ANYONE ACTS ON THIS.

      CNP INHIBITS RAF1 REGARDLESS OF WHAT IS UPSTREAM. FGFR3 blockade removes FGFR3-derived MAPK
      tone; it does not remove tone from FGFR1, IGF1R or the other receptors feeding the same node.
      CNP still suppresses that residue, so the arms are PARTIALLY additive rather than identical.

      NPR2 GAIN OF FUNCTION MAKES HUMANS TALL. That is an ELEVATION phenotype above normal stature,
      which is precisely what the FGFR3 arm's human evidence lacks - every FGFR3 human datum is
      either oncology or achondroplasia restoration. On CORR-203's own distinction the CNP axis has
      something the FGFR axis does not.

      AND THE ZONE DATA ARE MOUSE, one of them in a dysplasia model where the baseline is disordered.

    WHAT THE REST OF THE STACK LOOKS LIKE UNDER ROUND 234's SATURATION RESULT.

      GROWTH HORMONE - the only human FGFR-plus-GH combination on record is erdaseries2025 patient
      1, on growth hormone at a stable dose for SEVEN YEARS before erdafitinib, who then grew 19.6
      cm/year. GH could not have caused a spurt that began with the drug, but the combination was
      tolerated and the velocity was the highest recorded. That is one patient and it is the only
      evidence there is.

      ANASTROZOLE - under saturation, DURATION is the only lever left, and the two things that end
      exposure are discontinuation and FUSION. Anastrozole acts on the second. THAT PROMOTES THE
      AROMATASE ARM FROM SUPPLEMENTARY TO LOAD-BEARING: it is the only agent in the stack that acts
      on the variable rounds 228, 229 and 234 all converge on. It also interacts with the one mouse
      finding nobody has followed up - kot2026's elevated serum testosterone in treated males.

      THE COMPOUNDING FAILURE HAZARD - the stack now contains at least three agents that each
      independently widen or weaken the physis: the FGFR arm through rapid-growth-associated
      widening, the CNP arm through the same proliferative-zone expansion, and growth hormone,
      which is itself an established SCFE risk factor. NOBODY HAS EVER GIVEN THESE TOGETHER, and
      the failure mode they share is mechanical.
