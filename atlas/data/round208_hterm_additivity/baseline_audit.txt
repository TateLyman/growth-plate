[1] THE TWO POPULATIONS ARE NOT THE SAME MEASUREMENT
    RESTORATION, largest ratio : 1.53x  (hypophysectomised -> intact)
    ELEVATION,  largest ratio  : 1.25x  (trompet2024, SAG bead vs contralateral DMSO, tibia, 1 month)

    ROUND 205 QUOTED hunziker1994's 1.36x ALONGSIDE weber2025's 1.20x AS IF
    THEY WERE THE SAME KIND OF NUMBER. They are not. 1.36x is what growth
    hormone does to a HYPOPHYSECTOMISED rat, and it does not even reach the
    intact animal's 29.8 micrometres. It is a deficiency-correction number.

[2] WHAT THAT DOES TO THE STACK, AND IT CUTS BOTH WAYS
    AGAINST GH: the subject of this programme is NOT growth-hormone
    deficient. In a GH-replete animal h_term is ALREADY at the top of the
    restoration range - hunziker1994's intact controls sit at 29.8 against
    26.5 on GH-treated hypophysectomised. THERE IS NO EVIDENCE ANYWHERE THAT
    GH RAISES h_term ABOVE A NORMAL BASELINE, and the number the atlas has
    been carrying for it cannot support that claim.

    FOR THE OTHER AGENTS: the elevation numbers are measured against normal
    controls and they are real. Two independent agents - NPR3 loss and a
    hedgehog agonist - both give about 20 per cent ABOVE a normal baseline,
    in different species and different bones.

[3] SO THE HEADROOM ABOVE NORMAL IS ABOUT 21 PER CENT, NOT ZERO
    and it is NOT the same as the 40 per cent round 205 implied. Restated:
      - restoring a deficient plate to normal      up to 1.53x
      - pushing a NORMAL plate above its baseline   about 1.21x on present evidence
    The second number is the one that applies here, and it rests on two
    studies in two species with no combination arm between them.

[4] THE ADDITIVITY QUESTION, RESTATED CORRECTLY
    The question is NOT 'do GH and a CNP agent add on h_term'. On the
    evidence GH has no demonstrated h_term effect above normal at all, so
    there is nothing for it to add TO. The real question is whether the two
    agents that DO elevate above normal - the hedgehog arm and the
    natriuretic arm - share a ceiling. THEY HAVE NEVER BEEN GIVEN TOGETHER.
    And round 205 showed the hedgehog elevation does not survive to two
    months, so even the single-agent effect may not be bankable.
