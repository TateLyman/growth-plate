[1] THE ONE ROW THAT DECIDES THE TERM, AND IT GOES THE WRONG WAY
    PROLIFERATING CELLS PER COLUMN: 13 -> 11  (0.85x), N.S.
    At BOTH doses it is LOWER than vehicle. Vosoritide does not buy length
    by adding divisions to a column. Under the round-198 reserve model that
    is good news in isolation - divisions are the exhaustible term - but it
    forces the growth into one of the two remaining terms.

[2] AND THE CELL-SIZE ROW IS AN AREA, NOT A HEIGHT
    The column is printed as "Hypertrophic cell volume" and the UNIT IS
    um2. A volume is not measured in um2. This is a PROJECTED AREA, which
    CORR-190 forbids reading as a length, and which cooper2013 showed is
    the wrong quantity anyway - jerboa hypertrophic cells are 2.9x the
    VOLUME of mouse but only 1.58x the HEIGHT, because volume includes
    radial expansion that adds nothing to the axis of elongation.
    area 232 -> 286 um2 = 1.23x, NOT SIGNIFICANT
    IF the cell scaled isotropically, height would go as the square root:
        implied height ratio = sqrt(1.233) = 1.110x
    THAT IS AN ASSUMPTION, NOT A MEASUREMENT, and it is the weakest link in
    everything below. Hypertrophic cells do NOT enlarge isotropically.

[3] CLOSING THE IDENTITY, AND WHAT IS LEFT OVER
    growth rate            1.54x   (P < 0.05, the only significant term here)
    cells per column       0.85x   (N.S.)
    implied cell height    1.11x   (N.S., and derived from an area)
    RESIDUAL, i.e. everything not explained by those two: 1.64x

    In the Hunziker identity growth rate = amplification x POOL CONSUMPTION
    x terminal cell height, that residual sits on POOL CONSUMPTION - the
    rate at which resting-zone cells are recruited and spent.
    SO THE PRIMATE DATA READS: vosoritide raises growth 154% of baseline
    with FEWER cells per column and an unproven 1.11x on cell height,
    leaving roughly 1.6x to be carried by SPENDING THE RESERVE FASTER.
    THAT IS THE UNFAVOURABLE ASSIGNMENT. It puts vosoritide in the same
    category as growth hormone rather than in the free-axis category the
    stack has been assuming.

[4] WHY THIS IS A DIRECTION AND NOT A VERDICT
    (1) n = 4 PER GROUP, and the authors themselves write that the study 'was not powered for significance'.
    (2) BOTH decomposition terms - cells per column and cell area - are NON-SIGNIFICANT. Only the growth rate and the zone thicknesses clear P < 0.05.
    (3) The cell measure is a PROJECTED AREA (um2) and the conversion to height is an isotropy assumption this atlas has already shown to be false (cooper2013).
    (4) Cells per column was counted in the PROLIFERATING zone. That is not the amplification term of the identity, which is residence time in the proliferative compartment - it is a standing count, and a standing count can fall while flux rises.
    (5) No resting-zone cycle time was measured, so pool consumption is INFERRED as a residual rather than measured. A residual absorbs every error in the other terms.
    (6) 6 months in a growing monkey, with a growth plate that does not fuse on the same schedule as a human at bone age 16.

[5] WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT, AND IT IS CHEAP
    The sections exist. wendt2015 embedded left tibias in methyl
    methacrylate and cut five 7-um sections per animal, and ran calcein and
    oxytetracycline double labelling. RE-MEASURING TERMINAL HYPERTROPHIC
    CELL HEIGHT AXIALLY ON THOSE SAME SECTIONS converts a projected area
    into the term the decomposition needs, with no new animals. Adding a
    resting-zone cycle time would close the identity outright.
