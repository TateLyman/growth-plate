PART 1  THE EXCHANGE RATE - WHY h_term IS THE HIGH-LEVERAGE AXIS
========================================================================================

    one doubling of a flat proliferative chondrocyte adds 8-9 um to the axis
    that same cell then adds  40-50 um as it goes through hypertrophy
    RATIO                     4.4x to 6.2x

    A DIVISION IS THE CHEAP PART OF THE TRANSACTION AND THE EXPENSIVE
    PART OF THE BUDGET. It costs one unit of an exhaustible reserve and
    returns about a fifth of the length that the hypertrophy of the same
    cell returns for free. Every per-cent added to h_term is worth roughly
    five per cent added to division count, AND IT IS NOT DEDUCTED FROM
    ANYTHING.

PART 2  CAN h_term BE RAISED? YES - AND THIS IS THE FIRST ONE
========================================================================================

    weber2025, Npr3-/- mouse tail vertebrae against wild-type siblings:
        maximum hypertrophic cell height in the axis of elongation   +20%
        hypertrophic ZONE height at TV6                               ~2.0x
        proliferative zone height          significant in only 1 of 4 cartilages

    THE MEASUREMENT IS THE RIGHT ONE. CORR-189 forbids reading a zone
    height as a cell-height claim; this paper measures the cell directly,
    axially, and reports it SEPARATELY from the zone. It is the first
    intervention in this atlas to RAISE terminal cell height rather than
    lower it, which contradicts the standing text of
    g_l1_raise_terminal_cell_volume.

    BUT THE DECOMPOSITION SAYS h_term IS THE MINORITY OF THE EFFECT:
        zone 2.00x  /  cell height 1.20x  =  1.67x MORE CELLS PER COLUMN
    So about two thirds of the hypertrophic zone expansion is extra CELLS
    and about one third is bigger cells. NPR3 loss is mostly a
    cells-per-column agent with a real but minority h_term component.

    AND WHERE THE EXTRA CELLS COME FROM IS UNRESOLVED, WHICH IS THE
    WHOLE QUESTION UNDER A FIXED RESERVE. The proliferative zone is not
    significantly taller in three of four cartilages, and THE Npr3 ARM OF
    THIS PAPER CARRIES NO CALCEIN AND NO EdU - the flux measurements exist
    only in the mouse-versus-jerboa comparison. Extra hypertrophic cells
    can come from extra divisions (which SPENDS reserve) or from delayed
    clearance at the chondro-osseous junction (which does NOT). Npr3 mice
    are independently reported to have DELAYED ENDOCHONDRAL OSSIFICATION,
    which points at the second, and nobody has measured it.

PART 3  hunziker1994 AS A DOSE-RESPONSE - AND THE RESULT IS UNCOMFORTABLE
========================================================================================

    group      GR um/d  h_term  D /col/d      A |  h_term x    D x    A x
    saline          31    19.5       1.0  1.590 |      1.00   1.00   1.00
    IGF-I           92    27.3       3.0  1.123 |      1.40   3.00   0.71
    GH             163    26.5       6.0  1.025 |      1.36   6.00   0.64
    normal         284    29.8      10.0  0.953 |      1.53  10.00   0.60

    A = amplification, computed as GR / (D x h_term). The identity closes
    with no residual because all four quantities are from the same animals.

[3a] h_term SATURATES. POOL CONSUMPTION DOES NOT.
    step                    h_term gain    D gain   h_term per unit D
    saline -> IGF-I               40.0%     3.00x               0.200
    IGF-I -> GH                   -2.9%     2.00x              -0.029
    GH -> normal                  12.5%     1.67x               0.187

    THE FIRST STEP BUYS 40 PER CENT OF THE h_term FOR A TRIPLING OF POOL
    CONSUMPTION. EVERY STEP AFTER THAT BUYS ALMOST NOTHING AND KEEPS
    SPENDING. From IGF-I to fully normal, pool consumption rises another
    3.3-fold to buy a further 9 per cent of terminal cell height.

[3b] THE STACK CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY
    IGF-I : h_term 27.3 um at pool consumption 3/col/day
    GH    : h_term 26.5 um at pool consumption 6/col/day
    GH SPENDS THE POOL 2x FASTER AND DELIVERS -2.9% h_term.
    Under adult height = reserve x h_term, that is the wrong side of the
    curve. THE INFERENCE IS NOT 'USE IGF-I INSTEAD OF GH' - see the
    caveats below - IT IS 'THE SOMATOTROPIC DOSE THAT MAXIMISES ADULT
    HEIGHT IS THE LOWEST ONE THAT SATURATES h_term, NOT THE HIGHEST
    TOLERATED ONE', which is the opposite of how the axis is dosed.

[3c] WHAT WOULD KILL THIS READING, AND IT IS NOT EXCLUDED
    (i) THE DOSES WERE NOT MATCHED FOR EQUIPOTENCY. If GH and IGF-I lie on
        ONE dose-response curve, then 'IGF-I is more efficient' collapses
        into 'IGF-I was given at a lower dose', and the only surviving
        claim is the saturation shape - which is still the actionable one.
        The four points are non-monotonic in h_term against D (27.3 at
        D = 3 against 26.5 at D = 6), which is weak evidence GH sits BELOW
        the IGF-I curve, but 3 per cent at n = 6 is noise and is not
        claimed here.
    (ii) HYPOPHYSECTOMISED RAT. The background is GH-deficient, so GH is
        restoring an axis while IGF-I is bypassing it. Neither maps
        cleanly onto a GH-replete human adolescent.
    (iii) THE RAT DOES NOT FUSE, so no adult-height endpoint exists here.
    (iv) n = 6 per group, one study, 1994, never replicated with this
        readout set.

PART 4  DOES THE STACK CONTAIN THREE AGENTS FIGHTING OVER ONE TERM?
========================================================================================

    cooper2013 assigns h_term to IGF-1 directly: Igf1-deficient mice have
    THE SAME NUMBER of hypertrophic chondrocytes, each 30 per cent shorter
    in the axis of elongation, and removing IGF1 ABOLISHES the between-bone
    difference in cell height. hunziker1994 gives GH 1.36x h_term.
    weber2025 gives the CNP/NPR3 axis 1.20x h_term.

    SO GH/IGF-1 AND THE CNP AXIS MAY BE ACTING ON THE SAME TERM, AND THE
    ATLAS HAS BEEN ASSUMING THEY STACK. If h_term saturates - and part 3a
    says it does - then two h_term agents are SUB-ADDITIVE by construction,
    and the second one is paid for at full reserve cost for a fraction of
    the benefit. NOBODY HAS MEASURED TERMINAL CELL HEIGHT UNDER A
    COMBINATION. That is now the most decision-relevant unmeasured quantity
    in the stack.

    AND THE COMPARATIVE DATA SAYS DIVIDING FASTER IS NOT HOW LENGTH IS
    MADE. weber2025 measured the proliferation index by 2-hour EdU in
    growth cartilages differing more than TWOFOLD in daily elongation rate,
    in two species, and 'the fraction of S-phase chondrocytes is not
    significantly different'. Faster bones are not built by faster
    division. That is a third independent line against rate agents.
