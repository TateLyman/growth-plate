[1] THE PLATE IS STILL THERE WHEN GROWTH STOPS
==================================================================================
    herrmann2002 is the only observed spontaneous endpoint in this
    literature and it is an ARREST, not an emptying. Growth ceased
    at 24. THREE YEARS LATER the epiphyses were still anatomically
    open and the bone age was 16. A plate that had run out of cells
    would not still be a plate.
    So at the tissue level the human answer is: the cartilage
    remains and stops being used.

[2] AND THE ENDPOINT IS NOT SHARED
    herrmann2002 exhausted at 197 cm by 24. maffei2004 was still
    producing at 29. imre2025 was still producing at 31. The ESR1-
    null man was still producing at about 31 and cannot be stopped.
    ONE MAN'S PLATE QUIT AT 24 WHILE OTHERS RAN A DECADE LONGER.
    That spread is the direct evidence that the budget is not a
    fixed species constant, and it is larger than any drug effect
    in this file.

[3] THE VELOCITY IS THE CEILING OF THE DURATION LEVER, MEASURED
    ADULT spans only - herrmann2002 is excluded because its span
    starts at 14 and therefore contains pubertal growth:
        maffei2004: 1.44 cm/year
          imre2025: 0.83 cm/year
    mean 1.14 cm/year   [DERIVED, n=2]
    Removing oestrogen for an entire lifetime buys growth at ABOUT
    ONE CENTIMETRE A YEAR into the third decade. It does not buy a
    resumption of pubertal velocity. Anything in this atlas that
    treats delayed fusion as an open-ended lever should be read
    against that number.

[4] THE NEW NUMBER, AND IT POINTS THE OTHER WAY
    imre2025 closed the plates of a 31-year-old with transdermal
    estradiol 25 ug TWICE WEEKLY in SIX MONTHS. carani1997 and
    maffei2004 report the same direction. A decade of accumulated
    residual growth capacity is spent by six months of a low
    transdermal dose. THE OFF-SWITCH IS FAST AND CHEAP AND THE
    ON-SWITCH IS NEITHER - which is the asymmetry that makes an
    aromatase inhibitor worth more than anything acting inside the
    plate, and also the reason accidental oestrogen exposure is the
    dominant downside risk in this programme.

[5] WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE QUESTION
    The cartilage remains at cessation. Whether the cells IN it
    retain any capacity to divide has never been tested, in any
    species. Nobody has taken a senescent or arrested growth plate
    and attempted to restart it. The atlas holds that the ceiling is
    IMPOSED rather than intrinsic - culture restores maintenance
    methylation to resting-zone cells that were losing it in vivo -
    which predicts the cells are recoverable. That prediction has
    never been tested against a length endpoint.
