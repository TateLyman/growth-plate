# Round 200 — arrest or absence, and the measured ceiling of the duration lever

## The question chosen

Round 198 made fusion an exhaustion event; round 199 asked how to expand the budget. Both assume
the budget is **spent stock**. If the plate at cessation still holds cells that have merely
stopped being used, the problem is **arrest** — a different and more tractable problem.

## What resolved

**At the tissue level, the human answer is arrest.** `herrmann2002` is the only observed
spontaneous growth endpoint in the entire oestrogen-null literature: growth ceased at **24** at
~197 cm, and at **27, still untreated**, the hand X-ray showed **open epiphyses** with a bone age
of 16. A plate that had run out of cells would not still be a plate.

**And the endpoint is not shared.** herrmann2002 quit at 24. `maffei2004` was still producing at
29. `imre2025` at 31. The ESR1-null man of `smith2008` at ~31, and he cannot be closed by any
means. **One man's plate quit at 24 while others ran a decade longer** — a spread larger than any
pharmacological effect in this atlas.

## Two numbers the census did not have

The round-86 census screened 743 records and read 45 full texts to find 14 aromatase-deficient
males. **`imre2025` is the fifteenth and postdates it.**

| quantity | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| adult growth velocity, ages 25–31 | **0.83 cm/yr** | `imre2025` |
| adult growth velocity, ages 21–29 | **1.44 cm/yr** | `maffei2004` |
| mean adult velocity (n=2) | **1.14 cm/yr** | derived |
| time to fusion, transdermal E2 25 µg twice weekly, age 31 | **6 months** | `imre2025` |

`herrmann2002`'s apparent 2.70 cm/yr is **excluded** — his span starts at 14 and contains pubertal
growth. Pooling it would be an arithmetic error.

**1.14 cm/yr is the measured ceiling of the duration lever.** Removing oestrogen for an entire
lifetime does not restore pubertal velocity; it sustains a slow residual output into the third
decade. Every claim in this atlas treating delayed fusion as open-ended reads against that number.

## The asymmetry, which is the practical finding

A decade of accumulated residual capacity is spent by **six months of a low transdermal oestrogen
dose**. `maffei2004` agrees — oestradiol advanced a bone age frozen at 15 for eight years past 16
within the same six-month window.

**The off-switch is fast and cheap; the on-switch is neither.** That is the strongest argument in
this file for prioritising aromatase inhibition over anything acting inside the plate — and
equally the reason accidental or endogenous oestrogen exposure is the *dominant* downside risk in
this programme, not a background concern.

## Where this arm terminates

The cartilage remains at cessation. **Whether the cells in it retain any capacity to divide has
never been tested, in any species.** Nobody has taken a senescent or arrested growth plate and
attempted to restart it; a dedicated sweep for growth-plate reactivation returns nothing.

The atlas already predicts they are recoverable — `arm3_pool_ceiling_is_imposed_not_intrinsic`:
resting-zone chondrocytes losing maintenance methylation *in vivo* regain it in culture. **That
prediction has never been tested against a length endpoint.**

This arm ends at an unrun experiment: tissue-level answer **arrest**, cell-level answer **unknown**.
