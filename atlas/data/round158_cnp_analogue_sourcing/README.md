# Round 158 — sourcing the CNP analogues: what they cost and what is actually reachable

Researched 2026-08-09. **Prices are from public listings and payer/press sources; none is a quotation.**

## 1. The two drugs, priced

| | **Voxzogo** (vosoritide) | **Yuviwel** (navepegritide) |
|---|---|---|
| sponsor | BioMarin | Ascendis Pharma |
| dosing | daily SC | **once weekly** SC |
| US approval | 19 Nov 2021 | **27 Feb 2026** (accelerated) |
| US list | **~$320,000/yr**; net ~$240,000 after rebates | WAC **$38,325 / 4 injections** → **~$498,000/yr** |
| per vial (US) | ~$1,139 (0.4 mg) | — |
| Canada | CA$950/vial submitted → **CA$346,988/yr** | — |
| exclusivity | 8 patents; generic entry not before **~Oct 2030**, data exclusivity to **2034**; orphan exclusivity blocks biosimilars to **~2031**. One Paragraph IV challenge (eligible 19 Nov 2025) | **Orphan drug exclusivity granted April 2026** → ~2033 |
| EU | approved | MAA under EMA review, **decision expected Q4 2026** |

**Navepegritide is the more expensive of the two, by roughly 1.5×.** The once-weekly convenience does not
translate into a lower cost. Neither has a generic or biosimilar on any near horizon.

## 2. The label barrier — this is the binding constraint, not price

Both are indicated **only** for **achondroplasia** in children with **OPEN epiphyses** (Yuviwel ≥2 y).
The subject case is **bone age 16+ and non-achondroplastic** → off-label on two axes. Consequences:

- Manufacturer co-pay programmes (BioMarin RareConnections, up to $18,200/yr, $0 for eligible
  commercially insured; Ascendis equivalent) are **valid only for an FDA-approved indication** and
  exclude all government insurance. Off-label use will not qualify.
- Any lawful supply route — domestic, named-patient import, expanded access — runs through a prescriber
  willing to write it off-label.

## 3. Cheapest legitimate route found: a trial

**`NCT06382155`** — BioMarin Phase 2, **vosoritide in idiopathic short stature** (i.e. *not*
achondroplasia). Status **RECRUITING** as of May 2026; started Oct 2024. Design includes vosoritide dose
arms, an hGH comparator arm and placebo, and — importantly — **open-label vosoritide continuing until
near-final adult height**. That is free drug plus monitoring, indefinitely, in the correct indication.

**OPEN:** eligibility not yet checked against this case (age ceiling, open plates, treatment-naïve
requirement). Also `NCT` records for hypochondroplasia trials exist (`PMC11133798`, phase 2).

## 4. International differential pricing

Indian trader listings for Voxzogo span **₹25,388 – ₹121,000 per box** (IndiaMART, ExportersIndia,
GNH India, Indian Pharma Network) — a ~5× spread for nominally the same product. Named-patient import
services (e.g. everyone.org) operate lawfully in many jurisdictions **but require a prescription** and
supply at import-programme prices, not at Indian trader prices.

**Caution recorded, not editorialised:** a ~5× spread among unverified trader listings for a
$1,000-per-vial cold-chain biologic is the signature of a market where counterfeit and mishandled product
circulates. Verified pedigree matters more here than headline price.

## 5. Where the search stopped

Grey-market "research use only" peptide suppliers list navepegritide (e.g. InvivoChem V86319). These are
**not** recorded here as a sourcing option: RUO material carries no assurance of identity, purity,
sterility or endotoxin content, and the route of administration is weekly subcutaneous injection. That is
a product-quality hazard, not a paperwork one.
