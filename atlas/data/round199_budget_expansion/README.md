# Round 199 — every route to expanding the budget

## Why the enumeration had to be rewritten

Round 198 made the budget, not the closure, the binding constraint. The atlas's own coverage
audit had already reached the hard version: two men with zero lifetime oestrogen signal reached
197 and 204 cm, **one stopping at 24 with plates still anatomically open**. Growth stopped while
the door was open.

`every_mechanical_route_to_yield` (round 179) enumerated eight routes — and **all eight
redistribute a fixed budget**. Nothing enumerated the routes that would expand it.

## The existence proof

`ba2025` — deer antler, endochondral, **up to 2 cm per DAY** against *"the ~2 cm annual growth of
human growth plates"* (their comparison, their abstract). **365×**, in a mammal, same tissue type.
~240 cm of new bone per season — five human femurs, annually, from nothing.

Their attribution: a **vast stem-progenitor pool**; paracrine-supported proliferation;
intrinsically low tumorigenic potential; a **richly vascularised niche**; hybrid ossification via
PHEX⁺ intermediates. Against `rate = pool × amplification × h_term`: **two are pool terms, none is
an amplification claim.** This atlas has worked amplification hardest and pool least.

The point they don't make: an antler is **cast and regrown annually from pedicle periosteum**. The
budget isn't bigger — it's **renewable**, refilled from a reservoir *outside* the growth centre.
That is **route 8**, external recruitment, which round 179 listed as never costed.

And `hu2025`: **PRRX1** drives miR-143-3p in antler *reserve* mesenchymal cells, holding them
undifferentiated. PRRX1 is the marker of the human/mouse growth-plate **root** stem cell
(`chu2026`). Same conserved reserve-guard, in the one system where reserve is not limiting.

## The most actionable item: the enabling technology now exists

Round 179: zone-selective delivery is the only escape from the charge/discharge constraint, and
*"nothing achieves that today."*

`ye2026` — **CT-CM-NPs**: chondrocyte-membrane-coated nanoparticles with the collagen-II-binding
peptide **WYRGRL**, built explicitly because growth plate cartilage is *avascular and alymphatic*.
Delivered the Hedgehog agonist purmorphamine **into the plate in vivo** and **increased bone and
body length**.

That is cartilage-selective, pharmacological — therefore transient and dose-controllable —
delivery of a **pool** agonist. It converts the round-179 Hedgehog time-dependent-sign question
(`trompet2024` local pulse +3.63 % femur vs `orikasa2024` permanent deletion, no length, pool
leaked to osteoblasts) from untestable to testable.

## Two metabolic floors, both with available compounds, neither tested for gain

- `warren2023` — Nampt deletion in Prx1⁺ limb mesenchyme → limb shortening via **death of growth
  plate chondrocytes**; **nicotinamide riboside rescues**.
- `shtaif2026` — ColX-Cre *Sirt1* deletion (hypertrophic zone) → **10–11 % shorter male long
  bones**, 5–7 % female, plate height down in both; male KOs then **overshoot** on refeeding.

Both loss-of-function. By the round-197 inverted-U discipline, required ≠ addable.

## The cheapest untried axis in the file

The atlas established the growth plate ages by **losing** maintenance methylation, and flagged
that α-ketoglutarate and high-dose vitamin C — two common longevity supplements — supply the
demethylation machinery and push it the **wrong way**. It never enumerated the other side of the
same one-carbon ledger: **SAMe, betaine, choline, methionine, folate**, and **UHRF1** as the
DNMT1-targeting cofactor. Zero nodes. Zero tests on a growth plate.

## An enumerable hole, and a terminological confusion this atlas created

The word *senescence* has been used for two different things: the Baron–Nilsson **programmed**
downregulation of hundreds of growth genes, and **p16⁺ cellular senescence with a SASP**. The
second is a heavily drugged field. Two dedicated queries, 120 records, logged in
`gaps/search_log.yaml`: **the entire senolytic-in-cartilage literature is articular/osteoarthritic.
Nobody has looked for p16⁺ cells in a growth plate, or given a senolytic to a growing animal with
a length endpoint.**

## Still-unpursued, flagged not worked

- **The vascular inversion.** Antler growth centre is vascular; the human plate is avascular and
  its RZ is the most hypoxic compartment. Either the reserve is starved, or vascular invasion *is*
  fusion. Nothing distinguishes them and they predict opposite signs.
- **The species rate constant.** `delaney2014`: same programme, years in sheep vs weeks in mouse,
  offered as why large mammals get large. Never pursued.
