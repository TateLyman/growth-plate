    reservoir OUTSIDE the growth centre. ba2025's companion work
    identifies RXFP2+ mesenchymal stem cells in the antlerogenic

    THAT IS ROUTE 8 OF THIS ATLAS'S OWN ENUMERATION - external

    The largest natural experiment in endochondral growth runs on

[4] THE VASCULAR INVERSION, WHICH CUTS BOTH WAYS
    The antler growth centre is RICHLY VASCULAR. The human growth
    plate is avascular and alymphatic - ye2026 names exactly that
    as the reason drugs cannot reach it. The resting zone is the
    most hypoxic compartment in the plate.
    ONE READING: the human reserve is starved, and vascularity is
    part of what the antler buys. Against it: hypoxia is what keeps
    resting-zone cells quiescent and stem-like, and vascular
    invasion of a growth plate is what FUSION IS. Type H vessel
    ingrowth into the plate ends it.
    So vascularising a human plate is either the lever or the
    fusion trigger, and nothing in the atlas distinguishes them.

    chu2026: PRRX1 marks the 'root' stem cell of the human and mouse

    THE SAME TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR GUARDS THE RESERVE IN BOTH

    That is a quiescence switch with a known direction, in the one
