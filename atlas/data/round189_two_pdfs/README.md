# ROUND 189 — the two PDFs

Both supplied by the user. `silvestrini.txt` and `winer.txt` are the extracted full texts.

## `silvestrini2007` — WITHDRAWN as a contradiction

Round 185 called it "the only other rat study to measure the same structure under PTH and it found
nothing." It cannot contradict `ogawa2002`'s young cohort:

| | `ogawa2002` young | `silvestrini2007` |
|---|---|---|
| age | 6 wk | **3 months (adult)** |
| schedule | 80 µg/kg **5×/wk** | 80 µg/kg **3×/wk** |
| endpoint | LGR, CPR, thickness | **cartilage growth plate width only** |

`ogawa2002`'s **own adult cohort** also found no change in thickness or cell number. **The two studies
agree.** The assay wasn't insensitive — corticosterone in the same experiment did reduce plate width,
with recovery on withdrawal.

## `winer2025` — three things the abstract didn't say

1. **The schedule is TWICE DAILY** — 14 pulses/week, *more* frequent than the daily regimen round 185
   called 20× too frequent — sustained up to 6 years in adolescents, and HAZ **rose**.
2. **Two patients on GH were excluded.** The growth signal is not growth hormone.
3. **A FINAL-HEIGHT ENDPOINT.** CaR patients "attained or exceeded" midparental height, and a child
   treated from age 6 reached **final adult height consistent with MPH after a decade of PTH(1-34)**.

That last one is the integral endpoint. **Pool depletion produces early cessation and a final height
below target.** A decade of exposure across the whole growth period did not do that — the first
evidence in any species bearing on whether this drug spends the pool.

**Caveats:** replacement dosing in hypoparathyroidism (calcium correction is a large confound); APS-1
carries constitutional delay and hypogonadism, which is why that arm is flat; n=9 informative; dose
not stated; MPH is a wide target.

## Cutting the other way

`ogawa2002`'s own histology: cells per column **fell** in the PZ, density lower, cells more ellipsoidal,
while CPR rose — faster turnover through a shorter column, i.e. *reduced* residence time. That is the
pool-spending signature. Not clean (compared against basal, not concurrent, control).

And a targeted search confirms **nobody has measured a resting-zone cycle time under any PTH1R agonist
in any species.**

## Net

All three lines — human growth data, the only wild-type animal growth data, and the benign
immunogenicity profile — are now the **same molecule: teriparatide, PTH(1-34)**. Round 188's reversal
stands and is strengthened.
