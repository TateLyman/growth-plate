[1] THE PATTERN THE COMPLETE PACKAGE MAKES VISIBLE
================================================================================================
    Arms in an intact MALE animal: 4
      CG3-04 young male rats (unpublished, IND 1995)             -> NULL
      26-week rat toxicity study, males                          -> NULL
      R00796/R04296 bone quality, intact males                   -> NULL
      Winer 2010, randomised controlled trial in children        -> NULL, RANDOMISED
    Every one is null. The doses span 8 to 100 ug/kg/day and the
    durations span 18 days to 1 year.

    Every POSITIVE length result in the package is in a female
    animal, and every one of them travels with a large concurrent
    body-weight increase, in the same studies where the male arm
    LOST weight and showed no length change. The 26-week study is
    the cleanest case: females +2/+3/+4% femur length with BW gain
    x1.4-1.8 and food efficiency x1.4-1.7; males no length change
    with BW gain -5.2% and food efficiency x0.7.

    The FDA reviewer reached for the same explanation and recorded
    the sponsor's: that in females PTH enhances IGF-1 expression,
    'which may lead to increased femur length'. That is a systemic
    body-size mechanism, not a growth plate mechanism, and no data
    in the package tests it.

[2] WHAT THIS DOES TO THE INTERIM POSITIVES
    The two interim positives (+1.5 to +3.4%) are also female-only,
    because the second carcinogenicity study used FEMALE F344 rats
    exclusively. They are therefore inside the confounded class, not
    outside it. Round 194 read them as the clean age-gate answer.
    They still retire the age gate - the two windows agree - but they
    cannot be read as a plate effect in a male.

[3] THE HUMAN EVIDENCE IS A RANDOMISED NULL
    winer2010 is a 3-year randomised parallel trial in 12 children,
    8 of them male, comparing twice-daily PTH(1-34) against
    calcitriol. Both arms are eucalcaemic; only one delivers PTH1R
    pulses to the plate. Height percentile did not differ between
    arms OR across time. winer2018 extends the same cohort to ten
    years with height velocity Z normal throughout.
    This is the most direct test that exists and it is null.

[4] POWER - WHAT THE HUMAN NULL CAN AND CANNOT EXCLUDE
      min detectable difference at 50% power: 18.0 percentile points
      min detectable difference at 80% power: 24.8 percentile points
    A ~25 percentile-point difference is a very large effect. The
    randomised comparison therefore excludes only a large benefit.
    The tighter statement is the within-subject one: height
    percentile did not drift across three years, and height velocity
    Z stayed at reference for up to ten. A drug adding even 10% to
    growth rate would move a percentile track visibly over that span.

[5] WHAT IS STILL NOT MEASURED, ANYWHERE IN THE PACKAGE
    - bone length in ANY fusing species: both rabbit studies used
      mature or 9-month-old animals and did not measure length, and
      the monkey study excluded open plates by protocol
    - any growth plate histopathology, despite the physis being
      SECTIONED in every animal of both carcinogenicity studies
    - the words 'closure' and 'senescence' do not appear in the
      review at all
    - vertebral body HEIGHT, in any study, in any species
