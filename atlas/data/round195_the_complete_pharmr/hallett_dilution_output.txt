[1] THE CURVE IS BIPHASIC, WHICH IS THE POINT
    Weeks 0-5 fall 86.4 -> 5.13, a 16.8-fold loss. Weeks 5-12 fall
    5.13 -> 2.23, a 2.3-fold loss over a longer interval. The fast
    phase is the bulk proliferative population diluting out; the
    tail is the slow-cycling population the paper is about.

    --- FAST PHASE (wk 0-5) ---
      decay constant lambda   : 0.5458 / week   (R2 = 0.9926)
      half-time of the fraction:    1.27 weeks  =    8.9 days
      mean residence 1/lambda  :    1.83 weeks  =   12.8 days

    --- SLOW TAIL  (wk 5-12) ---
      decay constant lambda   : 0.1225 / week   (R2 = 0.9424)
      half-time of the fraction:    5.66 weeks  =   39.6 days
      mean residence 1/lambda  :    8.16 weeks  =   57.1 days

[2] WHAT THE SLOW TAIL BOUNDS
    lambda_obs = 0.1225/week combines division and efflux.
    Since lambda_division <= lambda_obs,
      T_stem(mouse resting zone) >= 57 days        [LOWER BOUND]
    If efflux out of the resting zone accounts for half the loss,
      T_stem >= 114 days.
    Both readings are far slower than any rat value in the graph.

[3] AGREEMENT WITH THE ROUND-194 EdU BOUND
    Round 194 swept the EdU labelling index over assumed S-phase
    durations and got 10-59 d, with the upper end unbounded because
    the labelling index's lower 1 s.d. bound touches zero.
    The dilution tail lands at 57 d, at the far end of that
    sweep. Two independent readouts of the same mice converge on the
    SLOW answer. The mouse resting zone divides on a timescale of
    months, not days.

[4] THE CONFLICT THIS CREATES WITH hunziker1994, AND WHY ROUND 183
    SURVIVES IT
    hunziker1994 author-stated T_stem, rat proximal tibia:
      hypophysectomised 50 d | +IGF-I 15 d | +GH 8 d | intact 6 d
    Mouse lower bound here: 57 d. That is 10x the intact rat
    value and the two cannot both describe the same quantity.
    Three candidate reconciliations, none excluded by these data:
      (a) the LRC gate (top 10% H2B-EGFP) selects the slowest tail of
          a heterogeneous resting zone, so this is not the mean;
      (b) mouse and rat resting zones genuinely differ;
      (c) hunziker1994's 'germinal cell' compartment is broader than
          the label-retaining fraction and includes faster cells.
    ROUND 183 IS NOT AFFECTED. Its conclusion is a set of RATIOS
    between four conditions measured by ONE method in ONE paper -
    GH gives 5.00x pool consumption, 1.36x terminal cell height and
    0.77x amplification. A systematic scale error in T_stem common to
    all four conditions cancels in every ratio. What does not survive
    is the ABSOLUTE amplification figure of 23-32 cells per
    progenitor, which scales inversely with T_stem and is now
    value_unverified.
