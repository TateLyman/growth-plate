## Enumerations established this round

- **Zero** studies anywhere report a PTH1R agonist increasing **final or adult** bone length or adult height in **any** species. Five broad queries built to surface a positive; all five hits concern PTH *levels* in disease or GNAS genetics, not agonist administration.
- `imaizumi2026`, n=622 paediatric dialysis patients: height velocity SDS falls **0.26 per doubling of intact PTH** (95% CI 0.49–0.02); restricted cubic spline monotonically decreasing. The human descending limb — though continuous tone, and CKD-confounded.

## The correction this round forced

**CORR-192** — "rate versus yield" was the wrong frame. Under `weise2001`, adult height = **reserve × terminal cell height**, not rate × time. A rate agent doesn't merely fail to add height; it brings fusion forward by as much as it raises growth. That reclassifies **growth hormone** — round 183 measured it at 5.00× pool consumption with amplification 0.77 — from "buys rate by spending pool" to a net cost under a fixed reserve. The GH-in-late-puberty human literature is not null, so the reconciliation is now an open item rather than a settled one.
