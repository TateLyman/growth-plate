====================================================================================
FIXED-RESERVE MODEL OF FUSION, AND WHAT IT DOES TO THE STACK
====================================================================================

weise2001, rabbit, a species that FUSES:
  fusion is triggered by exhaustion of proliferative potential;
  it occurs when the proliferation rate approaches zero;
  oestrogen accelerates senescence rather than ossifying directly.

THE CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY:
  adult height = reserve x terminal cell height, NOT rate x time.
  An agent that only raises rate brings fusion forward by exactly
  as much as it raises growth, and banks nothing. An agent that
  raises the RESERVE, or the height of each terminal cell, adds
  adult height.

====================================================================================
EVERY AGENT IN THE STACK, SORTED BY WHICH TERM IT TOUCHES
====================================================================================

anastrozole (aromatase inhibition)
    reserve : PRESERVES - removes the oestrogen drive that accelerates senescence, so the reserve is spent more slowly per unit time (weise2001: oestrogen accelerates programmed senescence and brings fusion forward)
    rate    : may LOWER - chou2025 shows oestrogen acting through GPER-1 sustains the PTHrP/Ihh ratio and the proliferative zone, so removing oestrogen may also lower proliferation
    VERDICT : the only agent in the stack whose primary action is on the RESERVE. This is why it works.

growth hormone
    reserve : SPENDS - round 183, hunziker1994 flux decomposition: GH raises resting-zone pool consumption 5.00x while amplification falls to 0.77
    rate    : RAISES - growth rate 31 to 163 micrometres per day in the hypophysectomised rat series
    VERDICT : buys rate by spending reserve. Under a fixed reserve that is a loan, not income, unless fusion is time-triggered - and weise2001 says it is not.

vosoritide / CNP
    reserve : UNKNOWN - assigned to terminal cell height from a hypertrophic zone thickness, which CORR-189 forbids reading as a cell-height claim
    rate    : RAISES in achondroplasia
    VERDICT : term assignment unverified; the h_term axis does not consume divisions, which would make it the safe axis if the assignment were secure.

erdafitinib (FGFR3 inhibition)
    reserve : UNKNOWN - gap g_l2_does_fgfr3_inhibition_raise_cells_per_column_or_only_zone_height
    rate    : RAISES in FGFR3 gain-of-function states
    VERDICT : unresolved, and it matters: if FGFR3 inhibition works by raising throughput it is a second GH.

teriparatide (PTH1R agonism)
    reserve : NEITHER - endogenous PTH1R tone PRESERVES the pool (correa2010: losing the effector collapses the resting zone from 5-plus layers to 1-2; hirai2011: postnatal receptor deletion destroys the plate), but the system is SATURATED, so adding agonist adds nothing
    rate    : NO CHANGE in any wild-type animal or randomised human trial
    VERDICT : not harmful to the reserve, and not useful either. The saturation is the whole finding.

====================================================================================
[1] THIS RETROSPECTIVELY EXPLAINS THE RAT TERMINAL TABLE
====================================================================================
    Round 194: femur length 32.8 -> 33.9 mm at 8 months under
    teriparatide, 34.2 -> 35.2 mm at 12 months, and 35 mm in EVERY
    arm at 26 months. A fixed reserve predicts exactly that shape:
    treated animals arrive at the ceiling earlier and stop. The rat
    never fuses, so the ceiling is asymptotic rather than abrupt,
    but the accounting is the same.

[2] AND IT CLOSES THE GAP THAT HAS BEEN OPEN SINCE ROUND 194
    The question was whether an accelerated six months is BANKED in
    a fusing species or merely brings fusion forward. weise2001
    answers it in a fusing species: the trigger is exhaustion, so
    acceleration is not banked. THE ANSWER IS NEGATIVE, and it is
    negative for GH as much as for teriparatide.

[3] THE NEW QUESTION THIS OPENS, WHICH IS STACK-SPECIFIC
    chou2025: oestrogen acting through GPER-1 SUSTAINS the growth
    plate PTHrP/Ihh ratio; blocking or deleting GPER-1 lowers it and
    expands the hypertrophic zone. Anastrozole removes oestrogen.
    If aromatase inhibition lowers growth-plate PTHrP tone, it moves
    the plate DOWN the inverted U established in round 197 - and a
    plate below the optimum is the one state in which a PTH1R
    agonist has ever added length.
    THE STACK MAY MANUFACTURE ITS OWN INDICATION. That is a specific,
    testable prediction and nobody has tested it.

[4] THE COUNTERWEIGHT, WHICH IS EQUALLY SPECIFIC
    Oestrogen has two opposing arms at the plate. Through GPER-1 it
    is pro-proliferative and pro-PTHrP (chou2025). Through the
    senescence programme it spends the reserve (weise2001). An
    aromatase inhibitor removes both. The reason it nets positive
    for adult height in humans is that the reserve arm dominates.
    Adding a PTH1R agonist to replace the lost GPER-1 arm would
    restore proliferation - which under a fixed reserve is the arm
    you did not want back.
    So the stack-specific hypothesis is self-limiting: the deficit
    anastrozole creates is a RATE deficit, and rate is not what sets
    adult height.
