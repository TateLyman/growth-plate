# DOMAIN 19 — REGENERATIVE, GENETIC AND ADVANCED MODALITIES (everything that is not a small molecule)
Enumerate every modality class that could in principle act on a growth plate, with its real state of
the art, its delivery route, and whether it has EVER been applied to cartilage or bone length.
CELL AND TISSUE: MSC and skeletal stem cell therapy; iPSC-derived chondrocytes; chondrocyte
implantation (ACI/MACI); physeal repair and bar prevention (interposition materials, fat, silicone,

GENETIC: AAV serotypes and cartilage tropism; lentivirus; LNP and mRNA; CRISPR nuclease, base editing,
prime editing, CRISPRa/CRISPRi, epigenome editing (dCas9-DNMT/TET/p300/KRAB); enhancer editing as a

(PS, MOE, LNA, PMO); siRNA and GalNAc and non-hepatic conjugates; aptamers; ribozymes; tRNA suppressors;
targeted protein degradation (PROTAC, molecular glue, LYTAC, KineTAC, AUTAC/ATTEC, RIPTAC); antibody

depot chemistries, and every published demonstration that ANY macromolecule reached a GROWTH PLATE.
