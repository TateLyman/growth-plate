# DOMAIN 10 — VASCULAR, NEURAL, IMMUNE, HAEMATOPOIETIC AND MICROBIAL INPUTS (complete inventory)
Enumerate every non-chondrocyte tissue and non-endocrine systemic input that reaches the growth plate.
VASCULAR: epiphyseal and metaphyseal blood supply; cartilage canals; the secondary ossification centre as
a vascular structure; vascular invasion and the chondro-osseous junction; VEGFA/B/C, VEGFR1/2/3, PlGF,
angiopoietins and TIE2, NRP1/2, HIF1A/EPAS1, PHDs, MMP9-mediated release of VEGF, type H vessels and
CD31hi/EMCNhi endothelium, PDGFB/pericytes, NOTCH-DLL4 in angiogenesis, osteoclast-derived PDGF-BB,
blood flow, perfusion, oxygen tension by zone, nutrient and solute delivery, lymphatics (or their
absence), the avascular barrier and diffusion distance, angiogenesis inhibitors and stimulators.
NEURAL: sensory and sympathetic innervation of bone and periosteum, NGF/TrkA, semaphorin 3A, CGRP,
substance P, neuropeptide Y, VIP, beta-adrenergic tone, leptin's central pathway, the hypothalamic
control of growth (GHRH, somatostatin, ghrelin, kisspeptin, MKRN3, DLK1, neurokinin B, GnRH pulse
generator), circadian and sleep architecture, slow-wave sleep and GH pulsatility, melatonin and the
pineal, stress/HPA axis, psychosocial deprivation and its reversal.
IMMUNE AND HAEMATOPOIETIC: IL-1 family, IL-6/gp130/sIL-6R, TNF, IL-17, IL-11, IL-10, TGF-beta as an
immune cytokine, interferons, the marrow as a niche, osteoclast and macrophage lineages, osteomacs,
mast cells, T and B cells in bone, complement, the inflammasome, chronic inflammation's suppression of
IGF-1 and of chondrocyte proliferation, and every approved biologic that blocks one of these.
MICROBIAL AND METABOLIC-HOST: gut microbiome composition, germ-free and gnotobiotic growth phenotypes,
SCFAs (butyrate, propionate, acetate) and their receptors/transporters, specific lactobacilli, bile
acids and FXR/TGR5, microbial vitamin synthesis, environmental enteric dysfunction, antibiotics and
growth (including agricultural growth promotion), helminths, probiotics and prebiotics.
