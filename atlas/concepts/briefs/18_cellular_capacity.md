# DOMAIN 18 — CELL-INTRINSIC CAPACITY LIMITS: TRANSLATION, RNA PROCESSING, GENOME MAINTENANCE, ORGANELLES
A chondrocyte that must double its mass and secrete an enormous matrix is limited by machinery nobody
in the growth literature treats as a lever. Enumerate every such capacity constraint and every human
disease that reveals one.
RIBOSOME AND TRANSLATION: ribosome biogenesis (RPL/RPS genes, RPSA, RMRP, POLR1A/B/C/D, TCOF1, UTP4,
NOL11, BMS1, WDR4), the ribosomopathies (Diamond-Blackfan, Shwachman-Diamond, cartilage-hair hypoplasia,
Treacher Collins, 5q- syndrome), nucleolar stress and p53, mTORC1 control of translation (4E-BP1, S6K1,
eIF4E, eIF4A, LARP1), eIF2alpha and the integrated stress response (GCN2, PERK, HRI, PKR, ATF4, CHOP,
GADD34, ISRIB), elongation factors (EEF1A, EEF2, EEF2K), tRNA synthetases and the human diseases they
cause, tRNA modification enzymes, codon usage, selenoproteins (SEPSECS, SECISBP2 and its short-stature
phenotype), and whether translational capacity has ever been RAISED experimentally with a growth endpoint.
RNA PROCESSING: the spliceosome and spliceosomopathies (RNU4ATAC/MOPD1-Roifman-Lowry-Wood, SF3B4/
Nager, EFTUD2, SNRPB/cerebrocostomandibular, TXNL4A/Burn-McKeown), minor spliceosome specifically,
NMD, RNA export, polyadenylation, RNA exosome (EXOSC), P-bodies and stress granules, RNA binding
proteins in cartilage (SERBP1, IGF2BP1-3, HuR/ELAVL1, MSI2, LIN28), and RNA modification enzymes.
GENOME MAINTENANCE: replication stress and origin licensing (the Meier-Gorlin genes ORC1/4/6, CDC6,
CDT1, GMNN, CDC45, MCM5 - a group whose defining feature is SHORT STATURE, so the reverse direction is
worth asking about), ATR/ATM/CHK1 (Seckel), the Fanconi pathway, Bloom/RECQL helicases, Cockayne/XP
and transcription-coupled repair, DNA ligase IV and NHEJ, telomere maintenance (TERT/TERC/DKC1/
shelterin) and the telomere biology disorders, ribonucleotide reductase, nucleotide pools, chromosome
segregation and the mitotic checkpoint (BUB1B/mosaic variegated aneuploidy), centrosome and
centriole genes (the primary microcephaly and MOPD2/PCNT set), and the cohesinopathies.
ORGANELLES AND METABOLISM: mitochondrial biogenesis and mitochondrial disease as a stature phenotype,
OXPHOS vs glycolysis by growth-plate zone, the pentose phosphate pathway, one-carbon and folate cycles,
NAD+ and sirtuins, the hexosamine pathway and O-GlcNAc, lipid synthesis and SREBP, peroxisomes and
the peroxisomal disorders, lysosome and autophagy/mTOR/TFEB, mitophagy, the ER-Golgi secretory
capacity ceiling for a cell secreting COL2A1 at ~70,000 CPM, proteasome capacity and the
proteasome-associated autoinflammatory syndromes, and chaperone capacity (HSF1, HSP70/90, arimoclomol).
