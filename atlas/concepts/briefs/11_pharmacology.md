# DOMAIN 11 — ALL DRUG AND EXPOSURE CLASSES VERSUS HEIGHT (complete inventory)
Enumerate EVERY drug class, and named agents within it, with any reported effect on longitudinal growth,
bone age, growth plate morphology or adult stature — in EITHER direction, in ANY species. Both directions
matter: a drug that shortens is a contraindication to find, a drug that lengthens is a lead.
Cover: growth hormone and secretagogues; IGF-1 preparations; somatostatin analogues; GH antagonists;
aromatase inhibitors; SERMs; SERDs and degraders; oestrogens and androgens including anabolic steroids
and SARMs; 5-alpha reductase inhibitors; GnRH agonists and antagonists; glucocorticoids by every route
and every molecule; mineralocorticoids; thyroid hormone and antithyroid drugs; insulin and every
antidiabetic class (metformin, sulfonylureas, TZDs, GLP-1RA, DPP4i, SGLT2i); statins and other lipid
agents; antihypertensives (ACEi, ARBs, beta-blockers, CCBs, diuretics, minoxidil, spironolactone);
anticoagulants and antiplatelets (warfarin, heparins, DOACs); NSAIDs and paracetamol and COX-2i;
opioids; antiepileptics (valproate, carbamazepine, topiramate, zonisamide, phenytoin, levetiracetam);
psychotropics (SSRIs, SNRIs, antipsychotics typical and atypical, lithium, stimulants including
methylphenidate and amphetamine, atomoxetine, guanfacine, buspirone, hydroxyzine, trazodone);
antibiotics (tetracyclines, quinolones and cartilage toxicity, aminoglycosides, macrolides);
antifungals (azoles, terbinafine); antivirals and ART including tenofovir; immunosuppressants
(calcineurin inhibitors, rapalogs, azathioprine, MMF, methotrexate); biologics (anti-TNF, anti-IL-6,
anti-IL-1, anti-IL-17, anti-IL-23, JAK inhibitors); all oncology classes (cytotoxics, platinum,
alkylators, anthracyclines, antimetabolites, taxanes, vinca, TKIs by target family, anti-angiogenics,
hedgehog inhibitors, immune checkpoint inhibitors, CAR-T, radioligand); epigenetic drugs (DNMTi, HDACi,
EZH2i, BETi, LSD1i, menin-MLL, IDHi); bone drugs (bisphosphonates, denosumab, teriparatide, abaloparatide,
romosozumab, calcitonin, strontium, fluoride); vitamin D and analogues; retinoids (isotretinoin,
acitretin, palovarotene, tretinoin, bexarotene); antihistamines; PPIs and H2 blockers; laxatives;
inhaled and intranasal steroids and non-steroid asthma drugs (montelukast, theophylline); immunotherapy
for allergy; anaesthetics; muscle relaxants; PDE inhibitors of every subtype; nitrates; vasopressin
analogues; carbonic anhydrase inhibitors; chelators (penicillamine, deferoxamine, deferasirox);
heavy-metal and industrial exposures; every supplement sold for height (arginine, ornithine, lysine,
glutamine, GABA, colostrum, deer antler, HGH-boosters, calcium, zinc, vitamin K2, MSM, collagen peptides,
creatine, DHEA, ashwagandha, Chinese and Ayurvedic height formulations); alcohol, nicotine, cannabis,
caffeine; illicit drugs.
