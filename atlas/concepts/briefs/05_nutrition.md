# DOMAIN 05 — NUTRITION AND METABOLISM
Macronutrients (energy, protein quality, leucine/lysine/tryptophan/arginine/glutamine/methionine/cysteine/
taurine, fat type incl. cholesterol, glycaemic load); micronutrients (zinc, iron, iodine, calcium, phosphate,
magnesium, copper, manganese, selenium, boron, silicon, vitamins A, D, K1/K2, C, B12, folate, B6, thiamine);
foods with claimed height effects (milk/dairy and WHY, meat, fish, eggs, soy); metabolic states
(undernutrition/stunting and catch-up, obesity, insulin resistance, diabetes, ketosis, fasting, dietary
restriction and refeeding); cellular metabolism (glycolysis vs OXPHOS in chondrocytes, glutamine, one-carbon/
SAM/betaine/choline, polyamines, NAD+, sirtuins, AMPK, mTOR nutrient sensing, autophagy, hexosamine,
sulfation/PAPS); population evidence (the secular trend, Dutch/Japanese/Korean increases, migration studies,
the milk hypothesis) and what best evidence says CAUSED them.
FOR EACH: direction; EFFECT SIZE IN CM where a human number exists; best evidence + PMID preferring RCTs;
and critically whether it is DEFICIENCY CORRECTION or TRUE ELEVATION above normal.
