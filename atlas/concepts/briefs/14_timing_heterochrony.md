# DOMAIN 14 — TIMING, HETEROCHRONY, SENESCENCE AND EPIGENETICS (complete inventory)
Enumerate everything that sets WHEN growth happens and when it stops — the PERIOD rather than the RATE.
Cover: growth-plate senescence as a division-counted programme; the resting-zone progenitor pool and its
depletion; clonal dynamics and stem-cell self-renewal at the plate; catch-up growth and its mechanism;
the "growth plate has a memory" literature; infancy-childhood-puberty (ICP) growth model; mid-childhood
growth spurt and adrenarche; the pubertal growth spurt, peak height velocity, and its timing determinants;
tempo vs amplitude of growth; bone age and every method of assessing it (Greulich-Pyle, Tanner-Whitehouse
2/3, Fels, BoneXpert, Sauvegrain elbow, Risser, Oxford, knee MRI, ultrasound, EOS); adult height
prediction methods and their errors; the heterochronic microRNAs (lin-4/let-7/LIN28A/LIN28B, miR-125,
HMGA2, IGF2BP1/2/3) and every other growth-plate microRNA (miR-140, miR-1, miR-433, miR-26b, miR-221/222,
miR-145, miR-483, miR-675); lncRNAs in cartilage; circRNAs; RNA modification (m6A writers/readers/erasers,
m5C, pseudouridine); DNA methylation clocks and cartilage-specific epigenetic ageing; DNMT1/3A/3B, UHRF1/2,
TET1/2/3, methyl donor supply (SAM, folate, B12, choline, betaine, glycine, NNMT); histone modifications
and every writer/eraser/reader family (PRC1/2, KMT2, SETD, NSD, DOT1L, KDM1/4/5/6, HDAC1-11, SIRT1-7,
HAT/p300/CBP, BET); chromatin remodellers (SWI/SNF, CHD, ISWI, INO80); 3D genome and enhancer-promoter
architecture, TADs and CTCF, and limb/growth-plate enhancers specifically; imprinting; telomeres and
telomerase in chondrocytes; replicative vs stress-induced senescence and SASP; cellular reprogramming
and partial reprogramming; quiescence and dormancy programmes; the cell cycle machinery in chondrocytes
(CDKN1A/1B/1C/2A/2B/2C, RB, CCND1, MYC, E2F); developmental tempo and species-specific clock speed;
seasonality of growth; and the question of whether a fused physis can ever be reopened.
