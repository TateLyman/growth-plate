# DOMAIN 08 — SYSTEMIC DISEASE AS NATURAL EXPERIMENT (complete inventory)
Enumerate EVERY human condition, acquired or genetic, in which stature or growth velocity is altered,
whether the alteration is the point of the disease or incidental. This is the CORR-295 correction: tall
phenotypes rarely reach a clinic, so they must be hunted deliberately. Cover BOTH directions and mark
which. Domains: chronic inflammatory disease (IBD Crohn/UC, juvenile idiopathic arthritis, SLE, vasculitis,
periodic fever syndromes/FMF/CAPS/TRAPS, sarcoid); coeliac and other malabsorption; cystic fibrosis;
chronic kidney disease and renal tubular acidosis; nephropathic cystinosis; Bartter/Gitelman; liver
disease and cholestasis (Alagille); congenital heart disease and cyanosis; chronic anaemia and
haemoglobinopathies (thalassaemia, sickle cell); chronic hypoxia and altitude; obstructive sleep apnoea;
asthma; every endocrinopathy (GH deficiency and insensitivity, Laron, hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism,
Cushing, congenital adrenal hyperplasia, precocious and delayed puberty, hypogonadism, hyperprolactinaemia,
diabetes mellitus type 1 including Mauriac, diabetes insipidus, hyperparathyroidism, hypoparathyroidism,
pseudohypoparathyroidism, rickets of every type, hypophosphatasia, X-linked hypophosphataemia,
aromatase deficiency and excess, oestrogen resistance, androgen insensitivity, 5-alpha reductase
deficiency, GnRH-related disorders, MC2R/FGD); every overgrowth syndrome (Sotos, Weaver, Marshall-Smith,
Malan, Tatton-Brown-Rahman, Beckwith-Wiedemann, Simpson-Golabi-Behmel, Perlman, PIK3CA-related,
Proteus, Cowden/PTEN, Klinefelter and other sex-chromosome aneuploidies, XYY, Marfan, congenital
contractural arachnodactyly, homocystinuria, MEN2B, LEOPARD, Cantu syndrome, Bannayan-Riley-Ruvalcaba,
X-linked acrogigantism, McCune-Albright, MAS/pituitary gigantism/acromegaly); every short-stature
syndrome not already in the dysplasia domain (Turner, Noonan and the RASopathies, Silver-Russell,
Temple, Prader-Willi, 3M, Meier-Gorlin, MOPD, Seckel, Bloom, Fanconi, Cockayne, Rothmund-Thomson,
progerias, Down, Williams, DiGeorge, Kabuki, CHARGE, Cornelia de Lange, Rubinstein-Taybi, Aarskog,
IGF1R deletion, SHOX deficiency/Leri-Weill/Langer, PAPPA2 deficiency, STAT5B deficiency, IGFALS
deficiency); mitochondrial disease; lysosomal storage (all MPS types, mucolipidoses, GM1); peroxisomal;
CDG; inborn errors of amino acid, carbohydrate, purine and lipid metabolism; immunodeficiency; HIV;
chronic infection and parasitism; malignancy and its treatment (chemo, cranial and spinal irradiation,
TBI, HSCT, glucocorticoid exposure); anorexia nervosa, ARFID, exercise-associated amenorrhoea, RED-S;
psychosocial deprivation dwarfism; iatrogenic causes of every kind.
