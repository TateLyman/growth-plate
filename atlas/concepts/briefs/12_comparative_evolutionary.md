# DOMAIN 12 — COMPARATIVE, EVOLUTIONARY AND DEVELOPMENTAL BODY SIZE (complete inventory)
Enumerate the mechanisms by which body size and limb length differ BETWEEN species, breeds, populations
and individuals — the natural experiments that have already produced extreme size differences.
Cover: dog breed size loci (IGF1, IGF1R, IGFBP4/HMGA2, STC2, SMAD2, GHR) and what each does; horse,
cattle, pig, chicken and sheep size/growth QTLs and the LCORL/NCAPG locus across species; giant and
dwarf mouse strains (Ames, Snell, little, lit/lit, GHR-KO, high-growth hg locus, myostatin nulls,
Booroola sheep, Belgian Blue and Piedmontese cattle); selective breeding for size; domestication
syndrome; island gigantism and dwarfism; Bergmann's and Allen's rules; the human tall and short
populations (Dutch, Dinaric, Nilotic, Tutsi vs pygmy peoples: Aka, Baka, Efe, Mbuti, Batwa, Andaman,
Philippine Negrito) and the DIFFERENT genetic mechanisms in each (GH/IGF-1 axis vs HERC2/OBSCN vs
growth-hormone insensitivity); secular trend in height and its drivers; heterosis and outbreeding;
inbreeding depression on stature; parental-origin/imprinting effects on size (IGF2, H19, GRB10, DLK1,
PLAGL1, MEST, PEG3, KCNQ1OT1, GNAS); polyploidy and cell size vs cell number as size mechanisms;
indeterminate vs determinate growth and species that never fuse their plates (fish, reptiles,
some amphibians, and which mammals retain growth); regeneration and blastema biology; limb allometry;
the evolution of the secondary ossification centre; giraffe, elephant and whale limb-length mechanisms;
sauropod and dinosaur growth-rate literature; ancient DNA and archaeological stature; hormonal
manipulation in livestock (rBST, beta-agonists, implants) and the size gained; growth-promoting
antibiotics; caloric plane of nutrition in livestock.
