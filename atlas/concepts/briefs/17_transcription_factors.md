# DOMAIN 17 — TRANSCRIPTION FACTORS AND THE GENE-REGULATORY NETWORK OF THE CHONDROCYTE
Signalling pathways are covered separately (domain 04). This domain is the NUCLEAR END: the
transcription factors, cofactors and regulatory DNA that actually execute growth-plate decisions.
Enumerate: the SOX trio (SOX9, SOX5, SOX6) and every SOX9 cofactor and post-translational
modification; RUNX1/2/3 and CBFB; SP7/osterix; MEF2C and MEF2D; the HDAC4/5-MEF2 axis;
FOXA2/FOXA3; FOXO1/3/4; FOXC1/2; the GLI family and their processing; the SMADs (1/5/8, 2/3, 4, 6, 7);
TCF/LEF and beta-catenin; NFAT family; NFkB subunits; ATF/CREB family (CREB1, ATF4, ATF6, CREB3L2);
HIF1A/EPAS1/HIF3A and ARNT; ESRRB/ESRRG; the nuclear receptors as TFs (VDR, RAR/RXR, THR, GR, ER, AR,
PPAR, NR4A, REV-ERB/NR1D1, ROR); TWIST1/2; PRRX1/2; MSX1/2; DLX5/6; PAX1/9; NKX3-2/BAPX1; SHOX/SHOX2;
HOXA/B/C/D clusters and posterior prevalence; MEIS/PBX/TALE; TBX2/3/4/5/15/18; ZBTB16; TRPS1;
EBF1/2/3; ZEB1/2; SNAI1/2; ID1-4; MYC/MYCN/MAX and MNT; E2F family and RB; TP53/TP63/TP73;
YAP/TAZ/TEAD as transcriptional output; STAT1/3/5A/5B/6; IRF family; KLF2/4/15; EGR1/2;
AP-1 (FOS/JUN family); SRF/MRTF; the ZNF/KRAB-ZNF genes that reach height GWAS (ZNF518A, ZFAT,
SCMH1, HMG20B and any others); LCORL/NCAPG; CHD8 and the chromatin-adjacent TFs; NFIA/B/C/X;
GATA family; TFAP2; the E-box and RUNX motif logic of cartilage enhancers.
Also: growth-plate-specific ENHANCERS and their genes (the Fgfr3 -29E element, the Hhip CE2/CE3
elements, the Sox9 enhancer cluster, the Ihh/Gli enhancers, the ACAN promoter), super-enhancers in
chondrocytes, CTCF/cohesin and TAD structure at skeletal loci, and any published chondrocyte
enhancer atlas, ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq, Hi-C or single-cell multiome resource.
For each TF: is it a positive or negative regulator of LENGTH; is there a human phenotype in either
direction; and — the point of the domain — is there ANY way to modulate it (degrader, PROTAC,
molecular glue, transcription-factor-targeted small molecule, oligonucleotide, decoy, CRISPRa/i).
