# DOMAIN 16 — DEVELOPMENTAL ORIGINS AND LIMB PATTERNING (complete inventory)
Enumerate the events that build the skeleton and therefore set its starting conditions and its ceiling.
Cover: gastrulation and lateral plate mesoderm; limb bud initiation (TBX5/TBX4, FGF10, WNT2B/8C);
the apical ectodermal ridge and FGF4/8/9/17; the zone of polarising activity, SHH and the ZRS enhancer
(and its human mutations); the progress zone vs early specification models; proximodistal patterning
(MEIS1/2, HOXA/D 9-13, HOXA11/HOXD11 and the zeugopod, HOX13 and the autopod); anteroposterior and
dorsoventral patterning (WNT7A, LMX1B, EN1); interdigital apoptosis; mesenchymal condensation
(SOX9, N-cadherin, NCAM, TGF-beta); joint interzone specification (GDF5, WNT9A, ERG, autopod
segmentation); the transition from cartilage anlage to growth plate; primary ossification centre
formation; secondary ossification centre formation and its timing (and the fact that stem-cell
self-renewal at the plate begins with the SOC); the perichondrium/periosteum and groove of Ranvier;
the borderline chondrocyte population; chondrocyte-to-osteoblast transdifferentiation; the origin of
the resting zone; fetal growth and its determinants (placental function, IGF2, uterine constraint,
maternal height and nutrition, gestational age, birth length); the developmental origins of health and
disease literature applied to stature; catch-up growth after IUGR/SGA and its cost; postnatal growth
canalisation; the relationship between birth length, infancy growth and adult height; congenital
limb reduction and duplication defects as informative failures; brachydactyly and its types;
symphalangism; the difference between patterning genes (which set number and identity) and growth
genes (which set size), and any gene that does both.
