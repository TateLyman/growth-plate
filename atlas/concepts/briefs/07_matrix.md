# DOMAIN 07 — MATRIX, MINERALISATION AND CELL VOLUME (complete inventory)
Enumerate every structural, enzymatic and physicochemical determinant of the growth-plate matrix and of
hypertrophic chondrocyte volume (h_term ~80% of longitudinal growth). Cover: all collagens present in
cartilage (II, IX, X, XI, VI, XXVII, XVI, I, III, V); aggrecan and its domains; the other proteoglycans
(versican, decorin, biglycan, fibromodulin, lumican, perlecan/HSPG2, epiphycan, PRELP, chondroadherin,
asporin); link protein/HAPLN1; hyaluronan and HAS1-3, HYAL1-3, CD44; COMP; matrilins; thrombospondins;
tenascin; fibronectin; elastin/fibrillin/fibulins/LTBPs/EMILIN; SPARC/osteonectin; SPP1; MGP; BGLAP.
Biosynthesis and processing: prolyl and lysyl hydroxylases (P4HA1-3, P4HB, PLOD1-3), LOX/LOXL1-4,
transglutaminase 2 and FXIII, procollagen C/N-proteinases (BMP1/TLL, ADAMTS2/3/14), PCOLCE, HSP47/SERPINH1,
FKBP10, CRTAP/P3H1/PPIB, ER chaperones and the secretory pathway, COPII (SEC23A/SEC24D/TANGO1),
Golgi glycosylation (B3GAT3, B4GALT7, B3GALT6, XYLT1/2, CHSY1/3, CSGALNACT1/2, EXT1/2, EXTL3, NDST,
HS2ST/6ST/3ST, SULF1/2), sulfation (SLC26A2, SLC13A1, PAPSS1/2, SLC35B2/B3, CHST3/11/12/13/14/15, UST,
GALNS, ARSB, ARSI, IMPAD1/PAP phosphatase), and every sulfatase and glycosidase.
Degradation: MMP2/9/13/14, ADAMTS1/4/5/7/9/12, cathepsins B/K/L/S, aggrecanase, plasmin/PLG/PLAT/PLAU/
PLAUR/SERPINE1/SERPINE2, cystatins, TIMPs 1-4, heparanase, elastase, neutrophil proteases.
Mineralisation: ALPL, ENPP1, ANKH, PHOSPHO1, PiT1/2 (SLC20A1/2), matrix vesicles, annexins A2/A5/A6,
PPi/Pi ratio, pyrophosphatases including PPA1, calcium/phosphate handling, FGF23/Klotho/PHEX/DMP1,
carbonic anhydrases, osteoclast resorption at the chondro-osseous junction, RANKL/OPG/RANK, MCSF, TRAP.
Volume and osmolyte biology: fixed charge density and Donnan, aquaporins (AQP1/3/4/9), NKCC1/SLC12A2,
KCC1-4/SLC12A4-7, WNK1-4, SPAK/OSR1, NHE1/SLC9A1, anion exchangers SLC4A2/AE2, VRAC/LRRC8A-E, TRPV4,
PIEZO1/2, TMEM16A/ANO1, ClC channels, NFAT5/TonEBP, organic osmolytes (taurine, betaine, myo-inositol,
sorbitol, glycerophosphocholine) and their transporters (SLC6A6, SLC6A12, SLC5A3, SLC5A11, SLC44A1,
SLC14A1 urea), aldose reductase AKR1B1, intracellular solute content by zone, dry mass vs osmotically
inactive fraction, glycogen, lipid droplets, cell swelling vs proteins-synthesis-driven growth.
Physical: matrix stiffness, tension, interstitial fluid flow, collagen fibril orientation, the
perichondrial ring of LaCroix and groove of Ranvier as a mechanical restraint.
