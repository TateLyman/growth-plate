# DOMAIN 21 — THE ACTUAL INTERVENTION LANDSCAPE (what has been TRIED, registered or marketed)
Enumerate what has actually been done to humans and animals to increase height — the trials, the
pipelines, the approvals, the failures and the fringe. This is a REGISTRY AND MARKET sweep, not a
mechanism sweep.
Cover, using clinicaltrials.gov, the EU CTR/EudraCT, ISRCTN, ANZCTR, ChiCTR, jRCT, CTRI and WHO ICTRP,
plus FDA Drugs@FDA / EMA EPARs / PMDA, plus company pipelines and press releases:
1. Every registered interventional trial with HEIGHT, HEIGHT VELOCITY, ADULT HEIGHT, FINAL HEIGHT or
   GROWTH as an outcome, in any indication — idiopathic short stature, SGA, GHD, Turner, Noonan,
   achondroplasia and other skeletal dysplasias, chronic disease, and healthy children.
2. Every approved drug anywhere in the world for a growth indication, including agents approved only
   in Japan, China, Korea, Russia or India that Western reviews omit.
3. Every agent in active clinical development for achondroplasia or hypochondroplasia and its stage
   (CNP analogues, FGFR inhibitors, soluble FGFR3 decoys, anti-FGFR3 antibodies, CNP-Fc fusions,
   parathyroid-related agents, and anything else).
4. Every FAILED or DISCONTINUED programme and the stated reason — failures are as informative as
   successes and are usually invisible to PubMed.
5. Long-acting GH formulations and their comparative efficacy.
6. Combination trials (GH + AI, GH + GnRHa, GH + oxandrolone, CNP + FGFR, etc.) and what each combination
   actually delivered in centimetres.
7. Off-label and grey-market practice: height-increase clinics, limb lengthening for stature, growth
   hormone in non-deficient children, and what the outcome literature says.
8. Supplements and devices marketed for height, with whatever evidence exists.
9. Veterinary and agricultural growth-promotion products.
10. Any n-of-1, compassionate-use, expanded-access or named-patient programme relevant to growth.
For every agent report: DOSE, ROUTE, POPULATION, DURATION, and the EFFECT IN CENTIMETRES OR cm/yr where
reported, plus the comparator.

PROSE: "EVERYTHING EVER SHOWN TO INCREASE HEIGHT IN A HUMAN, RANKED BY EFFECT SIZE";
"AGENTS APPROVED OR IN TRIALS OUTSIDE THE US/EU THAT WESTERN REVIEWS MISS";
"DISCONTINUED PROGRAMMES AND WHY THEY DIED".
