R436 full-concept-space enumeration. All rows derived from EXTERNAL search (Europe PMC REST, NCBI eutils,
web). Nothing here was taken from the atlas. Species is stated for every experimental claim.
Reviews are used as an INDEX and are flagged as such.

**Reading key.** HEIGHT DIRECTION = what the human/animal phenotype does to stature or bone length.
`loss→SHORT` is the overwhelming default and is *not* interesting on its own; the interesting rows are
`loss→TALL`, `gain→LONGER`, and any row where the capacity has actually been RAISED.
`OBSCURE? yes` = essentially absent from the mainstream growth/growth-plate literature.
