**Framing (per brief, CORR-358).** The load-bearing column is *DESIGN LIMITATION* — what the instrument
**cannot see by construction**. A heterozygous burden test cannot contain a recessive gene; a significance
threshold hides sub-threshold genes; an outcome definition selects on the outcome; an ontology collapsed to
the wrong unit cancels a gene against itself. Every claim of the form "nothing is left" inherits every one
of these.
