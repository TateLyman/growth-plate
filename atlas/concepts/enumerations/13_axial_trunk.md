# DOMAIN 13 — THE AXIAL SKELETON AND TRUNK
## R436 full-concept-space enumeration — external sources only

**Method.** Every row below was reached by external search (Europe PMC REST API, NCBI eutils
efetch, and targeted web/registry lookups). Nothing was derived from the atlas repository.
Species is stated on every claim. Where I could not verify a number I have written `UNVERIFIED`
rather than guess. Reviews are flagged as INDEX, not source.

**Why this domain.** The subject's residual growth is trunk-dominant. Essentially the entire
growth-pharmacology literature uses **femur, tibia, or naso-anal/body length** as its endpoint.
The single most important thing this enumeration can do is separate (a) what is *structurally
different* about an axial growth centre from a limb one, and (b) which agents — in any species —
have ever been given with a **vertebral** endpoint measured.
