# DOMAIN 02 — ENDOCRINE AND CIRCULATING-FACTOR AXES — FULL ENUMERATION (R436)

**Agent:** domain-02 endocrine enumeration. **Date:** 2026-08-15.
**Sources:** Europe PMC REST search (external), NCBI eutils, web search. **No atlas files were read
other than the two briefs.** All PMIDs below were returned by a live Europe PMC query in this session
unless explicitly marked `UNVERIFIED`.

**Reading the DIRECTION column.** "MORE = TALLER" means raising the axis (ligand level, receptor
signalling) is associated with greater linear growth / adult stature in humans; "MORE = SHORTER" the
converse; "BAND" means both directions shorten (an interior optimum). Direction is stated for the
*physiological* range unless noted.

**Reading ACTS AT PLATE?** = is the cognate receptor demonstrated in growth-plate cartilage (chondrocytes
or perichondrium), so that a systemic agent could act locally? YES / NO / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN.

## PROSE 1 — AXES WITH A **TALLER** DIRECTION AND AN **EXISTING OBTAINABLE DRUG**
*(the highest-value output; ranked by strength of the human evidence for the direction × obtainability)*

### TIER A — approved drug, human height/growth endpoint, direction correct

1. **Aromatase (CYP19A1) inhibition — anastrozole, letrozole, exemestane, testolactone.** The strongest
   human natural experiment in the whole domain: aromatase-deficient men have bone age far behind
   chronological age, unfused epiphyses, and keep growing into the third and fourth decades
   (PMID 9211678, 8530621, 20164294, 36504506). All four drugs are approved, oral and cheap. The
   direction is unambiguous: less oestrogen action = later closure. The *human interventional* evidence in
   CAH now includes an adult-height endpoint (PMID 39672600).
2. **Oestrogen receptor removal — fulvestrant, elacestrant, vepdegestrant (first approved PROTAC).**
   Same axis one step downstream. The ESR1-null man (PMID 8090165) is the phenotype these drugs
   partially mimic. Caveat: fulvestrant in 30 growing girls moved bone-age advance from 1.99 to 1.06 and
   left predicted adult height unchanged, because velocity fell with the clock — see PROSE 3.
3. **CNP analogues — vosoritide, navepegritide (both approved).** MORE CNP/NPR2 signalling = taller, and
   the human genetics run both ways: NPR2 gain-of-function causes overgrowth (PMID 22870295, 32282051)
   and NPR3 (the clearance receptor) biallelic loss causes extreme tall stature (PMID 30032985, 35233476).
   Navepegritide's label criterion is *open epiphyses*.
4. **rhGH (somatropin, lonapegsomatropin, somatrogon, somapacitan) and rhIGF-1 (mecasermin).** Correct
   direction, approved, but effect on *attained* height after mid-puberty is small; mecasermin is the
   answer only when the lesion is above IGF-1 (GHR/STAT5B/IGFALS/PAPPA2).
5. **Burosumab (anti-FGF23).** The direction is *lowering an inhibitor*: FGF23 excess in XLH causes
   rickets and disproportionate short stature, and burosumab improves growth (PMID 39787354, 40511857,
   38066669). Approved, paediatric.
6. **Crinecerfont (CRF1 antagonist, approved 2024–25) in CAH.** It does not act at the plate; it lets the
   glucocorticoid dose — the single most growth-suppressive drug class in paediatrics — be reduced
   (PMID 38828945 paediatric NEJM trial). A *subtraction* lever with an approved agent.
7. **Levothyroxine / liothyronine where thyroid tone is low.** T3 drives hypertrophic differentiation;
   restoring adequacy restores growth. Saturating: the endpoint plateaus at adequate replacement, and
   excess advances bone age. Also relevant: rhGH itself lowers free T4.

### TIER B — approved drug, direction plausible, **no human height endpoint**

8. **Sildenafil / tadalafil / vardenafil (PDE5).** The only approved way to raise cGMP other than the CNP
   analogues. Rat long-bone data exist (PMID 29920215) and bone-mass data (PMID 32513693); no human
   height endpoint, and PDE5A expression in human growth plate is doubtful.
9. **Cilostazol / milrinone (PDE3).** cAMP is a band, not an arrow — see PROSE 3.
10. **Resmetirom (approved THRβ agonist) and tiratricol/TRIAC.** A thyromimetic shelf that has never been
    pointed at the growth plate. THRA is the plate's isoform, so a THRβ-selective agonist may be exactly
    the wrong selectivity — but the existence of an *approved oral thyromimetic* was not previously in view.
11. **Metreleptin** where leptin is deficient (PMID 34002033 raises IGF-1 and linear growth; PMID 34718628
    improves growth in Rabson-Mendenhall).
12. **Testosterone / oxandrolone** in constitutional delay — a *timing* intervention, and the only human
    head-to-head favours testosterone.
13. **Sacubitril** (raises natriuretic peptides by blocking neprilysin) — but MME is essentially absent from
    the postnatal human growth plate, so the target is not there.

### TIER C — the direction is right and **the drug does not exist** (construction problems, not discovery)

14. **NPR3 blockade / an NPR3-occupying agonist.** Biallelic human LoF gives extreme tall stature
    (PMID 30032985). No marketed blocker. Endogenous route: **osteocrin/musclin is a natural NPR3 decoy and
    circulating osteocrin stimulates bone growth by limiting CNP clearance (PMID 28990933)** — and it is
    raised by mechanical loading, i.e. a drug-free lever.
15. **STC2 neutralisation** (STC2 inhibits PAPP-A → less free IGF at the plate). No anti-STC2 agent exists.
16. **MC3R agonism.** MC3R LoF humans are shorter with later puberty (PMID 34732894, Nature). No selective
    agonist. Note the direction pairs *taller and earlier puberty*, so it is not a free lunch.
17. **A DIO2 activator / local T3 delivery to cartilage.** None exists.
18. **PAPP-A / PAPP-A2 activity enhancement.** Both loss-of-function states are short (PMID 34272725);
    nobody has an activator.
19. **NPY-Y1 agonism at the plate** (PMID 36592126) — mechanism published, no agent, direction not
    validated on a length endpoint.

**Consequence:** for every one of these, an agent can only work through a systemic set-point change, and
"does the drug reach cartilage" is the wrong question — "does the hormone it controls reach cartilage" is
the right one.

## PROSE 3 — AXES THAT ARE **BANDS** (both directions shorten) — do not treat as arrows
- **cAMP / PTH1R** — Jansen's (constitutive PTH1R activation) is short; iPPSD/GNAS loss is also short.
- **Thyroid hormone** — deficiency arrests growth, excess spends the period.
- **Vitamin A / retinoic acid** — deficiency impairs growth, excess causes premature epiphyseal closure.
- **Oestrogen** — a *low* dose accelerates velocity; a higher dose closes the plate. Removing the receptor
  entirely removes the growth-promoting limb along with the closure signal, which is why fulvestrant in
  growing girls decelerated bone age **and** velocity and netted zero on predicted adult height.
- **RANKL/OPG** — resorption at the chondro-osseous junction is required for discharge; blocking it jams
  the plate (denosumab is a contraindication), and OPG loss is also pathological.
- **Vitamin D** — deficiency = rickets; megadose has no upside and antagonises Smoothened.
- **IGFBP-3** — needed to carry IGF-I, but sequesters it.

## PROSE 4 — **CONTRAINDICATIONS** this domain generates (drugs that LOWER height)
Glucocorticoids by any route · **denosumab** (jams the junction) · **teprotumumab** (anti-IGF1R) ·
**pegvisomant**, octreotide/lanreotide/pasireotide · **isotretinoin and palovarotene** (premature
epiphyseal closure; human height cohort PMID 40818595) · hypervitaminosis A (PMID 31202274) ·
**finasteride/dutasteride** (block the male plate's own 5α-reduction) · **setmelanotide** (MC4R agonism —
the LoF state is the tall one) · **NK3 antagonists (fezolinetant/elinzanetant)** if given before closure ·
FGF21 analogues (efruxifermin, pegozafermin) — FGF21 is a starvation/GH-resistance signal ·
liquorice/glycyrrhetinic acid (inhibits HSD11B2, raising local cortisol) · warfarin in the growing
skeleton (vitamin K antagonism) · high-dose oestrogen.

## PROSE 5 — THE **OBSCURE** ROWS THAT ARE MOST LIKELY TO MATTER
1. **Osteocrin / musclin (OSTN)** — an endogenous NPR3 decoy with a published bone-growth mechanism
   (PMID 28990933) that is raised by mechanical loading and measurable in serum.
2. **MC3R (PMID 34732894)** — a Nature-grade human result linking nutritional state to childhood growth
   and pubertal timing, with essentially no presence in the growth literature.
3. **DIO3 / consumptive hypothyroidism (PMID 24027558, 24693892)** — a tumour-derived enzyme that
   destroys thyroid hormone; the clearest proof that *local* thyroid hormone availability, not serum T4,
   is what the tissue sees.
4. **MC2R / familial glucocorticoid deficiency** — tall as a child, early puberty, adult height *below*
   target (PMID 39496238) — a human demonstration that lowering cortisol is not a free height lever.
5. **STC2** — a well-powered human height gene with zero chemical matter and an exosite-defined
   protein–protein interface (PAPP-A).
6. **Placental GH (GH2)** — a variant in the placental GH gene contributes to *adult* height
   (PMID 24035309); a prenatal window nobody works.
7. **HSD11B1 / cortisone reductase deficiency (PMID 21325058)** — local glucocorticoid amplification as a
   modifiable set point.
8. **STS / oestrone-sulfate reservoir** — a large inactive oestrogen depot with an investigational
   inhibitor (irosustat) and no growth data.
9. **NPY–mTORC1 in chondrocytes (PMID 36592126)** and **PACAP–hedgehog in chondrogenic cells
   (PMID 26230691)** — two neuropeptide axes with direct cartilage mechanisms and no growth literature.
10. **SRD5A1** — the male plate's own androgen-activating enzyme, and the two drugs that block it are among
    the most commonly self-prescribed medicines in young men.
