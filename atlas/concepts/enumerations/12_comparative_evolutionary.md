## MECHANISMS THAT PRODUCE LARGE BODY-SIZE DIFFERENCES AND HAVE A HUMAN ORTHOLOGUE

Ranked by the size of the natural experiment and the directness of the human read-across.

**1. The GH→GHR→JAK2/STAT5→IGF-1 axis, and specifically its SOCS-family brakes.** This is the axis that
nature reaches for again and again, in every domesticated species and in at least two independent human
populations. The *brakes* are the striking part. The mouse **high-growth (hg)** mutation — one of the largest
single-locus body-size effects in a mammal — is a deletion removing **Socs2**, and Socs2-null mice are giant
(19424848; 15690087). Independently, the strongest selection-plus-association signal in **Western African
Pygmies** sits on a chr3 cluster containing **CISH**, SOCS2's sibling and a direct inhibitor of GH-stimulated
STAT5 (22570615). Independently again, chicken **sex-linked dwarfism** is a GHR mutation whose downstream
signature is SOCS2 and CISH (33519913). And **Batwa** short stature maps to regions enriched for
growth-hormone-receptor and GH-regulation genes by a *different*, convergent route from the Baka (25136101).
Four separate natural experiments in three classes of vertebrate converge on the same three genes.
The receptor arm itself carries a hard human number: a **GHR stop codon on Sardinia removes 4.2 cm in
heterozygotes** (26366551), and a **GHR-knockout pig** now exists as a large-animal Laron model (29482569).

**2. LCORL/NCAPG — the most reproducibly selected stature locus in any domestic species.** One ECA3 QTL
explains ~18% of withers-height variance in German Warmblood horses (23418885). The causal-variant work is now
done: an **ACT→A frameshift in LCORL stimulates growth**, and **convergent truncating LCORL mutations were
found in commercial breeds of sheep, goats, pigs, horses, dogs, rabbits and chickens** (40094447). LCORL is a
top human height locus. The convergence is the point: eight independent domestication programmes reached for
the same gene by the same class of lesion.

**3. STC2 — a secreted brake with a validated point mutation and a mouse proof.** STC2 is a derived
size-reducing allele in dogs (24026177) and carries an **A60P missense in cattle sitting in a region conserved
across vertebrates; gene-edited mice homozygous for that natural variant weigh +11%** (40094447). STC2
inhibits the PAPP-A pappalysins that liberate IGF from IGFBPs, so this is a pericellular IGF-availability
lever rather than a hormone-level one. Of everything in this domain, **STC2 has the shortest path from an
animal natural experiment to a validated mammalian gain-of-size result.**

**4. HMGA2.** Derived allele reduces dog size (24026177); a **12.1 kb deletion causes the <1 kg Netherlands
Dwarf rabbit** and is recessive lethal (27986804); and **HMGA2 variants are now an accepted cause of human
Silver-Russell syndrome** (38516887). Three species, one gene, monotone direction.

**5. FBN1 — but allele-specifically, not gene-specifically.** The **Peruvian E1297G missense removes 2.2 cm
per copy and 4.4 cm homozygous**, described as the largest known effect for a common height variant, with
visibly disorganised fibrillin-1 microfibrils in homozygotes' skin (32499652). This runs in the *opposite*
direction to FBN1 loss-of-function. Fibrillin is a dose-and-quality trait, and the comparative data say the
gene has at least two opposing modes.

**6. Imprinted / parent-of-origin size control.** Three independent instances, all with numbers. The
**Sardinian KCNQ1 variant removes 1.83 cm only when maternally inherited** (26366551). The **ovine callipyge
locus** shows polar overdominance — expressed only in paternal heterozygotes — via a dodecamer long-range
control element that raises DLK1/RTL1 in *cis* without changing imprinting status (15498495). The **porcine
IGF2 intron-3 substitution** abolishes a **ZBED6** binding site (ZBED6 being a transcription factor
domesticated from a DNA transposon, present in all placental mammals including humans) and triples muscle IGF2
(20016685). Livestock genetics routinely fits imprinting terms; human height genetics essentially never does.

**7. Growth-plate structural genes as breed-defining alleles.** **ACAN** in miniature horses and miniature
zebu (32943661; 30305023); **PRKG2** splice-donor loss giving disproportionate dwarfism in Dogo Argentino dogs
with reduced endochondral ossification and premature physeal closure (34680883) — PRKG2 being the cGMP-
dependent kinase downstream of the CNP receptor; **SHOX** deletions in Shetland ponies causing ulnar and
fibular *overgrowth* (27207956); an **FGF4 retrogene** producing breed-defining chondrodystrophy (19608863).
Every one of these is a human skeletal-dysplasia gene reached from the other end.

**8. SHOX2 and the modular control of individual bone length.** Jerboa metatarsals elongate because **SHOX2
has gained expression there**, and **forced SHOX2 was sufficient to increase mouse distal limb length**
(34793695). The same paper reports that jerboa foot elongation occurs partly by **de-repressing latent growth
potential** — i.e. the capacity was already present and held down. That is the most encouraging single
sentence in this domain.

**9. FGFRL1 and giraffe stature.** Seven giraffe-unique amino-acid substitutions in FGFRL1, with gene-edited
mice showing higher bone mineral density (33731352). FGFRL1 is a decoy FGF receptor — the same pathway as
FGFR3, entered at a different node.

**10. TMEM263.** Chicken dwarfism gene → mouse null gives **proportional dwarfism with low circulating IGF-1
and reduced growth-plate length** → human BMD GWAS signal and at least one human fetal skeletal dysplasia
(38241182). A complete cross-species chain that almost nobody in human growth medicine has heard of.

**Also-rans worth recording as negatives.** Myostatin is the most famous animal size gene and it is a
**muscle** gene: Belgian Blues are not taller, human MSTN loss-of-function carriers gain >10% muscle with no
reported stature gain (41826329), and catfish *mstnb* knockouts are actually **shorter**, with fewer and
shorter vertebrae (37887041). β-adrenergic agonists likewise move muscle, not bone length.

## WHAT KEEPS AN INDETERMINATE GROWER GROWING, MECHANISTICALLY

**The one-line answer found in this search: it is the epiphysis, not the hormone.**

Teleost fish keep **entirely cartilaginous epiphyses for life**, and this supplies what is described as a
virtually unlimited chondro-progenitor pool constituting the whole epiphysis. The **secondary ossification
centre — an adaptation to terrestrial life — is built AT THE EXPENSE of those chondro-progenitors, and is
therefore stated to limit the growth potential of all terrestrial vertebrates** (31830758). In amniotes the
mechanism of chondrocyte generation *switches* at SOC maturation: before it, growth consumes chondro-
progenitors; after it, growth runs off a self-renewing stem-cell niche. So the human pool ceiling is not an
accident of endocrinology; it is the structural cost of having an epiphysis.

Why the SOC was worth that cost is separately answered: **SOC appears in amniotes, correlates with mechanical
load across whales, bats and jerboa, and mathematically reduces mechanical stress inside the growth plate.
Hypertrophic chondrocytes are the least stiff cells in the plate and die under load; the SOC protects them**
(33063669). The trade is load-protection bought with progenitor pool.

Four further mechanistic components, each supported:

1. **A permanently cartilaginous, perichondrium-fed growth zone.** Cartilage elongates by interstitial
   chondrocyte division, while **perichondral cells differentiate into chondrocytes and add circumferential
   matrix** — so the perichondrium is a continuous *external* source of new chondrocytes that a mammalian
   epiphysis largely walls off. Deer antler is the mammalian proof that this route still works: **~2 cm/day
   in an adult, from PEDICLE PERIOSTEUM-derived reserve mesenchyme, with periosteal cells self-renewing to
   ~80 population doublings without senescence** (37864097; 34663443).
2. **Rate, not arrest, is what changes with age.** Most teleosts slow with age (von Bertalanffy) without ever
   arresting. Indeterminate growth is therefore a *decelerating* curve with no terminus, not a constant rate.
3. **Reproduction, not time, sets the arrest point in the taxa that do arrest.** In the gecko *Paroedura
   picta* growth plates fuse **sex-specifically**, and the more abrupt closure and steeper deceleration in
   females coincides with increased ovarian activity. The authors conclude the endotherm/ectotherm difference
   is in the **magnitude of growth before versus after first reproduction, not in the mechanism** — growth-
   plate senescence itself is shared (35345458). Ovarian-hormone-driven epiphyseal closure is not a mammalian
   invention.
4. **Continuous versus cyclical is a schedule variable independent of rate.** Basal sauropodomorphs grew
   cyclically (seasonal arrest lines); derived Eusauropoda grew rapidly and **uninterrupted** (28654696).
   Some basal taxa already had uninterrupted growth, so the two are dissociable.

**Two important corrections to the folk version.**
- **"Ectotherms grow forever" is false.** A 164-species µCT survey found **complete growth-plate-cartilage
  resorption to be the predominant finding in lizards**, and reconstructs determinate growth as *ancestral*
  for squamates, with extended growth evolving repeatedly (33352069). This directly contradicts the widely
  cited claim that indeterminate growth is the ancestral vertebrate condition (26216720). **The two papers are
  unreconciled in the literature and I am not resolving them here** — the honest state is that determinate
  growth is far more common among amniotes than the textbook says.
- **"Big long-lived mammals keep growing" is false for the obvious test case.** Longitudinal data on
  semi-captive Asian elephants show **no indeterminate growth: height is essentially complete by ~15 y in
  females and ~21 y in males**, while males keep gaining *weight* (26464339). Continued weight gain is not
  continued skeletal growth, and the two are routinely conflated.

**What *does* extend growth in mammals is the SCHEDULE.** In cetaceans, some epiphyses fuse long after sexual
maturity. In primates, fusion runs as a regular multi-year sequence joint by joint whose *reason* has never
been explained (29265660). Rodents and elephants are cited as mammals with limited or delayed fusion despite a
small chondro-progenitor pool. The lever that comparative biology actually demonstrates is **delaying or
desynchronising fusion**, not raising rate.

## WHAT LIVESTOCK SCIENCE DOES TO INCREASE SKELETAL SIZE THAT HUMAN MEDICINE HAS NEVER TRIED

Six things, ordered by how transferable they look. **None of this is a recommendation; several carry costs
that would disqualify them outright.**

**1. Anabolic implants that deliberately increase FRAME SIZE — and the paradox they expose.** Combined
trenbolone acetate + estradiol implants raise circulating IGF-1 and IGFBPs, raise local muscle IGF-1, and
**increase frame size so much that implanted cattle must be fed to a heavier endpoint to reach the same
fatness** (review DOI 10.1080/09712119.2020.1751642; 39760134). The paradox is direct: **livestock science
implants oestrogen to make the frame bigger, while human paediatric endocrinology gives aromatase inhibitors
to remove oestrogen and keep the window open.** The reconciliation is in the same literature — **skeletal
maturity advances progressively with each additional implant** — so implants buy frame partly by spending
schedule, which is exactly the trade human practice refuses. This is the clearest worked example anywhere of
oestrogen's biphasic effect being *managed commercially* rather than avoided.

**2. Photoperiod.** Sixteen-hour light in prepubertal Holstein heifers raises **withers height**, body weight
and heart girth, raises prolactin and circulating IGF-1, hastens puberty **and does not limit body growth**
(16291627). This is a non-drug, non-nutritional, reversible environmental input acting on a linear skeletal
dimension. I found no human trial of photoperiod manipulation for stature in any age group. Prolactin and
melatonin biology exist in humans; nobody has run the experiment.

**3. Supraphysiological exogenous somatotropin in a genetically normal animal.** Livestock use of pST and
rBST operates at doses and durations that human GH practice does not approach, and the GH-transgenic mouse and
GH-transgenic salmon are the extreme versions. Two comparative cautions come with it: GH-transgenic mice
develop progressive glomerulosclerosis whereas IGF-1 transgenics do not (3132856), and GH-transgenic salmon
**"pay for growth potential with increased predation mortality"** (15504015). Also, **rBST is given to mature
lactating cows — it is a nutrient-partitioning intervention, not a stature one**, and should not be cited as
evidence that more GH makes a bigger skeleton.

**4. Micronutrient status as an explicit GATE on an anabolic intervention.** Livestock nutritionists ask
whether **zinc** status limits implant-induced growth and whether **copper** status limits β-agonist response
(34199133; 41706733). Human growth medicine measures vitamin D and rarely anything else as a modifier of drug
response. The framing — *the anabolic agent is only as good as the substrate/cofactor supply* — is standard
there and absent here.

**5. Modelling imprinting and dominance as usable variance.** Livestock evaluation fits parent-of-origin and
heterotic (dominance) terms as a matter of routine (25964798; 30290764). Human height genetics is almost
entirely additive, yet human height demonstrably carries **directional dominance worth ~1.2–3 cm at
first-cousin levels of homozygosity** (26131930; 22829771), confirmed within full-sibling pairs (31673082),
and at least one measured parent-of-origin height effect (KCNQ1, 26366551). Nobody has asked what a
*non-additive* human height architecture would suggest as a lever.

**6. Gene editing at validated large-effect natural variants.** The LCORL frameshift and the STC2 A60P
missense were explicitly nominated and patented as **safe gene-editing targets for animal molecular design
breeding**, and both were validated in gene-edited mice at +11% weight (40094447). Human medicine has never
had a comparable list of *naturally occurring, population-validated, large-effect* size variants proposed as
targets — and the useful transfer is not the editing, it is that **the animal work has already told us which
nodes tolerate a large gain-of-size lesion without a syndrome.**

**Two things livestock does that do NOT transfer, recorded so they are not proposed.** β-adrenergic agonists
and myostatin lesions move muscle, not skeletal length. Sub-therapeutic antibiotic growth promotion has no
skeletal-length endpoint in the modern literature I could retrieve, its mechanism is still debated, and the
class is being withdrawn.
