# DOMAIN 07 — MATRIX, MINERALISATION AND CELL VOLUME
## R436 full-concept-space enumeration (external search only; nothing derived from the atlas)

**Method.** Every row below was reached by external query — Europe PMC REST (`/search`, resultType=core),
NCBI eutils, and web search of registries/regulatory pages. No file in the growth-plate repository was read
other than the two briefs. Reviews are used as an INDEX and flagged. Where I could not verify a number,
gene, PMID or direction I have written **UNVERIFIED** rather than guessing.

**Column conventions.**
- *EFFECT ON LENGTH IF RAISED/LOWERED* — states the direction with the perturbation that produced it.
  "LOSS → shorter" is the overwhelmingly common direction; "LOSS → LONGER" rows are the prize and are flagged ⭐.
- *AGENT EXISTS?* — approved / clinical-stage / tool compound / none. A tool compound is not a drug.
- *OBSCURE?* — yes = rarely discussed in the mainstream longitudinal-growth literature.

| L9 | **Citrate (ANKH-exported)** | ~1.6% of bone mineral by weight; a hydration/apatite-platelet stabiliser | ANKH exports citrate as well as ATP — so citrate handling sits inside a known dysplasia gene | mechanism review, PMID 42438770 (INDEX) | none | **yes** |
| L10 | **Vascular invasion of the last lacuna (VEGFA/MMP9/chondroclast)** | the terminal event that converts a hypertrophic lacuna into bone; blocking it thickens the plate WITHOUT lengthening the bone | this is the classic charge-without-discharge trap of the whole domain | mouse; MMP9 classical, PMID UNVERIFIED | anti-VEGF agents (approved oncology) — a CONTRAINDICATION direction | no |

| J6 | ⭐⭐ **PERIOSTEAL RESECTION (releasing the mechanical restraint)** | the periosteum/perichondrial ring is a longitudinal TETHER on the plate | ⭐ **Periosteal resection INCREASED tibial growth velocity in EVERY lamb at EVERY time point, consistently and sustainedly — and the cellular basis was HYPERTROPHIC CHONDROCYTE AXIAL ELONGATION, not proliferation, not the MAGNITUDE of swelling, not matrix production** | lamb, real-time microtransducer + fluorochrome + stereology, PMID 19098649 | **surgical, already performed in humans**; authors propose it as an adjunct, incl. to counteract growth inhibition during distraction osteogenesis | **yes — the single cleanest demonstration that a MATRIX/MECHANICAL restraint caps h_term's AXIAL dimension** |
| J7 | **Growth-plate mechanobiology (Hueter-Volkmann / Stokes stress-growth coefficient)** | sustained compression suppresses and tension permits elongation; the matrix transmits the stress | direction: compression → shorter | survey, PMID 19540500 (INDEX) | mechanical/orthotic | no |
| J8 | **Hydrostatic pressure on growth-plate explants** | physiological vs injurious hydrostatic pressure change growth-plate transcriptome differently from articular cartilage | mechanism; no length endpoint | mouse ex vivo RNAseq, PMID 38144567 | none | **yes** |

## PROSE 1 — ANY MATRIX OR VOLUME NODE WITH AN AGENT POINTING IN THE LENGTHENING DIRECTION

Honest summary first: **the overwhelming majority of agents that touch this domain point at SHORTENING**,
because every one of them was built for a different indication — fibrosis, osteoarthritis, oncology, oedema,
osteoporosis. The domain is full of inhibitors of things we would want more of, and enzyme-replacement
therapies for deficiency states. Sorted by how close each really is:

**(a) APPROVED, but RESTORATION of a deficit (CORR-203-type caveat applies — these only lengthen a child
who is deficient, and say nothing about elevation above normal):**
- **Asfotase alfa** (ALPL/TNAP enzyme replacement, hypophosphatasia) — PMID 42297044, 42441318.
- **Burosumab** (anti-FGF23, XLH) — and the paediatric data are dose/timing-specific: starting **between
  1 and 4 years prevented further height deficit**, whereas starting above 4 years afforded only minimal
  growth improvement (PMID 41026619). That is an age-window result, not a general growth agent.
- **The MPS enzyme replacements** (elosulfase alfa for GALNS/Morquio A, galsulfase for ARSB/MPS VI, etc.).
- **Oral phosphate / calcitriol** for SLC34A1/SLC34A3 and CYP27B1 rickets (PMID 39461557, 41543768).
- **Oral N-acetylneuraminic acid** in NANS-CDG — an open-label pilot only (PMID 37340906).

**(b) CLINICAL-STAGE, correct-direction-on-paper, never tested on bone length:**
- ⭐ **TRPV4 antagonists.** This is the strongest item in the category. A **small-molecule TRPV4 inhibitor
  rescued the skeletal dysplasia phenotype of a conditional Trpv4 p.R594H knock-in mouse** (PMID 41574606,
  2026). TRPV4 antagonists have been taken into human trials for other indications. It is a *rescue of a
  gain-of-function*, so it does not establish that inhibiting wild-type TRPV4 lengthens anything — but it is
  the only case in this whole domain where a channel that sets chondrocyte volume/osmosensation has a real
  molecule with a demonstrated skeletal-length-relevant rescue.
- **ADAMTS-5 inhibitor GLPG1972 / S201086** — potent, selective, **orally bioavailable**, phase 2 completed
  in knee OA (ROCCELLA, PMID 37059327; PK/PD PMID 34859612; medicinal chemistry PMID 33719441). This is the
  only way anyone has found to raise aggrecan pharmacologically. ⚠ It raises aggrecan by *blocking
  catabolism*, which risks jamming the discharge step at the chondro-osseous junction.
- **PXS-5505 / lenumlostat / GB2064** — first-in-class oral pan-lysyl-oxidase inhibitor; phase I/IIa in
  myelofibrosis (PMID 40241543) and MYLOX-1 phase 2 (PMID 41784032). Direction ambiguous: BAPN in cartilage
  explants **expanded tissue** while reducing tensile integrity (PMID 17631390), so less cross-linking may
  permit more matrix expansion — but lathyrism is a skeletal disease.
- **Roneparstat / PG545 (heparanase inhibitors)**, **STNM01 (CHST15 siRNA)** — clinical-stage agents on
  matrix nodes with no skeletal endpoint in any species.

**(c) SURGICAL / MECHANICAL, and this is the one with a real positive length result:**
- ⭐⭐ **PERIOSTEAL RESECTION.** In lambs it increased tibial growth velocity **in every animal at every time
  point**, and the cellular mechanism was **axial elongation of the hypertrophic chondrocyte** (PMID 19098649).
  The authors explicitly propose it as an adjunct, including to counteract the growth inhibition of
  distraction osteogenesis. This is the only intervention in the entire matrix/volume domain with a
  demonstrated positive, mechanistically-localised effect on h_term in a normal large animal.

**(d) NUTRIENT / SUBSTRATE, i.e. things you supply rather than block (the shelf that is not empty):**
- **Sulfate donors** (oral sodium sulfate, MSM) for SLC26A2 → PAPSS2 → CHST3 flux. Human disease genetics
  are unambiguous that low sulfate shortens (G1–G3). **No sulfate donor has a bone-length endpoint in any
  species that I could verify.**
- **Zinc** — SLC39A13/ZIP13 loss produces a short-stature EDS by impairing collagen hydroxylation
  (PMID 18513683, 32295219, 36727144). Zinc status is therefore a free, measurable variable upstream of
  collagen chemistry, and this is essentially never discussed in growth-plate work.
- **Ascorbate** — required for prolyl-4-hydroxylase; adequacy only, and megadosing runs the wrong way on
  2-OG dioxygenases generally.
- **Magnesium, manganese** — cofactors for Golgi glycosyltransferases and inhibitors of apatite nucleation.
- **High-molecular-weight hyaluronan** (approved intra-articular) — direction plausible, wholly untested on
  the plate, and it cannot reach an intraosseous physis by joint injection.

**(e) AGENTS THAT ARE ALREADY-IDENTIFIED CONTRAINDICATION DIRECTIONS on this domain:**
loop diuretics (bumetanide/furosemide → NKCC1, PMID 20200963); amiloride/EIPA and DIDS (NHE1/AE2,
PMID 23059814); denosumab (RANKL, jams discharge); odanacatib-class cathepsin K inhibitors (pycnodysostosis
is short); acetazolamide / topiramate / zonisamide (carbonic anhydrase); warfarin (Gla carboxylation →
chondrodysplasia punctata phenocopy); anti-VEGF (blocks the terminal vascular event); hyaluronidase
(dissolves the osmotic engine); 4-methylumbelliferone (blocks HA synthesis); **proton-pump inhibitors** if
the PHOSPHO1 hypothesis is right (PMID 41415870); aldose-reductase inhibitors (epalrestat) which lower the
sorbitol osmolyte.

## PROSE 2 — COMPONENTS WHOSE HUMAN LOSS LENGTHENS RATHER THAN SHORTENS

This is the rarest class in the domain and I looked for it deliberately. Human loss-of-function in a matrix
gene shortens far more often than it lengthens — the base rate is heavily skewed, because ascertainment is
through clinics that see short children. The genuine exceptions I could verify:

1. ⭐⭐ **GPC3 (glypican-3) — the clearest case.** Hemizygous loss-of-function causes **Simpson-Golabi-Behmel
   syndrome: pre- and postnatal OVERGROWTH with tall stature and advanced bone age** (PMID 8589713 original;
   mutation update PMID 29637653; adult case mimicking acromegaly PMID 42220602). GPC3 is a genuine
   cell-surface heparan-sulfate proteoglycan of the pericellular matrix, and the reciprocal experiment exists
   in humans too — **duplications of GPC3/GPC4 cause UNDERgrowth** (PMID 31583675, 30048822). One gene, two
   directions, dose-responsive, in humans. Anti-GPC3 biologics exist (T-cell engagers, nanobodies —
   PMID 41041827) but are built for hepatocellular carcinoma.

2. ⭐ **FBN1 haploinsufficiency** — Marfan syndrome: long bones, dolichostenomelia, tall stature. The gene is
   allele-directional: specific TB5-domain missense alleles instead cause **acromicric/geleophysic dysplasia,
   which is short**. So "FBN1 loss lengthens" is only true of the haploinsufficient allele class.

3. ⭐ **FBN2** — heterozygous central-region variants cause **congenital contractural arachnodactyly** with a
   tall, slender habitus and dolichostenomelia (PMID 42299845, 39911746). Variants outside that central
   region behave differently (PMID 40406865), so again this is allele-specific.

4. ⭐ **SKI** — Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome, marfanoid habitus with arachnodactyly (PMID 40562530, 39638120).
   SKI is a SMAD corepressor rather than a matrix protein, but it sits in the same fibrillin–LTBP–TGF-β
   module and the phenotype is a matrix-availability phenotype.

5. ⭐ **TGFB3** — one reported de novo variant in the latency-associated peptide domain produced **overgrowth**
   with Loeys-Dietz features (PMID 24798638, with a published response PMID 24817670); most TGFB3 variants
   give a Marfan/LDS-overlap phenotype with variable stature (PMID 31898322, 39653386). Direction is
   **contested** and I am recording it as such.

6. ⭐ **PLOD1** (lysyl hydroxylase 1) — kyphoscoliotic EDS type 1 has a **marfanoid** habitus, so the
   heterozygous/hypomorphic direction of this collagen-modifying enzyme is not simply shortening. Contrast
   PLOD2 (Bruck), which is unambiguously short. **The two paralogues are not the same lever.**

7. ⭐ **CBS deficiency (classical homocystinuria)** — not a matrix gene but a matrix *chemistry*: excess
   homocysteine disrupts fibrillin/collagen cross-linking and produces a **marfanoid, tall** habitus
   (PMID 42255525, 42519297). It is the closest thing to a "pharmacological lathyrism that lengthens" that
   exists in humans, and it is a warning as much as a lead (thrombosis, aortic dissection).

**Mouse/animal loss-that-widens-or-lengthens, recorded because they are the same class:**
- ⭐ **CHAD (chondroadherin)** — null mice have a **WIDENED epiphyseal growth plate, most pronounced in the
  PROLIFERATIVE zone** (PMID 23755099). A matrix→α2β1 anchor whose removal enlarges the plate.
- ⭐ **FXYD2** — *reduced* expression associates with *increased* tibia length in the Longshanks selection
  line (PMID 34970538).
- **MPS IX (HYAL1 deficiency)** is the informative near-null on the other side: losing the main hyaluronidase
  is remarkably mild skeletally, which bounds how much HA turnover matters.

**A structural observation about the whole list:** every human "loss lengthens" entry is in the
**fibrillin–LTBP–TGF-β availability module or the cell-surface HSPG module**, i.e. in proteins that
SEQUESTER or PRESENT a morphogen — not in load-bearing structural proteins. Losing a structural matrix
protein shortens; losing a morphogen-restraining matrix protein lengthens.

## PROSE 3 — WHAT SETS TERMINAL CHONDROCYTE VOLUME

**The quantity itself.** Mean terminal hypertrophic chondrocyte volume is linearly related to elongation rate
across growth plates, ages and species — established in rabbits across five ages and four plates
(PMID 8893762) and tested against birds (PMID 8146454, where the mammalian relationship did **not** hold in
the same form — a species caveat that is usually dropped).

**The best current model — three phases (Cooper 2013, PMID 23485973, mouse, quantitative phase microscopy):**
1. **Phase 1** — proportional growth (dry mass and volume rise together).
2. **Phase 2** — **massive true SWELLING, in which cellular dry mass is significantly DILUTED.** The cell
   takes on water faster than it makes macromolecules.
3. **Phase 3** — **proportional dry-mass increase at LOW density.** ⭐ **It is the DURATION of phase 3 that
   varies most between rapidly and slowly elongating plates, and phase 3 is regulated LOCALLY by an
   IGF-dependent mechanism.** If one wanted to raise h_term, phase 3 duration is the parameter the data
   themselves nominate — and almost nothing in the literature works on it directly.

**The contested half (Bush/Hall, PMID 17786946, rat, living tissue, 2-photon).** Boyle–van't Hoff analysis of
in-situ chondrocytes across ~0–580 mOsm found the **osmotically INACTIVE fraction 39.5 ± 2.9 % (proliferative)
vs 47.0 ± 4.3 % (hypertrophic), not significantly different**, and osmotic **sensitivity identical**
(15.5 %volume·Osm in both zones). The authors concluded that true hypertrophy contributes more than swelling
and that the swelling assumption should be re-evaluated. Earlier EM morphometry agreed (PMID 3944163).
⭐ **The two datasets are reconcilable if the swollen phase is driven by SMALL OSMOLYTES rather than protein:**
small solutes are heavy on osmotic pressure and negligible on dry mass, which would dilute dry mass (Cooper)
while leaving the Boyle–van't Hoff inactive fraction nearly unchanged (Bush). **I could not find this
reconciliation published anywhere — it is my inference and is marked UNVERIFIED.** The intracellular
small-solute content of a hypertrophic chondrocyte, by zone, appears never to have been measured in any
species.

**The transport machinery, with the only direct length endpoints in the domain.**
- **NKCC1 / SLC12A2** — bumetanide inhibition reduces bone length in cultured rat metatarsals; NKCC1 is
  immunolocalised along the plate; siRNA knockdown abolishes regulatory volume increase (PMID 20200963,
  21847667). This is the single best-evidenced volume input.
- **NHE1 (SLC9A1) and AE2 (SLC4A2)** — EIPA and DIDS each cut rudiment growth ~50 % and shrank the
  hypertrophic zone; EIPA additionally lowered in-situ cell volume in both PZ and HZ. ⭐ The spatial pattern is
  the useful part: **NHE1 is high and flat along the plate and falls steeply in LATE hypertrophic zone, while
  AE2 is low in PZ, PEAKS in EARLY HZ, then falls — suggesting AE2 gates the proliferative→hypertrophic
  TRANSITION** rather than the terminal volume (PMID 23059814, 35877910).
- **SWELL1 / LRRC8A (VRAC)** — Col10a1-Cre deletion (i.e. hypertrophic-cell-restricted) disorganises
  hypertrophic chondrocytes in the condyle (PMID 34968783); this is the efflux arm.
- **Na/K-ATPase and its FXYD2 subunit** — the pump that powers everything above; lower Fxyd2 tracks with
  longer tibiae (PMID 34970538), and pharmacological NKA inhibition disrupts chondrocyte differentiation.
- **TRPV4** — activating mutations SHORTEN (metatropic dysplasia, SMD Kozlowski), and inhibition rescues the
  mouse (PMID 41574606). The osmosensor is therefore not a simple "more Ca²⁺ = more volume" node.
- **Aquaporins** set the RATE not the extent, and human AQP1-null individuals are healthy — so water
  permeability is not rate-limiting.

**The extracellular half — and it is a measurement hole.** The osmotic pressure that inflates both cell and
matrix ultimately comes from the **fixed charge density** of aggrecan's sulfated GAG chains. Every step that
sets that charge is enumerated above (G1–G28): sulfate supply (SLC13A1, SLC26A2), activation (PAPSS1/2),
Golgi delivery (SLC35B2/3 — for which Europe PMC returned **zero** titles), the phosphatase that prevents
product inhibition (IMPAD1), the linker (XYLT1, B4GALT7, B3GALT6, B3GAT3), the polymerases that set chain
LENGTH (CHSY1, CSGALNACT1/2), the sulfotransferases that set charge per disaccharide (CHST3/11/14, UST,
CHST15) and — newly, in 2026 — a **Golgi DESULFATASE, ARSL, that edits 4-O-sulfate back off during
biosynthesis** (PMID 42103217). ⭐ **A Europe PMC query for "growth plate" + "fixed charge density" returns
ZERO hits.** The parameter that physically explains ~half of h_term has been quantified in articular
cartilage and essentially never in a physis.

**The mechanical ceiling.** ⭐ Even with all the above intact, the terminal cell's AXIAL dimension is capped
by an external tether: **periosteal resection in lambs raised growth velocity in every animal at every time
point, and the mechanism was axial elongation of the hypertrophic chondrocyte specifically — not
proliferation, not the magnitude of swelling, not matrix production** (PMID 19098649). Read with the
directional-swelling observation in Bush 2008 (hypotonic swelling produced different dimensions from the
preferential lengthening seen in vivo), this says that **the cell's volume and the DIRECTION into which that
volume is expressed are separately controlled, and the direction is set by the matrix and the periosteum.**
Longitudinal collagen septa (J2) are the presumed intracellular-scale version of the same constraint and have
never been manipulated.

**Finally, a methodological caution that undercuts much of the older literature:** common fixatives are
hypertonic and shrink growth-plate chondrocytes, so any historical "hypertrophic cell volume" derived from
fixed tissue without a stated fixative osmolarity is suspect (PMID 20473830).
