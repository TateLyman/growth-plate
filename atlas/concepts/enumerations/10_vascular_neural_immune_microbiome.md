## R436 full-concept-space enumeration · external search only

**Method.** Every row was found by external query — Europe PMC REST `search` (title-field and
free-text, relevance- and citation-sorted), NCBI eutils `efetch` for abstracts, and web search for
regulatory material. Nothing was taken from the local atlas. **Every PMID below was returned by a
live query in this session**; where I quote a number or a direction it came from the title or the
abstract I actually retrieved. Anything I could not confirm is written `UNVERIFIED`.
Species is stated in every evidence cell. Reviews are labelled as reviews and used only as an index.

**Legend — EFFECT ON LENGTH:** `+` lengthens/permits · `−` shortens/suppresses · `0` measured null ·
`?` direction not established · `LOCAL+` local overgrowth without a systemic height gain ·
`REQ` demonstrably required (removal shortens; more is not shown to lengthen).

## PROSE 1 — INPUTS THAT REACH THE PLATE FROM OUTSIDE THE CHONDROCYTE AND HAVE A LENGTH ENDPOINT

Sorted by how directly the evidence attaches to a **length or stature** measurement, not to a proxy.

**Tier 1 — a non-chondrocyte input, a normal (non-rescue) animal or an unselected human population, and a
length/height number.**

1. **Endothelium + non-resorbing osteoclasts.** `PMID 30936475` puts bone elongation itself under the
   control of endothelial proteolytic activity and its interaction with osteoclasts. This is the single
   most direct statement in the retrieved literature that a *vascular* cell type sets long-bone length.
2. **Septoclasts.** `PMID 7730591` describes the cell; `PMID 19893052` shows that its deficiency
   produces a progressive growth-plate chondrodysplasia, and — importantly — that restoring osteoclasts
   with CSF-1 does **not** fix it. The vascular-invasion machinery has at least two dissociable arms.
3. **Soluble epoxide hydrolase inhibition (TPPU).** `PMID 42297360` claims promotion of **long bone
   growth** through H-type-vessel-coupled osteogenesis and septoclast activity. sEH inhibitors have been
   through phase 1 in humans (EC5026, GSK2256294) — so this is the rare case of a vascular-directed
   mechanism with an obtainable molecule. No bone endpoint exists in any human study (UNVERIFIED).
4. **Exercise → solute delivery → limb length.** `PMID 20930127`: runners had significantly longer limbs
   at both housing temperatures, with the mechanism measured as increased tracer entry into the plate.
   This is a delivery intervention with a length readout in a normal animal.
5. **Macrophages.** `PMID 39869647` / `PMID 37059596`: Csf1r-null rats have severely compromised
   **pre-weaning somatic growth**, fully reversed by wild-type bone-marrow transfer at weaning;
   `PMID 38526212` attributes the skeletal part specifically to osteal macrophages **in addition to**
   osteoclasts. `PMID 37167456` shows the embryo is spared — the requirement is postnatal.
6. **IL-1β + TNF-α.** `PMID 15476580` is the cleanest cytokine result: synergistic inhibition of
   **longitudinal growth** of cultured fetal rat metatarsals, partially reversed by IGF-I.
7. **TNF.** `PMID 36307458`: huTNFTg mice have significantly **decreased femur length** and growth-plate
   height, with suppressed Indian hedgehog and disorganised columns.
8. **gp130/STAT3.** `PMID 35039652`: chondrocyte-specific deletion causes **growth-plate fusion and
   stunting**. This makes the IL-6 family a *band*: too much IL-6 stunts (E2–E4), too little gp130
   signalling fuses the plate.
9. **Imatinib in children.** `PMID 38497150` (−0.76 SDS at 24 months, n=94) and `PMID 42223406` (reduced
   **final** height with prepubertal initiation). A drug acting on a pericyte/vascular receptor family,
   with human final-height data.
10. **Bacterial NOD2 ligands.** `PMID 36821686`: muramyl dipeptide and **mifamurtide** each sufficient to
    promote postnatal growth in malnourished mice via intestinal-epithelial NOD2 → IGF-1.
11. **MDCF-2.** `PMID 38833839`: a microbiota-directed food improving **linear** growth in a human RCT
    follow-up.
12. **Tocilizumab, canakinumab, infliximab.** `PMID 25504861`, `PMID 32727940`, `PMID 24237381`: three
    approved biologics with documented catch-up growth in children — the strongest human demonstration
    that removing a cytokine restores height velocity.

**Tier 2 — length measured, but in a deficit/rescue setting or with an internally inconsistent literature.**
Anakinra in NOMID (`PMID 20230645` positive for femoral/tibial growth vs `PMID 21240490` negative for
bone abnormalities); fracture-induced limb overgrowth (`PMID 31567782`); denervation (`PMID 3346314`
segment-specific and small; `PMID 970065` retarded with reduced circulation); OPG-null growth-plate
destruction (`PMID 14756239`); perlecan-null "wider but shorter" plate (`PMID 22421594`).

**Tier 3 — an input demonstrably reaching the plate, with NO length endpoint.** Type H vessels
(`PMID 24646994`); PDGF-BB (`PMID 25282358`); endothelial Notch (`PMID 24647000`); complement at the
ossification centre (`PMID 8831558`, `PMID 9134868`); CGRP fibres apposed to osteoclasts facing the plate
(`PMID 8717534`); melatonin MT2 on growth-plate chondrocytes (`PMID 25257530`); bone lymphatics
(`PMID 40970910`).

## PROSE 2 — WHAT IS KNOWN ABOUT SOLUTE AND OXYGEN DELIVERY TO THE HUMAN GROWTH PLATE

**The honest headline: almost nothing is known in *humans*. Every quantitative measurement below is
mouse, rat, rabbit, dog, pig, horse or chicken.** I found no study that measured oxygen tension, tracer
entry, perfusion or diffusion distance in a living human growth plate.

**What is established (animal):**

- **Three routes, not one.** Epiphyseal, metaphyseal, and a perichondrial ring vessel/plexus
  (`PMID 16342207`). This matters because it means "the plate is avascular" is a statement about the
  cartilage, not about its supply, and that three anatomically separable interventions exist.
- **A hard molecular-weight ceiling.** In `PMID 16342207`: fluorescein (332 Da) and dextrans up to
  10 kDa entered from all three directions, with metaphyseal and epiphyseal entry roughly equally
  permissive; **40 kDa and larger did not enter at all**, within a detection limit of a few percent of
  vascular concentration. Any biologic larger than ~40 kDa has no demonstrated route in.
- **A permissive mid-plate band and a pressure-driven flow.** `PMID 17496046` found a relatively
  permissive region at the plate midplane, where late-proliferative cells transition to early
  hypertrophic and where chondrocyte–perichondrium paracrine communication occurs; and showed that
  in-vivo transport is significantly affected by **fluid flow from the two chondro-osseous junctions**,
  presumably from a pressure difference between bone vasculature and cartilage. Delivery is therefore
  partly convective, not purely diffusive.
- **Delivery is modifiable by physiology, not only by chemistry.** Limb temperature changes tracer entry
  with core temperature and respiration held constant (`PMID 19372302`); chronic exercise changes it and
  changes **limb length** (`PMID 20930127`); IGF-I itself has been imaged entering the plate
  (`PMID 28798204`).
- **Oxygen has been measured by zone — once, in 1971.** `PMID 5580029` (Brighton & Heppenstall) reports
  oxygen tension in zones of the epiphyseal plate, metaphysis and diaphysis in rats and rabbits, in vitro
  and in vivo. The companion paper `PMID 5133323` measured plate oxygen tension **distal to an
  arteriovenous fistula** — i.e. the only experiment I found that perturbs limb perfusion and reads out
  plate pO₂. Neither abstract is deposited in PubMed, so the numeric values are **UNVERIFIED** here.
- **The plate behaves as if hypoxic, and that is functional.** HIF-1α is essential for chondrocyte
  survival in the plate interior (`PMID 11691837`); HIF-2α transactivates COL10A1/MMP13/VEGFA
  (`PMID 20495570`); oxygen tension sets VEGF isoform choice (`PMID 15135139`) and ANK/pyrophosphate
  handling (`PMID 19419319`); and proteoglycan **synthesis** peaks at 21% O₂ while **aggregation** peaks
  at 3% (`PMID 2045974`) — so oxygen does not have one direction of effect on matrix.
- **Marrow oxygen is now measurable and is dynamic.** `PMID 41218120`: marrow tissue pO₂ ~25 mmHg (~4%
  O₂) at baseline, rising >55 mmHg (~8%) after fracture — and the rise comes from **erythroid precursor
  expansion**, not angiogenesis. That is a haematopoietic control point over the oxygen environment
  adjacent to the plate that nobody has connected to growth.
- **Perfusion can be abolished mechanically.** Forced abduction/internal rotation obliterated femoral-head
  circulation in growing dogs (`PMID 359564`, `PMID 6490704`).
- **Venous outflow obstruction alone does nothing.** `PMID 1146509` — a clean null in rabbits against
  older dog and human claims.
- **Lymphatics are absent from the compartment.** `PMID 40970910` localises lymphatics to periosteum only,
  with VEGFR3 labelling *blood* vessels inside marrow. So there is no lymphatic clearance route from the
  plate; everything that arrives leaves by the venous side or is consumed.

**What is missing.** No human pO₂. No human tracer study. No measurement of the diffusion distance or
effective diffusivity in a *human* plate at any age. No study relating adolescent limb perfusion to
growth velocity. No pharmacokinetic measurement of any growth-promoting drug inside human growth-plate
cartilage. The 40 kDa ceiling — the single most decision-relevant number in this section — has never been
tested in human tissue.

## PROSE 3 — ANY NEURAL OR MICROBIAL INPUT WITH A DEMONSTRATED BONE-LENGTH EFFECT

### Neural — the honest answer is "weak, small, and segment-specific"

- **Denervation is the only direct manipulation with length numbers, and it is modest.** In rats,
  sciatic section made the **metatarsals 3–5% shorter** from the first week with no further divergence,
  the **femur <1% longer**, and the **tibia unchanged** (`PMID 3346314`). In rabbits, sciatic lesion
  retarded femoral and tibial growth **and reduced Sr-85 uptake in the bones**, so the authors attributed
  the effect to a *circulatory* disturbance rather than to the nerve per se (`PMID 970065`). In frog
  tadpoles, complete and partial sciatic denervation significantly affected tibial length and foot growth
  (`PMID 2783965`). Read together: nerves matter, the effect is small and distal-weighted, and at least
  one careful study attributes it to blood flow.
- **Sensory nerves are required for skeletal vascularisation and ossification** (`PMID 27568565`,
  NGF–TrkA), and Sema3A acts on bone through sensory innervation rather than on osteoblasts
  (`PMID 23644455`). Neither reports a bone-length endpoint.
- **The cartilage clock does have a length-adjacent endpoint.** Cartilage-specific *Bmal1* deletion
  produced growth retardation with a **shorter growth plate and shorter proliferative zone**
  (`PMID 31107137`) — a circadian, cell-autonomous input, and one of the few CNS-adjacent items with a
  plate measurement.
- **The sleep→GH chain fails its own decisive test.** Selective SWS disruption in pubertal children did
  **not** reduce GH secretion (`PMID 37283961`). CPAP raises GH/IGF-1 in adults with OSA
  (`PMID 24899762`) and adenotonsillectomy improves growth in children with OSA (`PMID 41731114`), but
  those are confounded by the disease. The mechanistic link most people assume is not supported by the
  one experiment that isolates it.
- **β-adrenergic blockade in growing infants is null on height** (`PMID 39615547`) — a large natural
  experiment against the sympathetic arm being rate-limiting in humans.
- **Psychosocial short stature remains the largest neural effect on human stature by magnitude**
  (`PMID 12705359`) and is fully reversible, but it has no identified molecular mediator.
- **Hypothalamic inputs act on the *period*, not the plate.** MKRN3, DLK1, kisspeptin/NKB set pubertal
  onset and therefore fusion timing (`PMID 42290210`, `PMID 37703313`, `PMID 42521916`). These are the
  neural inputs with the largest effect on final height, and they operate entirely upstream.

### Microbial — the answer is "yes, and it is the strongest non-endocrine external input in this domain"

- **`PMID 36821686` is the best single result.** Purified *L. plantarum* cell walls, **muramyl dipeptide**,
  and **mifamurtide** were each *sufficient* to sustain postnatal growth in malnourished conventional
  mice, working through NOD2 in intestinal epithelial cells to raise IGF-1 and insulin. Mifamurtide is
  **EMA-approved (Mepact), for high-grade non-metastatic osteosarcoma, in patients aged 2–30**, so this
  is a licensed molecule with an adolescent label whose mechanism has a demonstrated linear-growth effect
  in animals. I could find no study giving mifamurtide with a height endpoint (UNVERIFIED).
- **`PMID 27821775` (Yan 2016)** establishes the causal chain microbiota → IGF-1 → bone formation and
  growth, with SCFA supplementation restoring it after antibiotics.
- **`PMID 26912894` and `PMID 21907145`** show a single commensal strain rescuing juvenile growth under
  undernutrition in mice and flies; `PMID 28993620` identifies D-alanylated teichoic acid as the moiety.
- **`PMID 26912898`** shows the effect is transmissible: microbiota from stunted children transmits growth
  impairment to gnotobiotic mice, and defined taxa prevent it.
- **`PMID 38833839`** is the human endpoint: a microbiota-directed complementary food **improved linear
  growth** in Bangladeshi children with moderate acute malnutrition, over two years of follow-up.
- **`PMID 26898329`** — sialylated milk oligosaccharides produced microbiota-dependent lean-mass gain and
  **changed bone morphology** in gnotobiotic mice, and sialylated HMOs were scarcer in mothers of severely
  stunted infants.
- **The negatives are as informative.** WASH interventions were essentially null for linear growth in two
  large cluster-RCTs (`PMID 29396217`, `PMID 29396219`). Probiotics in severe acute malnutrition moved
  weight much more than length (`PMID 36725893`). Deworming has been tested in an RCT designed for growth
  (`PMID 26426270`). So "manipulate the microbiome, gain height" is true for *specific defined
  interventions in undernourished populations* and not for generic hygiene or generic probiotics.
- **The whole field is a restoration literature.** Every positive microbial result above is a rescue of an
  undernourished or germ-free animal, or of a stunted child. I found **no experiment that raised a
  well-nourished, conventionally colonised animal above its own control on bone length** by a microbial
  intervention. That is the single largest gap on this axis.
