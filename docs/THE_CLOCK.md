# The clock — can it be slowed, and can it be reversed?

**Date:** 2026-08-06

Everything before this reduced the height question to one thing: **the intrinsic,
division-dependent senescence programme.** Oestrogen accelerates it; removing oestrogen slows it
to ~71% and buys roughly +30 cm; the programme still runs. To go further you have to move the
programme itself, which has never been attempted.

This is what the record actually contains.

---

## 1. The clock's molecular identity — proposed, and untested for sixteen years

The finite-capacity model's **own authors falsified its simplest version.** `nilsson2005`:
**rabbit resting-zone chondrocytes from old and young donors undergo the same number of
population doublings in culture.**

> **The proliferative limit is not carried by the cell. It is imposed by the plate.**

That fact has been in this atlas since Phase 1 and was never put to work. It matters enormously:
**an imposed limit is a target in a way an intrinsic one is not.**

Their alternative proposal was **a progressive loss of DNA methylation tracking slow in vivo
resting-zone proliferation.** Sixteen years on it remains untested — and it is exactly the kind
of hypothesis a reprogramming experiment addresses.

---

## 2. The pool is not a closed compartment

**`qu2025` (Nature Communications) is the most important paper I have found for this question.**

1. **The long-lived progenitors that drive postnatal growth are not present at birth.** In fetus
   and neonate, reserve-zone progenitors *do not self-renew* — a cell recruited into a column
   exhausts fast and columns are short. **At about three postnatal weeks they begin to
   self-renew** and long columns appear. So the pool that sets final height is **constituted
   postnatally**, not inherited.
2. Those progenitors descend from **Gli1+ cells that sit mostly dormant through fetal life.**
3. **Damage recruits them.** Cartilage-targeted mosaic p21 overexpression — a genetic cell-cycle
   arrest — makes Gli1+ cells **expand inside the cartilage**, biased toward the **resting zone**,
   enriching the Ki67+ cycling fraction, and **growth is normal despite the damage.**
4. **The reparative cells come from Pdgfra+ cells OUTSIDE the cartilage.**

> **There is a reservoir, it lives in the surrounding stroma, and injury to the plate recruits it.**

That is the first mechanism in this atlas that could **add** to the budget rather than spend it
more slowly. It also supplies a mechanism for **catch-up growth** the atlas did not hold: catch-up
may not be only a paused clock — **it may be a refilled pool.**

The authors state the goal in their own words: *"stimulating the expansion of the pool of LLCPs at
the right time and location."*

---

## 3. Partial reprogramming already works in cartilage, in vivo, without dedifferentiation

`osk2026`: AAV-delivered **Oct4/Sox2/Klf4** into the mouse knee. Chondrocytes **kept their
identity** — no increase in stemness genes, which is the failure mode that makes in vivo
reprogramming dangerous. **Chondrocyte senescence and DNA methyltransferase expression were
markedly diminished**, with **TET2** as the pivotal mediator.

The DNMT/TET2 result lands directly on the methylation hypothesis from §1.

**But this is articular cartilage in osteoarthritis, not growth plate, and the endpoint is joint
integrity, not elongation.** Nobody has delivered OSK to a growth plate. Recorded as a
demonstrated *technique in the right tissue class*, not as a growth result.

---

## 4. What is honestly not established

- Every element is **mouse or rabbit**. The mouse plate never closes.
- The reserve is mobilised **by injury**, not by a drug.
- **The compensation restores *normal* growth. It has not been shown to exceed it.** A repair
  mechanism that returns you to normal is not a mechanism that goes beyond normal, and I will not
  conflate the two.
- I have read the *design* of the necessity experiment (Gli1-targeted diphtheria toxin) and not
  its outcome figures. Recorded as **tested**, not **established**.
- **The hazard is real and specific.** Gli1/Hedgehog signalling is oncogenic in bone and
  cartilage, and **osteosarcoma arises from this lineage during rapid growth.** No route here is
  safe by default.
- One paper that comes up in every search for "targeting growth plate senescence" (`naveed2025`)
  is a **two-page Letter to the Editor with no data.** Recorded so a future search doesn't mistake
  it for a result.

---

## 5. The first step costs nothing, and it can kill the whole programme

`avijgan2026br` applied Visium and Visium HD to human growth plate biopsies, and **their sections
span the plate, the surrounding tissue and the secondary ossification centre.** `chu2026`
sequenced 23,625 cells from four donors **including non-chondrocyte clusters.** Both are public —
avijgan's code and data are on GitHub.

> **Whether GLI1 and PDGFRA mark a population adjacent to the human resting zone is already
> answerable from published data, at zero cost.**

A positive answer justifies everything downstream. **A negative answer closes the most promising
route in this atlas, cheaply** — which is why it goes first.

## 6. The programme, in order

| # | step | cost | what it decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Re-analyse published human spatial/scRNA data for a GLI1+/PDGFRA+ perichondrial population** | zero — data is public | whether a human reservoir exists at all |
| 2 | Human growth-plate explant: does a Hedgehog agonist or AAV-OSK change the resting-zone label-retaining fraction? | tissue + reagents | whether the reserve is *pharmacologically* recruitable |
| 3 | Measure the human **growth fraction** by cumulative labelling (already drafted to Karolinska) | one protocol change | whether there is idle capacity before any reservoir is needed |
| 4 | Undamaged animal, pool expansion, **bone length as the endpoint** | full study | whether recruitment *exceeds* normal or merely restores it |

Steps 1 and 3 need no new tissue. **Step 2 is the first thing that requires anything I cannot
get myself.**

---

## 7. What this changes about the ceiling

The ceiling arithmetic now reads:

- **Oestrogen removal:** ~71% clock rate → roughly +30 cm, threshold-gated, bone cost possibly
  recoverable. **Bounded and roughly quantified.**
- **Clock modification:** the limit is **imposed rather than intrinsic** (§1), the pool is
  **refillable from outside** (§2), and the reprogramming technique **works in cartilage in vivo**
  (§3). **Unbounded — because unmeasured, not because it is large.**

That is the honest position. It is the first time in this project that the second row has had
anything in it at all.
