# 29280739 + 28146470 — PAPP-A / PAPP-A2 / stanniocalcin: the proteolytic gate on free IGF, and the largest rare-variant effect on human height

**Citations.**
- Dauber & Hwa, "Novel modulators of the GH–IGF axis: PAPP-A," *J Clin Res Pediatr Endocrinol* 2017;9(Suppl 2):1–8. PMC5790331. **Read in full.**
- Marouli et al., "Rare and low-frequency coding variants alter human adult height," *Nature* 2017;542:186–190. PMC5302847. **Read in full** (results, pathway analysis, STC2 functional characterisation, discussion).

## The axis
- **PAPP-A** (pappalysin-1) cleaves **IGFBP-4** (and -2, -5), at the cell surface, **only when IGF is bound to the binding protein** — a substrate-gated, autocrine/paracrine local amplifier of IGF signalling ([24572990](24572990.md)).
- **PAPP-A2** (pappalysin-2, chr 1q25.2, 46% homologous) cleaves **IGFBP-3 and IGFBP-5**, liberating IGF-1 from the ternary complex.
- **STC2** (chr 5q35.2, secreted homodimeric glycoprotein) is a **potent inhibitor of both PAPP-A and PAPP-A2**, acting by **covalent** binding and inactivation. **STC1** inhibits PAPP-A.
- **PAPPA, PAPPA2, STC2 and IGFBP4 all sit in genome-wide-significant height loci.**

## Exact numbers
**The human height variants — the headline.**
Of **83 rare and low-frequency coding variants** associated with adult height at genome-wide significance, **the variant with the largest effect size was in *STC2***:
- ***STC2* p.Arg44Leu** (rs148833559, MAF 0.096%, P_discovery = 5.7×10⁻¹⁵): **+1.9 cm**
- ***STC2* p.Met86Ile** (rs146441603, MAF 0.14%, P = 2.1×10⁻⁵): **+0.9 cm**
Both expressed at wild-type levels but with **clear partial defects in binding to PAPP-A and in inhibiting PAPP-A-mediated cleavage of IGFBP-4.** (The review quotes ~2.1 cm for the lead variant.)
**So partially disabling one secreted inhibitor of one protease adds roughly two centimetres to adult human height, and it is the largest such effect known.**

**The loss-of-function mirror.** Two families homozygous for *PAPPA2* mutations (p.D643fs25, p.A1033V): **height −2.8 to −3.8 SD**, progressive growth failure, **no pubertal growth spurt**, microcephaly, small chin, long thin bones, low BMD, delayed dental eruption. Biochemically **elevated total IGF-1, IGFBP-3, IGFBP-5, ALS and IGF-2, with low or low-normal free/bioactive IGF-1** and a markedly reduced bioactive/total ratio; GH elevated (lost negative feedback). The p.A1033V protein could not cleave IGFBP-3 or IGFBP-5. Heterozygous parents were of normal stature.
**rhIGF-1 treatment** (40–120 µg/kg twice daily): height velocity **3.7 → 7.6 cm/year** in one patient and **3.0 → 6.2 cm/year** in another, +0.4 SD each over a year; insulin resistance and BMD also improved. **One sibling developed severe headaches from intracranial hypertension and treatment was stopped**; symptoms resolved on withdrawal and his height SD fell from −2.9 to −3.0.

**Mouse models.**
- *Pappa* KO, *Pappa2* KO, *Stc1* KO, *Stc2* KO all viable. **hSTC2 transgenic: 26–34% neonatal mortality** without dysmorphology.
- **Birth weight: *Pappa* KO and hSTC1/hSTC2 transgenics ~30–40% lighter than wild type; *Stc2* KO 15% heavier.**
- **"The growth limiting (*Pappa* KO, *Pappa2* KO, STC1 and STC2 overexpression) and growth promoting (*Stc2* KO) effects of all KO and Tg mice persisted or became more apparent in post-natal growth."**
- ***Stc1* KO mice remained the same size as wild type throughout life** — STC1 matters less for growth.
- *Pappa2* KO: free IGF-1 decreased, total IGF-1 raised, IGFBP-5 raised.
- **"hSTC1 and hSTC2 Tg mice had severe impairment in post-natal, linear *axial* skeletal growth."** *Pappa* and *Pappa2* KO showed delayed bone formation/mineralisation. **No significant skeletal effect in *Stc1* or *Stc2* KO.**

**Pathway findings from the Nature paper.** Coding variants more strongly implicated skeletal-growth-specific pathways (ECM, bone growth) than non-coding GWAS signals did. The two gene sets **uniquely** implicated by coding variants were **"BCAN protein–protein interaction subnetwork" and "proteoglycan binding."** A cluster of 15 height-associated genes drives the proteoglycan/glycosaminoglycan pathways, enriched 27.6-fold for OMIM skeletal-growth-disorder annotation (Fisher P = 1.1×10⁻⁵); members include **SUSD5, GLT8D2, LOXL4 (a lysyl oxidase expressed in cartilage), FIBIN and SFRP4 — and *Sfrp4* knockout increases body length in mice.**

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**1. This is the proteolytic half of the gate that [27897169](27897169.md) identified epigenetically, and together they make one mechanism.** Hypertrophic cell size — the term carrying 59% of axial growth ([32852849](32852849.md), [18404738](18404738.md)) — is set by **local free IGF availability**, and local free IGF availability has exactly two controls:
   - **How much IGFBP there is.** *Igfbp3* and *Igfbp5* are direct H3K27me3/PRC2 targets in the hypertrophic zone; exogenous IGFBP3 or IGFBP5 alone shrinks hypertrophic cells ([27897169](27897169.md)).
   - **How fast IGFBP is cleaved.** PAPP-A and PAPP-A2 do the cleaving; **STC1 and STC2 inhibit them.**
   **That is a single, coherent, named node with human genetic validation at ~2 cm per allele, and it is the most actionable thing in this corpus.**
**2. It is a *local* amplifier, which is the property every systemic idea in [COMPOUNDS.md](../COMPOUNDS.md) lacks.** PAPP-A works at the cell surface and cleaves IGFBP-4 only when IGF is already bound. It does not raise systemic IGF-1; it releases IGF where the protease is. Against acromegaly — decades of supraphysiological systemic GH/IGF-1 lengthening only the mandible ([22518118](22518118.md)) — a protease that acts where it sits is the right shape of lever.
**3. The effect persists and strengthens postnatally, and in the overexpression model it is *axial*.** "Severe impairment in post-natal, linear axial skeletal growth" in hSTC1/hSTC2 transgenics is the one explicitly axial statement in my corpus about this axis, and it points at the spine — which is where [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) rev. 2 §13 says the surface count and the late closure are. **I have not read that primary (their ref 24) and it is now a priority.**
**4. It reframes two compounds I under-rated.** T3 raises *PAPPA* 2.7–3.4× in adult human articular cartilage from 60–76-year-olds ([38664820](38664820.md)); dasatinib raises *IGF1* and lowers *IGFBP1* and *IGFBP3* in adult human chondrocytes ([39402753](39402753.md)). **Both were already moving this node in adult human tissue, and neither paper measured hypertrophic cell volume.**
**5. And it names the druggable direction.** The growth-promoting move is **more PAPP-A/PAPP-A2 activity or less STC2**. STC2 is a secreted protein — the most tractable class for an antibody. The tools for the opposite direction already exist (an inhibitory anti-PAPP-A mAb with Ki 135 pM, [24572990](24572990.md)), which means the assays, the structural biology and the substrate chemistry are all in place.

## The dangers, stated plainly
The user asked specifically for this. They are not hypothetical.
- **PAPP-A is a validated oncology target *for inhibition*.** The anti-PAPP-A mAb was developed because PAPP-A-driven local IGF signalling promotes tumour growth, and it suppressed xenograft growth ([24572990](24572990.md)). **Enhancing PAPP-A activity enhances a pathway the field is trying to block in cancer.**
- ***Pappa*-knockout mice are long-lived with reduced age-related pathology** (20351075, 36542300 — both abstract-only in this container; on the needed list). **So the same manipulation that would raise growth appears to shorten lifespan and raise cancer risk in the inverse direction.** That is a real, quantified trade-off, not a caveat.
- **Intracranial hypertension.** One of four rhIGF-1-treated PAPP-A2-deficient patients developed severe headaches from raised intracranial pressure, requiring discontinuation. IGF-1 raises CSF production.
- **hSTC2 overexpression gave 26–34% neonatal mortality in mice** — so this axis is not benignly tunable in either direction.
- **Insulin resistance and glucose handling.** PAPP-A2-deficient patients had significant insulin resistance and pre-diabetes (improved by rhIGF-1); IGF-1 acts through insulin and hybrid receptors, with hypoglycaemia a known rhIGF-1 effect.
- **STC1 has roles in calcium and phosphate metabolism**, so STC-directed intervention is not confined to growth.

## Honest limits
- **The STC2 variants and the PAPPA2 mutations are lifelong, developmental.** Nothing shows that manipulating this axis **after epiphyseal closure** does anything. The +1.9 cm is an accrued developmental effect in people whose plates were open.
- The review is a conference-proceedings summary by the group that found the PAPPA2 patients; the mouse data are tabulated from many papers I have not read.
- Free/bioactive IGF-1 was measured in only one mouse model.
- n = 5 patients across two families; n = 4 treated with rhIGF-1.
- The "axial" claim is one sentence citing one reference I have not read.
- No growth plate histology, no hypertrophic cell volume, and no articular or vertebral tissue anywhere in either paper.
- The Nature paper's rare variants explain a small fraction of height heritability; effect sizes of 0.9–1.9 cm are for very rare alleles (MAF ~0.1%).
