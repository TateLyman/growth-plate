# PMID 38516888 — Kurenkova AD, ... Chagin AS. Stimulation of skeletal stem cells in the growth plate promotes linear bone growth (2024, JCI Insight)

Read in full (PMC, ~40k chars).

**Model.** Mice (Pthrp-creER tdTomato; Ptch1 cKO; Pthrp-mCherry). Rats (Wistar-Kyoto, bead implantation at P30).

**Findings.**
- **Age-dependent drug effect.** Systemic SAG (Smoothened agonist) at P10–16, before the niche forms, *reduced* stem-cell (epSSC) clone size and proliferation. At P30–36, after the SOC matures, it *increased* proliferation and doublets/triplets.
- **Genetic Hh activation** (Ptch1 loss in PTHrP+ cells) enlarged clones at both ages. At P25–27 pulse, 25 columns >30 cells (spanning the whole plate) vs none in controls. Clones were also *disorganised*.
- **Expansion.** SAG at P30–36 increased PTHrP-mCherry+ cells by 61% and expanded CD73+mCherry+ cells. Bulk RNA-seq: Wnt pathway downregulated (Hh creates a Wnt-inhibitory niche).
- **Intra-articular SAG** (P28/30/31): Tomato+ RZ cells 65.5 → 139.8 per mm² (p=0.017); hypertrophic size unchanged.
- **Rat SOC bead (7 µg SAG, once, P30).**
  - Femur longer at 1 month and more so at 2 and 6 months. Tibia longer at 2 and 6 months. Whole leg longer at all times.
  - Growth rate (calcein–xylenol) up at 1 month (femur) and 2 months (tibia).
  - Growth-plate height and terminal hypertrophic height up. Upper-50 µm proliferation up at 1 week.
  - Bead Hh activity lasted under 3 weeks (Gli1-LacZ), yet length effects persisted 6 months. **A transient pulse that expands the stem pool has durable growth consequences.** No OA at 6 months.
  - *Exact mm differences are only in figures; not in text.*
- **Context.** Hh inhibition → abrupt fusion. PTH1R ablation → fusion and stem-cell apoptosis.

**What it establishes for post-fusion growth.**
- Strongest causal evidence that **stem-cell number in a functioning niche sets growth output**. A one-time local intervention can lengthen bone for months.
- Only in animals with open, niche-bearing plates; not post-fusion.
- Note: the SOC (bony epiphysis) is "stable, relatively large, accessible" and adjacent to the stem cells, which is why it was chosen as a delivery site.

**Levers.**
- Hh activation of chondro-progenitors, once a niche exists, expands the pool.
- Mechanical relevance: cyclic compression induces Ihh in chondrocytes (Wu 2001, Tang/Ng; condylar data), so mechanical loading could be the Hh source.
- Reinforces that a self-sustaining effect needs a niche with spatial order. Unregulated proliferation (genetic Hh) disorganises.
