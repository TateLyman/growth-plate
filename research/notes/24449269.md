# PMID 24449269 — Ogawa H, Kozhemyakina E, Hung HH, Grodzinsky AJ, Lassar AB. Mechanical motion promotes expression of Prg4 in articular cartilage via multiple CREB-dependent, fluid flow shear stress-induced signaling pathways (2014, Genes Dev)

Read in full (PMC, ~52k chars).

**Findings.**
- One month of voluntary wheel running in 3-month-old (adult) mice roughly **doubles Prg4-expressing superficial cells**, mostly on the tibial plateau. Prg4 mRNA is up.
  - EdU shows a small rise in proliferation; the Prg4+ pool does not expand much. Running increases per-cell expression and recruits more cells into Prg4 expression.
  - Celecoxib (COX-2 inhibitor) reduces this to ~65%.
- **Fluid-flow shear stress (FFSS; ~7.6 dyn/cm² laminar)** on chondrocytes:
  - Induces Prg4 via secreted PGE2 (EP2 → PKA), **PTHrP (PTH1R → PKA)** and ATP (P2X7/pannexin-1, P2Y2 → Ca²⁺ → calcineurin → CRTC1/2 nuclear translocation).
  - These converge on CREB at two CRE sites in the Prg4 locus.
  - FFSS also raises **PTHrP** expression and COX-2 6-fold.
- Only superficial-zone (not middle-zone) chondrocytes are competent to induce Prg4. That marks the surface as the mechanically addressable progenitor compartment.
- Running in vivo raises phospho-CREB and nuclear CRTC1 in tibial articular chondrocytes.

**Relevance for the theory.**
- **Joint motion → fluid flow shear at the surface → PTHrP + PGE2 + ATP → CREB → superficial-zone/progenitor program (Prg4).**
- That is a mechanical route to establishing a surface PTHrP source, the same element that sustains condylar growth cartilage (Tsutsumi-Arai 2024: superficial PTHrP gradient → polymorphic progenitor proliferation, anti-exhaustion). It also echoes Xu 2013 (cyclic strain → PTHrP) and the physiological loading → PTHrP induction in the growth plate (39090666).
- Practical lever: articulating motion is the knob for the surface progenitor and PTHrP compartment. NSAIDs (COX-2 inhibitors) blunt it, which matters for any human protocol.
