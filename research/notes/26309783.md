# 26309783 — Treating proximal tibial growth plate injuries using PLGA scaffolds

**Citation.** Biores Open Access 2015;4(1):278–290. PMC4497685. Pilot animal study. Read in full.

## Model / method
New Zealand white rabbits. Proximal tibial physis injured medially, allowed to form a **bony bar over 3 weeks**, then the bar was resected and the defect filled with one of four treatments:
1. porous **PLGA** scaffold (blank)
2. PLGA + **IGF-I**
3. PLGA + IGF-I + **autogenous bone marrow cells** harvested at implantation
4. **free fat graft** (the clinical standard, Langenskiöld method) — control
Assessed at 8 weeks: µCT, medial proximal tibial angle (MPTA), lateral distal femoral angle (LDFA), coronal histology with zone identification.

## Exact findings
- **Blank PLGA** gave a **wider growth plate across the tibia** than the fat graft, with a thicker continual connection of chondrocytes and striations of cartilage below the plate.
- **PLGA + IGF-I** maintained a structured plate laterally and raised chondrocyte density medially, in **dispersed pockets** of reserve, proliferative, hypertrophic and degenerative cells.
- **PLGA + IGF-I + BMCs** produced "**a large, dense area of cartilage on the upper medial side containing mostly hypertrophic chondrocytes**" surrounded by reserve cells.
- Fat graft gave only a **thin** continual line of cartilage, plus a dense region of bone where tissue had regrown.
- **No statistically significant difference in angular deformity** between any groups, at any time point: MPTA fell in all groups between bar resection and 8 weeks; LDFA 90–96° with no between-group difference. n ≥ 3 per group, two animals in the IGF-I arm were infected.
- No chronic inflammation or fibrous tissue in any specimen.
- Background the paper supplies: ~18% of childhood fractures involve the physis; 30–65% of those produce growth disturbance; the Langenskiöld fat graft gives fair-to-poor results in ~60% of patients and bony bar recurrence in ~30%.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **IGF-I delivered locally from a degradable scaffold increased chondrocyte density in a physeal defect — and produced pockets and a lump, not a plate.** The cell-seeded arm made "a large, dense area of cartilage... mostly hypertrophic chondrocytes." That is the swelling failure mode again, now with the correct growth factor and the correct cells: mass without polarity. It is the clearest small-scale illustration of why §3 of [COMPOUNDS.md](../COMPOUNDS.md) puts architecture ahead of pharmacology.
2. **The scaffold alone widened the growth plate.** A blank porous polymer, no factor, no cells, beat the clinical standard on plate width and cartilage continuity. Geometry doing work again, consistent with the modulus-matched pillar scaffold in [37483459](37483459.md).
3. **And none of it corrected the deformity.** More cartilage, more chondrocytes, better histology, **no significant change in angle**. Histological improvement did not translate into a dimensional outcome. Worth holding against every gene-expression result in this corpus.
4. **The spatial-control problem in miniature.** Reserve, proliferative, hypertrophic and degenerative cells were all present — just not stacked in that order along the growth axis. The cells know their phenotypes; what they lacked was the instruction about direction.

## Levers
- **IGF-I from cell-free PLGA** — local delivery of the hypertrophic-swelling actuator ([31787777](31787777.md)), no second harvest surgery needed.
- **Autogenous BMCs at implantation** (no preculture) — the one arm that produced a large dense cartilage mass. Cell supply matters, which is the §2 wall of COMPOUNDS.md seen from the supply side.
- **Porous PLGA alone** as a bone-bridge-preventing, plate-widening filler.
- **Anti-lever: factor + cells without architecture.** Pockets and lumps, no angular correction.

## Honest limits
- Explicitly a **pilot study**; n ≥ 3 per group, two infected animals in one arm, no significant differences in any quantitative endpoint.
- **Growing rabbits with injured but open physes.** The goal is rescuing growth that would otherwise be lost, not creating growth in an adult.
- 8 weeks only. Histology is descriptive and representative-image-based; no quantification of chondrocyte density, plate width or limb length is reported.
- No measurement of limb length at all — only angles.

## Chase
- Ref 13 (Li et al.) — chitin scaffolds with precultured MSCs: **significant decrease in angular deformation and longitudinal discrepancy**. The positive result this study failed to reproduce; needed.
- Refs 14–16 — sodium hyaluronate/collagen and agarose scaffolds where **limb length improved**. Needed: these are the dimensional results.
- Ref 11, 12 — Langenskiöld method outcomes.
- The cartilage-targeting scFv–IGF-1 fusion (ref 106 of [37483459](37483459.md)) — the targeted version of what this paper delivered untargeted.
