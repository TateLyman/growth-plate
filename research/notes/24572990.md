# 24572990 — Indirect targeting of IGF receptor signalling in vivo by substrate-selective inhibition of PAPP-A proteolytic activity

**Citation.** Oncotarget 2014;5(5):1213–1222. PMC4011579. Read in full (Introduction and Results/Discussion).

## Model / method
Development and characterisation of **mAb 1/41**, a monoclonal antibody against a **substrate-binding exosite** of PAPP-A. Enzyme kinetics; A549 lung-cancer cells (high PAPP-A, IGF-responsive); cell-surface-bound PAPP-A; in vivo activity; **murine A549 xenograft model with intraperitoneal dosing**, with tumour tissue analysed for antibody penetration, IGFBP-4 proteolysis and AKT phosphorylation.

## Exact findings
- **PAPP-A associated with the cell surface cleaves IGFBP-4 only when IGF is bound to IGFBP-4**, thereby increasing IGF bioavailability for receptor activation **in an autocrine/paracrine manner**.
- **mAb 1/41 selectively and specifically inhibits IGFBP-4 proteolysis with Ki = 135 pM.**
- It inhibited IGF-receptor signalling (AKT phosphorylation) in A549 monolayers, was **effective against cell-surface-bound PAPP-A**, and inhibited PAPP-A activity in vivo.
- **Intraperitoneal mAb 1/41 significantly inhibited xenograft tumour growth**, with demonstrated tumour penetration, reduced IGFBP-4 proteolysis and reduced AKT phosphorylation.
- Framing: direct IGF-1R targeting (anti-ligand antibodies, anti-receptor antibodies, kinase inhibitors) has been "less fruitful than anticipated" in trials; targeting the upstream regulatory proteinase is the alternative.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **It proves the node is druggable and that the chemistry, assays and structural biology exist** — just pointed the other way. A substrate-selective exosite antibody with sub-nanomolar Ki, effective on membrane-bound enzyme and in vivo, is a mature tool.
2. **It establishes the property that makes PAPP-A interesting for this project and dangerous at the same time: it is a *local*, substrate-gated amplifier.** Cleavage happens at the cell surface and only on IGF-loaded IGFBP-4. It does not raise systemic IGF; it releases IGF where the protease sits. Against the acromegaly problem — systemic drive lengthening only the mandible ([22518118](22518118.md)) — locality is exactly what is needed. Against the cancer problem, locality is exactly how tumours use it.
3. **It is the clearest single statement of the risk.** This antibody exists because **PAPP-A-driven local IGF signalling promotes tumour growth, and blocking it shrinks tumours.** Any proposal to *raise* PAPP-A activity for skeletal purposes is proposing to do, systemically and deliberately, what this drug was built to prevent. That is the honest frame for the "even if dangerous" question.
4. **IGFBP-4 is the PAPP-A substrate, and it is a height GWAS gene** ([29280739](29280739.md)). The hypertrophic-zone substrates are IGFBP-3 and IGFBP-5, which are PAPP-A2's substrates and direct PRC2 targets ([27897169](27897169.md)). **So the growth-plate-relevant protease is PAPP-A2, not PAPP-A** — a distinction worth keeping, since the cancer tooling is all against PAPP-A.

## Levers
- **PAPP-A / PAPP-A2 proteolytic activity** as a local, substrate-gated IGF amplifier.
- **The substrate-binding exosite** as a druggable site (mAb 1/41, Ki 135 pM) — proof of tractability.
- **Anti-lever, and the main one: this is an oncology target for inhibition.** Enhancing it enhances a validated tumour-promoting axis.

## Honest limits
- Cancer pharmacology. No skeletal tissue, no growth plate, no cartilage, no bone length anywhere in the paper.
- Everything is **inhibition**. No agent here or elsewhere in my corpus *enhances* PAPP-A or PAPP-A2 activity.
- A549 xenograft in immunodeficient mice.
- Concerns PAPP-A and IGFBP-4, not PAPP-A2 and IGFBP-3/5, which is the growth-plate-relevant pair.
