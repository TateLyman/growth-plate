# PMID 34527791 — Hadaegh Y, ... El-Bialy T. The effect of low intensity pulsed ultrasound on mandibular condylar growth in young adult rats (2021, Bone Rep)

Read in full (PMC, ~38k chars).

**Model.** 19 female Sprague-Dawley rats, ~110–120 days old ("young adult ≈ human twenties"). LIPUS (low-intensity pulsed ultrasound) n=10 vs control n=9.
- LIPUS: 1.5 MHz, 200 µs pulses at 1 kHz repetition, 30 mW/cm² SATA, 1.1 cm² transducer. Bilateral TMJ, 20 min/day for 28 days under isoflurane (controls also anaesthetised).
- Calcein labels at d7 and d28.

**Results.**
- Gross length: condylar process and mandibular length slightly higher, **not significant**. A 3D deviation map suggested periosteal apposition at the application site.
- Cartilage: total fibrocartilage thicker (significant, middle and posterior regions); prechondroblastic cell number up; chondroblastic layer thicker and more cellular (middle region); more hypertrophic cells; more proteoglycan.
- Subchondral bone: calcifying-cartilage remnants plus new-bone fraction up 59% (vs 318.91% with forward positioning, Rabie 2004). Lower BMD and anisotropy (woven bone).
- **Calcein inter-label endochondral growth significantly higher** (p<0.01; magnitude in figure).
- Comparison: continuous bite-jumping in the same rat model gave significant condylar process and mandibular length increases over 30 days (Xiong 2004), with 2–6-fold layer increases vs LIPUS's 1.2–1.4-fold.

**Key background stated.**
- Mandibular condylar cartilage is a *secondary cartilage* from ALP+ periosteal cells. **It does not fuse; growth can continue after appendicular maturity** (Robinson 2017).
- Progenitor depletion at the condylar top occurs in mice only after 9 months.
- The cartilage–bone junction is sensitive to tensile forces from mandibular movement.
- Stepwise advancement works even in adults (Purkayastha 2008; Chaiyongsirisern 2009); each step should last ~6 months for bone to mature.

**What it establishes for post-fusion growth.**
- An adult secondary cartilage that never fuses keeps a responsive progenitor layer.
- Weak mechanical stimuli (LIPUS) give modest histological endochondral activation without measurable length.
- Larger physiological mechanical repositioning gives measurable length gain.
- **Magnitude and type of mechanical stimulus matter. Joint-level sustained strain >> ultrasound.**

**Levers.**
- Stepwise, progressive repositioning (a "ratcheting strain") is the adult-effective regime in the jaw.
- Ultrasound alone is a weak adjunct.
- The condyle is the prototype of a never-fusing, progenitor-covered growth cartilage in adults. Analogous limb sites are lacking, but the *periosteum-derived secondary cartilage* lineage is available everywhere periosteum exists (callus, osteophyte, condyle).

**Chase.** Robinson 2017 (condylar progenitor depletion after 9 months); Xiong 2004 Angle Orthod (bite-jumping length data); Purkayastha 2008 (stepwise in adults); Suzuki 2004 (IGF-1 injection in adult condyle); Kaur 2014.
