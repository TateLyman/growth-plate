# 37126720 — Modeling human skeletal development using human pluripotent stem cells

**Citation.** PNAS 2023;120(19):e2211510120. PMC10175848. Read in full (Results, disease models, Discussion, stated limitations).

## Model / method
Human iPSC (MCRIi001-A, MCRIi018-B, MCRIi019-A + isogenic mutant lines) → 6-day sclerotome protocol (BMP block at paraxial mesoderm, then HEDGEHOG augmentation) → **pellet at day 4** → FGF2 20 ng/ml × 14–28 d → "chondronoids". Two branches:
- **+ TGF-β3** from day 13 × 5 wk → articular chondrocytes (PRG4+, COL X−).
- **no TGF-β3** → spontaneous hypertrophy; **+ T3 (10 nM)** → terminal hypertrophy; then osteogenic medium × 3 wk → osteoblast/osteocyte.
Transfer to **rotary orbital culture** (fluid shear) at day 13–20 accelerates maturation. In vivo arm: day-42 hypertrophic chondronoids implanted subcutaneously in immunodeficient mice, harvested 13 wk.

## Exact numbers / findings
- Organoids **2.5–3 mm diameter**, uniform cartilage, no non-cartilage contaminating layer (unlike BMP4-driven protocols which leave a COL1A1+ rind and off-target melanocytes/neural cells).
- Reproducibility: **0 differentially expressed genes at D48** between independent differentiations of two lines; 156 genes (1% of transcriptome) at D69; 669 (4%) at D69+T3. PC1 (time) + PC2 (treatment) = 69% of variance; line identity only 7.3%.
- Timing of rotary transfer controls hypertrophy onset: transfer at day 6 leaves a non-cartilage outer layer; day 13/20/27 gives enlarged COL X+ cells. Early static (hypoxic) phase is needed for the sclerotome→chondrocyte step; hypoxia *inhibits* hypertrophy, so moving to aerated rotary culture is what releases it.
- T3 does **not** change early hypertrophic markers (COL10A1, IHH, MEF2C, SPP1) but raises late ones (SP7, VEGFA, DMP1, HIF1A, MMP13, ALPL) and suppresses COL2A1, COL9A1, COL11A1, SOX9, SOX5, SOX6. **Prior T3 is essential for optimal transition to bone-like organoids.**
- In vivo: 13-wk subcutaneous grafts were largely mineralised bone, BGLAP+ and SOST+, **human origin confirmed by Ku80 in both the cartilage and the bone** — i.e. the human hypertrophic chondrocytes themselves became the osteoblasts.
- In vitro transdifferentiation: COL2A1/ACAN down, COL1A1/BGLAP/SPP1/DMP1/SOST up; microCT-confirmed mineral. **40 of the 50 most highly expressed core matrisome genes of mouse in vivo osteocytes** were higher in the osteogenic organoids than in the hypertrophic ones; 12 of 13 canonical osteogenic TFs higher.
- Disease models reproduced: COL2A1 p.G1113C (hypochondrogenesis) — intracellular collagen II retention, sparse disorganised fibrils on TEM; COL1A1 p.W1312C (perinatal lethal OI) — reduced COL I in ECM and **no microCT-detectable mineral** (p < 0.005, n = 5), matching the patient's osteogenesis maturation defect.
- Circadian TFs (ARNTL/BMAL1, CLOCK, NPAS2, NR1D1/2, DBP, RORA, NFIL3, BHLHE41) rise with hypertrophy. Cites cartilage-specific *Arntl* knockout → **smaller proliferative zone, enlarged hypertrophic zone, reduced postnatal long bone growth**. IRX3/IRX5 up with hypertrophy and critical for the hypertrophic→osteoblast transition.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **The whole chain from a pluripotent cell to a mineralising osteocyte runs in a dish, with human cells, in defined-ish medium, with the transitions in the right order.** No step in endochondral ossification is a black box requiring an intact embryo.
2. **The articular/growth-plate fate decision is a single reagent at a single window.** TGF-β3 from day 13 gives PRG4+ permanent cartilage; its absence gives COL X+ transient cartilage. That is the same fork the previous paper exploited architecturally ([40118845](40118845.md)) — and it means the hypertrophy-resistant half of a two-domain construct is a medium choice, not a cell-source problem.
3. **Hypertrophic chondrocytes transdifferentiate into the osteoblasts** (Ku80+ human bone from human cartilage). So the "bone" end of any growth-plate-like construct does not need a separate implanted osteoblast population — the cartilage supplies it. This closes the gap in my Phase D objection in [THEORY.md](../THEORY.md) at the cell-biology level, though not at the in-situ-mechanics level.
4. **Shear accelerates it and compression is the authors' own stated next step.** They explicitly propose adding cyclic compression (citing 1 h/day entraining the chondrocyte clock and raising ECM output) to shorten time-to-hypertrophy. That is the mechanical program and the cell-construct program converging from the other direction.
5. **T3 is the terminal-hypertrophy switch, and it is a licensed, cheap, pharmacologically familiar molecule.** It is the one node in this entire paper that is an existing compound rather than a culture trick. Relevant to the compounds question, with the obvious caveat that systemic thyroid hormone is a whole-body intervention with a well-characterised toxicity profile and has never produced height in a euthyroid adult.

## Levers
- **TGF-β3 presence/absence at the chondroprogenitor stage** = permanent vs transient cartilage fate. The single most useful fork in the compound literature.
- **T3** = terminal hypertrophy + permission for chondrocyte→osteoblast transition. Needed for the bone end; would be exactly the wrong thing applied to the resting end.
- **Hypoxia→normoxia transition** as the hypertrophy timer (hypoxia holds cartilage, aeration releases hypertrophy). A mechanical/perfusion lever rather than a drug one, and it maps onto why avascular cartilage persists and vascular invasion commits.
- **FGF2 pulse** for the sclerotome→chondroprogenitor step (contrast BMP4, which gives off-target lineages).
- **Circadian entrainment (ARNTL/BMAL1) by once-daily cyclic load** — a mechanism by which a *timed* mechanical program would be expected to outperform continuous load. Directly supports the cycling in PCAC Phase B/C.

## Honest limits
- Entirely in vitro or ectopic subcutaneous. No construct was put in line with a load-bearing bone, and no longitudinal elongation was measured at all — this paper models the *lineage*, not growth. [40118845](40118845.md) is the one that measured elongation.
- Sclerotome = axial. Same caveat as the previous paper.
- Serum needed in the osteogenic medium (standing in for vascular invasion signals); the hypertrophic→osteoblast factors are undefined.
- 3 mm organoids, 13 weeks, immunodeficient mouse.
- Says nothing about adult cells, adult tissue, or a closed plate.
