# PMID 22858668 — Albro MB, ... Ateshian GA. Shearing of synovial fluid activates latent TGF-β (2012, Osteoarthritis Cartilage)

Read in full (PMC, ~26k chars).

**Methods.** Bovine (immature, adult) and adult human (57–61 y) synovial fluid sheared in tubing at 0–4000 s⁻¹ for up to 15 h. Active and total TGF-β1 measured by ELISA.

**Results.**
- Synovial fluid holds a large *latent* TGF-β1 reservoir: total 4.35 (adult bovine), 3.66 (immature bovine), 1.84 ng/mL (human). Active TGF-β1 is ~0.01–0.05 ng/mL.
- **Shear activates it in a dose- and time-dependent way:** 2.64 ng/mL (bovine, 15 h at 4000 s⁻¹); **human 0.60 ng/mL after 8 h at 3750 s⁻¹**. Total TGF-β1 is unchanged.
- The activation is protease-independent (inhibitors had no effect). Proposed mechanism: hyaluronan chain stretching/entanglement mechanically unfolds the latent complex.
- Activated TGF-β stays stable for ≥8 h after shearing stops.
- Physiological joint shear rates are 10³–10⁵ s⁻¹, so in vivo activation is likely higher.
- Caveat: high active TGF-β is pathological (osteophytes, fibrosis). Its anabolic optimum is 1–10 ng/mL.

**What it establishes for the theory.**
- **Joint sliding motion is itself a mechanical "drug-release" system.** Rubbing synovial fluid between articular surfaces releases active TGF-β, the master chondrogenic factor for adult progenitors (and the osteophyte trigger).
- That links the Prg4-null finding (friction drives chondrogenesis) and the Schätti/Gardner finding (compression + shear drives MSC chondrogenesis without exogenous TGF-β) to a physical mechanism: **sliding shear dose → active TGF-β dose → surface chondroprogenitor induction.**
- The dose is set by shear rate × duration, i.e. sliding speed, film thickness and session length. These are all mechanically programmable via motion devices (CPM) without any drug.
- Safety window: too much active TGF-β gives osteophytes and fibrosis. The motion dose must be titrated (intermittent sessions).
