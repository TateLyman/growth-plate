# PMID 30250253 — Debnath S, ... Greenblatt MB. Discovery of a periosteal stem cell mediating intramembranous bone formation (2018, Nature)

Read in full (PMC, ~65k chars incl. extended data legends).

**Findings.**
- Ctsk-cre labels periosteal mesenchyme from E14.5.
- **Periosteal stem cells (PSCs: CTSK+ CD200+ CD105− THY− 6C3−)**:
  - Show self-renewal (tertiary mesenspheres; serial transplantation in fat pad and kidney capsule) and sit at the apex of the PSC → PP1/PP2 hierarchy.
  - Are clonally tri-lineage in vitro (bone, fat, cartilage).
  - Express Runx2, Sox9, Nanog, Wnt5a.
  - Are present in long-bone periosteum *and* calvarial sutures. PSC numbers decline from P7 to P32.
- **At baseline, PSCs form bone intramembranously** (kidney capsule: no cartilage, no marrow). Non-CTSK endosteal MSCs form bone endochondrally.
- **After fracture, PSCs expand more than endosteal MSCs, contribute about half of callus chondrocytes, and when re-isolated from callus they form bone *endochondrally* (cartilage + marrow).** Injury induces intramembranous → endochondral plasticity.
- Osx deletion in PSCs (Ctsk-cre): cortical porosity ("double cortex"), lower periosteal bone formation rate, **slightly reduced long-bone length**, impaired fracture healing (non-union, persistent cartilage).
- **Humans:** a CD200+ CD105− CD90− CTSK+ population in adult femoral periosteum is multipotent (including chondrogenic in vitro) and forms intramembranous bone in vivo.

**Relevance.**
- The adult periosteum (human included) holds a self-renewing stem cell that is *intramembranous by default* but becomes *endochondral* under an injury/fracture context, i.e. instability and motion plus inflammation.
- So the periosteal/perichondrial cuff at a bone end is a source that can be *switched* into cartilage-forming mode. The question for a non-surgical theory is whether a mechanical context alone (motion, shear, TGF-β activation, micro-damage such as low-energy ESWT) can trigger that switch without fracture. No direct data; the fracture-callus analogy says motion → cartilage.
- Ties to Tsukasaki 2022: PSCs also supply Ihh to growth-plate resting-zone stem cells, a niche function.
