# PMID 35515998 — Mandibular condyle tissue reaction to LIPUS in young adult rats: µCT and histomorphometric datasets (2022, Data Brief)

Read in full (PMC, ~29k chars). Data companion to PMID 34527791.

**Exact numbers** (control n=8 vs LIPUS n=10, 28 days, 1.5 MHz / 30 mW/cm² / 20 min/day).
- **Endochondral bone growth (calcein inter-label, 21 days, middle region):** control 264.9±49.9 µm vs LIPUS **318.6±58.4 µm** (p=0.002), i.e. **+20%**.
- Total fibrocartilage thickness: 177.1±23.4 vs 241.3±65.5 µm (+36%, p<0.001).
- Proliferative layer: 43.0 vs 60.4 µm (+40%). Chondroblastic: 84.2 vs 103.3 µm (+23%). Fibrous: 55.9 vs 82.4 µm.
- Proliferative cell population 168.8 vs 215.4; chondroblastic 124.9 vs 160.8.
- Calcifying cartilage + new bone fraction: 17.6% vs 23.4%.
- Osteoid thickness: 6.0 vs 8.2 µm.
- BMD (middle) lower with LIPUS: 2.51 vs 3.40 mg/cm³ (woven, faster turnover).

**Critical interpretation.**
- *Control* 120-day rats still lay down ~265 µm of endochondral bone at the condyle in 3 weeks (~12.6 µm/day). These "young adult" rats are still actively growing at the condyle.
- So this is augmentation of ongoing growth, not reactivation after cessation. Still, a purely mechanical (acoustic) stimulus raised endochondral output ~20%.

**Levers.**
- Low-intensity acoustic pressure (nanomotion; ~MPa-level pressure oscillation at MHz with mean intensity 30 mW/cm²) acts as a chondrogenic and endochondral-rate amplifier in a responsive cartilage.
- It requires the cartilage to already exist. It is a gain knob, not a switch.
