# 29899452 — Targeted therapy in patients with PIK3CA-related overgrowth syndrome

**Citation.** Nature 2018;558:540–546. PMC7610773. Read (abstract and framing; full body not read — flagged).

## Model / method
A **postnatal mouse model** of PROS/CLOVES (congenital lipomatous overgrowth, vascular malformations, epidermal naevi, scoliosis/skeletal and spinal syndrome), caused by somatic mosaic gain-of-function *PIK3CA* mutations. Then **19 human patients with PROS treated with BYL719 (alpelisib)**, a PIK3CA inhibitor.

## Exact findings
- The postnatal mouse model partially recapitulates the human disease; **BYL719 prevented and improved organ dysfunction.**
- **In all 19 patients, BYL719 improved disease symptoms.** Specifically: "previously intractable vascular tumours became smaller, congestive heart failure was improved, **hemihypertrophy was reduced**, and **scoliosis was attenuated**."
- **"The treatment was not associated with any substantial side effects."**
- First direct evidence supporting PIK3CA inhibition as a therapeutic strategy in PROS.
- Alpelisib is now licensed for PROS (as Vijoice).

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **PI3K–AKT is a drug-controlled, postnatal, progressive skeletal and soft-tissue overgrowth programme, demonstrated bidirectionally in humans.** One direction by a mosaic activating mutation that produces progressive segmental overgrowth; the other by an approved small-molecule inhibitor that **reduces hemihypertrophy** in all treated patients. Few nodes in this corpus have human evidence in both directions.
2. **It is explicitly postnatal and progressive.** PROS overgrowth is not a fixed developmental endowment — it continues, and it can be arrested and partially reversed pharmacologically. That is the kind of programme [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) §17 is looking for: one that still runs in a grown body.
3. **It connects to the heterotopic-ossification arm of the corpus.** *Acvr1*^R206H signals through PI3K/AKT/mTOR, and mTOR and PI3K inhibitors reduce HO in FOP mice ([38672135](38672135.md)). So PI3K sits downstream of both a mosaic overgrowth mutation and a gain-of-function BMP receptor.
4. **And it names the obvious asymmetry.** The growth-promoting direction of this axis is a somatic oncogenic mutation. There is no agent that safely activates PI3K, and there should not be — *PIK3CA* is one of the most commonly mutated oncogenes in human cancer. **This is a programme one can switch off, not on.** Its value here is as proof that postnatal skeletal overgrowth is a real, maintained, drug-responsive process, not as a lever.

## Honest limits
- **I read the abstract and framing only**; the body is fetched but unread. Nothing above should be weighted beyond abstract level.
- Open-label, 19 patients, no control arm; outcomes are clinical improvement, not quantified length.
- **Hemihypertrophy "reduced" is the shrinking of a pathological overgrowth back toward normal — not evidence that anything can be made longer than normal.**
- The activating direction is a mosaic mutation in a major human oncogene.
