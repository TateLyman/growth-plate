# 24875294 — SHP2 regulates chondrocyte terminal differentiation, growth plate architecture and skeletal cell fates

**Citation.** PLoS Genet 2014;10(5):e1004364. PMC4038465. Read in full (Introduction, all Results, figure and supplementary-figure legends, Discussion).

## Model / method
Four arms.
1. **RNA-seq of primary chondrocyte pellet cultures** from *Ptpn11*^fl/−;CMV-CreERT2 mice ± daily 4-OHT (SHP2 depletion; >90% recombination at week 1, near-complete depletion at week 3), and wild-type pellets ± the **MEK1/2 inhibitor U0126**, harvested at weeks 1 and 3. Transcripts changing in 4-OHT-treated wild-type pellets were excluded.
2. **Postnatal mosaic inactivation in vivo**: *Col2a1-CreER; Ptpn11*^fl/fl; Rosa26^mTmG, tamoxifen for 5 days from week 1, analysed at weeks 3, 7, 10. ~50% of chondrocytes recombined.
3. **Fsp1-Cre; Ptpn11^fl/fl** (fibroblast inactivation), analysed at weeks 3 and 20 (n = 6).
4. **Excised human exostoses** from metachondromatosis (MC, *PTPN11*) patients, compared with a multiple hereditary exostoses (MHE, *EXT1/EXT2*) exostosis.

## Exact findings
**SHP2 loss and MEK inhibition both hold chondrocytes at the early-hypertrophic stage.**
- SHP2 depletion raised proliferative→early-hypertrophic transcripts and lowered late-hypertrophic ones. **Largest single change: *Ihh* up 6-fold.** 44% of a pre-defined "upper zones" transcript set rose >1.3-fold; 34% of the "lower zones" set fell >1.3-fold (20 up and 54 down at the stricter 1.6-fold, p < 0.05 threshold).
- **U0126 reproduced it**: 53% (w1) and 35% (w3) of the SHP2-responsive transcripts changed the same way (p < 0.001 vs chance). *Ihh* and *Panx3* up >3-fold. Late-HZ transcripts for **matrix degradation, apoptosis, angiogenesis and osteoclastogenesis** all fell; gene-ontology enrichment for matrix degradation and remodelling among the decreased set. qRT-PCR confirmed *Ihh* and *Phospho1* up, *Spp1* down.
- Transcription factors: **up** — *Mef2c*, *Tcf7*, *Dlx5*, *Msx2*, *Hey1*, *Sox9* (promote hypertrophy / inhibit terminal differentiation); **down** — *Foxp1*, *Rbpj* (inhibit hypertrophy / promote terminal differentiation), plus *Egr1* with its targets *Spp1* and *Hpse*, and *Fosl1*/FRA1.
- **Cell size increased** after SHP2 depletion or MEK1/2 inhibition (stated in the Figure S7 legend, where the rise in mitochondrial transcripts is attributed to more mitochondria per cell "due to the increase in chondrocyte cell size").

**In vivo, postnatally.**
- By weeks 7 and 10: **clear expansion of the vertebral growth plates, osteophyte-like lateral cartilaginous outgrowths, enchondroma-like cartilage islands below the plate, and an increase in chondrocyte cell size.** By week 10 some plates were completely resorbed while others remained expanded; severity varied between animals.
- **The COLX-positive (early-hypertrophic) zone expanded from the lowest ~3 cells per column in controls to >10 cells.** SPP1 (terminal marker) was minimal — "the hypertrophic zone was expanded due to an increase in the number of chondrocytes that had not yet reached the late-hypertrophic stage."
- p-ERK1/2 and FRA1, normally confined to the hypertrophic zone, were scattered and disorganised.
- **Osteoclast recruitment and vascular invasion were not significantly different.** The conversion hardware was intact; only the cells' readiness changed.
- **COLX-positive chondrocytes in the lateral outgrowths were arranged into columns oriented perpendicular to the main axis of the growth plate.**
- Enchondroma-like lesions "likely arose from chondrocytes displaced from the growth plate due to ectopic ossification in upper regions of the growth plate."

**Fibroblasts become chondrocytes, slowly, at ligament attachments.**
- Fsp1-cKO mice: **nothing at 3 weeks; by 20 weeks, exostosis-like outgrowths on the metacarpals and phalanges of all 6 mice**, and on fibula, tibia, femur and/or calcaneus in some.
- Ectopic cartilage at **bone–ligament attachment sites**, on the **cortical surfaces of femur and tibia**, in the **interosseous membrane between radius and ulna**, and **adjacent to ligament attachment sites on the articular surface of the tibia**. Fsp1-lineage fibroblasts "acquired the round morphology of chondrocytes."
- **Paracrine recruitment.** Lesions in both the Col2a1 and Fsp1 models contained mixtures of recombined (mutant) and **un-recombined (wild-type)** chondrocytes — "SHP2-deficient cells can signal to neighboring wild-type cells and induce them to inappropriately undergo chondrogenesis."

**Human lesions.** MC exostoses had variable, disorganised COLX. **The MHE (EXT1/EXT2) exostosis had the normal growth-plate architecture of a cartilage cap with a narrow COLX zone at its base.**

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
This paper changes three things in [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md).

1. **The coupling condition of §6 now has a druggable dial, and licensed drugs sit on it.** ERK/MEK signalling is what carries chondrocytes from **early-hypertrophic to late-hypertrophic (terminal)**. Turn it down and cells accumulate in the enlarged early-hypertrophic state with *Ihh* up 6-fold, the COLX zone expanded >3-fold, cell size up, and the degradation/apoptosis/angiogenesis/osteoclast programme off. Turn it up and terminal differentiation and replacement by bone proceed. **That is exactly the addition-versus-conversion dial §6 says the whole scheme turns on, and MEK1/2 inhibitors are approved drugs** (and the inverse, FGF-18/sprifermin, raises ERK and is the one compound with an adult human cartilage-thickening RCT, [26138203](26138203.md)). The two directions would have to be phased against each other, which is precisely the Stage-5 ratchet.
2. **It is a second, independent demonstration that *h_max* and *n* are separable, now in vivo and postnatally.** Cell size rose while the conversion step was blocked. Taken with the bumetanide experiment — 35% of longitudinal growth lost by shrinking cells with **hypertrophic cell number unchanged** ([20200963](20200963.md)) — the volume term is now independently manipulable in both directions by two unrelated interventions.
3. **Polarity is re-specifiable postnatally.** The lateral outgrowths contained **COLX+ columns oriented perpendicular to the growth plate's main axis.** A growth plate's growth direction is not fixed by the limb's embryonic axis; a new axis can be established in a postnatal animal. [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) relies on articular cartilage having the right polarity already, and flags osteophyte formation as the failure mode when polarity is wrong. This says polarity is a local property that a local perturbation can set — which is both the risk and the opportunity.
4. **Paracrine recruitment is an amplifier on *n*, and it is the only one I have found.** ~50% of chondrocytes were mutant, yet lesions contained large numbers of wild-type cells drawn in. [COMPOUNDS.md](../COMPOUNDS.md) §2's proliferation wall says no compound raises adult chondrocyte number. **Here a minority of initiating cells recruited a majority wild-type population into chondrogenesis.** If that paracrine signal can be identified, it is a route to the *n* term that does not require a mitogen.
5. **Fibroblasts at ligament attachments and on cortical surfaces are a competent reserve, and the knee is on the list.** Ectopic cartilage formed **on the articular surface of the tibia adjacent to ligament attachments**, and on femoral and tibial cortical surfaces. This is the same anatomical compartment as the Gli1+ periosteal and Scx+/Tppp3+ tendon progenitors ([35844802](35844802.md), [38672135](38672135.md)) and the groove of Ranvier ([28438606](28438606.md)) — now with a fourth, independent marker (Fsp1) and a defined molecular switch.
6. **MHE exostoses are organised ectopic growth plates.** The control comparison states it plainly: MHE cartilage caps "display relatively normal growth plate architecture" with a narrow COLX zone at the base. **So a properly zoned, functional growth plate can form de novo, postnatally, in an ectopic location, in humans, and produce bone.** That is the single most direct human proof that the boundary of [40118845](40118845.md) can assemble in an adult body without being manufactured — and it is the reason the EXT/heparan-sulfate literature now matters.

## Levers
- **MEK1/2 → ERK1/2** as the early-hypertrophic → late-hypertrophic (terminal) switch. **Down** = bigger cells, expanded COLX zone, *Ihh* up, conversion off. **Up** = terminal differentiation, matrix degradation, angiogenesis, osteoclastogenesis, replacement by bone. Approved MEK inhibitors exist; FGF-18 pushes the other way.
- **SHP2/PTPN11** upstream of it (allosteric SHP2 inhibitors exist, same direction as MEK inhibition).
- **Ihh** up 6-fold on SHP2 loss — the pre-hypertrophic signal that drives column proliferation via PTHrP feedback. So this node moves *both* terms.
- **The transcription-factor set**: *Mef2c*, *Tcf7*, *Dlx5*, *Msx2*, *Hey1*, *Sox9* (hold early-hypertrophic) versus *Foxp1*, *Rbpj*, *Egr1*, *Fosl1*/FRA1 (drive terminal).
- **The unidentified paracrine chondrogenic signal** that recruits wild-type neighbours. The highest-value unknown in this paper.
- **Fsp1-lineage fibroblasts at bone–ligament attachments** as a competent, addressable adult reserve.

## Honest limits
- **Genetic loss-of-function throughout.** No gain-of-function, no pharmacological attempt to *increase* growth, and no bone-length measurement anywhere in the paper.
- The phenotype is **a benign tumour**. Expanded plates, osteophyte-like outgrowths, enchondromas, and by week 10 **some growth plates completely resorbed**. The MEK-inhibition arm of any scheme would be aiming at a lesion and relying on timing to avoid it.
- Severity was variable between mice; the Col2a1 phenotype is reported qualitatively (expanded, disorganised) without plate-height or growth-rate numbers.
- U0126 is a tool compound, 3 weeks in pellet culture; approved MEK inhibitors have substantial systemic toxicity (rash, retinopathy, cardiomyopathy, ejection-fraction decline) and are oncology drugs.
- Fsp1-Cre is not a clean fibroblast driver and the paper does not address its known expression outside fibroblasts.
- Mouse, juvenile onset (tamoxifen at week 1). Nothing here starts from a fused plate or an adult skeleton.
- The "increase in chondrocyte cell size" is stated in text and a supplementary legend; I did not find a quantified volume or diameter measurement.

## Chase
- **Refs 11, 12, 13 — the earlier *Ptpn11* conditional models**, including inactivation in **groove-of-Ranvier perichondrial progenitors** inducing ectopic chondrogenesis and exostoses (ref 11), and the report that postnatal chondrocyte inactivation alters vertebral growth plate thickness and organisation (ref 13). Priority: ref 13 may contain the plate-height numbers this paper omits.
- **Refs 14, 15 — *Erk1/Erk2* inactivation in mesenchymal cells giving ectopic cartilage clusters in the perichondrium, and in chondrocytes disrupting columnar organisation, causing premature hypertrophy and delaying replacement by bone.** The cleanest statement of the ERK node; unread.
- Ref 2, 3 — the human MC *PTPN11* genetics and second-hit model.
- Refs 22, 23 — the narrow COLX zone at the base of MHE cartilage caps (**the organised ectopic growth plate**).
- Refs 5–7 — *IDH1/IDH2* gain-of-function in Ollier disease and Maffucci syndrome (enchondromas only).
- Ref 8 — *EXT1/EXT2* in MHE.
- **Anything identifying the paracrine signal from SHP2-deficient chondrocytes that recruits wild-type cells.** Not resolved here.
- Quantified cell-volume data for SHP2-depleted or MEK-inhibited chondrocytes. Not in this paper.
