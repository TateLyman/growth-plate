# 26138203 + 32098758 — Intra-articular sprifermin (recombinant human FGF-18) increases adult human cartilage thickness

**Citations.**
- Eckstein et al., *Arthritis Rheumatol* 2015;67(11):2916–2922. PMC5061102. Post hoc of the Lohmander phase 2 RCT.
- Eckstein et al., *Ann Rheum Dis* 2020;79(4):525–528. PMC7147175. Post hoc of **FORWARD**, NCT01919164.
Both read in full (abstracts, methods, results, discussion).

## Why this is the decisive benchmark
Sprifermin is **the only compound in the entire corpus shown, in a randomised placebo-controlled trial in adult humans, to increase the thickness of adult cartilage in vivo.** It is therefore the honest upper bound on what a drug — any drug, delivered optimally, straight into the target joint — currently does to adult human cartilage mass. Every claim about compound-driven adult growth has to be read against this number.

## Exact numbers
**Phase 2 (2015 post hoc).** n = 168, age ≥40 (mean 61), 69% women, symptomatic femorotibial OA not confined to the medial compartment. **Intra-articular sprifermin 10, 30 or 100 µg, once weekly × 3 weeks, repeated once at 3 months.** Coronal MRI at baseline, 3, 6, 12 months; femorotibial cartilage segmented across **16 subregions**; subject-specific location-independent thinning (ThCTnS) and thickening (ThCTkS) sum scores.
- Thinning: **−591 ± 617 µm** on 100 µg (n = 57) vs **−921 ± 777 µm** on placebo (n = 18). Mean difference **+331 µm (95% CI 24–685), P = 0.03**.
- Thickening: mean difference **+237 µm (95% CI 34–440), P = 0.028**.
- In the parent trial, the significant effects were on **total and *lateral*** femorotibial cartilage thickness; **no significant dose response in the central medial compartment** — i.e. the effect appeared where mechanical challenge is low, not where load is high.

**FORWARD (2020 post hoc).** Five arms, 1:1:1:1:1 — 30 µg q6mo, 30 µg q12mo, 100 µg q6mo, 100 µg q12mo, placebo (three weekly injections per cycle). Change to **24 months**, compared indirectly with Osteoarthritis Initiative healthy subjects.
- Thinning score: 100 µg q6mo vs placebo, difference **+334 µm (114–554)** — thinning reduced to a level similar to healthy subjects.
- Thickening score: **+425 µm (267–584)** for 100 µg q6mo, **+450 µm (305–594)** for 100 µg q12mo, **+139 µm (19–259)** for 30 µg q6mo; thickening "more than doubled versus healthy subjects."

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **A compound really can make adult human cartilage thicker, and the answer is ~0.2–0.45 mm summed across sixteen subregions over one to two years.** That is the ceiling, measured, in humans, with the drug injected directly into the joint. For scale: the hPSC two-domain construct in [40118845](40118845.md) elongated at **0.61 mm per week**. The gap is roughly two orders of magnitude, and it is a gap between a compound acting on existing tissue and an architecture generating new tissue.
2. **Thickening a joint surface is not lengthening a bone, and this trial makes the distinction concrete.** The thickening sum score adds up positive changes across 16 subregions of one knee. Even if every micrometre of it lay normal to the joint surface at the right place — it does not — the per-surface figure is a fraction of the total. Longitudinal gain from this would be invisible.
3. **The effect was largest where load was lowest.** Significant in the lateral compartment, absent in the central medial. The authors raise exactly the right question: whether anabolic drugs only work "in joint regions where mechanical challenges are potentially small and cartilage thickness may be static." If so, a compound's anabolic effect and the mechanical environment needed for *directed* growth are in tension — a point that bears directly on [THEORY.md](../THEORY.md), which asks for both at once.
4. **It confirms the invariant from the other direction.** FGF-18 is a genuine chondrocyte mitogen, and this is the cleanest test of a mitogen in adult human cartilage anyone has run. Sub-millimetre. The pattern across T3 ([38664820](38664820.md)), OSK ([41786976](41786976.md)), dasatinib ([39402753](39402753.md)), kartogenin ([33467170](33467170.md)) and Yoda1 ([35844802](35844802.md)) — matrix up, cell number flat or down — is not an artefact of in-vitro models. The one drug that was taken all the way into a human RCT with a dimensional endpoint landed in the same place.
5. **Note the methodological point the authors make about averaging.** Subregional cartilage change is highly variable between knees and often runs in opposite directions, cancelling out in a mean. Any future trial with a dimensional endpoint — including a hypothetical length endpoint — needs this kind of subject-specific, location-independent analysis or it will measure nothing.

## Levers
- **FGF-18 / sprifermin, intra-articular** — a real, trialled, dose-responsive adult human cartilage anabolic. Relevant as a component for maintaining a cartilage domain, not as a growth driver. Note FGF-18 also appears as an endogenous output of dasatinib treatment ([39402753](39402753.md)) and as a regulator of chondrocyte proliferation, hypertrophy and cartilage vascularisation in [37483459](37483459.md).
- **Thinning/thickening sum scores with ordered values** — the measurement method any serious attempt at this would have to adopt.

## Honest limits
- **Both papers are post hoc analyses** of trials whose primary endpoints were different, with multiple dose arms and small matched placebo groups (n = 18 and 12 and 7 in the 2015 analysis).
- OA patients aged ~60. Not healthy young adults.
- Thickness by MRI segmentation, not histology; sum scores are constructed endpoints, not direct measurements of a single dimension.
- Sprifermin never achieved a symptomatic benefit and has not been approved. Structural change without clinical benefit.
- No growth plate, no length, no polarity, no bone.

## Chase
- **Lohmander et al., the parent phase 2 RCT** (total and lateral femorotibial cartilage thickness, dose response). Primary.
- **Hochberg et al., FORWARD primary publication (JAMA 2019)** — the prespecified 2-year total femorotibial cartilage thickness result. Needed: the primary endpoint in absolute millimetres, not sum scores.
- The 5-year FORWARD extension data.
- Refs 11, 12 — the subregional variability papers motivating the location-independent method.
