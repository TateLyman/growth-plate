# 28473691 + 40323130 — Brachyury+ notochordal cells persist in the adult human nucleus pulposus, and raising TBXT dose gives benign notochordal tumours and chordoma

**Citations.**
- Thorpe et al., "Notochordal and nucleus pulposus marker expression is maintained by sub-populations of adult human nucleus pulposus cells," *Sci Rep* 2017;7:1501. PMC5431421. **Read in full** (abstract, introduction, methods and results framing).
- "The TBXT rs2305089 SNP links the benign notochordal cell tumour and chordoma," *J Pathol* 2025. PMC12146811. **Abstract read in full; body not read** (fetched, lower priority than the mechanism papers — flagged).

## Exact findings
**Adult human NP (28473691).** Gene expression (N = 60), immunohistochemistry (N = 56) and flow cytometry (N = 5) across a range of ages and degeneration grades.
- **NP markers FoxF1, Pax-1, keratin-8/18 and carbonic anhydrase-12, and notochordal markers brachyury (T), galectin-3 and CD24, are expressed in cells of the NP irrespective of age or degeneration.**
- Flow cytometry identified **a small number of CA12+ Gal3+ T+ CD24+ cells**, suggesting a **notochordal-like sub-population in adult NP tissue**.
- Adult NP cells have routinely been likened to articular chondrocytes, but produce a clearly distinct ECM with consequences for tissue hydration and stiffness.
- Conventional view the paper is correcting: "adult NP lacks identifiable notochordal cells."

**TBXT dose (40323130, abstract).** **Benign notochordal cell tumours (BNCTs)** — benign intraosseous notochordal proliferations in vertebrae — are **associated with chordomas and with the *TBXT* rs2305089 A-allele**, with enrichment of the AA genotype versus controls. In engineered hPSC mesoderm/notochord models the A allele gave **higher *TBXT* expression and activation of its regulatory network**; heterozygotes showed **enriched Wnt/β-catenin and EMT pathways and faster migration**. Conclusion: the A allele **predisposes to BNCTs and ultimately to chordomas**.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**This is the axial analogue of the exostosis story, and it is mostly a dead end for length — but an instructive one.**

1. **The adult human spine retains a proliferation-competent developmental lineage with a named master regulator.** Brachyury/TBXT is the transcription factor that drives notochord specification and **posterior axial elongation** in the embryo, and Brachyury+ cells are still present in adult NP at every age and degeneration grade. **That is the one place in the adult axial skeleton where a cell type belonging to the axial-elongation programme survives.**
2. **And the dose-response is known: more TBXT gives a benign tumour, then a malignant one.** Exactly parallel to the appendicular case — heparan-sulfate dose gives exostoses and then chondrosarcoma in 2–5% ([23821404](23821404.md)); ERK-low in groove progenitors gives enchondromas and osteochondromas and then chondrosarcoma ([25003010](25003010.md)); TBXT-high gives BNCT and then chordoma. **Three different lineages, three different molecular nodes, the same shape: adult proliferative competence is retained, is dose-gated by one node, and its uncontrolled form is a tumour.** That is now a pattern rather than a coincidence, and it is the strongest general statement my corpus supports about adult skeletal growth capacity.
3. **But a BNCT is an intraosseous mass, not a longer vertebra.** Notochordal proliferation fills marrow space; it does not add height at an endplate. **And the structure Brachyury elongates — the tailbud neuromesodermal progenitor pool — does not exist after embryogenesis.** Brachyury in an adult NP cell is a lineage marker in a terminal niche, not a latent elongation programme. **I am flagging this as the most seductive dead end I have found: the gene name is right, the cell is there, the adult proliferative competence is real, and the output is a tumour in the wrong geometry.**
4. **What the NP *is* good for is mechanical, and it is underrated.** The nucleus pulposus is a **hydrostatically pressurised fluid chamber** with a distinct, highly hydrated ECM. The vertebral endplate growth cartilage faces it rather than facing an articulating contact surface. Hypertrophic chondrocytes **tolerate hydrostatic stress and die under principal compressive and deviatoric stress at ~0.023 MPa without shielding** ([33063669](notes/33063669.md)), and the whole point of knee joint distraction is to remove contact stress while preserving fluid-pressure oscillation ([21565898](21565898.md)). **The disc supplies that condition anatomically, with diurnal cycling built in, at up to 48 endplate surfaces.** This is a structural argument for the axial site that [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) rev. 2 §13 does not make, and it needs the intradiscal-pressure and endplate primaries to stand up.

## Levers
- **The nucleus pulposus as a natural hydrostatic chamber** apposed to the vertebral endplate growth cartilage — Stage 4 shielding and Stage 5 cycling supplied anatomically, ×48.
- **Notochordal cell-conditioned medium**, which stimulates matrix production in NP explants and drives MSC differentiation toward an NP phenotype (28089818, 20565707, 26421447, 25370929 — all unread). A paracrine anabolic source adjacent to the endplate.
- **Anti-lever: TBXT / brachyury.** Dose-gated route to BNCT and chordoma; wrong geometry for length.

## Honest limits
- 28473691 is a **phenotyping study**: markers, immunohistochemistry and a small flow-cytometry panel. No function, no proliferation assay, no growth, no endplate.
- The CA12+Gal3+T+CD24+ population is "a small number" in n = 5, and the authors say only that it "suggests the possible presence" of a notochordal-like subpopulation.
- **Brachyury is not demonstrated to do anything in adult NP.** No lineage tracing, no perturbation.
- **For 40323130 I have read only the abstract.** The body is fetched but unread; nothing above from it should be treated as more than abstract-level.
- The hydrostatic-chamber argument in point 4 rests on intradiscal pressure and vertebral endplate anatomy papers I have **not** read (18590419, 4095820, 21213210 — on the needed list) plus 15296209, which came back abstract-only.
- Nothing here measures vertebral height, endplate growth, or stature.
