# PMID 25603997 — Kozhemyakina E, ... Lassar AB. Identification of a Prg4-expressing articular cartilage progenitor cell population in mice (2015, Arthritis Rheumatol)

Read in full (PMC, ~32k chars).

**Methods.** Prg4^GFPCreERt2 knock-in (heterozygous; Prg4+/−) crossed to R26-lacZ/mTmG. Tamoxifen at E17.5, or P21–P30; tracing to 18 months. EdU.

**Findings.**
- E17.5 Prg4+ cells sit only at the surface of the forming joint. Their progeny form **columnar clones spanning the full depth of articular cartilage by 2 months and extending below the tidemark (into calcified cartilage) by 12 months**. They never contribute to subchondral bone (Col1-CreER negative).
- **The deepest cells are "born first"**, i.e. appositional growth from a superficial progenitor pool that self-renews.
- P21-labelled cells reach at most two-thirds of the depth by 18 months, never the tidemark.
- Postnatal proliferation (EdU P21–P31) is mainly at or near the surface.
- **Expansion is greatest in the central tibial condyle, where load is highest. Wheel running induces Prg4 and proliferation of Prg4+ cells there** (their ref 27: Ogawa et al., Genes Dev 2014).
- Prg4 expression spreads into deeper zones with age.
- The opposing interpretation (Decker 2017) found mostly volume/rearrangement-driven growth with low proliferation. Decker suggests the Prg4+/− (lubricin-haploinsufficient) knock-in could itself raise turnover via friction.

**Relevance.**
- Juvenile articular cartilage grows **appositionally like a growth plate turned upside down**: a superficial progenitor zone feeds cells downward, and the oldest cells end up at and below the tidemark (calcified cartilage, then replaced by bone). That is a slow endochondral bone-end growth front, the same topology as the mandibular condyle (Tsutsumi-Arai 2024).
- **Mechanical loading (running) drives surface-progenitor proliferation and Prg4**, via the fluid-flow shear → CREB pathway (Ogawa 2014).
- Combined with Decker's caveat, slight loss of boundary lubrication may boost turnover. That is consistent with Prg4-null friction → chondrogenesis.
- In the adult mouse (≥1 month) the deep front is "frozen": new clones don't reach the tidemark within 18 months. The adult appositional engine runs very slowly, which is the rate to be raised.
