# 40118845 — Recapitulation of endochondral ossification by hPSC-derived SOX9+ sclerotomal progenitors

**Citation.** Nat Commun 2025. PMC11928506. DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-58122-9. Read in full (Results + Discussion + limitations).

## Model / method
Human iPSC/ESC (UC01, H1, H9, IMR904, NC3-1) → primitive streak → presomitic mesoderm → (somite-skipping: SAG + LDN193189 + XAV939) → **SOX9+ sclerotomal progenitors (scl-progenitors)**. Two divergent inductions from the same progenitor:
- **Chondro** route: spheroid + chondrogenic induction medium (low BMP2 + TGFβ3 + bFGF), day-14 → chondroprogenitors (Cps).
- **Osteo** route: micromass + **high-dose BMP2 (100 ng/ml) + SAG** → *osteogenesis-committed early chondrocytes* (COL10A1-negative, i.e. **not** pre-hypertrophied).
Then: fuse the two spheroid types 1:1, culture 7 d, transplant under mouse kidney capsule. NOD-SCID hosts; 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 16 week harvests.

## Exact numbers
- Derivation efficiency **99.21% ± 0.24%** across 5 hPSC lines (vs ~82% traditional).
- Expansion: maintained ≥2 months / ~15 passages, **2×10⁵ → 3×10⁹ cells (~15,000-fold)**, doubling time **3.882 days**. Requires simultaneous SHH activation + BMP inhibition + ROCKi + bFGF + KSR + BSA.
- **ITGA9** = surface marker allowing reporter-free FACS isolation (validated in E10.5 mouse sclerotome, Sox9+Pax9+Nestin−).
- **Fusion construct elongation (the central number): length 0.55 mm → 2.43 mm over 4 weeks; width 0.53 → 1.98 mm; aspect ratio 1.0 → 1.4. Linear regression growth rate 0.61 mm/week ≈ 31 mm/year, stated as "approximately half the growth rate of reported embryonic long bones" — and since a real long bone has two plates, the per-plate rate is comparable to a primary growth plate.**
- Proliferative zone itself elongated progressively from week 1 (own quantification, p = 1.0e-6 wk4 vs wk0).
- Nanoindentation of the 4-week construct: modulus **26.1 MPa (R/PZ) → 4.16 GPa (spongiosa)**, 160-fold; hardness **2.15 MPa → 0.51 GPa**, 240-fold. **Hypertrophic zone had lower modulus and hardness than resting/proliferating zones** — independent confirmation of the softness of the hypertrophic zone (cf. [33063669](33063669.md)).
- scRNA-seq at 4 weeks: full human growth-plate spectrum — epiphyseal/resting (COL2A1+ UCMA+ IGF2+ MKI67− TOP2A− PCNA−), proliferating (MKI67+ TOP2A+ PCNA+), prehypertrophic (IHH+ ALPL+ COLX-low), hypertrophic (COLX-high VEGFA+ MMP13+), osteoblast (SP7+ DLX5+ SPP1+ BGLAP2+ IBSP+). Integrates with primary **human embryonic long bone at PCW 8.0**.
- Human skeletal stem cells recoverable from the 8-week graft by the Chan gating: CD235a− CD45− TIE2− CD31− **PDPN+ CD146− CD73+ CD164+**.
- 16 weeks: cortical + trabecular bone, marrow cavity, adipocytes (human origin, SCD1+KU80+), host CD45+ haematopoiesis, LEPR+ stroma, CXCL12/SCF niche cytokines, EMCN+ sinusoids.
- Cps alone (no osteo partner): repaired proximal medial tibial plateau and distal femoral trochlea defects, PRG4+ superficial layer, **COL X and COL I negative at 12 weeks, no contribution to subchondral bone**, AkaLuc single-cell tracing showed no abnormal growth.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
This is the strongest existing demonstration that **the bottleneck is architecture, not drive** — the finding that has organised this whole project.

1. **A functional, polarised, elongating growth plate assembled itself** — "the formation of growth plate-like structures might be a cell-autonomous process." No exogenous PTHrP, no IHH gradient supplied, no printed scaffold, no imposed mechanics. What was supplied was a *juxtaposition*: one domain that refuses to hypertrophy next to one domain committed to ossify. The resting→proliferative→hypertrophic axis, the columns, and their orientation (longitudinal, pointing at the ossification centre) all emerged from that boundary condition.
2. **It did not form in either spheroid alone** (p = 0.0011 and p = 2.5e-7 for PZ width vs chondro-only and osteo-only). The interface *is* the growth plate. This is the single most important control in the paper for my purposes: it says a growth plate is not a tissue type you implant, it is a boundary you create between two tissue states.
3. **The magnitude clears the user's bar.** 0.61 mm/week ≈ 3 cm/year per interface, in cartilage derived from human cells, in a host with closed-ness irrelevant (ectopic). Nothing acting on existing adult tissue in this literature comes within an order of magnitude of that (cf. knee joint distraction, +0.6 mm/year, [21565898](21565898.md); vosoritide +1.57 cm/year but only with open plates, [42650460](42650460.md)).
4. **It is a human cell source that is expandable and markerable.** 15,000-fold expansion and an antibody-sortable surface marker are the two things that usually kill a cell-source paper; neither is missing here.
5. **The same progenitor makes the hypertrophy-resistant half.** The Cps held a cartilage fate for 12 weeks in a joint and never made bone — so the "upper" domain of the construct is not a tissue that drifts into ossification, which is the failure mode of every MSC-based cartilage construct.

## Levers
- **The two-domain boundary** as the design principle for any post-fusion growth attempt: a hypertrophy-resistant chondral domain apposed to an ossifying domain, with the ossification centre on the diaphyseal side so the polarity points the right way. The gradient self-assembles; it does not have to be engineered.
- **High BMP2 (100 ng/ml) + SAG** as the switch that produces an osteogenesis-*committed* early chondrocyte rather than a forced hypertrophic chondrocyte. The distinction matters: COL10A1 was absent at day 12 and only induced in vivo at 4 weeks, so the ossification step was endogenous and staged, not pre-run in a dish.
- **SHH activation + BMP inhibition** as the maintenance condition for the progenitor pool — the same pairing (Hh on, BMP/Wnt off) that the resting-zone niche uses in vivo. Convergent with the niche literature in [39563452](39563452.md)/[19098525](19098525.md).
- **ITGA9** for isolation.
- **Kidney capsule >> subcutaneous** for synchronised ossification (1 week vs still-cartilaginous). Vascular access governs the timing of the ossifying half.

## Honest limits (what this does *not* show)
- **Ectopic, not in-line.** Everything grew free in a kidney capsule or subcutis. Nothing here was inserted into a loaded long bone, and nothing shows the construct can push two segments of a fused adult diaphysis apart against periosteum, muscle and the surrounding soft-tissue envelope. The elongation measured is the growth of a free body, not work done against load.
- **Axial, not appendicular.** The authors flag it themselves: sclerotome makes vertebrae and ribs, not limbs. Whether this makes an appendicular growth plate is untested.
- **Embryonic scale and embryonic mechanics.** Final construct 2.43 mm; spongiosa hardness matched *fetal* bone. Scaling to a human metaphysis (tens of mm diameter) is unaddressed, and the 0.61 mm/week rate should not be assumed to survive scale-up — diffusion-limited cartilage is exactly the tissue where it would not.
- **Immunodeficient host.** NOD-SCID. Allogeneic hPSC-derived constructs in an immunocompetent recipient is a different experiment.
- **Requires transplantation for vascular invasion and marrow** — the ossifying half is host-dependent.
- **Not a compound.** This is a cell-and-architecture result. It belongs in the compounds document as the reason the compound question has the shape it does, not as an answer to it.
