# PMID 27499874 — Positional information in axolotl and mouse limb extracellular matrix is mediated via heparan sulfate

Phan AQ, Lee J, Oei M, Flath C, Hwe C, Mariano R, Vu T, Shu C, Dinh A, Simkin J, Muneoka K, Bryant SV, Gardiner DM. *Regeneration (Oxf)* 2015;2(4):182-201. PMC4857728. doi:10.1002/reg2.40

**What I read:** the complete PMC full text — abstract, Introduction, all seven Results sections with all six data tables transcribed in full, the complete Discussion, and all Materials and Methods subsections (ethics, animals/surgery, ECM preparation, artificial ECM preparation, ECM grafting, histology, RT-PCR with primer lists, in situ hybridization, GAG sulfation assay). Not an abstract read.

## Model
Axolotl Accessory Limb Model (ALM), 12–15 cm animals. Anterior stylopod full-thickness skin wound (2–3 mm square, muscle intact) + surgically deviated brachial nerve = an ectopic blastema that grows and then regresses without pattern. Into that wound: **decellularized ECM** (2 mol/L urea, 15 min) from anterior or posterior axolotl limb skin, or from **mouse** hindlimb skin (FVB/NJ, E11.5 → PN9), or a wholly **artificial ECM** (Sigma porcine intestinal mucosa heparan sulfate + Sigma calf-skin type I collagen). Endpoint: whole-mount Alcian Blue / Alizarin Red skeletal preparation at 3 months.

## Exact numbers

**Axolotl ECM, position-specific and opposite-signed (Table 1):**

| Graft | n | Blastema | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| No ECM | 23 | 21 (91%) | 0 |
| **Anterior ECM** | 8 | **0 (0%)** | 0 |
| Anterior ECM → posterior wound | 5 | 5 (100%) | 0 |
| **Posterior ECM** | 14 | 10 (71%) | **3 (30%)** |
| HepIII-treated anterior ECM | 15 | 14 (93%) | 1 (7%) |
| HepIII-treated posterior ECM | 12 | 4 (33%) | 1 (25%) |

Anterior ECM abolished blastema formation in 8/8 (p < 0.001) — and **heparin lyase III rescued it completely**. Posterior ECM induced elongated, tapered, jointed phalanx-like cartilage elements.

**Artificial ECM — collagen + purified HS only, and biphasic (Table 2):**

| Graft | n | Blastema | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collagen only | 6 | 6 (100%) | **0** |
| Low HS (2.5 mg/ml) | 7 | 3 (43%) | 1 (33%) |
| **Med HS (5.0 mg/ml)** | 21 | 19 (90%) | **8 (42%)**, p < 0.001 |
| High HS (10 mg/ml) | 5 | 5 (100%) | **0** |

Med-HS patterns contained multiple skeletal elements with joints, some bifurcated, all with distal tapering phalanx morphology. **Two off-the-shelf reagents at the right ratio induced patterned skeleton in 42% of blastemas; collagen alone did nothing.**

**The inhibitor is a bound factor, not the sugar itself (Table 3):** anterior ECM washed with 1 or 2 mol/L NaCl → blastema formation restored to 80% and 100% (p < 0.001), but **still zero pattern**. So HS-bound factors gate *blastema formation*; HS sulfation pattern gates *pattern formation*. Two separable layers.

**FGF2 and FGF8 are opposite-signed (Tables 4, 5):** anterior ECM + FGF2 → 83% blastema, **30% pattern**; after HepIII, **62% pattern**. Anterior ECM + FGF8 → 83% blastema, **0% pattern** at any HS state. Posterior ECM + FGF2 → **60% pattern** (vs 30% untreated, p = 0.03); posterior ECM + FGF8 → **0%**, FGF8 completely abolished posterior ECM's inductive power. Authors: FGF2 and FGF8 both displace an inhibitory factor (as NaCl does), but only FGF2 induces pattern.

**Sulfotransferases are the writers.** Axolotl: of 10 HS sulfotransferases, 6 expressed in regeneration, 3 differentially — **NDST2 and HS6ST1 anterior > posterior; HS3ST1 posterior > anterior**, peaking at medium–late bud. Anterior skin carries **2.5× more sulfated GAG** (p < 0.01) and a higher proportion of N-sulfated GAG.

**Mammalian positional information exists — and expires (Tables 7, 8):**

| Mouse donor stage | Anterior pattern | Posterior pattern |
|---|---|---|
| E11.5 | 0/8 | 0/8 |
| PN1 | 0/6 (and 50% blastema) | 0/18 (**22% blastema** — strongly inhibitory) |
| **PN3–4** | 0/9 | **3/8 (37%)** |
| **PN5–7** | 2/11 (18%) | **3/5 (60%)** |
| **PN9** | 1/6 (17%) | **0/6** |

HepIII abolished mouse posterior ECM's induction (0/10). And mouse sulfotransferase expression tracks it: NDST1, NDST2, HS6ST1, HS2ST1 anterior-high at PN3–4; HS3ST2 and HS3ST3a posterior-high; **by PN9 NDST1, HS3ST1, HS3ST2 and HS3ST3a are undetectable and NDST2 flips posterior.** The positional code and its functional output disappear together.

Mouse ECM induced **Alizarin-Red-positive calcified** elements from axolotl cells, not Alcian-Blue cartilage: "these cells differentiated into calcified tissue that would be the fate of mouse mesenchymal cells, rather than into cartilage that would be their normal axolotl fate." The matrix dictated the mammalian fate.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **Positional information is a chemically defined, cell-free, transplantable material property — specifically the sulfation pattern of heparan sulfate in loose connective tissue ECM.** Not a cell state, not a transcription factor. A matrix modification.
2. **It exists in mammals.** Mouse limb skin ECM carried position-specific, HS-dependent instructions potent enough to make axolotl cells build skeletal elements. This is the functional link between the amphibian literature and anything mammalian, and it is the reason the positional-identity route is not purely an axolotl curiosity.
3. **The mammalian positional system has a documented closing date — around PN9 in mouse — and the closure is enzymatic.** Four sulfotransferases drop below detection and one inverts. This is a *second* closure, independent of and earlier than growth-plate fusion: the skeleton stops knowing how much of itself there should be long before it stops being able to grow. For this project that reframes the problem: post-closure there may be two locks, not one — no responsive template (ACTIVATION.md §4) *and* no positional instruction.
4. **The repression is active, material and reversible.** Anterior ECM blocks regeneration outright; heparin lyase III, high salt, or FGF2 each lift it. This is the mechanistic resolution of de-repression instance #1 (the heparan-sulfate perichondrial anti-chondrogenic border, Surfen): HS is a presentation scaffold holding an anti-regenerative factor, and the factor is displaceable.
5. **Dose is biphasic and narrow.** Low HS blocks blastema formation, medium induces pattern, high blocks pattern. Along with the talarozole window in [40494878](40494878.md), that is twice in two papers that this class of lever has an optimum rather than a direction.
6. **FGF2 vs FGF8 is a clean sign split.** FGF2 induces and enhances pattern; FGF8 abolishes it. Relevant because the competence cocktail in [31026439](31026439.md) uses FGF-2 **and** FGF-8 together, and because FGF-18/sprifermin is the one adult-human cartilage compound with a measured effect. Which FGF matters, not "FGF signalling."

## Levers
- **Heparan sulfate sulfation state** — the actual code. Writers: NDST1/2, HS6ST1, HS2ST1, HS3ST1/2/3a. Erasers: SULF1/SULF2 (the authors cite Wang & Beck 2015 — sulf2 anterior-high in *Xenopus* hindlimb blastemas). Sulfotransferases and sulfatases are enzymes, i.e. in principle druggable; I have found no selective inhibitors yet and should look.
- **Heparanase / heparin lyase** — de-represses by destroying the scaffold.
- **FGF2** — displaces the inhibitor *and* posteriorizes.
- **Exogenous HS at an intermediate dose** — sufficient on its own to induce pattern.

## Anti-levers
- **FGF8** — abolishes pattern formation from both anterior and posterior ECM.
- Anterior-type (high N-sulfated, HS6ST1/NDST2-written) HS — actively anti-regenerative.
- HS too low or too high.
- PN9-and-later mammalian ECM — inert or nearly so.

## Honest limits
1. **Still an axolotl blastema as the readout.** The mouse contribution is the *matrix*; the responding cells are always axolotl, and always in a nerve-deviated wound. Nothing here shows a mammalian cell reading a mammalian positional code in an adult mammal.
2. **Donors were E11.5–PN9 mice. No adult mouse ECM was tested.** The PN9 result makes adult inertness likely but it was not measured — and that is exactly the experiment this project needs. Added to NEEDED_FULLTEXTS.md as an experiment, not a paper.
3. **HPLC-MS failed to detect HS compositional differences** ("data not shown"). The sulfation-difference model rests on sulfotransferase transcript levels plus a bulk Blyscan assay, not on direct structural characterisation. The authors are candid about this; it is the weakest link in the paper.
4. Several key comparisons are underpowered: posterior ECM + HepIII is n = 12 with 4 blastemas (p = 0.11, authors call it "noteworthy but not statistically significant" and decline to explain it); PN3–4 anterior vs posterior is p = 0.07; high-HS is n = 5.
5. ECM-induced patterns were consistently **hypomorphic** relative to live skin grafts. The authors' explanation is duration — cell-free matrix cannot sustain the signal — and they verified outgrowths had stopped changing over three observations across ~3 months.
6. **The inhibitory factor is never identified.** Salt-displaceable, HS-bound, FGF2-competable, anterior-restricted — and anonymous. This is a named gap.
7. Urea decellularization is crude; the authors note that further washing destroyed the activity, so what is being grafted is not a defined preparation.

## Chase list
- **The identity of the anterior HS-bound anti-regenerative factor.** Highest-value unknown in this paper. Candidate classes: an HS-binding BMP/Wnt antagonist (noggin, gremlin, sclerostin, SOST, Wise/SOSTDC1), or an HS-binding chemokine.
- **Wang & Beck 2015** — sulf1/sulf2 in *Xenopus* limb development and regeneration.
- **Nogami et al. 2004; Kobayashi et al. 2010** — HS6ST1 in the chick limb bud, the third species.
- **Phan et al. 2015** (posterior decellularized ECM induces digit-like structures) — cited in [31026439](31026439.md), same group; check whether it is this paper or a companion.
- Selective small-molecule inhibitors of NDST2, HS6ST1 or SULF1/2 — do any exist?
- Whether **HS sulfotransferase expression is position-graded in the adult human perichondrial groove or vertebral endplate**. If the code is still written there, the template question and the positional question collapse into one target.
