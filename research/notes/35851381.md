# PMID 35851381 — Tsukasaki M, ... Takayanagi H. Periosteal stem cells control growth plate stem cells during postnatal skeletal growth (2022, Nat Commun)

Read in full (PMC, ~47k chars).

**Model.** Mice: Prmt5flox/Δ Ctsk-Cre (selective loss of periosteal stem cells, PSCs); Ctsk-Cre iDTR ablation from 3 weeks; Ihhflox/flox Ctsk-Cre; Ihhflox/flox Col10a1-Cre; RANKL-flox control for Cre leak; kidney-capsule transplants.

**Findings.**
- PSCs (CD200+CD105− periosteal cells, Ctsk+) are progressively lost in the Prmt5 model through mis-splicing of Postn (periostin). Recombinant periostin partly rescues colony formation.
- Mice lose periosteal bone width *and* growth-plate column length, femur length and body size, worsening with age.
- Postnatal DT ablation of PSCs (from 3 weeks, 8 weeks DT) reduced bone length and width and progressively shortened columns.
- **Ihh is exclusively expressed in PSCs** among periosteal progenitors.
  - Ihhflox/flox Ctsk-Cre: severe loss of bone length and width; by 11 weeks *complete loss of the growth plate and trabecular bone*.
  - At 3 weeks the plate looked normal, but CD73+CD49e+ resting-zone stem cells (RZSCs) were reduced in frequency, number and Ki67; Ptch/Gli1 were down in RZSCs.
- Hypertrophic-chondrocyte Ihh (Col10a1-Cre) is essential early. PSC-derived Ihh becomes more important postnatally, as the hypertrophic zone regresses (~3 weeks onward).
- Ctsk-Cre does not leak into hypertrophic chondrocytes (RANKL control).
- PSCs alone transplant to make intramembranous bone. In fracture they can go endochondral (Debnath 2018).

**What it establishes for post-fusion growth.**
- The periosteum/perichondrium is an active niche partner for growth-plate stem cells, via horizontal Hh supply.
- Periosteal stem cells persist into adulthood and can make cartilage in fracture repair.
- Growth-plate maintenance late in growth depends on *lateral* Hh from the periosteum. The periosteum is therefore both a potential *source* of chondrogenic progenitors and a *niche-signal* provider in adults.

**Levers.**
- A post-fusion growth zone could be seeded from periosteal SSCs, the adult cell type closest to the perichondrial ring (groove of Ranvier).
- Ihh/Hh tone is required for RZ self-renewal. Mechanical loading is known to upregulate Ihh in chondrocytes (cf. condylar papers), so cyclic loading could be a non-drug Hh source.
- Periostin, a matrix protein strongly induced by mechanical strain in periosteum, supports PSC self-renewal. That is a mechanical route to a larger PSC pool.

**Chase.** Debnath S et al. 2018 Nature, "Discovery of a periosteal stem cell mediating intramembranous bone formation"; Newton 2019 (Shh from SOC); Duchamp de Lageneste 2018 (periosteum and periostin).
