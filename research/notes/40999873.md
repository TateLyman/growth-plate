# 40999873 — The Sirt1–Piezo1 axis promotes bone formation and repair in mice

**Citation.** Adv Sci (Weinh) 2025. PMC12667516. DOI 10.1002/advs.202510103. Read in full (Results §1–7 and the formulation sections; the RSV@GelDa and YC-RSV arms read in summary).

## Model / method
Mouse femoral fracture (intramedullary nail) **and a mouse femoral distraction osteogenesis (DO) model** (distraction started 1 wk post-op, 1 wk of stretching, analysed at 6 wk / 45 d). Chondrocyte-specific Piezo1 deletion: **Piezo1^fl/fl;Acan-CreERT2 + tamoxifen (cKO)**. Pharmacology: **Yoda1** (Piezo1 agonist), **SRT2104** (specific Sirt1 activator), **resveratrol (RSV)**, delivered as oral yeast-microcapsule formulations (YC-SRT, YC-RSV) and an RSV@GelDa hydrogel. scRNA-seq reanalysis (GSE150291, GSE154247). Human cartilage callus specimens. AlphaFold2/ChimeraX structural work.

## Exact numbers / findings
- **Piezo1 is chondrocyte-lineage-specific**: at single-cell resolution Piezo1 is highly and specifically enriched in the chondrocyte lineage while **Piezo2 is absent from chondrocytes**. Piezo1 rises through fracture healing (1→4 wk) and is **higher in DO cartilage callus than in resting or proliferating growth-plate chondrocytes**. **PIEZO1 is upregulated in human cartilage callus** vs control tissue.
- **Chondrocyte Piezo1 cKO:** smaller, less mineralised callus with larger unmineralised gaps; lower BMD, BV/TV, Tb.Th; more cartilage and fewer trabeculae at 2 and 4 wk; fewer Runx2+ cells. In the **DO model**: reduced bone mass in the distraction gap, **a large amount of fibrous tissue, trabeculae failing to close the gap**, and fewer Osx+, Ocn+, Bmp2+ and Vegfa+ cells.
- **The growth-plate finding — central for this project: Piezo1 loss in chondrocytes INCREASED growth-plate width and INHIBITED endochondral ossification of the growth plate**, with increased osteoclast formation (Fig. S2).
- **Yoda1** in the fracture model: higher callus mineralisation, trabecular bone volume and BMD, more Runx2. **In the DO model: increased callus mineralisation, thickened trabeculae, accelerated closing of the distraction gap, and more VEGFA.**
- **Sirt1 binds, deacetylates and activates Piezo1.** Co-IP confirmed binding; SRT2104 reduced Piezo1 acetylation; Sirt1 overexpression *and* pharmacological activation raised Piezo1 protein dose-dependently; confocal colocalisation. SRT and Yoda1 both produce Ca²⁺ influx, abolished in Ca²⁺-free Ringer and by si-Piezo1. SRT's effect was blocked by **3-TYP** (Sirt inhibitor) but not panobinostat (pan-HDAC) — so it is Sirt, not HDAC.
- **The mechanistically decisive detail:** in cells carrying the **L1341K/L1344K** mutations that break Piezo1 mechanical force transduction, **Yoda1 no longer opened the channel but SRT still did**. "The calcium flow caused by Piezo1 deacetylation is not via the conventional mechanical stress." Structurally, Sirt1 hydrogen-bonds Piezo1 at **K1525 (near the beam)** and **K2174 (C-terminus, highly conserved)**; K2174 is identified as the key site.
- **Piezo1 activation suppresses Sirt1 expression** — a negative feedback loop.
- **Oral YC-SRT localised only to the fracture site**, peaking at day 7 and maintained ≥14 days; release 6% over 100 h at pH 7.4 vs 12% at pH 6.6 (the acidic fracture microenvironment). Encapsulation efficiency 50.9%, loading 31.7 µg/mg. No change in body weight or major-organ histology.
- **SRT2104's pro-healing effect was largely abolished in Piezo1-cKO mice**, including the rise in Runx2 and Osx. **YC-RSV also promoted callus mineralisation.**

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **Piezo1 is the mechanical channel of the growth plate's conversion step, and it has existing chemical openers.** Deleting it in chondrocytes made the growth plate *wider* and stopped it ossifying — the same thicker-cartilage-without-conversion dissociation as palovarotene ([30226468](30226468.md)) and the same gap as knee joint distraction ([21565898](21565898.md)). Activating it does the reverse. This identifies the specific protein that converts load into longitudinal-growth throughput.
2. **A compound opens the mechanosensor by a route that does not require mechanics.** The L1341K/L1344K result is the key: Sirt1-mediated deacetylation opens Piezo1 even when the force-transduction machinery is broken. Pharmacology here is not merely mimicking load — it bypasses the need for it. For a closed adult diaphysis, where [23009361](23009361.md) shows the stiffness contrast makes it impossible to strain bone into growth (0.25–0.48 MPa cartilage vs ~2000 MPa bone), this is the one way around the physics that does not require surgery.
3. **It works in a distraction osteogenesis model — the only established procedure that actually lengthens adult human bone.** Yoda1 accelerated gap mineralisation and closure; Piezo1 cKO left fibrous tissue and an unbridged gap. DO is surgical and therefore outside the mechanical theory's constraints, but as a *compound* datum it is the strongest available: a small molecule changing the rate of real bone lengthening in an adult animal.
4. **Two orally available, already-existing compounds reach the same channel: SRT2104 and resveratrol.** SRT2104 has been through human trials in several indications. Resveratrol is sold over the counter. Both activate Piezo1 through Sirt1, and the effect requires chondrocyte Piezo1. Stated plainly: **this is the most plausible existing-compound route in the entire corpus to acting on the growth-plate conversion step, orally, in an adult.**
5. **And the same ceiling applies.** Nothing here increased chondrocyte number or produced length in an intact bone. Every outcome is mineralisation rate, bone volume, density and gap closure — throughput through a template that an injury or a surgeon created. Piezo1 cKO *widened* the plate, which means Piezo1 activation would be expected to *narrow* it: faster conversion of a finite pool, i.e. earlier exhaustion. Applied to a plate, Yoda1 or SRT2104 would plausibly accelerate closure, not delay it. That is the opposite of what height requires, and it is the single most important caution attached to this lever.

## Levers
- **Piezo1** — chondrocyte-specific (Piezo2 is absent), required for growth-plate endochondral ossification, mechanically gated, with openers available.
- **Yoda1** — direct agonist, requires intact force-transduction residues.
- **SRT2104** — Sirt1 activator, opens Piezo1 by deacetylation at **K2174**, force-transduction-independent, orally deliverable, prior human trial experience.
- **Resveratrol** — the natural Sirt1 activator route; poor bioavailability, hence the formulation work.
- **3-TYP** as the specific off-switch for the Sirt1 arm.
- **Acidic-microenvironment-targeted oral delivery (yeast microcapsules)** — a site-targeting mechanism that does not require injection, demonstrated to localise only to the active lesion. The spatial-control problem again, addressed by formulation rather than by route.
- **Anti-lever: the direction of effect on the plate.** Piezo1 activation should accelerate conversion and plate narrowing. For *delaying* closure, this axis would have to be inhibited, not activated — and Piezo1 cKO's wider plate with blocked ossification is cartilage that does not become bone, which is a swelling, not height.

## Honest limits
- Mouse throughout, except for PIEZO1/SIRT1 immunostaining of human callus specimens.
- Every endpoint is bone quality in a callus or a surgical distraction gap. **No measurement of bone length anywhere.**
- Growth-plate results are in a supplementary figure and reported only as width, ossification and osteoclast number; no longitudinal growth rate, no tibial length.
- Resveratrol's effect sizes rest on a custom formulation; the compound's clinical record is poor.
- Piezo1 is expressed in erythrocytes, endothelium, lung, bladder, pancreas and baroreceptors. Systemic Piezo1 agonism is not characterised for safety; the organ panel here is 4 weeks in mice.
- Piezo1 activation suppressing Sirt1 means the two arms interfere, which complicates any attempt to combine or sustain them.
