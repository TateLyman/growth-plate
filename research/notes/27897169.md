# 27897169 — EZH1 and EZH2 promote skeletal growth by repressing inhibitors of chondrocyte proliferation and hypertrophy

**Citation.** Nat Commun 2016;7:13685. PMC5477487. Read in full (Introduction, all Results, Discussion).

## Model / method
Mouse: **complete *Ezh1* knockout + cartilage-specific *Ezh2* knockout** ("Ezh1/2 mice"). Growth plate quantitative histology, BrdU, immunostaining, in situ hybridisation, **laser-capture microdissection of proliferative (PZ) and hypertrophic (HZ) zones with microarray**, ChIP for H3K27me3, siRNA, primary chondrocyte monolayer and pellet culture, **E18 fetal metatarsal organ culture**, and the EZH1/2 inhibitor **UNC1999**.

## Exact findings — this is the gate on the volume actuator
**The lesion is in IGF *availability*, not IGF or its receptor.**
- Terminal hypertrophic cell size was decreased in Ezh1/2 mice.
- **Igf2, not Igf1, is the predominant ligand in the HZ. Neither ligand, nor Igf1r, nor Igf2r differed between knockout and wild type.**
- **Only *Igfbp3* and *Igfbp5* were significantly upregulated, specifically in the HZ**, confirmed at protein level by IHC.
- **ChIP: H3K27me3 was significantly enriched at the *Igfbp3* and *Igfbp5* promoters in wild-type chondrocytes and absent in Ezh1/2 chondrocytes.** No H3K27me3 enrichment at *Ihh* or *ColX*. So IGFBP3/5 are **direct** PRC2 targets in the hypertrophic zone.

**The causal chain, closed four ways in culture.**
1. **UNC1999** (EZH1/2 inhibitor) reduced H3K27me2/me3, raised Igfbp3 (monolayer), raised Igfbp5 (pellet), raised **both** in metatarsal culture, and **markedly reduced hypertrophic cell size** in all three systems, with reduced *Col10a1* and *Ihh*.
2. **IGF-1 for 3 days significantly increased bone growth and hypertrophic cell size** in fetal metatarsals.
3. **High IGF-1 completely overrode UNC1999** — hypertrophic cell size was equally increased by IGF-1 alone and by IGF-1 + UNC1999. The mechanism is pure ligand sequestration.
4. **Exogenous IGFBP3 or IGFBP5 alone reduced hypertrophic cell size as much as UNC1999, with no additive effect when combined.** IGFBPs are sufficient.
- **IGF-1 did not significantly affect chondrocyte proliferation.** IGF-1 acts on *h_max* only.

**The proliferative zone, separately.** The decrease in proliferation is due in part to derepression of the CDK inhibitors ***Cdkn2a/b* (Ink4a/b)** — also a PRC2 target.

**Transcriptome.** PZ: 49 genes down, 231 up. HZ: 97 down, 146 up (p < 0.001, FC > 1.3). More genes up than down, as expected for loss of a repressive mark. **Targets of H3K27 repression depend on differentiation state** — little overlap between PZ and HZ. Pathway analysis implicated **cell-cycle G1/S and Wnt/β-catenin and glucocorticoid receptor signalling in the PZ**, and **IGF signalling plus thyroid hormone receptor and retinoid signalling in the HZ**.

**Human context stated in the introduction.** *EZH2* lies in a locus associated with adult human height; heterozygous *EZH2* or *EED* mutations cause **Weaver and Weaver-like syndrome — skeletal overgrowth with accelerated skeletal maturation**; *NSD1* and *SETD2* (H3K36 methyltransferases) cause Sotos and Sotos-like overgrowth; *DNMT3A* mutations cause a distinct overgrowth syndrome.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**This is the single most important mechanistic finding of the whole project after the two-domain boundary, because it identifies the gate on the term that carries 59% of axial growth.**

1. **Hypertrophic cell size is set by local free IGF availability, and local free IGF availability is set by IGFBP abundance.** Not by IGF concentration, not by receptor level. Change the binding proteins and the cells change size; flood the system with ligand and the binding proteins stop mattering. That is as clean a mechanism as this literature offers, and it is closed in both directions in three culture systems.
2. **IGFBP3 and IGFBP5 are direct H3K27me3 targets in the hypertrophic zone.** So **the brake on the volume actuator is literally an epigenetic repressive mark on two binding-protein genes.** [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) rev. 2 frames adult quiescence as active restraint rather than exhaustion; here the restraint is a chromatin mark with a named substrate and a named consequence.
3. **It explains two of my "weak" compound leads, which were in fact hitting this exact node and which I under-rated because I was measuring against cell number.** Dasatinib raises *IGF1* while lowering ***IGFBP1 and IGFBP3*** in adult human chondrocytes ([39402753](39402753.md)). T3 raises ***PAPPA* 2.7–3.4-fold** in adult human articular cartilage explants from 60–76-year-olds ([38664820](38664820.md)) — and PAPP-A is the protease that cleaves IGFBPs. **Both agents raise free local IGF in adult human cartilage. Neither paper measured hypertrophic cell volume, which is the endpoint that would have shown whether it mattered.**
4. **It is the fourth independent dissociation of *h_max* from *n*.** IGF-1 changed cell size and not proliferation; *Igf1*-null cells are 30% shorter with unchanged hypertrophic cell *number* ([23485973](23485973.md)); bumetanide removed 35% of growth with cell number unchanged ([20200963](20200963.md)); and four rat plates differing 3–7× in rate produce identical cell numbers ([18404738](18404738.md)).
5. **The PRC2 sign problem, stated honestly.** Mouse *Ezh1/Ezh2* double loss **impairs** growth; human heterozygous *EZH2* partial loss **causes overgrowth** (Weaver). These point opposite ways. Possible reconciliations: complete double knockout versus partial heterozygous loss (non-monotonic dose response); different cell types or developmental windows; or the human overgrowth arising through accelerated skeletal maturation rather than greater rate. **Unresolved, and it is the reason EZH2 inhibitors (tazemetostat is approved) should be expected to *shrink* hypertrophic cells rather than promote growth** — the opposite of what the Weaver phenotype naively suggests. Anyone reasoning from "EZH2 loss makes people tall" to "give an EZH2 inhibitor" would be acting against the direct experiment.
6. **It names the HZ-specific regulatory company: thyroid hormone receptor and retinoid signalling**, both already in my corpus as HZ levers ([38664820](38664820.md), [34793695](34793695.md)), now independently implicated by an unbiased pathway analysis of the hypertrophic zone.

## Levers
- **IGFBP3 / IGFBP5 abundance** as the direct brake on hypertrophic cell size. Lowering either raises cell size; raising either lowers it, sufficiently and non-additively with EZH inhibition.
- **PRC2 / H3K27me3** as the upstream repressor of those binding proteins (and of *Cdkn2a/b* in the PZ). **Direction matters: more PRC2 activity = less IGFBP = bigger cells.**
- **Local free IGF-1/IGF-2.** Exogenous IGF-1 overrides the whole brake and increases bone growth in organ culture.
- **IGF-2** as the actual predominant HZ ligand — not IGF-1. Worth noting against the entire GH/IGF-1 literature.
- **Cdkn2a/b (p16/p15)** as the PRC2-repressed proliferation brake — and p21/p16 are what OSK partial reprogramming lowers ([41786976](41786976.md)).
- **Anti-lever: EZH1/2 inhibitors (UNC1999, and by extension tazemetostat).** They raise IGFBPs and shrink hypertrophic cells.

## Honest limits
- **Mouse, genetic loss of function, juvenile/fetal.** No adult tissue, no closed plate, no articular cartilage.
- **The direction of the main experiment is growth impairment.** Nobody showed that reducing IGFBP3/5 or raising PRC2 activity makes a bone longer than normal.
- UNC1999 is a tool compound; the culture systems disagreed about which IGFBP rises (monolayer: Igfbp3; pellet: Igfbp5; metatarsal: both).
- Hypertrophic cell size is reported as reduced without, in the text I read, a quantified volume in fl or µm³.
- **The human/mouse sign conflict with Weaver syndrome is unresolved** and the paper does not resolve it either.
- IGF-1 at "high concentration" overriding the effect is a pharmacological override, not a physiological argument.
