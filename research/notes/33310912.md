# PMID 33310912 — Hydrostatic pressure prevents chondrocyte differentiation through heterochromatin remodeling (2021, J Cell Sci)

Read in full (PMC, ~50k chars).

**Model.**
- Primary mouse femoral-head articular chondrocyte progenitors (P20), immortalised and non-immortalised, plus ATDC5 cells.
- Cyclic hydrostatic pressure (HP) 1 MPa at 0.3 Hz for 6 h. Live imaging at 10 MPa constant.
- In vivo comparison: P2 mouse articular cartilage, surface vs medial zone.

**Results.**
- HP increased Sox9 protein (post-transcriptional), Col2a1, Mcam (progenitor), Acan and Col2a1 mRNA. It **decreased Pthr1, Runx2 and Col1a1**: anti-hypertrophic, anti-osteogenic.
- HP induced **quiescence**: lower EdU, G0/G1 accumulation, less transcriptional elongation (RNAPII-S2P), p21 up (not p16/p27), no cell death.
- Within minutes the nucleus shrinks. H3K9me3 constitutive heterochromatin falls (especially at the lamina) and H3K27me3 rises, giving slight chromatin decompaction. **Fully reversible within 3 h** of stopping HP.
- Suv39H1 knockdown (lowering H3K9me3) phenocopies HP: higher Sox9, lower hypertrophic markers, less replication.
- HP lowers replicative stress/DNA damage (γH2AX). Inducing replication stress (etoposide) lowers Sox9, i.e. pushes differentiation.
- In vivo P2 articular cartilage: the surface zone is Sox9-high, Ki67-low, γH2AX-negative and H3K9me3-low (high-pressure region). The medial zone is cycling and γH2AX+ with higher H3K9me3.
- Proposed sensing: mechanosensitive cation channels → ER Ca²⁺ release → H3K9me3 loss.

**What it establishes for the theory.**
- **Cyclic HP is a quiescence/progenitor-maintenance signal.** It keeps the surface progenitor pool of articular cartilage immature and non-proliferating. That is exactly why adult articular cartilage *does not* grow: the very surface where progenitors live is held quiescent by joint pressure.
- **Releasing or modulating HP** (cyclic periods of unloading, or lower pressure) lets progenitors enter the cycle and mature. Proliferation plus maturation is what growth needs.
- The effect reverses within 3 h, so the **duty cycle** (the fraction of time pressurised vs relieved) is a programmable switch between "maintain progenitor" and "proliferate/mature".
- Combined with Stokes/Noonan (90% of growth during recumbency, when the plate is unloaded): **growth happens in unloaded windows, while pressure periods preserve stemness**. A growth-sustaining mechanical program might alternate HP (preserve and renew the progenitor pool) with unloaded or tensioned windows (proliferation, hypertrophy, output).

**Levers.**
- HP duty cycle as a mode switch.
- HP amplitude (1–10 MPa physiological; 25 MPa suppressive).
- Unloading/traction windows for output.
