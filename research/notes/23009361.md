# PMID 23009361 — Narváez-Tovar CA, Garzón-Alvarado DA. Computational modeling of the mechanical modulation of the growth plate by sustained loading (2012, Theor Biol Med Model)

Read in full (PMC, ~20k chars).

**Model.**
- 2D finite-element growth plate: G = n_p × h_max, with h_max a function of the axial stress change Δσ.
- Validated against Stokes rat proximal tibia data at Δσ = +0.1, 0, −0.1, −0.2 MPa over 23 days (2.8–3.7% error).
- Assumes PTHrP–Ihh is insensitive to load (Turing-pattern stability; Villemure 2005 in vitro).

**Useful parameters (rat proximal tibia).**

| Δσ (MPa) | Reserve (µm) | Proliferative (µm) | Hypertrophic (µm) | Prolif. cells/column | Hypertrophic cells/column | G (µm/day) | h_max (µm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0.1 | 13.9 | 288 | 175 | 23 | 7 | 228.7 | 36.2 |
| 0 | 12.3 | 264 | 162 | 22 | 6 | 217.0 | 35.0 |
| −0.1 | 11.9 | 269 | 129 | 22 | 5 | 183.4 | 35.9 |
| −0.2 | 10.9 | 242 | 123 | 18 | 5 | 163.8 | 34.3 |

- Column spacing 40 µm.
- Tissue moduli: reserve zone 0.48 MPa, proliferative 0.25, hypertrophic 0.27; trabecular bone 2000 MPa. Growth cartilage is **~4 orders of magnitude more compliant** than bone.
- D_hmax: +0.35 per MPa in tension (piecewise in compression).

**Relevance.**
- Quantitatively, +0.1 MPa sustained tension gives only +5% growth, so tension is a modest gain knob. The dominant determinant is *whether a proliferating, hypertrophying column apparatus exists at all*.
- Stiffness contrast: once fused, the region is ~2000 MPa bone. Any applied tension or compression is borne by bone, and the strain is ~10⁻⁴ (µε range), not the ~10% that modulates cartilage.
- **So no external mechanical load can "stretch" fused bone into growth. A compliant cartilaginous layer must first exist for mechanics to act on.** This is the central constraint for the theory.
- The model omits the ring of LaCroix and dynamic loading.
