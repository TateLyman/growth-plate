# 37425751 — Novel mouse model of Weaver syndrome displays overgrowth and excess osteogenesis reversible with a KDM inhibitor

**Citation.** bioRxiv 2023 (preprint), PMC10327066. Read in full (Introduction, all Results, Discussion, Conclusion).

## Model / method
Knock-in mouse for **the most common human Weaver syndrome missense variant, EZH2 p.R684C**. Mouse embryonic fibroblasts for H3K27me3 quantification; *Ezh2*^R684C/+ heterozygotes for skeletal phenotyping by direct measurement and **micro-CT**; **dynamic histomorphometry** (sequential calcein and Alizarin Red at 5 weeks) for mineral apposition rate; bone-marrow MSC osteogenic differentiation with RNA-seq; and the **H3K27 demethylase (KDM6A/KDM6B) inhibitor GSK-J4**.

## Exact findings
- *Ezh2*^R684C/R684C MEFs: **global depletion of H3K27me3** — R684C is a loss-of-function/hypomorphic variant.
- **Female *Ezh2*^R684C/+ mice: significantly higher body weight at 8 weeks (p = 0.0006). No effect in males.**
- **Critically: no difference in femur length, tibia length, total body length, or distal femoral trabecular structure.**
- What did change was **cortical bone at the femoral mid-diaphysis**: cross-sectional tissue area significantly increased in both sexes (p = 0.032 male, p < 1×10⁻⁶ female), bone area/tissue area reduced in females (p = 0.028), cortical thickness trending up (p = 0.057).
- **Dynamic histomorphometry at 5 weeks: periosteal mineral apposition rate significantly increased in females (p = 0.001); endosteal MAR increased in both sexes (p = 0.033, p = 0.028).** So the phenotype is **excess osteoblast activity driving appositional (radial) growth.**
- *Ezh2*^R684C/+ BM-MSCs showed higher osteogenic potential in vitro; RNA-seq of derived osteoblasts showed **collective dysregulation of the BMP pathway and of osteoblast differentiation**.
- **GSK-J4 (KDM6A/6B inhibition) substantially reversed the excessive osteogenesis, both transcriptionally and phenotypically.**
- The authors' framing: "writers and erasers of histone marks exist in a fine balance to maintain epigenome state," and epigenetic modulating agents have therapeutic potential for Mendelian disorders of the epigenetic machinery. They note overgrowth is **more subtle in mouse models of Weaver syndrome than in human individuals.**

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**1. It resolves the PRC2 sign problem, and the resolution is that Weaver overgrowth is not longitudinal.** [27897169](27897169.md) showed that *Ezh1/Ezh2* loss in chondrocytes **impairs** growth by derepressing *Igfbp3/5* and shrinking hypertrophic cells, which looked contradictory against Weaver syndrome being an overgrowth disorder. **Here the Weaver mouse has normal femur, tibia and body length and instead has increased cortical cross-sectional area with raised periosteal and endosteal mineral apposition.** So:
   - **In the chondrocyte lineage, PRC2 loss costs *length*** (IGFBP derepression → smaller hypertrophic cells).
   - **In the osteoblast lineage, PRC2 loss adds *width*** (BMP derepression → more apposition).
   Two lineages, opposite signs, one mark. Human Weaver overgrowth is tall stature with **advanced bone age** — a maturation-timing phenotype plus soft tissue — and the mouse does not reproduce the height.
**2. Therefore EZH2 inhibitors are the wrong direction for length, and the correctly-signed epigenetic lever is the opposite one.** Tazemetostat and the EZH2-inhibitor class would, by [27897169](27897169.md), raise IGFBP3/5 and shrink hypertrophic chondrocytes. **The prediction that follows from these two papers together — and that nobody has tested — is that KDM6A/6B inhibition (GSK-J4 class) should deepen H3K27me3 at the *Igfbp3* and *Igfbp5* promoters, lower IGFBP abundance, raise local free IGF, and therefore increase hypertrophic cell volume.** That is a specific, cheap, falsifiable experiment: GSK-J4 on fetal metatarsal culture with hypertrophic cell size as the endpoint, as the mirror of the UNC1999 arm already run in [27897169](27897169.md). **I am flagging it clearly as my inference, not a published result.** The confound to watch is that GSK-J4 would simultaneously suppress osteoblast apposition, which may or may not matter for length.
**3. It demonstrates that the writer/eraser balance is pharmacologically tunable in bone in vivo-relevant cells.** That is the general point: the "active restraint" frame of [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) rev. 2 is implemented partly by histone marks, and those marks have approved and tool-grade drugs on both sides.
**4. And it hands the one lifelong-active growth mechanism a named epigenetic control.** Periosteal mineral apposition is the mechanism that stays active for life in fish, birds and mammals ([32852849](notes/32852849.md)), and here its **rate is raised by partial PRC2 loss and lowered by KDM6 inhibition.** Nothing in my corpus has asked whether periosteal apposition can be directed to add length rather than width — but this says its rate is drug-tunable, which is a prerequisite for asking.

## Levers
- **KDM6A / KDM6B (H3K27 demethylases)** and **GSK-J4** — the eraser side of the balance. **Predicted, untested, correctly-signed lever on hypertrophic cell volume.**
- **Periosteal and endosteal mineral apposition rate** as an epigenetically tunable, lifelong-active process.
- **Anti-lever: EZH2 inhibitors (tazemetostat, UNC1999).** Predicted to shrink hypertrophic chondrocytes via IGFBP derepression.

## Honest limits
- **A preprint.**
- **No longitudinal growth phenotype at all** — femur, tibia and body length all normal. The overgrowth is weight and cortical area, in females only for the weight and MAR effects.
- Sexually dimorphic and modest; the authors say so ("more subtle in mouse models than in human individuals").
- GSK-J4 was used **in vitro on differentiating BM-MSCs**, not in an animal and not on cartilage.
- No growth plate analysis, no chondrocyte measurement, no hypertrophic cell volume anywhere in the paper.
- GSK-J4 is a tool compound with substantial off-target activity, including on KDM5.
- My prediction in point 2 chains two papers in different cell types and different systems and has not been tested.
