# PMID 19442616 — Zhang P, Turner CH, Yokota H. Joint loading-driven bone formation and signaling pathways predicted from genome-wide expression profiles (2009, Bone)

Read in full (PMC, ~27k chars).

**Model.** Female C57BL/6 mice, ~14–16 weeks old. Ankle loading: 0.5 N, 5 Hz, 3 min/day for 3 days. Histomorphometry at 3 tibial sections; microarray at 1 h after the 3rd load; qPCR up to 2 weeks.

**Results.**
- Mid-tibial strain: ankle loading 12.4 µε/N vs knee loading 4.8 µε/N; 11 µε at 0.5 N.
- Cortical area +~10% at all sections (e.g. 0.51 → 0.56 mm² distal); thickness up.
- Periosteal BFR ×2.4 at the distal site; endosteal ×2.4 at the proximal site.
- Microarray: 441 genes changed. c-fos ×3.2, egr1 ×3.1, atf3 ×3.0, **MMP3 ×5.3, hyaluronan synthase 1 ×4.1**, TIMP1 ×3.6, Col3a1 ×3.6.
  - Pathways: PI3K, ECM–receptor, TGFβ (Tgfbr1, Smad1), Wnt (Wnt2, Fzd2, Wisp2).
- Col1a1, OPN, OCN and BSP up at 1 week; back to baseline at 2 weeks.

**Relevance.**
- Lateral joint loading upregulates ECM remodelling (MMP3) and HAS1 (hyaluronan synthesis). Hyaluronan is the osmotic/swelling matrix component of hypertrophic expansion and joint fluid.
- TGFβ is the same pathway implicated in periosteal chondrogenesis and osteophytes.
- The response is transient (gone at 2 weeks), which again shows **response decay**: sustained effects need repeated bouts.
- Still width only; no cartilage endpoint.
