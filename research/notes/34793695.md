# 34793695 — Interspecies transcriptomics identify genes that underlie disproportionate foot growth in jerboas

**Citation.** Curr Biol 2022;32(2):289–303.e6. PMC8792248. Read in full (Introduction, all Results and Discussion sections, Conclusion, STAR Methods).

## Model / method
Direct transcriptome comparison of **homologous growth cartilages** in mouse (*Mus musculus*) and the bipedal lesser Egyptian jerboa (*Jaculus jaculus*), which diverged ~50 Mya. The jerboa has **"mouse-like" forearms but extremely long metatarsals** — so metatarsal-versus-metatarsal and radius/ulna-versus-radius/ulna comparisons can be intersected to subtract species divergence unrelated to proportion. P5 cartilage. RNA-seq, **comparative ATAC-seq**, RNAScope in situ hybridisation, qRT-PCR, retroviral (RCAS) misexpression in chicken wing, and measurement of *Prrx1-Cre;Rosa-LSL-Shox2* mice.

## Exact numbers
**Scope.** About **10% of orthologous genes** have evolved expression in metatarsals independent of radius/ulna. **1,755 differentially expressed genes** associated with disproportionate metatarsal acceleration (1,514 differing in metatarsals and not radius/ulna; 241 with non-equivalent differences in both).

**The accelerator — and the one experimental gain measurement.**
- **Shox2**: 9.1-fold higher in jerboa metatarsals (padj = 2.2E-16) and 4.9-fold *lower* in jerboa radius/ulna (padj = 1.3E-50). Expressed in the **proliferative zone and perichondrium** of jerboa metatarsals; **not detectable in mouse metatarsals or in the metacarpals of either species.** Human *SHOX* loss of function causes short stature in Turner and Langer syndromes; rodents lack *SHOX* but have *Shox2*, required for proximal limb elongation and never before detected in hands or feet in any vertebrate.
- **The experiment: *Prrx1-Cre;Rosa-LSL-Shox2* mice (2–2.5× Shox2 overexpression in limb buds) had distal limb elements similar to controls at birth but grew more by eight weeks. The same absolute increment added +23.6% to metacarpal length and +9.1% to metatarsal length versus littermate controls.**
- Cis-regulation: a **139 bp region preferentially accessible in jerboa metatarsals**, upstream of *Shox2* in an intron of *Rsrc1*, created by joining 122 bp upstream and 17 bp downstream of an **~1 kb deletion in the jerboa genome**. The orthologous mouse region has no accessibility peak. Mouse *Rsrc1* intronic enhancers are known to drive *Shox2* and femur elongation (m741/hs741). A second jerboa-metatarsal-specific peak lies near *HoxB13*.
- Other derived transcription factors in jerboa metatarsals: **Pax1 62.7-fold higher (padj = 6.2E-16)** and **HoxB13 37-fold higher (padj = 3.1E-11)**, neither reported in mouse distal limbs at all; plus HoxB9 and higher **Prrx1**, which is associated with **elongation of bat wing digits**. 13 of 32 differentially expressed genes with enriched networks are transcription regulators. The HoxB9/HoxB13/Pax1/Prrx1 patterns "raise the possibility that exaggerated elongation of the metatarsals may be influenced by surrounding **perichondrium and connective tissues**."

**The brakes — the conceptually decisive half.**
- **Crabp1** (cellular retinoic acid binding protein 1): **10.6-fold higher in jerboa metatarsals (padj = 1.5E-92)** — the single most significant gene. A **perichondrium marker**, confirmed higher in jerboa metatarsal perichondrium by in situ. Crabp1 **sequesters retinoic acid in the cytoplasm**, blocking RA's anti-proliferative effect.
- **The pharmacological fact this rests on: "Exogenous RA… inhibits skeletal elongation in juvenile rats, and pharmacological inhibition of RA signaling accelerates rat metatarsal elongation in vitro, suggesting that endogenous RA suppresses metatarsal growth."**
- **Gdf10 / Bmp3b**, an inhibitory TGF-β ligand: **9.3-fold lower in jerboa metatarsals (padj = 1.3E-83)**, localised to the **proliferative zone**, strongly expressed in mouse. *Gdf10* is one of two genes **upregulated by RA or an RARγ agonist** in mouse limb culture. Proposed circuit: endogenous RA represses mouse metatarsal growth partly by raising Gdf10; higher Crabp1 in jerboa sequesters RA and lowers Gdf10.
- **Mab21L2**: **16.8-fold lower in jerboa metatarsals (padj = 1.8E-26)** while **3.9-fold higher in jerboa radius/ulna (padj = 3.9E-16)**. Expressed in mouse metatarsal resting zone, proliferative chondrocytes and perichondrium; **undetectable in jerboa metatarsals.** It inhibits BMP4 and immunoprecipitates with Smad1 and Smad1/Smad4. **Also lower in the elongated fetal bat wing digits** than in the short first wing digit, bat hindlimb digits and all mouse digits. **Retroviral misexpression in chicken wing made the humerus 1.9% shorter (p = 0.0221).** Human gain-of-function *MAB21L2* missense mutations cause **shortening of the proximal limb skeleton**. Correspondingly, BMP2, BMP4, SMAD1 and SMAD4 networks are enriched and predicted activated in jerboa metatarsals.

**Pathway enrichment.** Cytoskeletal organisation; **proliferation and hypertrophy of connective tissue**; suppression of apoptosis; signatures that when perturbed cause hypoplasia, growth failure and short stature. Most significant canonical pathway: **activation of melatonin degradation** (with melatonin cited as sufficient to promote growth plate chondrogenesis). Increased adherens/tight-junction and **integrin / integrin-linked kinase** signalling. **Increased biosynthesis of heparan, chondroitin and dermatan sulfates.** **Increased Rho-family GTPase signalling with suppression of its inhibitor RhoGDI** — Rho GTPases regulate chondrocyte proliferation and **the size of the hypertrophic chondrocyte zone**, and disrupting them shortens limb bones. **WNT/β-catenin network enriched and activated.** IGF-1 itself not differentially expressed but **its network is enriched, and IGF-2 and AKT1 are differentially expressed**; the authors' earlier work (Cooper 2013, [23485973](23485973.md)) established that **IGF-1 is necessary to establish the hypertrophic chondrocyte size differences that drive differential growth rate in mammals including jerboas.**

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**1. "De-repressing latent growth potential" is the right frame, and it now has two independent sources.** The authors' conclusion is that jerboa foot elongation occurs **in part by releasing brakes that hold mouse metatarsals back**, naming Crabp1, Gdf10 and Mab21L2. Independently, the heparan-sulfate literature concludes that the perichondrium is "an **anti-chondrogenic border**… essential to **restrain and contain** cartilage growth" ([23821404](23821404.md)). **Two unrelated literatures, one conclusion: a slow or quiescent skeleton is actively braked, not exhausted.** That is a materially stronger premise for [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) than "the apparatus is frozen," because brakes can be released without supplying anything.

**2. It supplies the single biggest correction to [COMPOUNDS.md](../COMPOUNDS.md) — the RARγ mirror hypothesis is confirmed.** I wrote in Node E that palovarotene (RARγ agonist) ablates growth plates and shortens tibiae by 13–19% ([30226468](30226468.md)), that retinoids close human plates early, and that "reducing RARγ signalling might delay closure — and nothing in this literature tried it." **It has been tried: pharmacological inhibition of RA signalling accelerates rat metatarsal elongation in vitro.** And the fastest-elongating mammalian bone known has 10.6-fold more RA-sequestering Crabp1 and 9.3-fold less of the RA-induced inhibitor Gdf10. **Retinoic acid is an endogenous brake on longitudinal growth, blocking it accelerates elongation, and the natural experiment, the gene expression and the pharmacology all agree.** That promotes RA-pathway antagonism from a speculation to the best-supported de-repression lever in the corpus.

**3. One transcription factor adds 10–24% of bone length, postnatally, in a mammal.** Shox2 overexpression from a limb-mesenchyme driver gave +23.6% metacarpal and +9.1% metatarsal length by 8 weeks, with no difference at birth. **That is the magnitude the user has been asking about, achieved by a single defined molecular change, accruing after birth.** It is not a drug and it is not an adult, but it bounds what a single node is worth.

**4. The brakes are in the perichondrium and the proliferative zone — the same compartment everything else points to.** Crabp1 is a perichondrium marker; Shox2 appears in jerboa metatarsal proliferative zone **and perichondrium**; Gdf10 sits in the proliferative zone; Mab21L2 in resting zone, proliferative zone and perichondrium; and the HoxB9/HoxB13/Pax1/Prrx1 patterns made the authors suspect the **surrounding perichondrium and connective tissues** drive the phenotype. Together with Ctsk+ CCPs ([25003010](25003010.md)), CD90/CD105/STRO-1 groove progenitors ([25164565](25164565.md)), Gli1+ periosteal cells, Scx+/Tppp3+ and Fsp1+ cells, **the perichondrial rim is now where the progenitors, the brakes and the accelerators all sit.** That is a strong and specific redirection of target: not the cartilage interior.

**5. Rho GTPase signalling sets hypertrophic zone size, which is a second handle on *h_max*.** Up in jerboa, with RhoGDI suppressed. Converges with YAP/TAZ and RhoA appearing as the recurring mechanochemical node across the corpus ([38672135](38672135.md), [35844802](35844802.md), [39402753](39402753.md), [33063669](33063669.md)) and with RhoA/ROCK promoting proliferation and reducing hypertrophy in [31787777](31787777.md) — note the sign tension there, which is unresolved.

**6. BMP promotes elongation and the brake on it is weak and multigenic.** BMP receptor loss causes chondrodysplasia; Bmp2/Bmp4 overexpression in chick limbs increases element size; Mab21L2 is a weak BMP inhibitor whose removal is permissive. **So BMP is an accelerator of normal, ordered elongation — not only the heterotopic-ossification driver my corpus has treated it as.** The difference is where it acts and whether HS is restricting its range.

**7. And one caution that cuts against part of the HS story.** The fast-growing jerboa metatarsal shows **increased** heparan, chondroitin and dermatan sulfate biosynthesis, whereas exostoses arise from HS *deficiency*. More HS means a tighter morphogen gradient and more ordered growth; less HS means a leaky gradient and a lateral lump. **That is consistent rather than contradictory, and it sharpens the design rule: for ordered longitudinal growth you want the gradient *steep and correctly oriented*, not simply de-restricted.**

## Levers
- **Retinoic acid pathway antagonism** — the best-supported de-repression lever: RA inhibition accelerates metatarsal elongation pharmacologically; Crabp1 (sequestration) up 10.6×; Gdf10 (RA-induced inhibitor) down 9.3× in the fastest mammalian bone.
- **Gdf10/BMP3B inhibition** — an inhibitory TGF-β ligand in the proliferative zone, down 9.3× in jerboa.
- **Mab21L2 reduction** → BMP2/4–SMAD1/4 activation. Convergently low in both jerboa metatarsal and bat wing digit.
- **Shox2** — +23.6%/+9.1% distal bone length on overexpression; proliferative zone and perichondrium.
- **Rho GTPase signalling / RhoGDI suppression** — sets hypertrophic zone size.
- **IGF-2 and AKT1**, with the IGF-1 network enriched — the *h_max* phase-3 axis ([23485973](23485973.md)).
- **WNT/β-catenin activation**; **integrin/ILK** cell–ECM signalling; **melatonin** (cited as sufficient to promote growth plate chondrogenesis).
- **Pax1, HoxB13, HoxB9, Prrx1** — derived or expanded expression domains, probably perichondrial.
- **Steep, correctly oriented morphogen gradients** (more sulfated GAG), not de-restricted ones.

## Honest limits
- **Comparative transcriptomics of P5 neonatal cartilage.** Correlation, not causation, for all but three genes.
- The only gain-of-function growth measurements are **Shox2 overexpression in mice (embryonic driver, measured at 8 weeks)** and **Mab21L2 misexpression in chicken (1.9% shortening)**. Everything else is expression.
- Shox2 was driven by *Prrx1-Cre* from limb-bud stages — **this is developmental, not adult, and not a drug.** Genetic misexpression of Shox2 also *shortens* proximal elements, so the effect is position-dependent.
- **Crabp1 and Gdf10 knockout mice had no detected abnormal phenotype** — though the authors note bone lengths were not precisely measured. The de-repression model is assembled from several studies, not demonstrated.
- The RA-inhibition-accelerates-elongation result is **in vitro rat metatarsal culture**, cited (their ref 64), and I have not read it. **Given how much weight I am putting on it, that is the most important gap this note creates.**
- No adult animal, no fused plate, no articular cartilage, no stature.
- Jerboa draft genome incompleteness precluded a genome-wide ATAC analysis.
- Melatonin enrichment is for melatonin *degradation*, so the direction of the inferred lever is ambiguous.
