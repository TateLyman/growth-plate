# PMID 22048899 — Schätti O, Grad S, Goldhahn J, Salzmann G, Li Z, Alini M, Stoddart MJ. A combination of shear and dynamic compression leads to mechanically induced chondrogenesis of human MSCs (2011, Eur Cell Mater)

Read in full (PDF, 12 pp).

**Model.** Human bone-marrow MSCs from 5 donors aged 17–70, in fibrin–polyurethane scaffolds. **No exogenous growth factors (no TGF-β).**
- Ball-on-scaffold bioreactor at 1 h/day, 5 days/week, for 3 weeks:
  - (A) free swelling;
  - (B) dynamic compression at 1 Hz, 0.4 mm on a 0.4 mm offset (10–20% strain);
  - (C) ball oscillation shear ±25° at 1 Hz on a 0.4 mm offset;
  - (D) compression + shear.

**Results.**
- GAG/DNA up in all loaded groups.
- **Only compression + shear** significantly upregulated Sox9 (p≤0.01 vs all), Col2 (p≤0.001), aggrecan and COMP. Col1, Col10 and ALP were unchanged, so the phenotype was stable and non-hypertrophic.
- Col2:Col10, Col2:Col1, Col2:ALP and AGG:Col1 ratios were highest in group D.
- Col II protein was detected only in group D. Sulphated matrix reached ~800–900 µm below the sheared surface, suggesting paracrine spread.
- The 17-year-old donor's cells responded far more than the older donors' (45–70), but elderly cells still responded.
- Neither compression alone nor shear alone was sufficient.

**What it establishes.**
- **Purely mechanical chondrogenic induction of adult human progenitors is possible:** articulating-type multiaxial loading (cyclic compression + sliding surface shear) drives MSCs to cartilage without growth factors. This is the in vitro analogue of the Prg4-null finding (friction/shear at an articular surface makes chondroprogenitors) and of joint-motion-dependent cartilage in callus.
- The signal reaches ~1 mm deep from the sheared surface.
- The surface layer favours a stable, non-hypertrophic phenotype. Making that cartilage into *growth* cartilage would also require a hypertrophy/ossification phase (e.g., by reducing shear and adding compression or hypoxia at depth).

**Levers.**
- **Compression + shear = chondrogenic switch** for adult progenitors (Sox9/Col2).
- Repurposing: continuous passive motion (CPM) machines provide exactly this combined motion. The authors explicitly link the result to CPM rehabilitation.
- Age reduces the response, but it is not abolished.
