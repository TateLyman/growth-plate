# PMID 31026439 — FGF, BMP, and RA signaling are sufficient for the induction of complete limb regeneration from non-regenerating wounds

Vieira WA, Wells KM, Raymond MJ, De Souza L, Garcia E, McCusker CD. *Dev Biol* 2019;451(2):146-157. PMC6589380. doi:10.1016/j.ydbio.2019.04.008

**What I read:** the complete PMC full text — abstract, Introduction, all four Results-and-discussion sections, Conclusion, and all Materials and Methods subsections (husbandry, surgical procedures, RNA/cDNA, qRT-PCR, growth-factor bead synthesis and implantation, RA treatment, positional-information regenerative assay, DiI labelling, whole-mount cartilage staining, histology, RARE identification) plus supplementary legends. Not an abstract read.

## Model
Mexican axolotl (*Ambystoma mexicanum*), 5–8 cm, Accessory Limb Model (ALM). A 2 × 2 mm full-thickness skin excision confined to one circumferential quadrant of the forelimb stylopod — a **superficial, non-regenerating wound**, no amputation, no nerve deviation, no tissue graft. Gelatin microsphere beads soaked in growth factors implanted under the 48 h wound epidermis; RA by single i.p. injection at mid-bud stage.

## Exact numbers and design
- **Compound-only protocol:** beads soaked in **FGF-2 + FGF-8 + BMP-2, 300 ng/µl each**, implanted at 2 days post-wound → 25/38 wounds formed blastemas → at mid-bud (7–10 days) a single i.p. RA injection (150 µg/g body weight; animals kept dark 2 days to limit photo-inactivation).
- **52% of RA-treated blastemas generated ectopic structures, including up to three complete limb axes.** Limbs contained all three segments (stylopod, zeugopod, autopod) with complete anterior/posterior asymmetry (radius and ulna, digits 1–4).
- **0/20 PBS-bead controls formed any ectopic limb structure**; only 10% formed even a blastema. The surgery and the bead alone are not sufficient.
- Nerve staining (acetylated tubulin) at 3 and 7 days: robust in deviated-nerve wounds, **low in GF-bead and untreated wounds** — the cocktail substitutes for neurotrophic signalling rather than attracting nerves.
- Marker analysis (qRT-PCR, 32 limbs per injury type per axis, 4 blastemas pooled per replicate): in **anterior** blastemas RA significantly **decreased Alx4** (anterior marker) by 7 days and significantly **increased Shh** by 7 days; no change in mature anterior tissue. In **posterior** blastemas RA **abolished Shh by 2 days** and modestly increased Alx4.
- Bead size controls migration: no bead → up to 2 complete axes; 0.5 or 1.0 mm bead → up to 3. DiI-labelled dermal cells were significantly more spatially restricted with a 1.0 mm bead.

## The permanence result — the reason this paper matters most
- RA-induced ectopic structures were **amputated and followed 7 weeks: they regenerated.** **94% restored their original pattern; 6% patterned *more* complex structures.**
- Full-circumference skin grafts from those structures, moved into a *new* anterior ALM, **induced ectopic growths** — i.e. they carry posterior positional information they did not have before.
- By contrast, Shh- or FGF-8-induced structures (Nacu et al. 2016) **do not regenerate when amputated**, and their grafts only induce in posterior sites — they are still anterior.

So: **RA stably and heritably rewrites positional memory. Shh and FGF-8 transiently supply a positional cue the cells read and then forget.** As of this paper, RA is the only molecule that induces limbs with complete PD *and* AP axes in the ALM.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **A complete skeletal structure can be built from a superficial wound by compounds alone** — four signalling molecules, no amputation, no graft, no nerve surgery. This is the clean existence proof that the "goal" of a wound is set pharmacologically, not surgically.
2. **The architecture is two-step and the order matters.** FGF-2/FGF-8/BMP-2 creates a *competent* state; RA then *rewrites the goal* in that state. Neither alone does it. This is the same architecture the reindeer paper implies ([36493752](36493752.md)): a ground state of competence, then a threshold signal.
3. **Mature tissue is refractory.** Alx4 and Shh were unchanged in mature limb tissue at both timepoints: "the mature tissue is resistant to RA-mediated reprogramming while the blastema tissue is reprogrammed." The gate on the goal changer is the competence step, not the RA step.
4. **Permanence is achievable and is specific to RA.** The user's question — can the injury/positional state be tricked *permanently* — has a documented yes here, with the discriminating control (Shh/FGF-8 fail the same test).
5. **CRABP1 again.** The closing discussion cites Monaghan et al. 2012: FGFR1, BMP2R and **crabp1** are all significantly higher in regenerating limb wounds than non-regenerative flank wounds, and crabp1's elevation is **nerve-dependent**. The authors' conclusion: "regenerative competency could, in part, depend on the ability of a tissue to respond to FGF, BMP, and RA signaling." CRABP1 is the velvet/fetal-human marker from [36493752](36493752.md). Independent literatures, same gene.

## Levers
- **RA / RAR agonism** — the positional-goal rewriter, and the only one shown to be heritable. See [40494878](40494878.md) for the CYP26 route to the same thing with a clinical-stage drug.
- **FGF-2 + FGF-8 + BMP-2** — the competence cocktail; substitutes for the nerve. Note FGF-18/sprifermin and BMP-2 already exist as human-administered proteins.
- Sequence and staging are themselves the lever: RA at mid-bud, not at wounding.

## Anti-levers
- Shh or FGF-8 alone — incomplete structures (no stylopod, symmetric autopod), and no permanence.
- Anterior ECM grafted posteriorly — nothing.
- Bead-induced migration restriction — supernumerary, disordered axes. Delivery geometry is not neutral.

## Honest limits
1. **Axolotl.** Nothing here is mammalian. The output is cartilage (Victoria blue) in a regeneration-competent animal.
2. **The RA dose is enormous** (150 µg/g body weight, systemic i.p.) and RA at that scale is teratogenic. The CYP26-inhibition route in [40494878](40494878.md) is the pharmacologically sane version of the same manipulation.
3. **52% penetrance**, and the pattern output is frequently wrong (bulbous masses, supernumerary axes). The authors attribute the supernumerary phenotype to the delivery vehicle, not the biology.
4. **The wound-specific signals remain unidentified** — the authors say so explicitly. Wounding is still an irreducible input in this protocol; the paper replaces the nerve and the graft, not the wound.
5. Bulbous masses either had anterior-only identity or an identity outside the limb field — i.e. the rewrite can produce a structure with no coherent positional goal at all.
