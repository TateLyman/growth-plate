# 41786976 — Local delivery of OSK factors enables partial cellular reprogramming to mitigate osteoarthritis

**Citation.** Exp Mol Med 2026. PMC13049178. DOI 10.1038/s12276-026-01662-x. Read in full.

## Model / method
**AAV2** carrying a single cassette **Oct4-P2A-Sox2-T2A-Klf4** under the EFS promoter (no c-Myc), titre >1×10¹² gc/ml, **injected intra-articularly** into mouse knees. AAV2-EGFP as mock. Models: destabilisation of the medial meniscus (DMM) and anterior cruciate ligament transection (ACLT); treated on the day of surgery and again at 4 wk, assessed at 8 wk. A separate post-onset arm injected at 4 and 8 wk after fibrocartilage had already formed, assessed at 12 wk. Lineage tracing with Col2α1-CreERT;Rosa26-tdTomato. WGBS for a DNA-methylation clock. ATDC5 cells for the in vitro osteogenic-transition assay. siTet2 for mechanism.

## Exact numbers / findings
- **Targeting is cartilage-specific.** SOX2 co-localised with COL2 in articular chondrocytes; minimal in OCN+ subchondral bone and minimal in synovium.
- **No hyperproliferation**: Ki67+ fraction identical between AAV-Mock and AAV-OSK.
- **Blocks the osteogenic transition.** Under osteogenic conditions ATDC5 cells upregulate Col1, Runx2, Osx; OSK suppressed all three and maintained Acan and Col2 while lowering Mmp13. ICC confirmed suppression of RUNX2 and OSX. In vivo, RUNX2 rose in tdTomato+ chondrocytes after DMM and fell with OSK.
- **In vivo disease outcomes (DMM and ACLT):** lower OARSI score, restored COL2:COL1 ratio, raised ACAN, suppressed MMP13 and **COL X**, reduced subchondral bone volume fraction (BV/TV), reduced pain sensitivity (von Frey), better grip strength and balance beam.
- **Epigenetic rejuvenation.** P21 down; **DNMT3a down**; predicted DNA-methylation age of OSK-treated cartilage lower than mock and **younger than chronological age (negative delta age)** — though the authors state the sample size (n = 3) precludes statistical significance. WGBS showed a reshaped global methylation landscape; differentially methylated promoters enriched for cytokine activity and inflammatory response; KEGG for Th17 differentiation, actin cytoskeleton regulation, osteoclast differentiation, JAK–STAT.
- **TET2 is the mediator.** Of Tet1–3, only Tet2 rose with OSK. siTet2 abolished every OSK effect: anti-inflammatory action, maintenance of chondrocyte phenotype under osteogenic conditions, joint-surface protection, subchondral bone protection, OARSI improvement, ACAN, COL2:COL1, MMP13 and COL X suppression, and the behavioural gains.
- **Fibrocartilage → hyaline cartilage in situ**, when started 4 weeks *after* the lesion.
- Safety precedent cited: sustained AAV-OSK in retinal ganglion cells restored youthful methylation for **10–18 months of continuous expression with no proliferation or tumour formation**. c-Myc omitted deliberately; the paper explains c-Myc's only role in reprogramming is to relieve the OSK-induced p16/p21 proliferation pause, and that chronic MYC is what drives tumorigenesis.
- **Contrast with Lin28a reprogramming (their ref 52):** Lin28a restored chondrocyte stemness (high Nanog) and promoted regeneration, but **increased osteophyte volume** — the authors read it as reprogramming chondrocytes into progenitors that then form osteophytes. OSK deliberately does not do this.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **Adult articular cartilage can be epigenetically rejuvenated in vivo, locally, with an existing delivery platform, apparently without tumours.** Methylation age below chronological age, P21 down, DNMT3a down, sustained expression tolerated for well over a year in another tissue. Whatever else is true, "the adult chondrocyte epigenome is fixed" is no longer a defensible premise.
2. **But OSK is the brake, not the accelerator — and that is the point of it.** It suppresses RUNX2, OSX, COL1 and COL X, does not raise Ki67, and does not thicken anything. It *stabilises* a cartilage identity. Read against [40118845](40118845.md), OSK is a candidate for manufacturing and holding the **hypertrophy-resistant domain** of a two-domain construct — the resting-zone end — not for producing length.
3. **Lin28a is the striking counterexample in the opposite direction, and nobody seems to have chased it.** A single reprogramming factor, delivered to a joint, reprogrammed articular chondrocytes into progenitors that **formed new bony outgrowths**. That is adult articular cartilage generating de novo osteochondral mass under a defined molecular input. It was reported as an adverse effect. In the frame of this project it is the only compound-level observation of adult joint-surface cartilage building new bone, and it deserves its own full read.
4. **TET2 is a specific, named, druggable-in-principle node.** Every effect required it. DNA demethylation at TET2 is the mechanism by which an adult chondrocyte's identity becomes plastic again. That gives a molecular handle on "unlocking" cartilage that does not require viral transcription factors — and, equally, warns that TET1 inhibition *prevents* OA (their ref 56), so the TET family is bidirectional and tissue-context-dependent.
5. **It demonstrates the delivery route that any serious local approach would use.** Intra-articular AAV2, chondrocyte-selective, sparing subchondral bone and synovium. The spatial-control problem that sinks every systemic idea in this corpus ([22518118](22518118.md) acromegaly, [37770652](37770652.md) garetosmab) is addressed here, in a joint, in a published experiment.

## Levers
- **AAV2 intra-articular OSK (no c-Myc)** — epigenetic rejuvenation, cartilage-selective, identity-stabilising, non-proliferative, with a 10–18-month safety precedent in retina.
- **TET2** — the obligate mediator; DNA demethylation as the unlock.
- **DNMT3a / P21** — the senescence and methylation markers that moved.
- **Lin28a** — the one reported reprogramming factor that made adult articular cartilage grow new bone. Flagged here as the highest-priority unread lead in the compound phase.
- **Anti-lever: expecting rejuvenation alone to produce growth.** Ki67 did not move. A younger epigenome in a chondrocyte with nowhere to go and no conversion front produces better cartilage, not a longer bone.

## Honest limits
- Mouse, 8–12 weeks, surgical OA models. No human data, no large animal.
- n = 3 for the methylation clock, and the authors explicitly say it is underpowered; the "negative delta age" is suggestive only.
- AAV gene therapy, not a compound. Dose, timing, long-term oncogenicity, biodistribution and vector persistence are all listed by the authors as open.
- Measures disease scores, not dimensions. No thickness, no length, no mechanical testing.
- Says nothing about growth plates, closed or open, and nothing about longitudinal growth. Its relevance here is entirely mechanistic.

## Chase
- **Ref 52 — the Lin28a chondrocyte reprogramming paper (stemness restored, osteophyte volume increased). Top priority.**
- Ref 23 — sustained AAV-OSK in retinal ganglion cells, 10–18 months, no tumour (Lu et al.).
- Ref 25 — rejuvenation of nucleus pulposus cells reversing intervertebral disc degeneration.
- Ref 16 — DNMT1/DNMT3a up and DNMT3b down in human OA cartilage and DMM mice.
- Ref 56 — TET1 inhibition prevents OA (the opposite-sign TET result).
- Ref 34 — the DNA methylation age model used.
- Refs 50, 51 — OSK suppressing maladaptive hypertrophic maturation and osteogenic differentiation.
