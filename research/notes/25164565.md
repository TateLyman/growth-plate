# 25164565 — Vascularization of primary and secondary ossification centres in the human growth plate

**Citation.** BMC Dev Biol 2014;14:36. PMC4236517. Read in full (Background, all Results, Discussion, Conclusions).

## Model / method
**Human growth plates from polydactylic digits** (surgical specimens, n = 12 for morphometry; 8 immature plates at the cartilage-anlage or early-central-ossification stage). Immunohistochemistry, confocal laser scanning microscopy, RT-qPCR and quantitative morphometry (cells/mm² in 10 fields of 1 mm², per zone) with: **CD34, CD31** (endothelium), **α-SMA** (smooth muscle), **CD133, CXCR4, VEGFR-2** (endothelial/haematopoietic progenitors), **CD90, CD105** (mesenchymal progenitors), **RUNX2, DLX5** (terminal chondrocytes), **RANK, RANKL, OPG**, CD45.

## Exact findings
**The conversion front.**
- New vessels in ossification centres formed by **sprouting of CD34+ (CD31-negative, i.e. immature) endothelium from the pre-existing bone-collar vessels, or by intussusception — not by de novo vasculogenesis**. No CD133+VEGFR-2+ endothelial progenitors were found in POCs or SOCs despite extensive searching.
- Those immature vessels **lacked α-SMA+ abluminal coverage**; CD90+ mesenchymal precursors "tend to seek physical contact" with the CD34+ sprouts, and the authors argue co-ordinated development of CD34+ endothelium and subendothelial CD90+ pericyte precursors is the prerequisite for successful vessel formation.
- Vascularisation occurs **before** hypertrophy, mineralisation and chondrocyte death.
- The gating mechanism: **VEGF is stored in the cartilaginous ECM and released when MMP-2, MMP-9 and MMP-13 degrade it**, whereupon it binds VEGFR1/2 on endothelium and pre-osteoblasts, "favouring vascular invasion and cartilage replacement by bone." Hypertrophic chondrocytes also secrete RANKL and Ihh.
- Hypoxia in the avascular anlage **raises anabolic metabolism** and HIF-1α, which is needed for chondrocyte survival, stimulates collagen II and induces VEGF isoforms.
- The whole resting → proliferating → hypertrophic → calcification progression "happens within hours."
- Stated flatly: **"There is no evidence of in vivo one step trans-differentiation of chondrocytes to osteocytes."** (This contradicts the Ku80+ human-bone-from-human-cartilage result in [37126720](37126720.md) and the mouse transdifferentiation literature it cites. A real, unresolved tension — see Honest limits.)

**The groove of Ranvier (GOR) and ring of LaCroix (ROL) — the finding that matters here.**
- **The GOR and ROL contain stabilised vessels: CD34+ endothelium WITH α-SMA+ smooth-muscle coverage, and their vascularisation was "more developed than in the corresponding ossification centres."** Capillaries present from the onset of the GOR.
- **The majority of resident GOR cells are mesenchymal: CD90+ and CD105+, CD45-negative** (excluding myeloid origin). No CD133+ cells in the GOR itself; the surrounding perichondrium and periosteum were **highly vascularised** and did harbour CD133+ progenitors.
- "**The GOR was shown to maintain their progenitor properties very similar to bone marrow stem cell niches and harbours an extensive vascular network.**" Rabbit GOR precursors express **STRO-1, Jagged1 and BMPR1a** with MSC morphology.
- **Spatial organisation of the niche: CD90+/CD105+ progenitors sit in the basal layer of the GOR; on differentiating they lose CD90/CD105, move to the apical part, and are then dispersed throughout the cartilage.**
- **Migration, in vivo:** after 10–12 days of BrdU feeding in mouse, these cells incorporate BrdU; transfected GOR cells "initially migrated back to the perichondrial groove and later **deeper into the epiphysis or to the surface of the articular cartilage**."
- **Functional requirement: "An intact perichondrial zone with optimal vascularization is important for longitudinal bone growth, as Salter-Harris type IV fractures within the GOR led to severe growth disturbances."**
- The ROL's mechanical role: it "keeps the cells from oozing out under axial loading."
- The GOR/ROL only exist once a growth plate has formed — digits with a single cartilage anlage had none.
- Cited for scale: **CD105+CD90− progenitors from 15.5 dpc fetal bone, in Matrigel under the adult mouse kidney capsule, recruited host vessels, produced donor-derived ectopic bone through a cartilage intermediate, and generated a marrow cavity with host long-term-reconstituting HSCs.**

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**1. It identifies the one compartment at every joint that is simultaneously perfused, progenitor-rich, proliferative, migratory into the articular surface, and mechanically contained.** Everything about the groove of Ranvier is right for this purpose:
- **Perfused, with mature α-SMA-stabilised vessels — better vascularised than the ossification centre itself.** This is operationally decisive. Articular cartilage is avascular and must be reached by diffusion; the GOR can be reached by the circulation. **Any systemically delivered agent reaches the groove and does not reach the interior of articular cartilage.** [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md) aims at the articular interior; this says the pharmacologically reachable target is the rim.
- **Populated by CD90+/CD105+/STRO-1+/Jagged1+/BMPR1a+ CD45− mesenchymal progenitors**, with a defined basal-to-apical differentiation geometry.
- **Proliferative** (BrdU-incorporating over 10–12 days) and **migratory into the epiphysis and onto the articular surface.**
- **Required for longitudinal growth** — physeal injury confined to the groove causes severe growth disturbance.
- **Mechanically contained by the ring of LaCroix**, which stops cells extruding under axial load. A niche with a wall.

**2. Converging on the same compartment from a sixth direction.** Ctsk+ CCPs ([25003010](25003010.md)), CD90+/CD105+/STRO-1+ cells (here), Gli1+ periosteal stem cells ([35844802](35844802.md)), Scx+/Tppp3+ tendon-sheath cells ([38672135](38672135.md)), Fsp1+ fibroblasts at ligament attachments ([24875294](24875294.md)) and the groove as the lateral-expansion source for articular cartilage ([28438606](28438606.md)) are all the same anatomical rim. **The *n* reservoir is not in dispute; it is the perichondrial/periosteal groove at the joint margin.**

**3. The conversion front is gated by matrix-bound VEGF release, which is an MMP step.** VEGF sits stored in the cartilage ECM and is liberated by MMP-2/9/13. So Stage 3 of ACTIVATION.md has a specific, enzymatic trigger that does not require adding VEGF — it requires degrading matrix in the right place. That converges with heparanase doing the analogous job for HS-bound factors at the chondro-osseous junction ([23821404](23821404.md)): **the conversion front works by releasing factors already stored in the matrix.** Two independent matrix-release mechanisms, both enzyme-gated, both concentrated at the front.

**4. The vasculature comes from the bone collar by sprouting, not from circulating progenitors.** So the conversion front cannot be supplied by injected or recruited endothelial cells; it has to be reached by extension of an existing local vessel bed. That argues the front must be adjacent to perfused bone — which the subchondral plate and the metaphysis are, and the mid-cartilage is not.

**5. Hypoxia is the anabolic state and vascularity is the conversion state.** HIF-1α raises anabolic metabolism and collagen II in the avascular anlage while also inducing VEGF. This is the same hypoxia→normoxia timer that released hypertrophy in the hPSC chondronoids ([37126720](37126720.md)), now in human tissue. **Perfusion state is a cyclable variable that sets build-versus-convert**, which is a non-pharmacological lever for the Stage-5 ratchet.

## Levers
- **The groove of Ranvier / ring of LaCroix** as the target compartment: perfused, CD90+/CD105+/STRO-1+/Jagged1+/BMPR1a+, proliferative, migratory onto the articular surface, mechanically walled, and required for longitudinal growth.
- **MMP-2/9/13-gated release of matrix-bound VEGF** as the conversion trigger.
- **Perfusion / hypoxia state** as the build-versus-convert switch (HIF-1α anabolic; vascular invasion converting).
- **CD34+ sprouting from the bone collar + CD90+ pericyte precursors** as the actual vessel-forming pair; no role for circulating endothelial progenitors.
- **Jagged1 (Notch) and BMPR1a** on GOR progenitors — two receptor handles on the niche not previously in my corpus.

## Honest limits
- **Human polydactylous digits — embryonic/fetal and juvenile tissue.** Nothing adult, nothing fused. The GOR properties are from developing plates, and the GOR's adult fate is not addressed here at all.
- Descriptive immunohistochemistry and morphometry. No intervention, no growth measurement, no function test.
- n = 12 for morphometry; several key observations are reported as "data not shown."
- The migration and BrdU data are cited from mouse studies (their refs 9, 41), not generated here.
- **Direct conflict with [37126720](37126720.md):** this paper states there is no evidence of one-step chondrocyte-to-osteocyte transdifferentiation in vivo, while the hPSC work shows Ku80+ human bone arising from Ku80+ human cartilage, and the mouse literature supports transdifferentiation. Unresolved, and it matters for whether the bone at the front is supplied by the cartilage or by invading osteoprogenitors.
- Polydactylous digit plates may not be representative of load-bearing long-bone physes.
