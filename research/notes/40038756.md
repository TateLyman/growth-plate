# PMID 40038756 — Does extracorporeal shockwave therapy treat leg length discrepancy? An experimental animal study (2025, Arthritis Res Ther)

Read in full (PMC, ~26k chars).

**Model.**
- 96 Sprague-Dawley rats, 4 weeks old (open plates).
- A **single session** of focused ESWT on the left proximal tibial physis: 0.1 / 0.25 / 0.5 mJ/mm², 800 impulses, 4 Hz (Storz Duolith SD1). Sham controls.
- Sacrificed at 7, 13 and 25 weeks.

**Results.**
- Left tibial length increment vs sham was higher in all ESWT groups. The left/right ratio was highest at 0.25 mJ/mm² at 25 weeks. (Absolute left length vs time was not significant; magnitudes are only in the supplement/figures. The effect is modest and the reporting is weak.)
- No histological damage, no TUNEL+ apoptosis.
- IL-1β mildly up (microinflammation) through 25 weeks. **SOX9, BMP2 and BMP4 up in the physis**, best at ≤0.25 mJ/mm².
- Cited:
  - ESWT increases chondrogenesis and growth in cultured fetal metatarsals.
  - Radial ESWT upregulated SOX9/Col II in cultured *human growth plate*, and lengthened rabbit tibiae.
  - High-energy ESWT (0.6 mJ/mm², 3000 shots ×3) thickened rabbit physes.
  - Very high energy damages plates (Yeaman).
  - ESWT induces neovascularisation at the tendon–bone junction.

**What it establishes.**
- A **non-invasive, clinically approved acoustic-mechanical device (ESWT)** can upregulate SOX9 and BMP2/4 in growth cartilage, with a small length gain in growing animals. Low energy is pro-growth; high energy is destructive (possibly usable for epiphysiodesis).
- ESWT is also known to induce neovascularisation and progenitor activation, and it is used for nonunion. That is relevant for *creating* cartilage/callus-like responses in adult bone without surgery.

**Lever.** ESWT is a repurposable, deep-reaching (focused) mechanical stimulus.
- Low-energy focused ESWT can raise SOX9/BMP in cartilage and possibly induce chondro-/osteogenic progenitor activity at a targeted adult bone site (e.g., the epiphyseal scar or perichondrial ring).
- Nonunion data show it can trigger an endochondral repair response in adults *without an incision* (callus-like microdamage response).
- Caveat: an ESWT-induced reaction in adult bone is usually *osteogenic* (bone densification) rather than a sustained cartilage front.
