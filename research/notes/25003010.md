# 25003010 — From an orphan disease to a generalized molecular mechanism: PTPN11 loss-of-function mutations in the pathogenesis of metachondromatosis

**Citation.** Rare Dis 2013;1:e26657. PMC3927490. Review/commentary on Yang et al. 2013 (their ref 15). Read in full (all five sections).

## Why it is here
**This paper names the progenitor population that supplies the *n* term, shows it is present and migratory in adult articular cartilage, and identifies the single molecular dial — ERK — that moves every term in G = n × h_max at once.** It is the keystone for the revised version of [ACTIVATION.md](../ACTIVATION.md).

## The cell
- *Ctsk-Cre; Ptpn11*^fl/fl mice unexpectedly developed **large numbers of enchondromas and osteochondromas at hip, knee and ankles by 12 weeks**, phenocopying human metachondromatosis (MC). **Not transferable by bone-marrow transplant**, so not osteoclast-derived despite the Ctsk driver.
- Lineage tracing (Rosa26-lacZ, Rosa26-YFP) revealed **a previously undefined subset of cathepsin K-expressing cells residing in the groove of Ranvier**, named **"Cathepsin K-expressing Chondroid Progenitors" (CCPs)**.
- CCPs carry MSC markers **CD44, CD90, Stro-1, CD166** and differentiate to bone, fat and cartilage ex vivo.
- **CCPs migrate out of the groove into epiphyseal cartilage and into articular cartilage**, where they can stay quiescent or differentiate and **replenish cartilage** (their Fig. 2). The groove of Ranvier is described as "a reservoir of cartilaginous stem cells/progenitors for the germinal layer of the growth plate and possibly for the superficial layer of articular cartilage."
- **In adults: a low number of Ctsk+ cells exist in healthy adult mouse articular cartilage, and their number increases during OA progression, mainly in clusters of proliferating chondrocytes.** Migratory cartilage stem cells have been reported in OA cartilage, expressing Stro-1, VCAM-1 and Notch.

**The specificity control is decisive.** Deleting *Ptpn11* in **Col2a1+ proliferating** chondrocytes (Col2-CreERT2 + tamoxifen) or **Col10a1+ hypertrophic** chondrocytes produced **no osteochondromas over 6 months of observation**, only occasional solitary enchondromas. **The lesions come from the groove, not from the plate.**

## The dial
- SHP2 is required for RAS/ERK activation downstream of most RTKs. **ERK represses IHH/PTHrP production.**
- The normal circuit: **FGF18, expressed predominantly in the perichondrium, signals through FGFR3 and ERK MAPK to negatively regulate IHH** in pre-hypertrophic and hypertrophic chondrocytes. IHH then induces **PTHrP** in the periarticular perichondrium. IHH stimulates chondrocyte proliferation; the IHH/PTHrP feedback loop **inhibits differentiation of proliferating chondrocytes and sustains their proliferation.**
- **In Shp2-deficient CCPs (and in a chondroprogenitor line after *Ptpn11* knockdown), ERK activation was compromised and the cells showed increased Ihh and PTHrP expression, excessive proliferation, and enhanced chondrogenic differentiation.**
- On polarity, stated outright: **"Any change in the strength or territory of FGF and IHH signaling can influence the polarization of physeal chondroid cells, allowing them to grow in the wrong direction or location."**
- IHH is also required for ossification of the perichondrium, which **couples chondrogenesis to osteogenesis** at the bony collar; deregulated IHH may therefore also cause defective perichondrial ossification and permit outgrowth.
- Constitutively active Hedgehog or PTHrP signalling likewise causes enchondromas and osteochondromas. Enchondromas are "assumed to result from a failure in terminal differentiation."

## Treatment data
- Blocking HH signalling in the mouse MC model ameliorated disease. The Smoothened inhibitor **PF-04449913 significantly slowed progression, reduced cartilaginous tumour burden and dramatically improved mobility**.
- **At those doses, and at the IPI-926 doses used for human basal cell carcinoma, linear growth was not affected.** Other SMO inhibitors named: LDE-225, TAK-441, IPI-926, BMS-833923. IPI-926 adverse effects in humans: abdominal pain, diarrhoea, hyponatraemia, arthralgia.
- MC lesions **can regress spontaneously**, or transform to chondrosarcoma. >30 cases reported; prevalence unknown.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
**1. Taken with [24875294](24875294.md), ERK is one dial that moves every term at once — and this is the most consequential finding of the whole project after the two-domain boundary itself.**

| ERK state | Effect | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Groove-of-Ranvier CCPs proliferate excessively and become chondrogenic → **raises *n*** | this paper |
| **Low** | Ihh up (6-fold on SHP2 loss) and PTHrP up → sustains proliferation, **inhibits differentiation, and is the anti-exhaustion signal** | this paper, [24875294](24875294.md) |
| **Low** | Chondrocytes held at early-hypertrophic, COLX zone expands from ~3 to >10 cells per column, **cell size increases → raises *h_max*** | [24875294](24875294.md) |
| **Low** | Late-hypertrophic programme off — matrix degradation, apoptosis, angiogenesis, osteoclastogenesis all suppressed → **conversion blocked** | [24875294](24875294.md) |
| **High** | Terminal differentiation, matrix degradation, vascular invasion, replacement by bone → **conversion proceeds** | [24875294](24875294.md) |

**So the ratchet of ACTIVATION.md §Stage 5 — build phase, then convert phase — is a single pathway cycled, not five separate interventions.** Low-ERK phase: expand the progenitor pool, raise Ihh/PTHrP to prevent exhaustion, let cells swell, hold conversion. High-ERK phase: convert. And there are agents on both sides: **approved MEK1/2 inhibitors** push ERK down; **FGF-18/sprifermin** — the one compound with an adult human cartilage-thickening RCT ([26138203](26138203.md)) — pushes it up through FGFR3. This is a dramatic simplification of the proposed mechanism and it was not visible before reading these two papers.

**2. The proliferation wall of [COMPOUNDS.md](../COMPOUNDS.md) §2 has a named exception.** Every compound I had found raised matrix and left cell number flat or lower. **Here, lowering ERK in a specific, marked, adult-present progenitor population caused *excessive proliferation*.** The wall was a property of the cells being addressed (mature articular chondrocytes), not of adult cartilage as such. The right target is the groove, not the plate and not the cartilage interior.

**3. The progenitor is in the right anatomical place already, and it already migrates into articular cartilage.** The groove of Ranvier wraps the periphery of the epiphyseal cartilage at every joint. Ctsk+ CCPs migrate from it into epiphyseal and articular cartilage, and their number **rises in adult OA**. This converges with the independent identification of the groove as the lateral-expansion progenitor source for articular cartilage ([28438606](28438606.md)), with Gli1+ periosteal stem cells ([35844802](35844802.md)), with Scx+/Tppp3+ tendon-sheath cells ([38672135](38672135.md)), and with Fsp1+ fibroblasts at ligament attachments ([24875294](24875294.md)). **Five markers, one anatomical compartment: the perichondrial/periosteal rim at the joint margin.**

**4. The specificity control answers a question ACTIVATION.md left open.** Deleting the same gene in plate chondrocytes did nothing in 6 months; deleting it in the groove produced lesions at hip, knee and ankle by 12 weeks. **The adult target is the perichondrial rim, not the cartilage.** That is both a sharper target and a reason the articular-interior approach of ACTIVATION.md may be aimed at the least responsive cells in the joint — which is exactly what Decker found ("adjacent articular chondrocytes stay passive", [28438606](28438606.md)).

**5. The polarity rule is now stated twice, independently.** "Any change in the strength or territory of FGF and IHH signaling can influence the polarization of physeal chondroid cells, allowing them to grow in the wrong direction or location" — and in the HS literature, exostoses align to the de-restricted gradient at 90° to the native plate ([23821404](23821404.md)). **Growth direction is set by the territory of the Ihh/FGF gradient.** At a joint surface that territory is already axial, which is the strongest argument yet for ACTIVATION.md's choice of site.

**6. IHH couples chondrogenesis to osteogenesis at the perichondrium.** So the same signal that expands the cartilage also licenses the bony collar to follow it. That is the coupling condition of §6 having a shared upstream regulator, which is what would make matched rates achievable rather than a knife-edge.

**7. And the failure mode is the disease.** Uncontrolled ERK-low in these cells is metachondromatosis: enchondromas, osteochondromas, skeletal deformation, avascular necrosis of the femoral epiphysis, and chondrosarcoma. The honest reading is that **the build phase of this scheme is a cartilage tumour that has to be converted on schedule**, and that the conversion phase is what distinguishes a growth plate from a neoplasm.

## Levers
- **MEK1/2 → ERK1/2** as the master dial. Approved inhibitors (trametinib, selumetinib, binimetinib, mirdametinib) push it down; **FGF-18/sprifermin** pushes it up via FGFR3. Both directions available, one of them with an adult human RCT.
- **SHP2/PTPN11** upstream; allosteric SHP2 inhibitors exist (same direction as MEK inhibition).
- **Ctsk+ CCPs in the groove of Ranvier** — CD44+ CD90+ Stro-1+ CD166+, migratory into articular cartilage, present in adult healthy cartilage and increased in adult OA. **The addressable *n* reservoir.**
- **IHH/PTHrP** as the proliferation-sustaining, differentiation-inhibiting, anti-exhaustion output of low ERK — and as the coupler of chondrogenesis to perichondrial ossification.
- **Gradient territory** as the polarity control.
- **Smoothened inhibitors (PF-04449913, LDE-225, IPI-926, TAK-441, BMS-833923)** as the off-switch, with the useful observation that **linear growth was unaffected at the doses used** — i.e. a brake that can be applied without costing growth.
- **Anti-lever: sustained ERK suppression.** That is metachondromatosis.

## Honest limits
- **A short commentary/review on the authors' own paper** (Yang et al. 2013, their ref 15). Every primary datum is secondary here, and some results are cited as "manuscript in preparation."
- **Genetic loss-of-function, juvenile mice.** No pharmacological ERK manipulation aimed at growth, no adult onset, no closed plate, and **no bone-length measurement anywhere**.
- The phenotype is a tumour syndrome. Lesions at hip, knee and ankle by 12 weeks, plus avascular necrosis of the femoral epiphysis in human MC.
- CCP identity rests on Ctsk-Cre lineage tracing plus MSC surface markers; the relationship of CCPs to bona fide MSCs is explicitly unresolved, as is whether Col2a1+/Col10a1+ cells derive from the Ctsk lineage.
- Whether the occasional enchondromas in the Col2/Col10 models came from native chondrocytes or from migrated CCP progeny is unresolved.
- MEK inhibitors as a class carry rash, retinopathy, reduced ejection fraction and cardiomyopathy; they are oncology drugs, and nothing here used one.
- The ERK dial is assembled by me from this paper plus [24875294](24875294.md). Neither paper proposes cycling it, and nobody has tested it.
