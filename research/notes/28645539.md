# 28645539 — Gli1-labeled adult mesenchymal stem/progenitor cells and hedgehog signaling contribute to endochondral heterotopic ossification

**Citation.** Bone 2018;109:178–186. PMC5801258. Read in full.

## Model / method
Cre-lox genetic lineage tracing in a **BMP-dependent, injury-induced transgenic mouse model of endochondral HO** (Nse-BMP4). Two candidate adult progenitor lineages compared: **Gli1-CreER** (perivascular, known to drive injury-induced organ fibrosis) and **CD133**. Hedgehog signalling mapped at lesion sites. Systemic Hh inhibition tested therapeutically.

## Exact findings
- **Both lineages labelled many normal tissues, in distinct patterns, but only Gli1-CreER-labelled stem/progenitor cells contributed to *all stages* of endochondral HO.** CD133 contributed nothing to the lesions.
- **The trigger in adults was trivially small: a shallow skin cut (≈5 mm) was sufficient to initiate endochondral HO** in adult Nse-BMP4 animals. The injury does not have to be at the site, or deep, or involve the skeleton.
- **Hedgehog signalling was dysregulated in a specific spatial pattern: increased *surrounding* the lesion and diminished *within* it.** A gradient, not a level.
- **Systemic Hh inhibition was insufficient either to prevent initiation or to treat existing endochondral HO** — in contrast to a previous report for *intramembranous* HO. The authors propose this as a real difference between the two kinds of HO.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **The competent adult progenitor is Gli1+ and perivascular — it is everywhere there are vessels.** The same population that [35844802](35844802.md) fate-maps as the periosteal stem cell recruited into a fracture callus, and that Piezo1 agonism mobilises. Two independent literatures, one cell. That is the cell any post-fusion scheme would be addressing, whether it means to or not.
2. **In an adult, the injury threshold for initiating de novo endochondral bone is astonishingly low — a 5 mm skin cut.** Given the signal (here a BMP4 transgene), the permissive insult is almost nothing. This sharpens the two-input conclusion from [28629737](28629737.md) and [38672135](38672135.md): the hard input is the *signal*, not the *injury*. Which cuts both ways. It means a controlled local initiation is plausible without major trauma; it also means that **any systemic manipulation that supplies the signal makes every subsequent scratch, injection or sprain a potential ossification site.** That is the single clearest argument against a systemic compound for this purpose, and it comes from a measurement, not an opinion.
3. **Hedgehog's role here is positional, and systemic Hh manipulation failed.** High outside the lesion, low inside. A level is not a lever; the gradient is. And a systemic Hh inhibitor — the class that includes vismodegib — changed neither initiation nor established disease in endochondral HO. Read against the earlier finding in my corpus that a hedgehog-agonist bead only worked where a niche already existed, Hh emerges as a *positional* signal that reports where you are, not a drive that can be turned up from outside. That demotes the Hh agonists (SAG, purmorphamine) considerably as candidate compounds — which is just as well, since they are tumour-promoting.
4. **Endochondral and intramembranous HO are pharmacologically different.** A drug that blocks one need not touch the other. Any claim that a compound "controls ectopic bone" has to say which kind.

## Levers
- **Gli1+ perivascular/periosteal progenitors** — the named, traceable, addressable adult cell that completes endochondral ossification. The convergence point of the FOP literature, the fracture literature and the Piezo1 literature.
- **Local BMP signal + minimal injury** as the initiation pair.
- **Anti-lever: systemic hedgehog agonism or antagonism.** Ineffective here on endochondral HO, positional rather than dose-like in its action, and (for agonists) carrying the tumour risk of the pathway vismodegib was built to block.

## Honest limits
- A transgenic BMP4-overexpressing mouse. The signal is supplied genetically and constitutively; nothing here shows a drug can supply it.
- Lineage tracing shows *contribution*, not necessity — no ablation experiment.
- Gli1-CreER labels broadly in normal tissues, so "Gli1+" is a large and heterogeneous population, not a defined stem cell.
- No measurement of length, polarity or direction. The lesions are ectopic masses.
- Hh findings are descriptive plus one failed therapeutic test.

## Chase
- The prior report that systemic Hh inhibition *does* prevent **intramembranous** HO (the contrast the authors draw).
- Gli1-CreER perivascular progenitors in injury-induced organ fibrosis (the paper that motivated the choice of lineage).
- Any study quantifying the **minimum injury** required for HO initiation in this model, and whether distance from the lesion site matters.
