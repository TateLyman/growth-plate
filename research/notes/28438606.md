# PMID 28438606 — Decker RS, ... Pacifici M. Cell origin, volume and arrangement are drivers of articular cartilage formation, morphogenesis and response to injury in mouse limbs (2017, Dev Biol)

Read in full (PMC, ~48k chars).

**Methods.** Gdf5-CE, Prg4-CE, Dkk3-CE and ROSA-CE mice crossed to Confetti or single reporters. 2-photon/SHG imaging, cell-volume quantification, and a focal osteochondral defect at 2 months.

**Findings.**
- Mouse tibial articular cartilage thickens mainly between P14 and P28 (to ~10 µm superficial + 15 µm intermediate + 90 µm deep zone at 2 months).
- **Thickening is driven by zone-specific cell volume increase (~8-fold in intermediate/deep zones by 2 months) and rearrangement of non-daughter cells into vertical "stacks", not by apposition of clonal columns.** Proliferation is minimal after P7 (~3% by EdU at P28). Collagen goes from isotropic to anisotropic, aligned with the stacks.
- Deep-zone cell diameter is ~60% of the SOC's Col X hypertrophic chondrocytes and <50% of metaphyseal growth-plate hypertrophic cells. **Chondrocytes set their volume by location.**
- The cell death (TUNEL) seen in adults is mainly in the deep zone.
- The higher proliferation reported by Kozhemyakina 2015 may reflect **Prg4-heterozygous mice with reduced lubrication → more friction → more turnover** (authors' suggestion). That fits the Prg4-null friction → chondrogenesis link.
- Lateral expansion of articular cartilage with epiphyseal growth likely recruits progenitors from the **groove of Ranvier** (preliminary).
- **Injury.** Synovial Prg4-lineage CD44+/P75+ MSC-like cells proliferate massively (70% of synovial cells) and fill defects. Adjacent articular chondrocytes stay passive.

**Relevance.**
- Articular cartilage "growth" is mostly **cell swelling plus rearrangement**, the same physical actuator as growth-plate hypertrophy (Cooper 2013) but capped at a smaller set-point. Raising the deep-zone volume set-point (more hypertrophy-like swelling in aligned stacks) would thicken the cartilage. If coupled to replacement at the tidemark, that would advance bone.
- Adult articular chondrocytes are poor responders. The responsive progenitor reservoirs are the **synovium (Prg4+ CD44+ P75+)** and the **groove of Ranvier / perichondrial ring** at the cartilage margin. Any adult articular growth program would draw cells mainly from these margins and the surface, not from the cartilage interior.
- Friction increases turnover/proliferation. That fits the shear → TGF-β → chondroprogenitor loop.
