# PMID 42552306 — Human growth plates house resting zone sub-populations with features of quiescent stem cells (2026, Bone Res)

Read in full (PMC, ~44k chars).

**Material.** Distal femur and proximal tibia biopsies from 8 healthy adolescents (12–14 y) undergoing epiphysiodesis for tall stature. Spatial transcriptomics (Visium RRST, CytAssist, Visium HD), RNAscope, TEM, ex vivo thymidine-analogue culture. Mouse matrix-vesicle proteomics.

**Findings.**
- **Quiescence.** Human resting-zone (RZ) chondrocytes have low mRNA, predominantly nuclear mRNA, more heterochromatin and more lysosomes. They re-enter the cycle ex vivo (IdU/CldU labelling after removal from tissue).
  - Hallmarks of quiescent adult stem cells; the same pattern holds in mouse label-retaining cells.
- **Markers.** 138 zone markers. RZ-specific: CHRDL2 (BMP antagonist), SFRP5 (Wnt antagonist), UCMA, ZNF550.
  - Pathway analysis: RZ maintenance involves *repressed BMP and Wnt*.
  - CHRDL2/SFRP5+ cells are the *least* quiescent RZ cells. ZNF550-enriched spots are most quiescent.
- **Other zones.** NKX3-2 is enriched in the proliferative zone (represses hypertrophy). SGMS2 is a new hypertrophic marker that sits in matrix vesicles and promotes mineralization.
- **Caveats.** Authors stress there is no direct evidence of a human epiphyseal stem-cell niche. Human RZs are large compared with rodents.

**What it establishes for post-fusion growth.**
- Human growth-plate progenitors are quiescent, stem-like cells that leave quiescence when freed from their milieu (ex vivo). Their environment, not only intrinsic exhaustion, holds them in G0.
- Molecular niche signature: Wnt- and BMP-inhibited.
- After fusion these cells are physically gone (replaced by bone), so it does not show adult persistence.

**Levers.**
- A de novo adult growth zone would need a Wnt/BMP-low pocket for its progenitor compartment. That contrasts with the Wnt/BMP-high environment of ossifying bone.
- Mechanical relevance: Wnt/β-catenin is strongly mechanosensitive in bone, rising under load. So the progenitor compartment should be *mechanically quiet* (shielded), consistent with the SOC-shield idea.
