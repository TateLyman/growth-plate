# 39402753 — Senolytic therapy combining dasatinib and quercetin restores the chondrogenic phenotype of human osteoarthritic chondrocytes

**Citation.** Aging Cell 2025;24:e14361. PMC11995296. Read in full (Results §1–6, Discussion).

## Model / method
Human articular chondrocytes (hAC) from OA patients, used at **high passage (p ≥ 4) as a model of dedifferentiation/senescence**, plus **intact human cartilage explants** (OARSI grade 1–2, moderately degenerated) and porcine cartilage. Treatments: **dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q) for 3 days**, then analysed immediately (d3) and 4 d after withdrawal (d7), also d14 in tissue. Comparators: dasatinib alone, quercetin alone, **navitoclax** (BCL-2-family senolytic). Doxorubicin-induced senescent cells used as a SASP-conditioned-medium source. ATDC5 as a chondroprogenitor line. Bulk RNA-seq, 3-D pellet redifferentiation assay (28 d), CPII ELISA, IHC.

## Exact numbers / findings
- **Senolysis confirmed**: cytotoxic selectively to high-passage hAC; **p16/CDKN2A-positive cells markedly reduced in human cartilage explants (P < 0.0001)**. CDKN1A/p21 mRNA fell only 4 d after withdrawal.
- **Chondroanabolic restoration — the headline.** ACAN up at d3 and d7; **COL2A1 up ~7-fold at d3 and ~30-fold at d7** (i.e. the effect grew after the drug was removed); SOX9 up at d3. More GAG (Alcian blue), more COL2 protein by IF, and in human cartilage *tissue* confirmed by CPII ELISA and COL2 IHC.
- **Redifferentiation capacity restored**: 3-day D+Q pretreatment significantly improved the 28-day 3-D pellet chondrogenesis score of dedifferentiated hAC.
- **It is dasatinib, not senolysis.** Dasatinib alone reproduced the effect (SOX9 up at d3, COL2A1 up at d7, more GAG). **Quercetin alone did nothing.** **Navitoclax cleared p16+ cells just as well but did NOT raise COL2A1 or ACAN and did NOT restore redifferentiation capacity.** The authors' conclusion: "the elimination of senescent cells alone does not promote chondroanabolism and this effect is particularly related to dasatinib."
- **Proliferation went down, not up.** Ki-67+ fraction fell; alamarBlue growth rate fell after withdrawal; dasatinib reduced proliferation dose-dependently; RNA-seq showed downregulation of cell-cycle, DNA replication, p53 and cellular-senescence gene sets.
- **Mechanism — YAP-1 inhibition.** YAP1 mRNA slightly down; **YAP target genes CTGF, BIRC5 (survivin) and CYR61 all downregulated**; phalloidin showed clear cytoskeletal remodelling after dasatinib.
- RNA-seq: **348 up, 426 down**. Up: COL2A1, COL9A1/2/3, COL11A2, MATN4, SOX9, SNORC, and growth factors **FGF18, IGF1, TGFB2**; FGFR2/3/4 up with a **reduced FGFR1/FGFR3 ratio** (a shift toward chondroanabolic FGFR signalling); SERPINA1/A3 (α1-antitrypsin); SOD3. Down: IGFBP1 and IGFBP3 (the anti-anabolic IGF-binding proteins), IL6, IL7R, IL26, IL32, CXCL1, MMP13, **ADAMTS-1 and ADAMTS-5**, RIPK3. KEGG up: PI3K-Akt, TGF-β; FoxO regulated both ways.
- **Paracrine**: medium from senescent or high-passage cells suppressed COL2A1 in healthy low-passage hAC; medium from D+Q-treated cells *raised* it. The effect propagates through the secretome.

## What it establishes for post-fusion growth
1. **Dasatinib — an approved oral drug — restores the chondrogenic programme of adult human chondrocytes, in human tissue, from a 3-day exposure, with the effect still growing 4 days after withdrawal.** COL2A1 at ~30-fold is not a marginal signal. The relevant conclusion is the same one [38664820](38664820.md) and [41786976](41786976.md) reach from different directions: the adult human chondrocyte phenotype is a *maintained* state that existing drugs can shift.
2. **But once again: more matrix, fewer cells.** Ki-67 down, growth rate down, cell-cycle gene sets down. This is the third independent compound in this phase ([38664820](38664820.md) T3, [41786976](41786976.md) OSK, and now dasatinib) that improves adult cartilage *quality* while leaving or reducing cell *number*. Across this entire literature I have not found a compound that increases chondrocyte number in adult tissue without also being an oncogenic or HO-driving signal. That is the central, repeatable obstacle to a compound-only route to centimetres: **longitudinal growth is cell-number-limited, and the safe compounds are all matrix-limited.**
3. **Senolysis is not the active principle, which kills a tempting hypothesis.** Navitoclax cleared the same senescent cells and produced none of the anabolic effect. So "clear senescent chondrocytes and the tissue will grow again" is directly contradicted by the cleanest available control. Growth-plate senescence being progenitor exhaustion ([39563452](39563452.md), [19098525](19098525.md)) does **not** imply that removing senescent cells restores growth capacity.
4. **The mechanism is YAP inhibition — the same node, opposite sign, as Yoda1.** Dasatinib suppresses YAP/CTGF/CYR61 and makes cartilage; Piezo1 activation raises YAP/β-catenin and converts cartilage to bone ([35844802](35844802.md)); FOP progenitors raise YAP and make ectopic bone ([38672135](38672135.md)); hypertrophic chondrocytes die above ~0.023 MPa through YAP–p73–caspase-3 ([33063669](33063669.md)). **YAP/TAZ activity is the single recurring switch between "stay cartilage" and "become bone" across every arm of this project.** Low YAP = cartilage maintained; high YAP = conversion and ossification. That is the axis a staged intervention would have to cycle, and both directions have existing small molecules (dasatinib / verteporfin down; Yoda1 / Sirt1 activators up).
5. **The IGF arm is unlocked, not just the matrix arm.** IGF1 up, IGFBP1 and IGFBP3 down — i.e. both more ligand and less sequestration. IGF-1 is the driver of the third, largest phase of hypertrophic chondrocyte swelling (Cooper; [31787777](31787777.md)), which is the actual length actuator. A drug that raises local free IGF-1 in adult cartilage is acting on the right variable.
6. **FGFR1/FGFR3 ratio as a tunable.** FGFR3 activation shortens bone through the plate (achondroplasia) and all the approved drugs inhibit it ([42650460](42650460.md)); here the ratio shift is described as pro-anabolic in articular chondrocytes. Opposite signs in different tissues; a caution against reading any FGFR result across compartments.

## Levers
- **Dasatinib** — oral, approved, 3-day pulse sufficient, effect persists and amplifies after withdrawal, works in intact human cartilage. Mechanism: YAP-1 inhibition + SASP suppression + IGF/FGF18 release.
- **YAP/TAZ as the cartilage↔bone switch**, with existing agents in both directions.
- **IGFBP1/IGFBP3 suppression** as a way to raise local free IGF-1 without giving IGF-1.
- **FGF18** (sprifermin exists as a clinical cartilage anabolic) and **TGFB2** as the endogenous anabolic factors induced.
- **Anti-lever: senolytics as a class.** Navitoclax is the control that says senolysis per se does nothing here.

## Honest limits
- In vitro and ex vivo only. High-passage cells are a model of senescence/dedifferentiation, not of an aged plate.
- OA cartilage from OA patients; no healthy young human tissue.
- Small n (mostly 3–8), several results "a clear trend" without significance (SASP factors).
- **Nothing was measured in millimetres.** No thickness, no mass, no length, no mechanics. Gene expression and staining only.
- Dasatinib is a multi-kinase (BCR-ABL/SRC) inhibitor with a substantial systemic toxicity profile, including pleural effusion and pulmonary hypertension. Nothing here speaks to chronic use in a healthy person.
- No growth plate anywhere in the paper.

## Chase
- Nie et al. 2019 — dasatinib-mediated YAP-1 inhibition (the proposed mechanism).
- Kirsch et al. 2022 — the doxorubicin-induced chondrocyte senescence model used here.
- Jeon 2018 / Coryell 2021 / McCulloch 2017 — chondrocyte SASP and senescent-cell clearance in joints.
- Any study measuring **cartilage thickness or volume** after dasatinib or D+Q in vivo. None cited; this is the obvious missing experiment.
