# PMID 38394209 — Identification of the metaphyseal skeletal stem cell building trabecular bone (2024, Sci Adv)

Read in full (PMC, ~59k chars).

**Model.** Mice. scRNA-seq (P7.5) of skeletal cells and Col10a1-traced hypertrophic chondrocyte (HC) progeny. Sstr2-CreERT2 and Sstr2-CreXER knock-ins; Col10a1-Cre;Pdgfrb-LSL-Dre dual tracing (CPYT); Confetti clonal tracing; kidney-capsule and bone-injury transplants; Hgs deletion.

**Findings.**
- Metaphyseal SSCs (mpSSC: Sstr2+ or Pdgfrb+Kitl−) sit immediately beneath the growth plate and derive *exclusively* from hypertrophic chondrocytes, which dedifferentiate into them.
- Short-chase HC progeny are 51.4±8.8% Sstr2+ at P11 and 45.5±11.3% at P40.
- They are clonal, self-renewing, trilineage (9/30 clones; 3/30 after two passages). They make "bony organoids" with Sox9+ cartilage in the kidney capsule and repopulate callus after bone injury, including Sox9+ chondrocytes.
- mpSSC-traced cells form >90% of HC descendants at P15–P480.
- mpSSC Ki67+ fraction declines: 38.2% (P7), 19.0% (P30), 9.0% (P180). They persist in old mice.
- Hgs deletion in HCs blocks HC→mpSSC conversion and reduces trabecular bone.

**What it establishes for post-fusion growth.**
- Hypertrophic chondrocytes are not end-stage. They dedifferentiate into stem cells that can re-make cartilage in injury contexts.
- In adult (P480) mice, cells derived from former growth-plate cartilage persist in marrow as stem cells with chondrogenic potential.
- So in adults, marrow and metaphyseal stromal populations retain growth-plate ancestry and cartilage-forming capacity, given the right context (callus).

**Levers.**
- The adult marrow/metaphyseal stroma contains chondrocompetent SSCs, a possible seed for an induced cartilage layer *inside* bone.
- Callus formation proves adult SSCs can build growth-plate-like cartilage (Sox9+) when the mechanical/biological context demands it. Fracture callus is the canonical adult endochondral program, triggered by instability, i.e. interfragmentary motion.
