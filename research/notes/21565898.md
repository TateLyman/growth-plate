# PMID 21565898 — Intema F, ... Lafeber FP, Mastbergen SC. Tissue structure modification in knee osteoarthritis by use of joint distraction: an open 1-year pilot study (2011, Ann Rheum Dis)

Read in full (PMC, ~23k chars).

**Design.**
- 20 adults (48±7 y) with severe tibiofemoral OA. Surgical knee joint distraction (KJD) with an external fixator: two spring-loaded monotubes on bone pins.
- ~5 mm distraction for 2 months with **full weight-bearing allowed; the springs permit ~3 mm axial oscillation without articular contact**. CPM sessions every 2 weeks.
- Follow-up 1 year: radiography (KIDA), quantitative MRI, PIIANP/CTXII.

**Results.**
- Mean joint space width (JSW) 2.7 → 3.6 mm; minimum JSW 1.0 → 1.9 mm.
- **MRI cartilage thickness (ThCtAB) of the affected compartment 2.4 → 3.0 mm (p<0.001)**: femur 1.00 → 1.41, tibia 1.36 → 1.56 mm. Denuded bone 22% → 5%.
- **~+0.9 mm/year** of cartilage thickening, progressive *after* frame removal and sustained for years in early patients. For comparison, typical OA loss is ≤0.2 mm/year.
- Collagen II synthesis/breakdown ratio rose. Subchondral density fell during distraction, then normalised.
- WOMAC and pain improved markedly.
- Complications: pin-tract infections 17/20, pulmonary embolism 2/20.
- Authors' mechanism: **removal of contact stress while preserving intermittent fluid-pressure oscillations** (springs) restores chondrocyte anabolism. Subchondral bone resorption/turnover releases TGF-β/BMP/IGF stored in bone matrix.

**What it establishes for the theory (key human evidence).**
- **Adult human articular cartilage (age ~48) can regrow by ~0.5–0.6 mm within a year** once compressive contact is removed and intermittent hydrostatic pressure is kept. This is the largest documented adult-human cartilage thickening, and it is mechanical in origin.
- It validates the "unloaded but pressure-oscillated" window (Phase 3 of the theory) as strongly anabolic in adult human cartilage.
- Bone turnover under the cartilage during offloading (resorption, then normalisation) is the coupling step that would be needed for net bone-end advance.
- KJD itself is surgical (pins) and an established procedure, so it is excluded as the answer. Its **mechanical principle** (separation + spring-modulated oscillation + time) is what the theory repurposes non-invasively.
- What KJD did *not* show: bone lengthening. Cartilage thickened, but there is no evidence the tidemark/subchondral front advanced axially. The gain is cartilage volume, not bone. Converting it to bone requires the conversion step.

**Chase.** Wiegant 2013 (23954704, 2-year sustained); Jansen/Mastbergen randomised KJD vs HTO/TKA trials; van Valburg 1995 (7559696, ankle distraction); animal KJD histology (canine Groove-model distraction studies).
