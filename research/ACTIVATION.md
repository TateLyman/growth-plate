# Activating the boundary: the least-complex route

How to switch on the two-domain growth interface in an adult, in situ, without manufacturing it. Third document in the series, after [THEORY.md](THEORY.md) (mechanics) and [COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md) (pharmacology). Built from the same 84 full texts, notes in [notes/](notes/).

---

## 0. The answer in one paragraph

**You don't build the boundary. It is already there, in every synovial joint, correctly polarised — and it is stalled.** Adult articular cartilage is a growth plate turned upside down: a self-renewing Prg4+ progenitor layer at the surface, vertical cell stacks with aligned collagen below it, a deep zone whose cells are the same lineage as hypertrophic chondrocytes, and an ossification front at the tidemark that the same cells cross. Its growth direction is surface → bone, which lengthens the segment. Growth rate in any such apparatus is governed by **G = n × h_max** — a cell-production rate times a terminal cell height ([23009361](notes/23009361.md)) — and in adult articular cartilage **both terms are suppressed, by different mechanisms, each with its own existing lever**. *n* is frozen: clones labelled at P21 never reach the tidemark in 18 months, where embryonic clones cross it by 12 ([25603997](notes/25603997.md)). *h_max* is halved: adult deep-zone chondrocytes are **under 50% the diameter of metaphyseal growth-plate hypertrophic cells**, with an 8-fold volume change already demonstrated in the same cells at the same site at a different age ([28438606](notes/28438606.md)). **Restarting the treadmill means un-freezing *n* at the surface and raising *h_max* at depth, and keeping the two rates matched so thickness holds constant while length accrues.** That is the whole mechanism. §5 reduces it to two inputs.

## 1. The plate is already there

The mapping, term by term. This table is the document.

| Juvenile physis | Adult articular cartilage | Present? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resting zone**: PTHrP+ self-renewing progenitors, anti-exhaustion gradient | **Superficial zone**: Prg4+ self-renewing progenitors; only superficial-zone cells are competent to induce Prg4 | **yes**, quiescent | [25603997](notes/25603997.md), [24449269](notes/24449269.md) |
| **Proliferative zone**: clonal columns, 40 µm spacing, ~22 cells/column | **Mid/deep zone**: vertical non-clonal "stacks", collagen anisotropic and aligned with them | **yes**, ~3% EdU at P28, minimal after P7 | [28438606](notes/28438606.md), [23009361](notes/23009361.md) |
| **Hypertrophic zone**: h_max ≈ 35 µm, swells ~20-fold in 12 h | **Deep zone**: cell diameter **<50%** of metaphyseal hypertrophic cells, ~60% of SOC hypertrophic cells; 8-fold volume rise P14→P28 | **yes**, set-point halved | [28438606](notes/28438606.md), [33063669](notes/33063669.md) |
| **Chondro-osseous junction**: vascular invasion, cartilage→bone | **Tidemark / calcified cartilage**: Prg4-lineage cells cross it and are replaced by bone; adult cell death concentrated in the deep zone | **yes**, frozen in adults | [25603997](notes/25603997.md), [28438606](notes/28438606.md) |
| **SOC** shielding the fragile hypertrophic cells from compressive and deviatoric stress | **Poroelastic cartilage** converting surface contact into interstitial fluid pressure (hydrostatic, which hypertrophic cells tolerate) | **probably** — unverified, see §9 | [33063669](notes/33063669.md) |
| **Polarity**: growth away from the ossification centre | **Polarity**: surface → bone, i.e. the bone end advances toward the joint and the segment lengthens | **yes, already correct** | [25603997](notes/25603997.md) |
| **Progenitor top-up**: groove of Ranvier / perichondrial ring | **Groove of Ranvier** at the cartilage margin; **synovial Prg4-lineage CD44+/P75+ MSC-like cells — 70% proliferate and fill a defect** | **yes**, injury-gated | [28438606](notes/28438606.md) |

Nothing in that column has to be built, implanted, printed or manufactured. Every element of the two-domain construct in [40118845](notes/40118845.md) — a hypertrophy-resistant progenitor domain, an ossification-committed domain, the interface between them, the polarity, the aligned matrix channels, the progenitor reservoir — is **already present and already in the right arrangement** in an adult knee, hip or ankle. The construct is a from-scratch reproduction of what an articular surface is.

**The two opposing readings reconcile into the same conclusion.** Kozhemyakina ([25603997](notes/25603997.md)) reads articular growth as appositional from a surface pool; Decker ([28438606](notes/28438606.md)) reads it as cell-volume increase plus stack rearrangement with minimal proliferation. Decker suspects Kozhemyakina's proliferation was inflated by the Prg4-heterozygous reporter — less lubricin, more friction, more turnover. **Both are describing G = n × h_max, one term each.** Kozhemyakina measured *n*; Decker measured *h_max*. And Decker's proposed confound is itself a third lever (§4, Stage 1b).

## 2. Why this is the least-complex route

Four things it does not require.

1. **No new tissue.** The interface exists. The alternative — the hPSC two-domain construct — needs a reporter line, a 6-day sclerotome induction, two divergent 14-day inductions, a fusion step, and transplantation, and it has only ever been grown ectopically in a mouse kidney capsule ([40118845](notes/40118845.md)).
2. **No implant, scaffold or print.** The aligned collagen that channels swelling longitudinally is already there, already anisotropic, already aligned with the stacks ([28438606](notes/28438606.md)).
3. **No polarity engineering.** Surface-to-bone is the direction that lengthens the segment. Get it wrong and you build an osteophyte; here the anatomy supplies it for free.
4. **No new cells — and this is the one that matters.** See §3.

## 3. The volume actuator goes around the proliferation wall

[COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md) §2 established the obstacle that kills every compound route: **adult longitudinal growth is cell-number-limited, and every safe compound raises matrix while leaving cell number flat or lower.** T3, dasatinib, OSK, kartogenin, Yoda1, sprifermin — six agents, same shape.

**The hypertrophic volume set-point is not a cell-number variable.** It is a per-cell volume change, and it is the actual length actuator: hypertrophic chondrocytes swell ~20-fold in 12 hours ([33063669](notes/33063669.md)), and the largest and most IGF-1-dependent phase of that swelling is what produces length (Cooper, via [31787777](notes/31787777.md)). In **G = n × h_max**, *h_max* is the term that does not need a single extra mitosis.

And the headroom is measured, in the same cell type, at the same anatomical site:
- Adult articular deep-zone chondrocytes: **<50% the diameter** of metaphyseal growth-plate hypertrophic cells, ~60% of SOC hypertrophic cells.
- Diameter scales as the cube root of volume, so <50% diameter is **<12.5% of the volume**.
- Those same cells already execute an **~8-fold volume increase** between P14 and P28.
- Decker's own conclusion: "**chondrocytes set their volume by location.**"

A set-point determined by location is a set-point determined by local signalling and mechanics — which means it is addressable, and it is the only quantity in this entire corpus with a demonstrated order-of-magnitude of unused range in adult tissue.

**So the least-complex activation is not "make more chondrocytes." It is "let the ones already stacked in the right direction swell to the size they are capable of, and convert the bottom of the stack to bone at the same rate the top is replenished."**

## 4. The chain

Each stage names what it has to achieve, the input that achieves it, and what is already proven.

### Stage 1 — un-freeze *n*: wake the surface progenitor pool
**Input: cyclic articulating motion.** Fluid-flow shear at the surface (~7.6 dyn/cm² laminar) → secreted PGE2 (EP2→PKA) + **PTHrP** (PTH1R→PKA) + ATP (P2X7/pannexin-1, P2Y2→Ca²⁺→calcineurin→CRTC1/2) → convergence on **CREB** at two CRE sites in the *Prg4* locus. One month of voluntary wheel running in **3-month-old adult** mice roughly **doubles Prg4-expressing superficial cells**, mostly on the tibial plateau, with raised phospho-CREB and nuclear CRTC1 in vivo ([24449269](notes/24449269.md)). Expansion is greatest in the central tibial condyle, **where load is highest** ([25603997](notes/25603997.md)).

This also builds the **surface PTHrP source** — the same element that sustains the mandibular condyle and protects it from progenitor exhaustion ([38058151](notes/38058151.md), [28643917](notes/28643917.md)), which is why the condyle never fuses. It is the anti-exhaustion arm of the design, supplied mechanically.

Motion does a second job here. At an articulation, periosteal Sox9+Runx2+ progenitors stay cartilage **only while movement continues**; paralysis, or blocking stretch-activated channels, turns that cartilage into well-ossified bone ([21600197](notes/21600197.md)). Movement is the switch that holds joint-margin tissue in a cartilage state.

And it supplies the fate fork for free. In [37126720](notes/37126720.md) the permanent-versus-transient cartilage decision is TGF-β3 present or absent. **Sliding shear physically activates latent synovial TGF-β** ([22858668](notes/22858668.md)) — no exogenous protein required.

*Compound amplifiers at this node, if the mechanical input alone is insufficient:* **sprifermin/FGF-18** (+425 µm thickening at 24 months, the only compound with an RCT showing adult human cartilage thickening, [26138203](notes/26138203.md)); **dasatinib** (COL2A1 ~30-fold, and critically **IGF1 up with IGFBP1 and IGFBP3 down**, i.e. more free IGF-1 locally — the Phase-3 swelling driver, [39402753](notes/39402753.md)); **kartogenin** (condensation by day 3, hypertrophy-silent, [33467170](notes/33467170.md)). *Anti-lever:* **COX-2 inhibitors**. Celecoxib cut the running→Prg4 response to ~65% ([24449269](notes/24449269.md)) and blunted physeal repair ([37483459](notes/37483459.md)). NSAIDs are directly counterproductive at this node.

### Stage 1b — the friction knob (hypothesis, flagged)
Decker's proposed confound is a lever: **reduced boundary lubrication raises turnover.** Prg4-null surfaces become chondrogenic and endochondral for 12 months ([25603997](notes/25603997.md) and its Prg4-null references). But running *raises* Prg4. The resolution I'd propose, and it is untested: **high Prg4 maintains the surface progenitor pool (Stage 1); low Prg4 / high friction drives conversion (Stage 3).** If so, lubrication state is a cyclable variable serving opposite stages. Stated as a hypothesis, not a finding.

### Stage 2 — raise *h_max*: lift the deep-zone volume set-point
**This is the actuator and the crux.** The target: move adult deep-zone cells from <50% toward the metaphyseal hypertrophic diameter, inside collagen channels that are already aligned to direct the swelling longitudinally.

Known inputs: **local free IGF-1** (drives the largest phase of hypertrophic swelling; raised endogenously by dasatinib's suppression of IGFBP1/3, and deliverable in targeted form by the **cartilage-targeting scFv–IGF-1 fusion** that already exists, [37483459](notes/37483459.md)); **subchondral bone turnover**, which during joint offloading resorbs and **releases TGF-β, BMP and IGF stored in the bone matrix** ([21565898](notes/21565898.md)) — the body's own local depot, opened mechanically; and **T3** as the terminal-maturation licence (EPAS1, ANKH, PHOSPHO1 up in **adult human** explants aged 59–76, [38664820](notes/38664820.md)).

**Mechanics alone will not do this stage.** +0.1 MPa sustained tension buys only ~+5% growth; "the dominant determinant is whether a proliferating, hypertrophying column apparatus exists at all" ([23009361](notes/23009361.md)). Mechanics builds and holds the apparatus; it does not set the set-point. Stage 2 is where a compound is genuinely required rather than merely helpful.

### Stage 3 — license conversion at the tidemark, and only there
**Input: Piezo1 → YAP/β-catenin → BMP2 + VEGFA, from the resident cell.** Piezo1 is enriched specifically in the chondrocyte lineage (Piezo2 is absent from chondrocytes) and in Gli1+ periosteal progenitors. Chondrocyte-specific Piezo1 deletion **widens the plate and blocks its endochondral ossification**; Yoda1 does the reverse, and in distraction osteogenesis it accelerated gap mineralisation while the knockout left an unbridged fibrous gap ([40999873](notes/40999873.md), [35844802](notes/35844802.md)). **Sirt1 opens Piezo1 by deacetylation at K2174 even when the force-transduction residues are mutated** — so SRT2104 and resveratrol reach the channel by a route that does not need mechanics, and an oral acid-targeted formulation that localises only to active remodelling sites already exists.

**Why this stays at depth rather than ossifying the whole tissue** is the stress-type gradient that articular cartilage already maintains: hydrostatic-dominant and low-deviatoric at the surface (maintains cartilage, keeps YAP low), rising principal-compressive and deviatoric at depth (licenses ossification, raises YAP). Chondrocyte death under load tracks **principal compressive stress, not hydrostatic** ([33063669](notes/33063669.md)). The same gradient is what [37483459](notes/37483459.md) measures as a mechanoregulatory signal falling along the long axis through the hypertrophic zone, progressively licensing ossification.

### Stage 4 — shield the hypertrophic cells
Hypertrophic chondrocytes are the most mechanically fragile cells in the body — ~25% the stiffness of columnar chondrocytes, dying by **YAP–p73–caspase-3** at loads as low as **0.2 N (~0.023 MPa)** without a secondary ossification centre to shield them, and tolerating 25× more with one ([33063669](notes/33063669.md)). Adult articular cartilage has no bony layer *above* its deep zone. Raise the deep-zone volume set-point under normal weight-bearing contact and you should kill exactly the cells you are trying to enlarge. This is a plausible reason articular cartilage evolved not to hypertrophy.

**The solution is already measured in adult humans, and it is the KJD mechanism: remove contact stress while preserving intermittent fluid-pressure oscillation.** Knee joint distraction — ~5 mm separation for two months, springs permitting ~3 mm axial oscillation without articular contact, full weight-bearing allowed — took affected-compartment cartilage thickness from **2.4 → 3.0 mm at one year** (femur 1.00→1.41, tibia 1.36→1.56 mm), denuded bone 22% → 5%, progressing at ~0.9 mm/year and sustained for years, against a typical OA loss of ≤0.2 mm/year ([21565898](notes/21565898.md)). That is the largest documented adult-human cartilage thickening and it is mechanical in origin. It is simultaneously the shield and the strongest Stage-1/Stage-2 input.

### Stage 5 — the ratchet: cycle, because the stages want opposite mechanical states
Stage 1 wants articulating contact and shear. Stages 2 and 4 want contact removed with oscillation preserved. They cannot run at once, and two independent lines say a sustained unchanging stimulus fails anyway: static tension's growth-promoting effect **fades to control levels** with time, and the condylar advancement response decays after ~3 weeks, so a sustained effect needs *changing* stimuli ([31787777](notes/31787777.md)). **Dynamic tension is the most anabolic regime for growth cartilage**; static 0.2 MPa is more damaging than dynamic 0.2 ± 0.06 MPa at 0.1 Hz.

There is also a specific reason to put conversion in the unloaded phase: unweighting is reported to **accelerate tidemark advancement** (9101369 — title-level only, *not read*, see §9), and recumbency opens an output window in the growth plate ([19540500](notes/19540500.md), 15502578 needed). Loaded phase: addition and recruitment. Unloaded-but-oscillated phase: swelling and conversion. **Alternate.**

## 5. The minimum viable version

Strip everything optional. What remains:

> **Two inputs.**
> **(1) Cyclic articular loading, phased against unloaded-but-pressure-oscillated intervals** — which supplies, for free: surface progenitor recruitment and Prg4/PTHrP (Stage 1), latent TGF-β activation and therefore the permanent-cartilage fate fork, the stress-type gradient that keeps ossification at depth (Stage 3), hypertrophic-cell shielding during the output phase (Stage 4), subchondral release of stored IGF/TGF-β/BMP (Stage 2), and the anti-adaptation cycling (Stage 5).
> **(2) One sustained agent that raises the deep-zone volume set-point and licenses the conversion front** — the *h_max* term and the throughput at the tidemark, which mechanics demonstrably cannot supply ([23009361](notes/23009361.md): +5% from +0.1 MPa).

Everything in [COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md) beyond input (2) is amplification of a stage that the mechanical input already addresses. **Six of the seven jobs are done by one input that is not a drug, not a device and not a surgery — it is phased loading of a joint.** That is why this is the least-complex route: the boundary, the polarity, the channels, the progenitors and the shield are anatomical givens, and one physical variable drives six of the seven stages.

Optional, in descending order of evidence: **sprifermin** at Stage 1 (adult human RCT), **dasatinib** at Stage 1–2 (raises free IGF-1 by releasing it from IGFBPs, human tissue), **targeted scFv–IGF-1** at Stage 2 (built and tested), **T3** at Stage 2–3 (adult human explants, and the highest OA risk — see §9), **kartogenin** at Stage 1 (rat, in vitro only).

One addition worth its complexity: a **single marrow-access or margin event** at the start. Adult articular chondrocytes stay passive on injury; the responders are **synovial Prg4-lineage CD44+/P75+ MSC-like cells, 70% of which proliferate and fill a defect** ([28438606](notes/28438606.md)), plus the groove of Ranvier. The de novo endochondral literature says a signal without an injury does not initiate ([28629737](notes/28629737.md)), and in a primed adult the sufficient injury was **a 5 mm shallow skin cut** ([28645539](notes/28645539.md)). Bone-marrow stimulation is an existing routine procedure and has already been combined with cartilage cell sheets in eight patients ([37483459](notes/37483459.md)). It buys the *n* term a starting bolus. It also makes this a procedure rather than a behaviour, which is the complexity cost.

## 6. The coupling condition is the whole ballgame

**Length accrues only if the rate of addition at the surface equals or exceeds the rate of conversion at the tidemark.** Three outcomes, and two of them are failures already documented in this corpus:

| Addition vs conversion | Result | Already observed as |
|---|---|---|
| addition **>** conversion | cartilage thickens, **no length** | palovarotene (thicker articular cartilage, osteochondral masses, **tibia 13–19% shorter**, [30226468](notes/30226468.md)); chondrocyte Piezo1 cKO (**wider plate**, ossification blocked, [40999873](notes/40999873.md)); systemic anti-VEGF (**hypertrophic zone expanded**, shortening worse, [37483459](notes/37483459.md)); PKD inhibition (more cartilage, less bone, delayed healing); IGF-I + marrow cells on PLGA (**"a large, dense area of cartilage… mostly hypertrophic chondrocytes"**, no angular correction, [26309783](notes/26309783.md)) |
| addition **<** conversion | cartilage thins, **this is osteoarthritis** | the entire OA literature; T3's transcriptional signature overlaps **37 GWAS OA risk genes** ([38664820](notes/38664820.md)) |
| addition **≈** conversion | **treadmill: constant thickness, bone end advances, segment lengthens** | a growth plate |

**KJD achieved exactly half of it.** Cartilage thickened by 0.6 mm in a year in adult humans, and there is no evidence the tidemark or subchondral front advanced axially ([21565898](notes/21565898.md)). The gain was cartilage volume, not bone. **Stage 3 is the missing half, and Piezo1/Sirt1 is the only existing lever in this corpus that acts on it.**

So the design problem is not biological, it is a **control problem**: two rates, opposite failure modes on either side, and a measurable error signal (cartilage thickness by quantitative MRI, which KJD already used). Hold thickness constant while the bone end advances, and you are growing. That framing is what makes this tractable, and it is also what makes "take these compounds" the wrong shape of answer — an open-loop push on either rate lands in one of the two failure rows.

## 7. Can it reach centimetres?

Honest arithmetic with the assumptions stated, because the binding number is one I have not read.

**Per-surface ceiling.** Four articular surfaces per leg lie on the length axis: femoral head, distal femur, proximal tibia, distal tibia. Their corresponding juvenile physes contribute roughly 0.8, 0.5(–0.9), 0.3 and 0.2 cm/year in mid-childhood — call it **~1.8 cm/year per leg at full juvenile physeal throughput**.

**Restored fraction.** Restoring the articular treadmill to a given fraction of juvenile throughput across those four surfaces gives:

| Restored fraction of juvenile physeal throughput | Gain per leg per year |
|---|---|
| 10% | ~0.18 cm |
| 25% | ~0.45 cm |
| 50% | ~0.9 cm |
| 100% | ~1.8 cm |

**Why 25–50% is not absurd.** *h_max* has a measured ~2× linear and ~8× volumetric headroom in the same cells at the same site ([28438606](notes/28438606.md)). *n* is not zero but frozen — P21 clones reach two-thirds of depth in 18 months, just never the tidemark ([25603997](notes/25603997.md)) — and adult wheel running already **doubles** the Prg4+ surface pool in a month ([24449269](notes/24449269.md)), while adult human cartilage already thickens at ~0.9 mm/year under KJD alone ([21565898](notes/21565898.md)).

**Why it might be far worse.** Nothing above establishes that the tidemark can be driven at all in an adult. **The adult human tidemark advance rate is the number this entire calculation rests on, and I have not read it** — it is Tier 0 of [NEEDED_FULLTEXTS.md](NEEDED_FULLTEXTS.md) (7410471, 2124371, 9101369, 3446655, 893504). Until those are in hand, §7 is a bound-setting exercise, not a prediction. It also assumes four surfaces can be driven simultaneously, that the knee's two surfaces do not interfere, and that nothing adapts away — and adaptation is exactly what [31787777](notes/31787777.md) documents.

This is still roughly **5–20× the 0.5–2.5 mm/year/leg** I estimated in [THEORY.md](THEORY.md) §5, and the difference is entirely the *h_max* term, which that estimate omitted because it only counted tidemark advance.

## 8. The decisive first experiment

Designed so a negative result ends it, and so that it uses no cell manufacturing and no novel device.

**Does phased loading plus a conversion signal move the tidemark in a mature animal?** Mature rabbit or sheep — **not** rat or mouse, which never fuse their plates ([THEORY.md](THEORY.md) §7). Four arms: sham; phased loading alone (the KJD mechanical principle, cycled against articulation); conversion signal alone (Piezo1/Sirt1 route); both. Endpoints, in order of importance:
1. **Tidemark position by sequential fluorochrome labelling** — the direct measurement of whether the front advanced. This is the single number the whole scheme turns on.
2. Segment length by radiographic landmarks against the contralateral limb.
3. Cartilage thickness (the coupling error signal).
4. Deep-zone cell diameter (did *h_max* move?).
5. Prg4+ surface cell count and EdU (did *n* move?).
6. Osteophyte and OA scoring (which failure row are we in?).

**Kill criteria.** No tidemark advance in the combined arm → Stage 3 has no lever and the route is dead. Thickness rises with no front advance → the palovarotene row; the scheme is an osteophyte generator. Thickness falls → the OA row; stop. No change in deep-zone cell diameter → the *h_max* assumption of §3 is wrong, and with it the argument that this bypasses the proliferation wall.

The positive-control arm that makes the whole thing interpretable already exists: **the adult mandibular condyle**, which runs this exact apparatus, never fuses, and is mechanically reactivatable in adult primates ([THEORY.md](THEORY.md) §2, §7).

## 9. Honest tensions and unknowns

**Things I am asserting that the corpus does not settle.**

- **The adult tidemark advance rate is unknown to me.** Five papers, Tier 0, unread. §7 and arguably the whole scheme are hostage to it.
- **"Unweighting accelerates tidemark advancement"** (9101369) is a *title*, not a reading. Stage 5's rationale for putting conversion in the unloaded phase is weaker than it looks until that is read.
- **The poroelastic shield is assumed.** That surface contact arrives at the deep zone as hydrostatic rather than compressive stress is the biphasic-fluid-load-support literature (10710186, 14614932), both unread and both Tier 0. If it is wrong, Stage 4 has no shield, and raising *h_max* under load kills the cells.
- **Piezo1 has opposite signs in the two contexts I am chaining together.** Blocking stretch-activated channels at an *articulation* turns secondary cartilage into bone ([21600197](notes/21600197.md)); blocking Piezo1 in a *callus or plate* blocks ossification and widens the cartilage ([40999873](notes/40999873.md)). My proposed resolution — that mechanical signalling diverts uncommitted periosteal pre-osteoblasts out of intramembranous bone into the endochondral route, and then separately sets throughput along that route — is a hypothesis I constructed, not something either paper states. If it is wrong, Stage 3 may ossify the surface instead of the front, which is the osteophyte failure.
- **Stage 1b (lubrication as a cyclable variable) is my inference** from Decker's suggested confound plus the Prg4-null friction data. Untested.
- **Kozhemyakina vs Decker is not formally resolved.** I have treated them as measuring different terms of the same equation. That is the most parsimonious reading, not an established one.
- **The *h_max* headroom is a developmental observation, not a demonstrated adult response.** Deep-zone cells achieved an 8-fold volume rise between P14 and P28. Nobody has shown an *adult* deep-zone chondrocyte can be made to do it.
- **T3's signature is osteoarthritis.** Thirty-seven of its 247 differentially expressed genes in adult human cartilage are GWAS OA risk genes, and CCDC80, ANKH and CDON move the same way in T3-treated explants, early OA and progressive OA ([38664820](notes/38664820.md)). Stage 2–3 and the disease are, transcriptionally, close to the same instruction. §6's coupling condition is my answer to that, and it is an argument, not evidence.
- **Piezo1's expression outside cartilage** — red cells, endothelium, lung, bladder, pancreas, baroreceptors — and systemic Sirt1 activation are not characterised for this use.
- **No one has measured bone length under any of these agents.** That gap is flagged in [COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md) and it is still the single most conspicuous absence in the field.
- **The mechanics contradiction is unresolved**: [37483459](notes/37483459.md) says hydrostatic pressure drives ossification and dynamic compression drives growth; [19540500](notes/19540500.md) and [23009361](notes/23009361.md) say the opposite. Stage 3's entire spatial logic depends on which is right.

**On scope.** This document describes a mechanism and the experiment that would test it. It is not a protocol, and it contains no doses, schedules, combinations or sourcing, for the reasons set out in [COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md) §8. The specific point worth repeating here is §6: an open-loop push on either rate lands in one of the two documented failure modes — a joint full of osteophytes, or osteoarthritis — and both have been produced in animals by exactly the agents named above.

## 10. The chain, condensed

```
  ARTICULAR SURFACE                        G = n × h_max
  ─────────────────
  Prg4+ superficial progenitors   ← STAGE 1  cyclic articulating shear
  (self-renewing, quiescent)                 → FFSS → PGE2/PTHrP/ATP → CREB → Prg4
                                             (adult running doubles the pool)
          │  n  ← frozen: P21 clones never reach the tidemark in 18 mo
          ▼
  Vertical stacks, aligned collagen          [channels already present]
          │
          ▼
  Deep zone                        ← STAGE 2  raise the volume set-point
  <50% of hypertrophic diameter               local free IGF-1 (IGFBP release),
  8× volume headroom, same cells              subchondral stored TGF-β/BMP/IGF,
          │  h_max ← halved                   T3 terminal licence
          ▼
  ─── TIDEMARK ───────────────────  ← STAGE 3  license conversion, here only
  calcified cartilage                         Piezo1 → YAP/β-catenin → BMP2 + VEGFA
          │                                   (Sirt1 opens it without force)
          ▼
  SUBCHONDRAL BONE  ── advances ──►  segment lengthens

  THROUGHOUT:  STAGE 4  shield the hypertrophic cells — contact stress removed,
                        fluid oscillation preserved  (the KJD mechanism,
                        +0.6 mm/yr measured in adult humans)
               STAGE 5  alternate loaded and unloaded-oscillated phases,
                        because the stages want opposite mechanical states
                        and a static stimulus adapts away

  COUPLING:    addition ≈ conversion  →  constant thickness, advancing bone
               addition > conversion  →  osteophyte
               addition < conversion  →  osteoarthritis
```

**Six of the seven jobs are done by one non-pharmacological input. One job — the volume set-point and the conversion front — needs a compound, and Piezo1/Sirt1 is the only existing lever for it. The boundary does not need building; it needs un-freezing, and the rates need matching.**
